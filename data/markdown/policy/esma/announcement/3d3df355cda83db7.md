This document presents ESMA’s annual calculation of market capitalisation and market capitalisation ratios of EU
Member States for the reference years 2024 and 2025.
The figures are published in accordance with Article 20 of Directive (EU) 2025/50 (FASTER Directive) and the related
regulatory technical standards on the calculation methodology.
Under the FASTER Directive, Member States whose market capitalisation exceeds 1.5% of the total EU market
capitalisation for four consecutive years will be subject to specific requirements related to withholding tax relief. 
Based on the application of this methodology, twelve Member States are above the relevant threshold for both years
2024 and 2025 (FR, DE, NL, ES, SE, IT, IE, DK, BE, FI, LU and PL).
