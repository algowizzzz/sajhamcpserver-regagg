Instructions for Filling out the Quarterly Report of Employment Status Form 
1. Name of Originating Entity (OE): Fill in legal name of the entity as it appears on the New York issued license or registration certificate. 
2. NMLS Number: Fill in the unique identifier assigned to the Originating Entity by the Nationwide Multistate Licensing System & Registry (NMLS). 
3. Quarter Ending: Provide the quarter end date. Example, March 31, 20xx. 
4. Name of MLO: For this column, list only the MLOs hired or terminated during the quarter, regardless of whether such individuals are licensed by 
the Department. Note: All MLOs working under Temporary Authority must be reported. Name of the MLO must be the legal name exactly as it 
appears on the NMLS. 
5. MLO NMLS Number: Fill in the unique identifier assigned to the MLO by the NMLS. 
6. Date Hired: Provide the date during the quarter each MLO was hired by your company, regardless of whether the individuals began engaging in 
mortgage loan origination activities on such date . 
7. Date Terminated: Provide the date the MLO was terminated or voluntarily resigned 
8. Dismissal for Cause: Select “Yes” or “No” to indicate whether the MLO was dismissed for cause. 
9. Reason for Termination: Provide the reason the MLO was terminated . If the MLO was allowed to voluntarily resign to avoid termination for 
cause, you must provide the Department with information if the termination was relatedto a violation of applicable state or federal laws and regulation 
or alleged fraud.
