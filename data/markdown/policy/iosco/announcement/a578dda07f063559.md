1 
 
 
Resolution of the Presidents’ Committee 
regarding 
the Nominated Board Members 2026-2030 
 
(Resolution 1/2026) 
 
 
CONSIDERING that in July 2022, the Presidents Committee, pursuant to Section 17 
of the By -laws approved the composition of the IOSCO Board including the 19 
Nominated Members which were from jurisdictions with the largest markets (based on 
measures of equity market capitalization, debt market issuance, assets under 
management and derivatives trading) (Presidents Committee Resolution 1/2022); and  
 
 
CONSIDERING that Presidents Committee Resolution 1/2022 required that the list of 
Nominated Members be reviewed every four years to adequately reflect the evolution 
of the global securities markets; and 
 
 
CONSIDERING the framework to determine the largest markets adopted by the 
Presidents Committee in January 2019 (Presidents Committee Resolution 1/2019);  
 
 
 
IT IS AGREED that the list of Nominated Members for the period 2026-2030 shall be 
as set forth in Annex A below, replacing the list of Nominated Board Members for the 
period 2022- 2026 in Annex A to Resolution 1/2022 with effect from the inaugural 
meeting of the new IOSCO Board that will take place during the 2026 annual meeting 
of the organization.
