Action Plan for signing IOSCO MMoU 
 
Sl 
No. 
Questions Answers 
 
1 
 
Can you confirm that you have 
undertaken a self-assessment of your 
legislation using the MMoU 
questionnaire to check if it is sufficient 
to meet the standard of IOSCO MMoU?   
 
If according to the self-assessment you 
believe that your current legislation is 
sufficient to meet the standard of 
IOSCO MMoU, please go to question 3.   
 
 
 
2 
 
If your current legislation is not 
sufficient to meet the IOSCO MMoU 
standard, please provide us with some 
detail of the changes which need to be 
made and when and how do you plan to 
accomplish the changes? 
 
 
 
 
3 
 
 
 
Will you need to sign any cooperation 
agreement with any other authority (for 
example Central Bank, Anti Money 
Laundering Authority,  Company 
Registrar etc,)   in your jurisdiction to 
meet the standard of IOSCO MMoU 
questionnaire? 
 
If yes then please provide some detail 
including when and how you plan to 
accomplish this task. 
  
 
 
 
4 
 
Provide us with approximate dates of 
when you will be able to start filling in 
the MMoU questionnaire after you 
have accomplished the necessary 
changes.

5 
 
Provide us with approximate dates 
when the revision of the answers to the 
MMoU questionnaire will be 
completed?  
 
 
 
6 
 
Provide us with approximate dates of 
when the final answer to the MMoU 
questionnaire will be approved by the 
governing body of your authority?  
 
 
 
7 
 
Provide us with approximate dates of 
when the MMoU questionnaire will be 
submitted to IOSCO for verification? 
 
 
 
8 
 
Do you need Technical Assistance (TA) 
to amend your legislation?  
 
If yes, then please provide some detail 
including what type of TA you may 
need. 
 
 
 
 
Please note that your MMoU questionnaire is expected to be submitted to IOSCO for verification  
within one year after approval of your Associate Membership.
