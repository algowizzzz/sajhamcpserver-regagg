10th IOSCO/PIFS-Harvard Law School 
Global Certificate Program for Regulators of Securities Markets 
June 15 – 19, 2026, Madrid, Spain (Phase I) 
December 7 – 11, 2026, Cambridge, MA, United States (Phase II) 
 
Dear IOSCO Members, 
Registration is now open for the tenth edition of the  IOSCO/Program on 
International Financial Systems and Harvard Law School (PIFS -HLS) Global 
Certificate Program for Regulators of Securities Markets.  
IOSCO and PIFS -HLS jointly developed this two-phase program to offer  
IOSCO members an executive education program that is exclusively tailored 
for regulators of securities markets. 
Attendees have up to three years to complete both phases of the Global 
Certificate Program. Successful attendees will be awarded a joint certificate of 
participation issued by IOSCO and PIFS-Harvard Law School. Also, IOSCO 
members may attend any part of the program on a non-certificate basis. 
Phase I consists of online learning materials  as well as  a one week -long in-
person module on regulation  and compliance. The in-person module will be 
conducted at IOSCO's premises in Madrid (Spain) from June 15-19, 2026, and 
will cover the fundamentals and intricacies of securities regulation and 
compliance and related current critical issues . The sessions will be delivered 
primarily by current or former securities regulators. 
Phase II consists of a one week-long in-person module that will be conducted 
on campus at the Harvard Law School in Cambridge (Massachusetts, US) from 
December 7-11, 2026. This one week-long session will examine current and 
future regulatory challenges and emerging issues  and will be delivered by 
leading academics, securities regulators and leading public policy makers.  
While any IOSCO member employee may attend the program, it is specifically 
designed for regulatory staff with strong leadership potential and approximately 
five to seven years of regulatory experience. 
For registration, program details and more information, please visit 
www.iosco.org/training/global-certificate-program.
