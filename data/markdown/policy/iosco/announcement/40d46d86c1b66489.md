IOSCO’s Committee on Inve stment Management (C5) drafted this paper following a mandate

(CIS) valuation pr inciples with a major fo cus on polic ies and procedures re lated to the

The valuation of CIS asse ts potentiall y presents conflic ts be tween the interests of those who

operator could also undervalue the CIS’s assets and cause the CIS to sell them to affiliates at an 
art
