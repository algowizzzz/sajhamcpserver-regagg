CALL FOR PAPERS 
Conference on Diversity, Equity and Inclusion in Economics, Finance, and Central Banking 
22-23 March, 2027 
Rome, Italy 
 
 
European Central Bank, Bank of Canada, Bank of England, Banco de España, Banque de France, 
and Banca d’Italia are organizing the Conference on Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion in Economics, 
Finance, and Central Banking, to be held on 22–23 March 2027. The conference will be hosted by 
Banca d’Italia and will feature keynote lectures by  Annamaria Lusardi (Stanford)  and Alexandra 
Roulet (INSEAD). 
 
Call for papers 
 
We invite submissions presenting cutting-edge research on methods, measurement, and empirical 
analyses related to equity, diversity and inclusion (ED&I) across areas relevant to the economics 
and finance profession and to central banking activities. In particular, we seek to attract academic-
style papers on topics including, but not limited to: 
• ED&I and labour market  outcomes: how diversity along dimensions such as gender, race, 
age, mental or physical disability status, and socio-economic background shapes labour market 
outcomes, particularly within the economics and finance professions and in relation to 
technological change. 
• ED&I and financial markets:  financial inclusion and the role of diversity in shaping 
households’ and firms’ financial behaviour, access to financial services, investment choices, 
borrowing decisions, risk-taking, and participation in financial markets. 
• ED&I and monetary policy: how diversity and inclusion influence monetary policy and central 
banking, from internal decision-making processes to the heterogeneous effects of inflation and 
macroeconomic shocks across demographic groups. 
Submissions from groups traditionally underrepresented in economics and finance are especially 
encouraged. 
Submission & Selection 
The deadline for submissions is 30 October 2026. Authors are invited to submit their papers via 
Conference Maker at this link. Submissions should be in the form of completed manuscripts . 
Contributions will be selected by the programme committee, which consists of researchers from each 
of the six central banks. Authors selected to present will be notified by 30 November 2026. 
Conference organization 
The conference will begin on the morning of Monday, 22 March, and conclude in the early afternoon 
of Tuesday, 23 March. It will take place at the headquarters of Banca d’Italia in Rome. The 
programme will feature presentations of academic research selected  through this call for papers, 
contributions from Human Resources professionals from central banks, and a policy panel 
discussion. Participants are kindly expected to attend the conference in full.

The conference is sponsored by Banca d’Italia. There is no registration fee, and meals during the 
conference will be provided for all participants. Banca d’Italia will also cover travel and 
accommodation costs for all successful applicants from academic institutions, subject to a cap. 
Please, direct all inquiries and correspondence to dei2027@bancaditalia.it
