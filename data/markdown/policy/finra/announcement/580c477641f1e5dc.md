Step-by-Step Guide:
Verifying Your Identity  
with Self Service
IAL2 / AAL2
Our Self-Service pathway allows you to verify your identity 
by uploading identity documents and a video selfie. This 
self-directed process guides you through each step on your 
screen, allowing you to complete your identity verification 
independently and at your own pace, whenever it’s most 
convenient for you.
Self-Service takes 5-10 minutes to complete.  
To verify, you’ll need:
 ✓ A smartphone with a camera
 ✓ Photo ID (U.S. driver’s license or state ID, U.S. passport,  
or U.S. passport card)
 ✓ Social Security number
The organization you’re verifying for requires that your identity 
be verified at the IAL2/AAL2* level. This document walks you 
through the steps for verifying your identity at IAL2/AAL2 using 
our Self-Service pathway.
If you already verified your identity at IAL2/AAL2, you can verify your identity by simply signing in with 
your ID.me log in and consenting to share your information
*Identity Assurance Level 2 (IAL2)/Authenticator Assurance Level 2 (AAL2) is a classification set by the Department of Commerce’s 
National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), a U.S. government agency responsible for developing standards in technology 
and security.

The Steps
1 Get started
Go to your organization’s website  
and select the ID.me button.
Sign in with
2 Sign in or create an account
Sign in to your existing ID.me account. 
If you don’t have an ID.me account, 
select Create an Account and follow 
the prompts to add your email 
address, create a password, and add 
multi-factor authentication to protect 
your account.
3 Choose how to verify
If you are given a choice for how  
to verify, select Self-Service.

4 Allow ID.me to collect  
your information
Your information is only collected  
to verify your identity and help prevent 
fraud. We always ask for consent  
to collect your Biometric Information.
5 Take photos of your  
identity document
To take a photo of your driver’s license or 
State ID from your mobile device, select 
Take a photo. If you want to take photos 
of your passport or passport card, select 
Change document.
6 Upload photos of your  
driver’s license or state ID
Select Take photo, then take a photo  
of the front and back of your document.
Remember: You’ll need to have  
the original version of each of your 
documents. You cannot use copies.

8 Get ready for a video selfie
We compare your selfie to the photo on 
your identity document to ensure that 
you are you. Select Take video selfie.

Copyright © 2024 ID.me, Inc.help.id.me
9 Begin the selfie process
You will allow access to your camera, 
then take your photo. When you’re 
ready, select Start. 
10 Review selfie tips
Review the tips for a successful video 
selfie, then, when you’re ready,  
select Start liveness detection.
11 Grant camera access
Allow ID.me to access your camera.

Copyright © 2024 ID.me, Inc.help.id.me
12 Take the selfie
Make sure there’s good lighting  
and hold the phone in front  
of your face. Wait until you get  
a “Processing Video Selfie” screen.
Here are some tips for taking a good selfie:
Avoid Glare
If you notice glare, step back 
from the light source.
Don’t hide your face
Avoid clothing, like hats, 
scarves, masks, or anything 
else that can hide your face.
It’s a selfie!
Make sure no one else is in view.
Set up lighting
Whether it’s natural light or from 
a lamp, for example, make sure 
the light is in front of you.
Position the camera
Hold the camera directly  
in front of your face, about  
a foot away.
Check your background
Stand in front of a simple, plain 
background, if possible.
13  Enter your physical address
When prompted, enter your most recent 
home address.

Copyright © 2024 ID.me, Inc.help.id.me
14 Enter your phone number
If prompted, enter your phone number.
15 Enter SSN
Enter your Social Security number.
16 Confirm information
Review your information to ensure  
it is accurate. 
If everything is correct, select Yes. 
If something is inaccurate, select No 
and update your information.  
Your information will be reviewed 
before you can proceed.
You may be asked to allow ID.me  
to review your credit profile. This “soft” 
check will not affect your credit score. 
Select the checkbox for the Fair Credit 
Reporting Act to continue.

Copyright © 2024 ID.me, Inc.help.id.me
17 Confirm your phone number
You’ll receive a text message to confirm 
your phone number. 
Click the link in the text. 
18 Confirm your email address
To help prevent fraud, confirm the 
email address displayed is yours.
19 Share your information
From the Authorize screen, review  
the information that the organization 
will receive and then select Allow  
to consent to share that information 
with the organization.
Have trouble verifying?
If you aren’t able to verify on your own, you may be 
prompted to finish your verification on a video call 
with an ID.me agent. 
For answers to the most common verification 
questions, browse the ID.me Help Center.

Contact Support page.
