Login information for Webex Attendee 
Attendees may listen and watch, but cannot speak or appear on the video. Only the Host of the meeting 
can unmute an attendee. 
We suggest that you join at least 1 5 minutes before to allow yourself sufficient time to connect and 
check your connection.  
We highly recommend that you connect via a desktop or laptop computer for better audio/video.  
Joining the meeting 
• Enter your name and institution: 
o First name field: your full name (eg First name, Last name)  
o Last name field: your institution (eg BIS) 
This will help us to identify you if you have a question. 
• Please enter your email address.  
• Click Submit and reconfirm by clicking Join in the next window 
• Select Webex audio connection and click Connect Audio. 
All participants will be muted on entry to the meeting. Attendees remain muted for the duration of the 
meeting.  
To ask a question or provide a comment  
Please send a Chat message in the Chatbox in Webex to the Presenter (s). Other attendees will not be 
able to see your comment or question, but the Presenters will.  
Technical issues 
Please send a message to  the Host by using the meeting’s Chatbox function. You may also email this 
meeting’s contact person: iais@bis.org.
