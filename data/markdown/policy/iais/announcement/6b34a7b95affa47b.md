1. What is the IAIS Core Curriculum? 
The IAIS Core Curriculum Section (the “Core Curriculum”) is a learning tool provided 
by the International Association of Insurance Supervisors (the “IAIS”). The purpose of 
the Core Curriculum is to provide training materials in a variety of topics of relevance 
to insurance supervisors around the world and to foster the implementation of the 
IAIS Insurance Core  Principles. The Core Curriculum is publicly available from the 
IAIS website.
