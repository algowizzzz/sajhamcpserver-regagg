Reservation Process 
Room reservations at the aforementioned hotels can be made by filling out the 
reservation forms and emailing them to the addresses on the reservation forms. The 
hotel should be informed directly via email if there are any additional amendments. 
 
Please note that in order t o successfully make a hotel reservation at the indicated 
special rates, it is necessary to fill out one of the booking forms that you can find below 
and email it to the hotel and/or to your respective Liaison Officer. 
 
We kindly request that you avoid maki ng reservations through the hotel website, any 
hotel-aggregating third -party websites, or in any manner other than by emailing the 
registration form directly to the hotel or Liaison Officer, because in that case the special 
rate will not be applicable and the booking request might be rejected. 
 
In case the participant prefers not to provide the hotel with his/her credit card details by 
email, it is possible to leave these fields in the form empty.  Once the manager receives 
the form he/she will contact the participant for more details.  Alternatively, the booking 
form can be sent by fax. 
 
If you have any doubts or difficulties, please contact your respective Liaison Officer.
