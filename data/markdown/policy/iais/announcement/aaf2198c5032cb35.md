Implementation and Assessment 
Committee
Jose (Pepe) Lopez Hoyo, Chair of the Implementation and Assessment Committee
IAIS Annual Conference
8-9 November 2018, Luxembourg

Overview of the IAC work
Finished a strategic review on the IAIS Coordinated 
Implementation Framework
o Guide our future implementation work
o Shift the current focus from policy development towards 
implementation
Four pillars of implementation programme
1) Assessment and Implementation Monitoring
2) Supervisory Capacity Building 
3) Supervisory Cooperation 
4) Supervisory Practices

Assessment and Implementation Monitoring 
Standards Assessment Working Group

assessment activities
Enhancement on the IAIS Assessment Processes 
o Peer Review Process (PRP)
o Member Assessment Process (MAP)
o Self-Assessment Portal (SAP)

o PRP on Supervisors and Supervisory Powers (ICPs 1 and 2), 
and nearly 70 jurisdictions participated  
o Phase 2 assessment on Liquidity Management and Planning 
among Group-wide Supervisors

Key Themes for 2019 (1) 
Looking forward to 2019, the IAC will continue our efforts to operationalize the 
Coordinated Implementation Framework with an eye towards the likely 
focus of our 2020 – 2024 Strategic Plan. 
Key themes for 2019 include:
 Continue implementation of our assessment tools
o Peer Review Process 
o Member Assessment Process
o Self Assessment Portal 
 Engagement on emerging issues
o Supporting the FinTech Forum
o Launching a small group addressing Cyber Underwriting
o Continued collaboration with Sustainable Insurance Forum 
8

Key Themes for 2019 (2) 
 Supporting effective supervisory practices 
o Application papers 
o Interactions with the Supervisory Forum
 Enhancing Capacity Building 
o Core Curriculum update
o Continued work with key partners (eg, A2ii, FSI, etc) to support 
 Enabling Supervisory Cooperation 
o Supporting additional Signatories for the IAIS MMoU 
o Strengthening regional engagement
