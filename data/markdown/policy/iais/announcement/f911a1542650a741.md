1PUBLIC
Update on Common Framework for the 
Supervision of Internationally Active Insurance 
Groups (ComFrame)
IAIS Global Seminar
13 June 2019, Buenos Aires

2PUBLIC
What is the purpose of ComFrame?
• To establish supervisory standards focusing on the effective group-wide supervision of
Internationally Active Insurance Groups (IAIGs).
• To provide qualitative and (in a future phase) quantitative supervisory requirements
tailored to IAIGs.
• To assist supervisors in:
o Addressing group-wide activities and risks;
o Identifying and avoiding supervisory gaps;
o Coordinating supervisory activities efficiently and effectively between the group-wide and other
involved supervisors.
• To provide supervisors with a common language on supervision of IAIGs.

3PUBLIC
At what stage is ComFrame development?
• Public consultation on draft overall ComFrame in mid-2018.
• The outcome of the 2018 public consultation:
o To be communicated in June 2019;
o To be discussed at a public call on 28 June 2019, 14:00 CEST.
• ComFrame material is now close to finalised (with the exception of changes related to
the Holistic Framework development, which will be released for public consultation
soon).
• 14 November 2019 – adoption of ComFrame at Annual General Meeting.

4PUBLIC
How will the IAIS support implementation of ComFrame? 
• Preparation for the following areas prioritised for 2019:
o Identification of IAIGs by group-wide supervisors and
o Supervisory cooperation and coordination in supervisory colleges.
• Supporting implementation of ComFrame after its adoption:
o Developing relevant materials;
o Organising trainings and workshops for supervisors;
o Continuing dialogue with the industry and other stakeholders.
• Assessment of observance of ComFrame after an initial stage of implementation:
o Discussion on this area at an early stage.
o A draft assessment methodology for ComFrame to be released for public consultation in June
2019.
