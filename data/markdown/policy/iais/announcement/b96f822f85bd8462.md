A Proposal From US Life Insurers To 
Establish Jurisdictional Factors For 
Commercial  Mortgages

Importance 
• Commercial Mortgages Are A Material Asset Class 
For US Life Insurers 
– For US Life Insurers, Mortgages Make Up 10.3% Of 
Invested Assets* vs. 5.1% For Non-US Life Insurers 
• Commercial Mortgages Are A Preferred Asset Class, 
With Strong Yields And A Favorable Loss History 
• US Commercial Mortgages Are Often Long Duration 
Assets And Can Be Tailored To Liabilities.  
• Commercial Mortgages Provide Diversification And 
Risk Reduction For Portfolios Concentrated In Public 
Fixed Income Securities.
