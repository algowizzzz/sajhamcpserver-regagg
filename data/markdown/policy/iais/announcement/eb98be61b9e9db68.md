Login information for all participants 
All participants will be muted on entry to the meeting. Panelists  can unmute themselves, but we ask that 
you remain muted until you are called upon.  
Attendees may listen and watch, but cannot speak or appear on the video. Only the Host of the meeting 
can unmute an attendee. 
We suggest that you join at least 30 minutes before the start of the meeting to allow yourself sufficient 
time to connect and check your connection.  
We highly recommend that you connect via a desktop or laptop computer for better audio/video.  
 
Joining the meeting 
• Enter your name and institution: 
o First name field: your full name  
(eg First name, Last name)  
o Last name field: your institution (eg BIS) 
This will help us to identify you if you have a question. 
• Please enter your email address (eg IAIS@BIS.ORG) 
• Click Submit and reconfirm by clicking Join  in the 
next window 
• Select Webex audio connection and click Connect Audio. 
To ask a question or provide a comment  
Please send a Chat message in the Chatbox in Webex to the Panelists  
Other attendees will not be able to see your comment or question, but the Panelists will.  
Technical issues 
Please send a message to the Host of the meeting should you require assistance in testing your audio 
/ video connection, or email IAIS@bis.org, for any technical issues.
