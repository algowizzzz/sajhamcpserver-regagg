The proportionality principle
o ICPs apply to insurance supervision in all jurisdictions regardless 
of the level of development or sophistication of the insurance 
markets and the type of insurance products or services being 
supervised. 
o Nevertheless, supervisors need to tailor certain supervisory 
requirements and actions in accordance with the nature, scale and 
complexity of individual insurers so that they are commensurate 
with the risks posed.
