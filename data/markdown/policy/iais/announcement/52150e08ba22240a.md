This IAIS stakeholder event is intended to provide an additional forum for interaction during the 
consultation of the draft Holistic Framework for Systemic Risk in the Insurance Sector. This forum is not 
a substitute for submitting comments in writing via the IAIS consultation tool. The deadline for comments 
to the consultation is 25 January 2019 24.00 CET (Basel time). The consultation document and IT tool 
are available here. 
The presentations will be made available on the IAIS website (link).
