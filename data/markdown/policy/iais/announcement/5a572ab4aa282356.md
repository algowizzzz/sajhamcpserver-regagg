IAIS 2020-2021 Roadmap – Key considerations
Executive Committee Dialogue 2020
The IAIS has adjusted / prioritised its 2020-2021 Roadmap guided by the following key 
considerations:
• Alignment with the FSB principles on response to Covid-19 – in particular:
o Providing operational relief to Members and insurers, while at the same time not rolling-back the 
implementation of regulatory reforms or compromising the underlying commitment to the finalisation 
and implementation of key international standards;
• Relevance of projects for the IAIS’ overall Covid-19 response;
• Whether projects are close to completion or already far advanced;
• Whether projects or activities contribute to the strategic pivot outlined in our 2020-2024 
strategic plan; and 
• Taking into account the broad interests of the IAIS diverse membership, with a particular 
focus on our Emerging Market and Developing Economy (EMDE) Members.

9PUBLIC
IAIS 2020-2021 Roadmap – Key updates 1/2
Covid-19 Risk Assessment: 
o Holistic Framework Global Monitoring Exercise (GME) refocused to enable timely monitoring of 
Covid-19 in 2020
o Risk dashboard of key market indicators
Covid-19 Policy Measures: 
o Information-sharing and analysis of Covid-19 related supervisory responses
o Deeper-dive analysis of identified priority topics
Collaboration with the FSB and other Standard-Setting Bodies (SSBs):
o Cross-sectoral risk assessment
o Supervisory responses to identified vulnerabilities
o Intensified coordination and cooperation
Stakeholder engagement: In-person events in June (Global Seminar) and November 
(Annual Conference) cancelled and replaced with virtual programme

10PUBLIC
IAIS 2020-2021 Roadmap – Key updates 2/2
Global Monitoring Exercise (GME):
o Reporting to the FSB and public on outcomes of 2020 GME postponed to October 2021 – data 
submission deadline extended to next year
Insurance Capital Standard (ICS), Aggregation Method and Comparability Assessment:
o Data submission deadline for participating Volunteer Groups extended to 31 October 
o The Capital, Solvency and Field Testing Working Group (CSFWG) has increased its engagement 
with Volunteer Groups 
o Public consultation on high-level principles for comparability criteria postponed to November 
2020
Supporting material (Issues Papers and Application Papers):
o Papers close to completion or far advanced will be finalised remotely
o Public consultation in some cases deferred and/or consultation periods extended
