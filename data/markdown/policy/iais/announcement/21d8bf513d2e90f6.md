1 Purpose of the note  
The purpose of this note is to provide stakeholders with background information on the content 
of the consultation package and the process of its development.  
In general, the package presents : (1)  the outcome of the 2018 public consultation on  
ComFrame; and (2) new revisions released for public consultation. More specifically, the  
following elements are included in the package: 
• Revisions to ComFrame and ICPs based on comments received during the 2018 public 
consultation on overall ComFrame: 
o Revisions to ComFrame (published for information); 
o Minor editorial changes for consistency with ComFrame in ICPs 10 (Preventive 
Measures, Corrective Measures and Sanction) , 12 (Exit from the Market and 
Resolution) and 16  (Enterprise Risk Management for Solvency Purposes) 
(published for information); 
o Changes in the Introduction to ICPs and ICP 7 (Corporate Governance)  for 
consistency with ComFrame development (released for public consultation); 
• Draft revised Glossary (released for public consultation); 
• Draft ComFrame Assessment Methodology (released for public consultation) 
• Draft revised ICP 22 (Anti-Money Laundering and Comba ting the Financing of 
Terrorism) (released for public consultation); 
• Revisions to ICP s and ComFrame related to the holistic framework development 
(released for public consultation)1. 
The IAIS is now seeking feedback on the revisions to the IAIS supervisory material  through 
public consultation. Feedback on this material is invited by 15 August 2019 at 24:00 CEST. 
Only comments submitted via the web- based consultation tool will be considered. After this 
deadline, the consultation tool will be closed and it will no longer be possible to submit any 
further comments. Feedback received by this deadline will enable the IAIS to further develop 
the material for finalisation.

3 Background information 
3.1 Revision of ICPs 
The following draft revised ICPs related to ComFrame were endorsed by the Ex ecutive 
Committee, subject to potential further changes prior to their adoption at the 2019 IAIS Annual 
General Meeting, and published on the IAIS website for information purposes: 
• draft revised ICP Introduction and Assessment Methodology and draft revised ICPs 1, 
2, 3, 9, 10, 12 and 25 in November 2017; and 
• draft revised ICPs 8, 15, 16 and 20 in November 2018. 
Following development of ComFrame, revisions have been made in the Introduction to ICPs 
and ICPs 7, 10, 12 and 16. Since revisions to ICPs 10, 12 and 16 are of editorial nature, they 
are published only for information (and not for comments). The changes to the Introduction to 
ICPs and ICP 7 are more substantive and are published for comment. 
Independent from ComFrame development, ICP 22 (Anti-Money Laundering and Combatting 
the Financing of Terrorism), which was adopted in October 2013, has been reviewed and 
updated in order to improve its usefulness to supervisors and enhance consisten cy with the 
Financial Action Task Force (FATF) Recommendations.2  
3.2 Development of ComFrame 
The public consultation on draft overall ComFrame took place between 31 July and 30 October 
2018. This was related to the Introduction to ComFrame and ComFrame material in ICPs 5, 
7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 16, 23 and 25.

3.3 Development of policy measures of the holistic framework 
The Consultation Document on the Holistic Framework  was subject to public consultation 
between November 2018 and January 2019, which included proposals for supervisory policy 
measures and powers of intervention. The IAIS has now developed draft revised ICPs and 
ComFrame material, taking into account the comments received from the public consultation 
last year. 
Draft revisions are integrated in ICP 9 and ComFrame, ICP 10, ICP 16 and ComFrame, ICP 
20 and ICP 24.  
Please note that there is a separate public consultation webpage, with a dedicated cover note, 
for revisions related to the holistic framework for systemic risk in the insurance sector.  
3.4 Revision of the IAIS Glossary 
The glossary provides definitions of the main terms used by the IAIS. The IAIS undertook a 
review of the IAIS Glossary terms and definitions that were adopted a t the Annual General 
Meeting in November 2015. The revised definitions are released for public consultation.  The 
review focused on two aspects: 
• Deleting existing terms and adding new terms to reflect revisions to ICPs and the 
development of ComFrame. Generally, the Glossary aims  to contain only terms that 
are used in more than one ICP, or that have a specific meaning in insurance or in the 
ICPs.  
• Revising the definitions of the terms to ensure consistency within the Glossary as well 
as between the Glossary terms and the draft revised ICPs and ComFrame. 
Terms related to ICP 14 and 17 were not part of this review, but instead will be reviewed when 
ICPs 14 and 17 themselves are being revised in the coming years. 
3.5 Development of the ComFrame assessment methodology 
To complement the ICP  Assessment Methodology, the IAIS developed a draft ComFrame 
Assessment Methodology. Comments via public consultation are welcomed, after which the 
IAIS will finalise the methodology as part of Overall ComFrame in November this year.
