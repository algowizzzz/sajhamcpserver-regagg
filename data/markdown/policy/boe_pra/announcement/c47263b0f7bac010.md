Banks in scope of ring-fencing as of 22 April 2026  
As of 22 April 2026, the following UK banking groups included entities which were 
ring-fenced bodies (RFBs) pursuant to section 142A of the Financial Services and 
Markets Act 2000 (FSMA) (as amended): Barclays, HSBC, Lloyds Banking Group, 
NatWest Group, and Santander UK.
