## Target audience

The Consultation Paper regarding the Regulatory Technical Standard on macroprudential analyses proposes both quantitative and risk-based criteria to determine which undertakings and groups should consider macroprudential elements in their Own Risk and Solvency Assessments (ORSA) and in their application of the Prudent Person Principle (PPP).
