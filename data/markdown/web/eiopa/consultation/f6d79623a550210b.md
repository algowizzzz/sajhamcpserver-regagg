## Target audience

EIOPA is running a consultation on the treatment of proportional reinsurance treaties with features that may jeopardise the balance (“commensurateness”) between the solvency capital requirement (SCR) relief triggered by the treaty and the effective transfer of risk they provide. EIOPA’s final guidance would eventually form an Annex to its earlier Opinion on risk mitigation techniques, which sets out general principles for assessing reinsurance.
