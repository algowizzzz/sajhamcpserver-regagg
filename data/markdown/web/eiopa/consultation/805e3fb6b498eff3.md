## Target audience

This consultation focuses on the valuation of insurance or reinsurance undertakings and groups for the purposes of resolution, covering valuation 1 and 2. The draft RTS provide methodologies for assessing the value of the assets and liabilities of undertakings in the context of resolution and the methodology for calculating the buffer for additional losses to be included in provisional valuations.
