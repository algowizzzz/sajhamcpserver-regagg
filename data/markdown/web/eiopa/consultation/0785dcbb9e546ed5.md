Give input to our open consultations

EIOPA organises written consultations, surveys and feedback requests in order to receive comments from all interested parties, including market participants, consumers and other end-users.

EIOPA invites interested parties to send any comments by email or by using the tool indicated in the consultation paper, within the indicated deadline, using the provided paper specific, customised template and quoting the reference of the consultation paper. EIOPA will make all comments available on its website, except where respondents specifically request that their comments remain confidential. You may find information on how EIOPA processes personal data in the context of public consultations and surveys in the [privacy statement](/document/download/cfc5d019-4dd0-4b47-8e0e-f1a733881259_en?filename=Privacy-statement%20-%20Public%20consultations%20and%20Surveys.pdf).

An overview of planned public consultations is available [here](/document/download/098b8b4d-0b4d-48e5-af1f-c27e1903dc26_en?filename=Planned%20public%20consultations%20for%202026.pdf).
