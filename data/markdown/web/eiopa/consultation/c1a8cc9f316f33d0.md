## Target audience

EIOPA (European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority) welcomes comments on the Consultation Paper on technical advice for the review of the IORP II Directive (Institutions for Occupational Retirement Provision Directive).

Please send your comments to EIOPA by Thursday, 25 May 2023, 23:59 CET responding to the questions in the survey below.

## Respond to the consultation

The response period for this consultation has ended. Thank you for your input.
