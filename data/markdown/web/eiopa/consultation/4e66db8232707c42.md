## Description

The European Supervisory Authorities (ESAs) welcome the European Commission’s consultation on a Renewed Sustainable Finance Strategy as part of its European Green Deal. The ESAs support the Commission’s ambition to strengthen the European policy framework to enhance the resilience of the financial sector to environmental, social and governance (ESG) risks and facilitate the ongoing transformation towards a more sustainable economy.
