# Solvency II Single Rulebook

The Single Rulebook provides access to key regulatory and supervisory texts within the scope of EIOPA’s activity. A pilot Rulebook for Solvency II is being published, comprising Directive 138/2009/EC, Delegated Regulation (EC) 2015/35, Delegated Regulation (EC) 2016/467, Delegated Regulation (EC) 2018/1221, Implementing Technical Standards adopted by the European Commission, as well as EIOPA’s Guidelines, Opinions, Supervisory Statements and Questions and Answers.

This Single Rulebook is meant as a documentation tool, and to promote the consistent application of the regulatory framework. For the authentic version of EU legislation users should refer to the Official Journal of the European Union.

To ensure the Rulebook meets users’ needs and to secure future updates, EIOPA invites stakeholders to share their experience with the Rulebook.
