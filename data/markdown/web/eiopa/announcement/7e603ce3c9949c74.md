## Description

EIOPA submitted to the European Parliament its opinion on the discharge decision.

The document, adopted by the Board of Supervisors, provides an overview of the measures taken by EIOPA in the light of the observations and comments made by the European Parliament in respect of the implementation of the budget for the financial year 2024.
