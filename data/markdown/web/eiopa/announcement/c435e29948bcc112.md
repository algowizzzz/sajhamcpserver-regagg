The European Supervisory Authorities (ESAs) proposed options for presenting information on performance scenarios to be tested during the upcoming consumer testing exercise by the European Commission under the Level 2 Review of the PRIIPs KID Regulation.  
For further details please refer to the letter from the Joint Committee of the ESAs to the European Commission and Annex to the letter.

Publication date
:   23 May 2019
