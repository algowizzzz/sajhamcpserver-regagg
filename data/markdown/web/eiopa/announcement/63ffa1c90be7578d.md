EIOPA submitted to the European Parliament its  opinion on the discharge decision. The document, adopted by the Board of Supervisors, provides an overview of the measures taken by EIOPA in the light of the observations and comments made by the European Parliament in respect of the implementation of the budget for the financial year 2018.

In the opinion, EIOPA points out specific actions taken related for example to enhanced cooperation on conduct risks with National Supervisory Authorities, supervisory convergence, closer supervision in some Member States, staff policies and procurement.
