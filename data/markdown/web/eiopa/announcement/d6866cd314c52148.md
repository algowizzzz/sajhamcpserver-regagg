Page Content

The European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority (EIOPA) is conducting a review of its website by gathering feedback from all website users. Your feedback will ensure that your user experience and views are taken into account in developing a user-friendly and accessible website.

The survey should take no longer than five minutes to complete.

[Click here](https://goo.gl/K7BXXr) to participate in the survey.
