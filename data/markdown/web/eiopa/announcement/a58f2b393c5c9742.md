## Biography

Marc Andries is the DORA Joint oversight Director for the European Supervisory Authorities (EBA, EIOPA, ESMA), leading the new mandate of the authorities for the oversight of critical ICT providers to the European financial sector. He previously worked for the French supervisory authority and has extensive experience in ICT risk and payment security in international settings. Third-party risk is one of the key challenges for the digital resilience of the financial sector.
