As of September 2020, the European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority (EIOPA) has discontinued producing and publishing extraordinary processes for risk-free interest rate term structures (RFR) and symmetric adjustment to equity risk (EDA).

EIOPA will continue to monitor the financial situation closely to support insurance and reinsurance undertakings in their own monitoring of their solvency and financial position.

The production of monthly RFR/EDA will continue.
