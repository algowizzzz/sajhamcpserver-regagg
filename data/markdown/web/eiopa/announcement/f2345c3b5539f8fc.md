## Description

The December 2022 Financial Stability Report examines key risks and developments in Europe's insurance and occupational pensions sectors. Additionally, the report presents four topical focuses, notably about

* the impact of rising inflation and interest rates on insurers’ financial position,
* insurers’ use of derivates and related liquidity needs,
* the growing relevance of life portfolio transfers, as well as
* non-life insurers’ underwriting exposure to physical climate change risk.
