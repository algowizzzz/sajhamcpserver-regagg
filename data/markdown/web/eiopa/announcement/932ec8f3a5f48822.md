# IDD (Insurance Distribution Directive)

The IDD Single Rulebook provides access to key regulatory texts, guidelines as well as Q&As on regulation within the scope of EIOPA’s activity.

This IDD Single Rulebook is meant as a documentation tool, and to promote the consistent application of the regulatory framework. For the authentic version of EU legislation, users should refer to the Official Journal of the European Union.

To ensure the Rulebook meets users’ needs and to secure future updates, EIOPA invites stakeholders to share their experience with the Rulebook. Comments and suggestions can be sent to rulebook![at](/modules/contrib/spamspan/image.gif)eiopa [dot] europa [dot] eu (rulebook[at]eiopa[dot]europa[dot]eu).
