## What to bear in mind when buying and using insurance and pensions products

Insurance and pensions products are important for protecting and helping consumers in their daily life, notably to cope with serious difficulties (e.g. floods, fire, car accident etc.).

Before buying an insurance or a pension product, it is advisable to obtain more information.

Find out more about how to buy a product, how to keep track of it, how to handle a claim or a complaint, and how to cancel an insurance or pension product.

[Learn more](/consumers/buying-and-using-insurance-and-pensions-products_en)
