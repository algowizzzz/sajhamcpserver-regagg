Publication date

## Description

The main objective of the Opinion is to foster an effective cost supervision across the EU in order to enhance the value for money offered to members and beneficiaries, the cost efficiency of IORPs and the affordability for sponsors.

The opinion is accompanied by an impact assessment and the [feedback statement](/document/download/66c0c08b-d76f-4d12-a7db-34a2862ac96f_en?filename=Feedback%20statement%20on%20comments%20received%20on%20the%20draft%20Opinion%20on%20the%20supervisory%20reporting%20of%20costs%20and%20charges%20of%20IORPs.pdf) addressing the comments received during the [public consultation.](/consultations/consultation-draft-opinion-supervisory-reporting-costs-and-charges-iorps_en)
