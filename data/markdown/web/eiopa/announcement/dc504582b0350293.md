## Description

This Annual Report details the Authority’s main achievements in 2016 across its main activities:

* to strengthen the protection of consumers;
* to improve the functioning of the EU internal market in the field of pensions and insurance;
* to strengthen the financial stability of the insurance and occupational pensions sectors; and
* EIOPA to be a responsible, competent and professional organisation.
