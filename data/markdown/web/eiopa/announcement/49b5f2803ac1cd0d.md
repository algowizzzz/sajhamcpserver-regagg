## Biography

Manuela Röser is an experienced leader at SAP, currently heading the DORA Compliance Governance team. With a strong background in strategy, operations, and customer engagement, she has driven major initiatives in digital transformation and regulatory readiness. With a strong focus on collaboration and trust, she supports SAP’s transformation in a fast-changing regulatory landscape.
