The EIOPA Occasional Research Papers present the joint work of external researchers and academics in collaboration with EIOPA experts, using Solvency II and IORPs reporting data. The publications explore key topics within the EU’s (re)insurance and occupational pension fund sectors.

Using robust data, rigorous empirical analysis and sound theoretical foundations, EIOPA’s Occasional Research Papers explore topics relevant to current policy and regulatory debates, discussing potential policy implications of the research findings. However, these papers do not provide policy recommendations, as their purpose is to inform and stimulate discussion, rather than to advocate for specific policies.

*Any views expressed in an EIOPA Occasional Research Paper are those of the authors and do not necessarily reflect the official stance of EIOPA, its member institutions, or any institution with which the authors may be affiliated.*
