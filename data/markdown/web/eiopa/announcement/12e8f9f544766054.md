## Description

The Costs and past performance report analyses the costs, charges and value for money characteristics of retail investment products within EIOPA’s remit over the period 2020 to 2023.

EIOPA collected data for this report from a sample of insurance undertakings in participating Member States on the following products: unit-linked (UL), profit participation (PP), hybrid (HY), and personal pension products (PPP), which can be either IBIPs or non-IBIP.
