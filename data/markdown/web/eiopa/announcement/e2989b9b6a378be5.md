## Description

The European Supervisory Authorities respond today to the European Commission’s (EC) [rejection](https://finance.ec.europa.eu/document/download/d7f731c6-39a7-42e5-bd4b-f28434b7d51d_en?filename=240723-letter-esma-dora-register-information_en.pdf) of the draft Implementing Technical Standards (ITS) on the registers of information under the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA). The ESAs raise concerns over the impacts and practicalities of the proposed EC changes to the draft ITS on the registers of information in relation to financial entities’ contractual arrangements with ICT third-party service providers.
