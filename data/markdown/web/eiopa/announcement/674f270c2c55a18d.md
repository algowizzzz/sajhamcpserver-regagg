***Update on 2 October 2025:*** On 1 October 2025, FSC issued a new ban on ZAD DallBogg: Life and Health AD due to persistent and newly identified issues. This means that the company is still prohibited from distributing new cross-border products and services or extending existing ones. For more detail, read FSC's press release in [Bulgarian](https://www.fsc.bg/kfn-nalaga-nova-zabrana-za-transgranichna-dejnost-na-zad-dallbogg-zhivot-i-zdrave-ad/).

On 10 June 2025, Bulgaria's Financial Supervision Commission (FSC - Комисия за финансов надзор) imposed a temporary ban on the provision of new and the extension of the term of existing cross-border products and services of ZAD DallBogg: Life and Health AD (ЗАД „ДаллБогг: Живот и Здраве“ АД) – under the freedom to provide services across the European Economic Area concerning all classes of insurance for an initial period of 3 months as of 1 July 2025.

The insurance business activities of ZAD DallBogg: Life and Health AD will continue as usual in all Member States until 30 June 2025 (incl).

FSC, as the home supervisory authority, is in close contact with ZAD DallBogg: Life and Health AD to ensure effective consumer protection and fair treatment of all policyholders. FSC is also working on defining the conditions that ZAD DallBogg: Life and Health AD would have to meet for therestriction to be lifted. All concerned National Competent Authorities are closely cooperating.

More details will be provided shortly.
