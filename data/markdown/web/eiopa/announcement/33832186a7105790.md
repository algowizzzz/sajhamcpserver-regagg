Publication date

## Description

The report is the outcome of a pilot exercise on impact underwriting that EIOPA conducted with 31 volunteering insurance undertakings from 14 European countries in 2022. It provides an overview of the main findings of the pilot exercise regarding current underwriting practices and challenges mentioned by the participating insurance undertakings.
