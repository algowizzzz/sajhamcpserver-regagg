## Description

The 2024 Consumer Trends Report highlights the main trends in consumers’ experience with insurance and pension products.

The report explores four key areas:

* the role of digitalisation in shaping the insurance and pension sectors,
* the transformatory power of AI in insurance,
* consumers’ appetite for supplementary pensions in light of increasing pension gaps,
* and the real/perceived value for money of insurance and pensions products.

The findings of the report are underpinned by various data sources, including the results of [EIOPA’s Eurobarometer survey.](/tools-and-data/eurobarometer-2024-consumer-trends-insurance-and-pension-services_en)
