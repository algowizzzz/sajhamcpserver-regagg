The [EU Digital Finance Platform](https://digital-finance-platform.ec.europa.eu/) is a new collaborative space bringing together industry and public authorities. Among other functionalities, such as the European fintech map, it enables [the cross-border testing framework](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/library/efif_procedural_framework_for_cross-border_testing.pdf). You will find below the list of innovation facilitators in the EEA.
