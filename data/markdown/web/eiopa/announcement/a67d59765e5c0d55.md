## Description

The repository contains 127 national initiatives that provide consumers with helpful information on how to improve their financial literacy.

*If you are interested in initiatives aimed  to help consumers find their way in the insurance and pensions sectors, check our [Financial education map](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/content/financial-education-map_en).*
