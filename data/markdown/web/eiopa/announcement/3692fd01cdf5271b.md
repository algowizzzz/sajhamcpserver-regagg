## Description

This factsheet answers consumers’ most frequently asked questions about sustainable finance. It provides tips to consumers considering buying financial products with sustainability features, including loans, investments, insurances and pensions.

The factsheet provides answers and suggests steps consumers can take to understand how their financial choices could contribute to a more sustainable future.
