## Why do we need open-source tools?

Data and models on climate-related risks are crucial to improve the accuracy of climate risk assessment.

However, tools enabling modelling are not easily available on the market.

In its [application guidance on running climate change materiality assessment and using climate change scenarios in the ORSA](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/eiopa-publishes-application-guidance-how-reflect-climate-change-orsa-2022-08-02_en), EIOPA clearly depicted the availability of tools to be one major challenge SMEs will face when doing such exercise.

More tools and data are currently available or being developed. But it is still challenging to get access to the right tools or data. Cost and user-friendliness of such tools are one of the main hurdles that companies face.

EIOPA believes that easy-to-use open-source tools would be extremely beneficial and wishes to support the development of open-source models.

More and easy-to-use open-source models have the potential to reduce the costs of running climate change analyses, can increase innovation, the use of cat models, and raise awareness of the risks.

Ultimately, this could bring benefits to policyholders as well as to the insurance industry, and to the supervisory community.

## CLIMADA-App: a case study supported by EIOPA

### **What is CLIMADA-App?**

The CLIMADA-app is an open-source modelling tool developed by EIOPA that combines a user-friendly interface with the open-source catastrophe model [CLIMADA](https://wcr.ethz.ch/research/climada.html).

Launched as a case study to encourage further development and facilitate stakeholder engagement in catastrophe modelling, the initiative aligns with EIOPA's mission to bridge climate science and the insurance industry. Climada-app is designed primarily to support small and medium insurance companies and NCA’s in their NatCat and climate risk assessment.

The app enables NatCat and climate risk analysis by allowing users to select default LitPop exposure data or import their own at various granularity levels (latitude/longitude, regional/NUTS or country level). It automatically processes data by disaggregating to latitude/longitude coordinates for analysis and then aggregating results at regional/NUTS and country levels. Users can explore exposure, hazard, and risk assessment results through the interface and download them in Excel or PDF formats.

### New version

EIOPA released a new version of the Climada-App in June 2026.

The recent updates have expanded functionality to allow EIOPA and external stakeholders to conduct European-level risk assessments for multiple perils, including diversification benefits analysis. The new release supports computation of non-additive metrics, such as return period losses, at EEA level and supports analysis of current and forward-looking physical climate change risks on the European insurance sector. Continuing EIOPA's commitment to promoting open-source modelling and data for catastrophe risk and climate change, the update introduces new peril scenarios for coastal flooding, earthquakes, and wildfires, along with security and efficiency enhancements.

### **How to get and to use CLIMADA-app?**

The user interface is a free software. Users can download it free of costs, use it, share it and even modify it.

The source code is also available.

The CLIMADA-app is a desktop application. It has been tested for Windows.

The installation and use of the user interface is done at the user’s discretion and risk.

## Other initiatives promoting open-source approaches

Recently, the cat modelling industry — like many other data-driven industries — has seen a push towards open-source data and industry-wide collaboration.

We mention here below a non-exhaustive list of such initiatives.

Global Earthquake Maps (GEM) - Global Earthquake Model Foundation

The OpenQuake Engine is the Global Earthquake Model Foundation’s (GEM) state-of-the-art, open-source software collaboratively developed for earthquake hazard and risk modelling.

[Learn more](https://www.globalquakemodel.org/)

![Global Earthquake Maps | Global Earthquake Model Foundation](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/styles/oe_theme_medium_no_crop/public/2023-05/GEM-LOGO-Red-RGB-300DPI.png?itok=zJ4zEwCr)

Global Earthquake Maps | Global Earthquake Model Foundation

Risk Modelling (RMSG) - Insurance Development Forum

The Risk Modelling Steering Group (RMSG) is dedicated to improving global understanding and quantification of natural hazards and disaster risk through the use, development and sharing of the re/insurance sector’s risk analytics capability.

[Learn more](https://www.insdevforum.org/working-groups/rmsg/)

![Insurance Development Forum](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/styles/oe_theme_medium_no_crop/public/2023-05/IDF_pos_logo_RGB.png?itok=sCvO39YH)

Insurance Development Forum

The Climate Impact Explorer - Network for Greening the Financial System (NGFS)

The Climate Impact Explorer developed by the Network for Greening the Financial System (NGFS) provides first-hand access to projections of physical climate risks at the national and subnational level. Scenario and warming level dependent impacts are provided for chronic and acute climate changes, as well as direct damages for selected sectors.

[Learn more](https://climate-impact-explorer.climateanalytics.org/)

![The Climate Impact Explorer - the Network for Greening the Financial System (NGFS)](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/styles/oe_theme_medium_no_crop/public/2023-05/819291_header_ngfs.png?itok=QM40QJn9)

The Climate Impact Explorer - the Network for Greening the Financial System (NGFS)

Oasis Loss Modelling Framework - Open source catastrophe modelling platform

An open-source catastrophe modelling platform, free to use by anyone.

[Learn more](https://oasislmf.org/)

![Oasis Loss Modelling Framework - Open source catastrophe modelling platform](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/sites/default/files/styles/oe_theme_medium_no_crop/public/2023-05/OASIS.jpg?itok=vyOp0RuV)

Oasis Loss Modelling Framework - Open source catastrophe modelling platform

DRMKC Risk Data Hub

Risk Data Hub is a web platform of European wide risk data and methodologies for Disaster Risk Assessment.

[Learn more](https://drmkc.jrc.ec.europa.eu/risk-data-hub/#/)
