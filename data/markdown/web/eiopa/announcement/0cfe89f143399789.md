The event aims to facilitate a dialogue among industry stakeholders, patient associations, and regulatory bodies to identify areas of potential unfair treatment and explore solutions.

The programme will feature expert speakers and discussion to debate key questions and promote a more inclusive insurance market for consumers  with HIV or diabetes.
