## Description

The June 2022 Financial Stability Report examines the main trends for the European (re)insurance and pensions sectors as well as the macroeconomic landscape with its implications.

It considers the impact of inflation driven by stressed supply chains and the geopolitical situation that has further exacerbated price pressures. Additionally, it analyses the risks related to climate change and cyber security while also including a thematic article exploring whether stress test results influence insurers’ dividend distribution and share buyback activities.
