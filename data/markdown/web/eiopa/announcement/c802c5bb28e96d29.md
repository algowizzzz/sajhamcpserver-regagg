On 05 November 2020, Národná banka Slovenska (NBS), the Slovakian national supervisory authority, has issued an interim measure to NOVIS Insurance Company, NOVIS Versicherungsgesellschaft, NOVIS Compagnia di Assicurazioni, NOVIS Poisťovňa a.s. (NOVIS) prohibiting free disposal of its assets. The information, which might be of interest to NOVIS policyholders, is available on the [NBS website](http://www.nbs.sk/sk/dohlad-nad-financnym-trhom-prakticke-informacie/vyroky-vykonatelnych-rozhodnuti/20201112-01).
