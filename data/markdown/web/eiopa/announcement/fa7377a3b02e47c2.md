## Biography

Dr. Marie Scholer Mendez has joined EIOPA in April 2019 as a Senior Expert on Policy Sustainable Finance. In this function, she leads the work to position EIOPA as a centre of excellence for catastrophe model and data.

Prior to EIOPA, Ms Scholer worked for the reinsurance sector at R+V Re in Germany as a Risk controlling Actuary and for insurance sector at Zurich insurance in Switzerland as a Senior Catastrophe Modeller. She has extensive knowledge in natural catastrophe models as she worked for a catastrophe model vendors (RMS) and holds a PhD in geophysics.
