## Description

The European Commission’s [proposal](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:52021PC0582) for an Insurance Recovery and Resolution Directive (IRRD) was published in September 2021. The legislative proposal is currently under consideration by the European Parliament and the Council of the European Union.  
A recent [EIOPA Staff paper](/document/download/89fcc1fa-2544-4f29-bdab-2f6e72ec5c76_en?filename=EIOPA%20staff%20paper%20on%20the%20proposal%20for%20an%20Insurance%20Recovery%20and%20Resolution%20Directive%20-%202022.pdf) provides an overview of the most relevant aspects of the proposal. In order to continue increasing the understanding of the proposal, EIOPA staff drafted the current document aiming at providing answers to some frequently asked questions by a number of different stakeholders.
