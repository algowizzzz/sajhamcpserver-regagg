What's next for consumer protection in Europe's rapidly evolving financial market? Be part of the conversation at the *13th Joint ESAs Consumer Protection Day*in Rome.

![13th Joint ESAs Consumer Protection Day](/sites/default/files/2026-07/CPD26_banner.png)

Under the theme “***Ensuring fair access, better outcomes for consumers and responsible innovation in a changing financial landscape***”, this year's programme will explore challenges related to access to financial services, effective consumer redress and the implications of AI-driven financial advice.

## Event information

📅 **Date**: Wednesday, 4 November 2025, 9:00 - 15:30 CET

📍 **Location**: Teatro Quirino, Via delle Vergini, 7, Rome, Italy

ℹ️ **Event format and attendance:** In-person only, no streaming will be available.

Attendance is free of charge.

💬 **Event language**: English

✉️ **Contact**: cpd2026![at](/modules/contrib/spamspan/image.gif)esma [dot] europa [dot] eu (cpd2026[at]esma[dot]europa[dot]eu)

## Panels

**Panel 1:** Ensuring safe and fair access to financial services: which challenges ahead?

**Panel 2:** Product design and consumer remedies: delivering effective post-sale redress

**Panel 3:**Use of AI by consumers to obtain financial advice: implications for consumer protection

*Further details about speakers will be published in due course.*

## Registration

**Deadline for registration**: Friday, 25 September 2026.

Registration may close earlier if the venue reaches its maximum capacity before the deadline.

**Registration confirmation:**Official registration confirmation will be sent by the organisers in due course after ensuring that fair distribution is provided to all stakeholder categories, including the public and consumer representatives. **Please wait with booking**your travel and accommodation **until official registration confirmation is provided**from our side.

**Costs/reimbursements:**Subject to conditions, consumer representatives who are members of an officially recognised body in their respective EU Member State are eligible for reimbursement.

## Additional information about the event

The Joint ESAs Consumer Protection Day is organised every year by the [European Banking Authority (EBA)](https://www.eba.europa.eu/homepage), the European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority (EIOPA), and the [European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA)](https://www.esma.europa.eu/), collectively known as the three European Supervisory Authorities (ESAs).
