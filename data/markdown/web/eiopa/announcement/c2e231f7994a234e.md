## COVID-19 measures

The Coronavirus/COVID-19 outbreak has caused significant disruption to the economy, business and people.

EIOPA is therefore working in close cooperation with national supervisory authorities to mitigate the impact of the outbreak on the insurance and occupational pensions sectors in Europe.
