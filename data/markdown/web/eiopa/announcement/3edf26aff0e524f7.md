## EIOPA's mission

EIOPA seeks to enhance European societal resilience by contributing to the stability and sustainable development of the European insurance and occupational pensions sectors, for the benefit of citizens and society more broadly.

**Operating in supervisory unity** – thereby promoting consistent implementation of regulatory requirements via high-quality supervisory practices, effective enforcement measures, and sound technical advice – **EIOPA strives to protect the rights of policyholders, pension scheme members and beneficiaries and to foster public trust in the EU’s insurance and occupational pensions sectors.**

[Learn more](/about/mission-and-tasks_en)
