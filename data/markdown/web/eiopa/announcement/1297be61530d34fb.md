About the Eurobarometer

In 2024, EIOPA conducted an EU-wide Eurobarometer survey with 25951 online interviews of consumers aged 18 and above to gather insights into consumer trends in the insurance and pensions sectors.

Consumer and behavioral research are essential to EIOPA’s work, enabling us to capture more effectively the perception and the experience that consumers, policyholders and beneficiaries have with insurance and pension services.

EIOPA used the findings of the Eurobarometer survey, together with other data sources, in the Consumer Trends Report.

## Ownership of savings and insurance products

Household (62%) and vehicle insurance (57%) are the two most common insurances consumers typically own.

## Value for money of savings and insurance products

Consumers agree that vehicle (69%) and household (68%) insurance savings products offer more value for money.

## Buying/renewing of savings and insurance products

A private pension (39%) is the less purchasable / renewed insurance product because of high fees, difficulties, understanding the product or poor performance, or limited coverage.

## Living financially comfortably in retirement

Consumers with private and occupational pension schemes (65%) felt more confident about living financially comfortably in retirement.

In contrast, only 36% of consumers without a private pension scheme feel that they will have enough money to live comfortably during their retirement years.

## Purchasing insurance online

Most consumers (60%) agreed that they can find customized insurance products and services online that fit their needs and preferences.

However, only 30% of consumers trust that chatbots offer accurate and complete support in their purchase.

## Trust in insurance providers

56% of consumers trust that their employer is involved in the distribution and/ or design of insurance and private pension plans to ensure a good and adequate retirement outcome.

## Insurance products abroad

64% of consumers don’t purchase insurance products from a provider based in another country, even if they operate in their country of residence.

## Private pension products

The primary reasons why consumers don’t have a private pension scheme are insufficient financial resources (28%) and lack of awareness (19%).

Customers in the Netherlands (25%) and Denmark (22%) without a private pension agree that their main reason is their full satisfaction with the occupational pension scheme.

## Details

* The survey was conducted via computer-assisted web interviewing (CAWI), using Ipsos online panels and their partner network. A share of respondents in Malta and Luxembourg was recruited via social media networks.
* Fieldwork: between 17 and 25 July 2024
* Target population: EU citizens, 18 years and over
* Coverage: EU27
* Number of interviews: 25 951
* Sample size per country: 500 in LU, CY, MT; 1000 in all other countries
