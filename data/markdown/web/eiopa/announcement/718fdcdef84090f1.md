The Board of Supervisors of the European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority (EIOPA) has decided to extend the term of Fausto Parente, the current Executive Director of EIOPA, for another five years. The decision is based on the evaluation of the work delivered during the first mandate and expectations for the next term.

Fausto Parente, Executive Director of EIOPA, said: “I am proud to continue to serve EU citizens as EIOPA Executive Director for the next five years. During these challenging times, I am committed to lead the organisation in an efficient and effective way. Together with our dedicated staff, national authorities and stakeholders, I am confident we will continue to make a difference for the insurance and pensions market, supervisory community and most importantly, for the European citizens.”
