## Description

On this page, the preparatory guidelines on System of Governance are published. The preparatory guidelines, which  applied from 1 January 2014 to 1 January 2016, aimed to facilitate a proper preparation for the implementation of  Solvency II. From 1 January 2016, a new version of the guidelines apply.

Press the below button to get to the current version of the guidelines on System of Governance.

[Guidelines on System of Governance](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/content/guidelines-system-governance_en)

*According to Art 73 of the EIOPA Regulation referring to language arrangements, EIOPA issues its Guidelines in all EU official languages (with the exception of Irish, according to Council Regulation (EU) No 1257/2010).**In case of divergence between different versions, please inform EIOPA info{at}eiopa.europa.eu about the provision in question. EIOPA will clarify this as soon as possible.*
