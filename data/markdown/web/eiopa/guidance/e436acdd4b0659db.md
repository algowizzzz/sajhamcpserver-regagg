## Description

The revised Guidelines on undertaking-specific parameters – that can replace standards parameters in standard formula capital requirement calculations – correct outdated legal references and streamline the guidelines without changing their intended meaning. In line with [EIOPA’s balanced approach to simplifying regulation](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/eiopa-follows-comprehensive-and-balanced-approach-simplification-amid-push-competitiveness-2025-04-08_en), the number of individual guidelines is reduced by 21%.

*The revised Guidelines will become applicable two months after the publication of translations into all official EU languages. Until then, the* [*Guidelines issued in 2015*](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/publications/guidelines-undertaking-specific-parameters_en) *are applicable.*
