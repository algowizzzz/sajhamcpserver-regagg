Publication date

## Description

The updated Guidelines on methods for determining market shares for supervisory reporting align the existing framework with the legal changes introduced throughout the Solvency II Review.

*The revised Guidelines will become applicable on 30 January 2027. Until then, the*[*Guidelines issued in 2015*](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/publications/guidelines-methods-determining-market-shares-reporting_en)*are applicable.*
