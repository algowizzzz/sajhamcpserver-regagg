# Committee on Derivatives (Committee 7)

Committee 7 focuses on issues related to all types of derivatives products and markets. Committee 7’s membership consists of experts on exchange-traded and over-the-counter derivatives, the G20 reforms on OTC derivatives, and commodity derivatives.

Committee 7 was formed in 2017 by merging the former IOSCO Committee on Commodity Derivatives and the IOSCO OTC Derivatives Task Force (OTCDTF). To date, Committee 7 has carried out G20 mandated projects on "[Principles for Oil Price Reporting Agencies](/library/pubdocs/pdf/IOSCOPD391.pdf)" and "[Principles for the Regulation and Supervision of Commodity Derivatives Markets](/library/pubdocs/pdf/IOSCOPD358.pdf)" and other important IOSCO projects, including "Efficient Resiliency", "Third Party Service Providers", and "Storage and Delivery Infrastructures".
