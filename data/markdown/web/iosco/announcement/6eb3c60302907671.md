Membership

Over 200 members from over 130 jurisdictions regulate 99% of the world's financial markets. We offer three categories of membership - Ordinary, Associate and Affiliate.
  
  
Regulators make up our core membership. We also welcome members who benefit from opportunities to actively engage with policymakers.
