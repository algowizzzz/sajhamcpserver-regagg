# Annual Meetings

---

## Upcoming Annual Meetings

IOSCO members meet every year at the Annual Meeting (AM) to discuss important issues related to world securities and futures markets. The IOSCO 51st Annual Meeting will take place on 08-19 May 2026.  
Registrations are now open: [Event Registration Form](/members_area/meeting-registration/?MeetingID=430&virtual=1)
