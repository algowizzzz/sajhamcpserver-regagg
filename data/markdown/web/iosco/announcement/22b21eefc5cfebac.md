Monitoring Group Welcomes Important Step in Implementing its Recommendations with the Establishment of the International Foundation for Ethics and Audit
https://www.iosco.org/news/pdf/IOSCONEWS685.pdf
<p>Madrid, 27 March 2023<br><br>The Monitoring Group (MG) is responsible for the overall governance of the international audit and ethics standard-setting system, including conducting periodic effectiveness reviews. In July 2020, the MG issued its <a href="https://www.iosco.org/about/monitoring\_group/pdf/2020-07-MG-Paper-Strengthening-The-International-Audit-And-Ethics-Standard-Setting-System.pdf" target="\_blank">recommendations Strengthening the International Audit and Ethics Standard-Setting System</a> (MG Recommendations).<br>In December 2022, the MG <a href="https://www.iosco.org/about/monitoring\_group/pdf/2022-12-02-MG%20Monitoring%20Group%20Reports%20on%20Progress%20to%20Implement%20Recommendations%20to%20Strengthen%20the%20International%20Audit%20and%20Ethics%20Standard-Setting%20System.pdf" target="\_blank">reported</a> on the progress made to implement the MG Recommendations and today, the MG is pleased to recognize the <a href="https://www.ethicsandaudit.org/news-events/2023-03/new-international-foundation-ethics-and-audit-strengthens-independence-standard-setting-system" target="\_blank">announcement</a> of the establishment of the International Foundation for Ethics and Audit (IFEA or Foundation). One of the MG Recommendations is that “the standard-setting Boards should be housed in a separate legal entity outside of and independent from” the accounting profession. The creation of the Foundation marks a significant milestone in implementing the MG Recommendations in order to create a more independent and inclusive standard-setting structure that is responsive to the public interest in the development of timely, high-quality audit, assurance, ethics, and independence standards. The International Auditing and Assurance Standards Board (IAASB) and the International Ethics Standards Board for Accountants (IESBA) (collectively the Standard-Setting Boards) will now be housed in a separate, independent legal entity as outlined in the MG Recommendations.</p>
