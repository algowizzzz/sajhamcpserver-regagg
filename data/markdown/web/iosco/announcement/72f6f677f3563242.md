# Presidents Committee

The Presidents Committee is composed of all the Presidents (Chairs) of ordinary and associate members and meets once a year during the Annual Conference. It is chaired by the President of the ordinary member hosting the Annual Conference. Affiliate members may attend the Presidents Committee meeting to hear detailed reports on the work and key developments of IOSCO.
