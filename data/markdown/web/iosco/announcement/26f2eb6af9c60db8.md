# Asia-Pacific Regional Committee

The APRC is one of four regional committees constituted by IOSCO to focus on regional issues relating to securities regulation in the Asia-Pacific region.

## Members of the Asia-Pacific Regional Committee

### Chair

Ms. Julia Leung, Chief Executive Officer, Securities and Futures Commission, **Hong Kong**

![Ms. Julia Leung](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Julia-Leung-250.png)

Ms Julia Leung has been Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC) since 1 January 2023.

Ms Leung joined the SFC in March 2015 as Executive Director of the Investment Products Division. She became Executive Director of the Intermediaries Division in June 2016 and took on the additional capacity of Deputy CEO in March 2018.

In more than 25 years of public service, Ms Leung has extensive experience in financial regulation, market development and international cooperation. Prior to joining the SFC, Ms Leung was the Under Secretary for Financial Services and the Treasury from August 2008 to December 2013. Before that, she served at the Hong Kong Monetary Authority for 14 years, spending the last eight years as the Executive Director responsible for financial cooperation with Mainland and other international regulators.

Ms Leung graduated from The Chinese University of Hong Kong and earned a master's degree from Columbia University in the City of New York. In 2014, she authored a book on how Asia has surmounted various financial crises.

### Vice Chair

Mr. Tuhin Kanta Pandey, Chairman, Securities and Exchange Board of India, **India**

![Tuhin Kanta Pandey](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Tuhin-Kanta-Pandey-250.jpg)

Shri Tuhin Kanta Pandey took charge as Chairman, Securities and Exchange Board of India in Mumbai on March 01, 2025.

Shri Pandey is an IAS Officer of Odisha Cadre belonging to 1987 batch. Before joining SEBI he served as Union Finance Secretary, Revenue Secretary and Secretary, Department of Investment & Public Asset Management (DIPAM), Secretary, Department of Public Enterprises and Secretary, DOP&T.

Shri Pandey has held many significant positions in Union Government and State Government of Odisha, in addition to serving a stint in the Regional Office of the United Nations Industrial Development Organisation (UNIDO).

In the Union Government, his previous organisations include Planning Commission (Now Niti Aayog), Cabinet Secretariat and Ministry of Commerce.

In State Government of Odisha, he served as administrative head in the Departments of Finance, Health, General Administration, Commercial Taxes and Transport.

In his career spanning over more than three decades, Shri Pandey has wide experience in the areas of Economy & Finance, Industrial Development, Tax policy and administration and has been instrumental in bringing about several institutional innovations, new policies and systems in the Departments he has served.

He holds a Masters Degree in Economics from Panjab University, Chandigarh and Master of Business Administration from the University of Birmingham (UK).

### Board Representative

Mr. Lim Tuang Lee, Assistant Managing Director (Capital Markets), Monetary Authority of Singapore, **Singapore**

![Lim Tuang Lee](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Lim-Tuang-Lee-250.jpg)

Mr Lim Tuang Lee is the Assistant Managing Director of the Capital Markets Group, overseeing the regulation and supervision of capital markets. The group is responsible for the licensing and supervision of exchanges, clearing houses and capital markets intermediaries, including financial advisers.

Mr Lim represents MAS at various international committees, including the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) and the ASEAN Capital Markets Forum (ACMF). He chaired the IOSCO FinTech Task Force from March 2022 to May 2026, which among others, worked on issues related to crypto-assets, decentralised finance (DeFi), tokenisation, artificial intelligence and RegTech/SupTech. He is currently the ordinary Asia-Pacific Regional Committee (APRC) Representative to the IOSCO Board, and Vice-Chair of the ACMF.

Mr Lim has a Bachelor of Accountancy (First Class Honours) from the Nanyang Technological University and a Master degree in Finance (with specialisation in Risk Management and Financial Engineering) from the Université de Lausanne, Switzerland. He also holds an Executive Master of Change from INSEAD.

### Board Representative

Mr. Mohammad Faiz Azmi, Chairman, Securities Commission, **Malaysia**

![Mohammad Faiz Azmi](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Mohammad-Faiz-Azmi-250.jpg)

Dato' Mohammad Faiz Azmi became Executive Chairman of the Securities Commission Malaysia in June 2024. He chairs the ASEAN Capital Markets Forum in conjunction with Malaysia’s ASEAN Chairmanship in 2025, and leads the development of the Capital Market Masterplan 4 (2026–2030).

He spent over 30 years with PwC and served as Chairman of PwC Malaysia for 11 years until 2023. He led PwC's Global Islamic Finance Team (2007–2012), served as Malaysia’s Financial Services Leader and joint Head of Audit, and advanced ESG and Shariah Investing initiatives.

Dato’ Faiz advised the Government on Accrual Accounting, financial restructurings and investigations, as well as being Administrator for several entities.

A Fellow of ICAEW and member of MIA, he also served as President of MIA, Chairman of MASB and President of KLBC. He studied law at Durham University, joined the English Bar and holds Honorary Doctorates of Laws from Nottingham and Durham.

### Board Representative

Prof. Pornanong Budsaratragoon, Secretary-General, Securities and Exchange Commission, **Thailand**

![Pornanong Budsaratragoon](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Pornanong-Budsaratragoon-250.jpg)

Pornanong Budsaratragoon, PhD, CFA, CIA, is Secretary-General of the Securities and Exchange Commission of Thailand, appointed on 18 September 2023. She concurrently serves as Commissioner and Board Secretary of the SEC Thailand, Chairperson of the Capital Market Supervisory Board, and Board Member of the Bank of Thailand, as well as Member of the Financial Institutions Policy Committee and Commissioner of the Office of Insurance Commission.

Pornanong brings extensive expertise in capital market regulation and corporate governance, having led key committees governing debt securities, structured products, derivatives, market intermediaries, and asset management. She holds a DBA in Finance and a BBA in Quantitative Analysis from Chulalongkorn University, and an MBA in Management Information Systems from the University of Dallas.

Prior to her current role, she served as a distinguished, full-time academic at Chulalongkorn University, where she headed the Banking and Finance Department and chaired the MSc Program in Corporate Governance. A prolific author and researcher, she was appointed Full Professor of Business Administration in Finance in 2021 and has served on the boards of multiple national financial and academic institutions.
