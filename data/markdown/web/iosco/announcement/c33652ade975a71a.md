# Inter-American Regional Committee

The IARC is one of four regional committees constituted by IOSCO to focus on regional issues relating to securities regulation in the Inter-American region. The IARC meets generally in conjunction with the Council of Securities Regulators of the Americas (COSRA).

## Members of the Inter-American Regional Committee

### Chair

Ms. Christina Rolle, Executive Director, Securities Commission of The Bahamas, **Bahamas**

![Ms. Christina Rolle](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Christina-Rolle-250.jpg)

Ms. Christina R. Rolle is Executive Director of the Securities Commission of The Bahamas, having been appointed 26 January 2015.

Ms. Rolle holds an MBA from Kellogg School of Management, Northwestern University, an LLM (Corporate and Commercial Law) from the London School of Economics and is an alumna of Harvard Business School. She has more than 30 years of experience in the financial services industry, which includes various senior managerial positions.

During her tenure at the Securities Commission of The Bahamas, Ms. Rolle has directly overseen the development and enactment of key pieces of legislation, including the Investment Funds Act, 2019; the Financial and Corporate Service Providers Act, 2020; the Digital Assets and Registered Exchanges Act, 2020 and 2024; the Securities Industry Act, 2024; and the Carbon Credit Trading Act, 2022.

Ms. Rolle has served three terms as Vice Chair of the Inter-American Regional Committee (IARC) and was elected as Chair for the term 2026-2028.

### Vice Chair

Ms. Shanna Lespere, Deputy Chief Executive Officer, Bermuda Monetary Authority, **Bermuda**

![Ms. Shanna Lespere](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Shanna-Lespere-250.jpg)

As Deputy Chief Executive Officer of the Bermuda Monetary Authority (Authority or BMA), Shanna Lespere provides executive leadership for the BMA’s operational, human capital and regulatory strategies. She supports the Chief Executive Officer in developing recommendations to the Board of Directors and leads the implementation of agreed Board strategies, exercising executive responsibility for the BMA’s business under authority delegated by the Board. Mrs. Lespere joined the BMA in 2004 and has held a range of senior leadership roles across supervision, licensing and operations, including Assistant Director (Investment Group), Director of the Insurance Department, Director of Licensing and Authorisations, Director of Supervision (Insurance – Complex Institutions), Director of International Affairs, and Director of Operations. She was promoted to Chief Operating Officer in 2015 and appointed Deputy CEO in 2022. Prior to the BMA, she was Vice President at Aon Insurance Managers (Bermuda) Ltd. and worked as a Senior Accountant with PricewaterhouseCoopers (Bermuda). Mrs. Lespere is a Chartered Professional Accountant (CPA) of Canada, an FCPA (CPA Bermuda), a Chartered Director, and a Gallup-Certified Strengths Coach.

### Board Representative

Mr. Roberto E. Silva, Chairman, Comisión Nacional de Valores, **Argentina**

![Mr. Roberto E. Silva](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Roberto-Silva-250.jpeg)

Roberto E. Silva is the Chairman of Argentina’s Securities Commission (CNV). He is a lawyer (University of Buenos Aires) with an LL.M. (University of Illinois) and an expert in capital markets, financing, trusts, banking law, and foreign exchange controls. He currently serves his second term as Board Representative in IOSCO’s Inter-American Regional Committee (IARC) and is a member of its Finance and Audit Committee (FAC), and a member of the IDB’s FintechLac Executive Committee. He frequently participates in the OECD’s Corporate Governance and Financial Markets Committees.

Mr. Silva was a partner and board member at Marval O'Farrell Mairal (1993-2023) and previously worked at Shearman & Sterling (1991-92). He was Chair of the IBA Banking Law Committee and Secretary of the Argentine Chamber of Trusts and Direct Investment Funds in Productive Activities (CAFIDAP). He also participated in the FATF 4th Round of Mutual Evaluations for Argentina (2024). Likewise, he has been consistently recognized by Chambers and Partners and The Legal 500.

He has collaborated with Harvard’s PIFS, UBA, Catholic University of Argentina, Austral and Di Tella Universities, among others.
