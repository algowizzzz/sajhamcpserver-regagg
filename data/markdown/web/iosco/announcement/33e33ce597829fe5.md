# Growth and Emerging Markets Committee

The Growth and Emerging Markets Committee is the largest Committee within IOSCO, representing over 75% of the IOSCO's ordinary membership. The Committee seeks to promote the development and greater efficiency of emerging securities and futures markets by establishing principles and minimum standards, providing training programs and technical assistance for members and facilitating the exchange of information and transfer of technology and expertise.

The GEM comprises 94 members and 23 non-voting associate members who include the world's fastest growing economies and 10 of the G-20 members. Emerging economies are expected to represent a growing portion of IOSCO membership as new members continue to join.

IOSCO is the only international standard setter that has a Committee solely responsible for emerging market issues. This inclusiveness increases IOSCO's effectiveness and positions it to play a bigger part in shaping the global regulatory framework: The GEM has been allocated a seat on the IFRS Foundation Monitoring Board.

## Members of the Growth and Emerging Markets Committee

### Chair

Dr. Islam Azzam, Executive Chairman, Financial Regulatory Authority, **Egypt**

![Dr. Islam Azzam](/about/chairs/images/tiny/DrIslamAzzam2-tiny.jpg)

Dr. Islam Azzam currently serves as Acting Executive Chairman of the Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA), pursuant to Presidential Decree No. 116 of 2026. He brings extensive leadership experience across Egypt’s financial sector, having most recently served as Executive Chairman of the Egyptian Exchange from August 2025 to March 2026, and previously as Deputy Chairman of the FRA from 2021 to 2025, where he oversaw capital markets, insurance, and non-banking financial activities.

Dr. Azzam’s earlier roles include Advisor to the Chairman of the Mortgage Finance Authority and Assistant to the Chairman of the FRA. He also served as Managing Director of the SME Investment Fund “Bedaya” and Advisor to the Minister of Investment. He began his professional career as an Economic Analyst within the FRA’s Market Surveillance Department.

In academia, Dr. Azzam is Professor of Finance at the American University in Cairo, where he led the Finance Department and Graduate Studies Program for over 15 years. He has held several international academic appointments, including at Stellenbosch University and London School of Economics.

He serves on the boards of multiple institutions, including the Anti-Money Laundering Unit, the Egyptian Arab Land Bank (as Chair of the Risk Committee), and the Financial Services Institute. Dr. Azzam holds a PhD in Economics from the University of California Irvine and has published widely in finance, econometrics, and monetary policy.

### Vice Chair

Mr. Khaled Alhomoud, Board Commissioner, Capital Market Authority, **Saudi Arabia**

![Mr. Khaled Alhomoud](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Khaled-Alhomoud-250.jpg)

Mr. Khaled Alhomoud was appointed as a member of the Board of Commissioners of the Capital Market Authority in October 2017. Prior to being Commissioner, Mr. Alhomoud has served in. He was the Head of Mutual Funds Department from 2006 to 2013. In 2013 Mr. Alhomoud was appointed as the CMA Deputy for Strategic and International Affairs.

Beside Mr. Alhomoud role as a Commissioner, he chairs the CMA’s Supervisory Committee for the Implementation of Risk Methodology. In addition, Mr. Alhomoud serves as a Board Member of the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZATCA). He also chairs the Nomination and Remuneration Committee at ZATCA.

Prior to joining the CMA, Mr. Alhomoud worked for the Saudi Arabian Monetary Agency (Central Bank) as Head of Investment Accreditation Manager responsible for development of professional certificates.

Mr. Alhomoud earned his BA in accounting with Honors from King Saudi University, Riyadh – KSA and an MBA from Kent State University in the USA, specializing in finance. Mr. Alhomoud attended several management and leadership programs with prestigious universities such as Harvard, London Business School, INSEAD and IMD.

### Board Representative

Mr. Tarik SENHAJI, Chairman and CEO, Autorité Marocaine du Marché des Capitaux, **Morocco**

![Mr. Tarik SENHAJI](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Tarik-SENHAJI-250.jpg?v=1)

A graduate of Ecole Polytechnique in Paris and the Ecole Nationale de la Statistique et de l’Administration Economique (ENSAE PARIS), Mr Tarik SENHAJI has nearly 30 years of experience in the financial sector, both in Morocco and internationally.

He began his career in Washington, D.C., at the International Finance Corporation (World Bank Group), where he served as a Financial Officer. He then moved to London, holding various senior positions in capital markets within major international banks, including Société Générale, Dresdner Kleinwort Benson, Calyon CIB, and Natixis CIB.

Upon returning to Morocco, Mr SENHAJI served as Chief Executive Officer of Ithmar Capital, a Strategic Investment Fund, and as Chairman of the Moroccan Agency for Tourism Development. In March 2020, he was appointed Chief Executive Officer of the Casablanca Stock Exchange.

In October 2025, His Majesty King Mohammed VI appointed him Chairman and CEO of the Moroccan Capital Market Authority (AMMC).
