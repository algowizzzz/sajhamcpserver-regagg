# Our leadership

---

![Mr. Jean-Paul Servais](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Jean-Paul-Servais-250.png)

##### Mr. Jean-Paul Servais *Chair of the IOSCO Board and Chair of the European Regional Committee (ERC)*

Jean-Paul Servais is the Chairman of the IOSCO Board. He is also Chairman of the IOSCO European Regional Committee.

Mr Servais is the Chairman of the Belgian Financial Services and Markets Authority (FSMA) and a board member of several international supervisory bodies for the financial sector such as ESMA and the ESRB. He sits on a number of supervisory colleges that coordinate the (cross-border) supervision of several financial institutions and infrastructures.

He teaches at the Université Libre de Bruxelles (ULB, University of Brussels), where he is part-time professor in International Business Law and in the specialized Master in Tax Law.

![Dr. Islam Azzam](/about/chairs/images/tiny/DrIslamAzzam2-tiny.jpg)

##### Dr. Islam Azzam *Vice-Chair of the IOSCO Board and Chair of the Growth and Emerging Markets (GEM) Committee*

Dr. Islam Azzam currently serves as Acting Executive Chairman of the Financial Regulatory Authority (FRA), pursuant to Presidential Decree No. 116 of 2026. He brings extensive leadership experience across Egypt’s financial sector, having most recently served as Executive Chairman of the Egyptian Exchange from August 2025 to March 2026, and previously as Deputy Chairman of the FRA from 2021 to 2025, where he oversaw capital markets, insurance, and non-banking financial activities.

Dr. Azzam’s earlier roles include Advisor to the Chairman of the Mortgage Finance Authority and Assistant to the Chairman of the FRA. He also served as Managing Director of the SME Investment Fund “Bedaya” and Advisor to the Minister of Investment. He began his professional career as an Economic Analyst within the FRA’s Market Surveillance Department.

In academia, Dr. Azzam is Professor of Finance at the American University in Cairo, where he led the Finance Department and Graduate Studies Program for over 15 years. He has held several international academic appointments, including at Stellenbosch University and London School of Economics.

He serves on the boards of multiple institutions, including the Anti-Money Laundering Unit, the Egyptian Arab Land Bank (as Chair of the Risk Committee), and the Financial Services Institute. Dr. Azzam holds a PhD in Economics from the University of California Irvine and has published widely in finance, econometrics, and monetary policy.

![Toshiyuki Miyoshi](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Toshiyuki-Miyoshi-250.jpg)

##### Mr. Toshiyuki Miyoshi *Vice-Chair of the IOSCO Board*

Appointed on 22 July 2025, Toshiyuki Miyoshi is Vice Minister for International Affairs at the Financial Services Agency (FSA) of Japan. He represents the FSA at the Financial Stability Board (FSB) and serves as Chair of the Executive Committee of the International Association of Insurance Supervisors (IAIS), Chair of the IFRS Foundation Monitoring Board and Vice Chair of the Corporate Governance Committee, Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD).

Previously, Mr. Miyoshi was a member of the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision and an alternate member of the FSB’s Standing Committee on Supervisory and Regulatory Cooperation. He also worked on supervision of Japan's major banks and insurers, as well as on cross-sectoral supervisory policies. He has working experience at Japan's Ministry of Finance on government debt management and the International Monetary Fund (IMF) on the international monetary system.

He holds a BA in law from the University of Tokyo and an MPhil in Politics from the University of Oxford.

![Grant Vingoe](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Grant-Vingoe-250.jpg)

##### Mr. Grant Vingoe *Vice-Chair of the IOSCO Board*

Grant Vingoe is Chief Executive Officer of the Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) and a member of the OSC’s Board of Directors.

He chairs the Policy Coordination Committee of the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA) and serves on the board of the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO). He also chairs the Joint Regulators Committee overseeing the CSA’s Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments and holds leadership roles in Canadian standard-setting bodies and international sustainable finance forums.

A former partner at leading global law firms, he advises regulators, issuers, and financial services clients on cross-border securities law and financial markets.

He holds a J.D. (Osgoode Hall Law School) and an LL.M. (New York University School of Law) and was called to the bars of Ontario (1983) and New York (1985).

![Mark T. Uyeda](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Mark-T-Uyeda-250.jpg)

##### Mr. Mark T. Uyeda *Vice-Chair of the IOSCO Board*

Mark T. Uyeda has been a Commissioner of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) since June 2022. He served as the SEC’s Acting Chairman from January 21 to April 21, 2025. Mr. Uyeda also serves as Vice Chair of the OECD’s Corporate Governance Committee.

Mr. Uyeda joined the SEC staff in 2006, where he has served on the executive staff and in the Division of Investment Management. He has also served on details to the U.S. Department of the Treasury, the U.S. Department of Labor, and the U.S. Senate Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs.

Prior to the SEC, he served as the Chief Advisor to the California Corporations Commissioner under Governor Arnold Schwarzenegger and worked as a corporate and securities attorney in Washington, D.C. and Los Angeles.

Mr. Uyeda received his undergraduate degree in business administration from Georgetown University and his legal degree (J.D.) with honors from Duke University.

![Mr. Rodrigo Buenaventura](/v2/img/gs/r.buenaventura.jpg)

##### Mr. Rodrigo Buenaventura *Secretary General*

Rodrigo Buenaventura took over as Secretary General in January 2025.

He served four years as Chair of the Spanish financial markets regulator, CNMV, until December 2024, and as such was a member of the ESMA and IOSCO boards. Rodrigo chaired several policy committees within ESMA (Investor Protection, Market Integrity and Markets) and IOSCO (Sustainable Finance Task Force).

Previously, he developed a career in financial markets regulation and supervision, starting at CNMV in 2005 as Head of International and then Secondary Markets, joining the then newly created ESMA as its first Head of Markets (2011 to 2017) and then back at CNMV as Director General for Markets until 2020. He specialized in post-trading, market abuse supervision, corporate reporting, market structure and corporate governance.

After graduating in Economics in Madrid, he spent the first eleven years of his career (1993-2004) in the private sector as a financial consultant at a leading Spanish consulting company (AFI). First as a fixed income and monetary policy analyst, then as partner of the firm and member of the executive committee, focusing on asset and liability management, securities issuance, debt management, rating advice and public finance.
