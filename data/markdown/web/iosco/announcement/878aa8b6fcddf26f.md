# Africa / Middle-East Regional Committee

The AMERC is one of four regional committees constituted by IOSCO to focus on regional issues relating to securities regulation in the African / Middle-East region.

## Members of the Africa / Middle-East Regional Committee

### Chair

H.E. Waleed Saeed Al Awadhi, Chief Executive Officer, Capital Market Authority, **United Arab Emirates**

![H.E. Waleed Saeed Al Awadhi](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Waleed-Saeed-Al-Awadhi-250.jpg)

H.E. Waleed Saeed Al Awadhi is Chief Executive Officer of the UAE’s Capital Market Authority, where he leads regulatory development in line with international standards and supports the country’s national economic priorities.

He previously served as Chief Operating Officer at the Dubai Financial Services Authority (DFSA), overseeing core regulatory, operational, and digital transformation functions. He plays an active role in global financial regulation through his engagement with IOSCO, where he serves as Chair of the Africa and Middle East Regional Committee and as a member of the IOSCO Board. He is also a global assessor for the Financial Action Task Force (FATF).

H.E. Al Awadhi serves on several national boards and committees and has held senior leadership roles across banking, media, real estate, and technology. He holds a Master of Laws in Commercial Law and is an alumnus of Harvard Business School.

### Vice Chair

Dr. Emomotimi John Agama, Director General, Securities and Exchange Commission, **Nigeria**

![Dr. Emomotimi John Agama](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Emomotimi-John-Agama-250.jpg)

Dr. Emomotimi John Agama is the Director-General of the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC), Nigeria, the apex regulator of the Nigerian capital market. With over three decades of experience in capital market regulation, finance, and economic policy, he has built a distinguished career driving market reforms, institutional development, and regulatory innovation.

Before his appointment as Director-General in April 2024, he served as Managing Director of the Nigerian Capital Market Institute (NCMI), where he led major capacity-building and market education initiatives across Nigeria and Africa. Since joining the SEC in 2004, he has held several strategic leadership positions, including Head of Market Infrastructure and Innovation and Head of Registration and Exchanges.

Dr. Agama has played a key role in modernising Nigeria’s capital market framework, particularly in areas relating to digital assets, investor protection, sustainable finance, and market integrity. He currently serves as Vice Chairman of IOSCO’s Africa/Middle East Regional Committee (AMERC) and Chairman of the West Africa Securities Regulators Association (WASRA), contributing to regional and global regulatory cooperation and capital market development.

### Board Representative

Mr. Tariq Al Shehab, Commissioner, Capital Markets Authority, **Kuwait**

![Tariq Al Shehab](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Tariq-Al-Shehab-250.jpg)

Tariq Al Shehab is a Commissioner at the Capital Markets Authority, where he also serves on the Audit Committee, chairs the Staff and Remuneration Committee, chair of the Steering and Coordination with IOSCO Committee and heads the Market Development Committee.

With over two decades of experience in capital markets, brokerage, and banking, he is recognized for driving strategic transformation and strengthening market infrastructure in Kuwait. Additionally, Tariq served as a board member of the Kuwait Stock Market and its committees for the period from 2010 to 2016.

He previously served as Chairman, Vice Chairman, and CEO of Watani Financial Brokerage Company, and led the Brokerage Division at Watani Investment Company. In these roles, he developed innovative trading products, expanded brokerage operations, and ensured regulatory compliance while enhancing competitiveness.

His impact includes leading major market development initiatives in collaboration with the Kuwait Stock Exchange and regulatory authorities, as well as building high-performing teams and modern trading systems. Earlier in his career, he held managerial roles at National Bank of Kuwait. He holds a bachelor’s degree from Kuwait University and has completed executive training at Harvard Business School and Columbia University.
