# Shape the future for IOSCO

At IOSCO Secretariat, you will be part of an international organization that is shaping the future of financial market regulation.
As the leading international standard-setter in the securities sector, we offer a unique opportunity to work with top regulators and global institutions, contributing to policies that impact financial markets around the world.
  
  
Joining IOSCO means:

![](/v2/img/working-with-us/global-impact-600x300.webp)

##### Global Impact

Play a key role in developing and implementing internationally recognized regulatory standards endorsed by the G20 and FSB.

![](/v2/img/working-with-us/collab-600x300.webp)

##### Collaboration & Influence

Work closely with leading international organizations, including the IMF, World Bank, and other regulatory bodies, to promote financial stability and innovation.

![](/v2/img/working-with-us/professional-growth-600x300.webp)

##### Professional Growth

Engage in cutting-edge discussions on global regulatory reform and gain exposure to high-level policy development and implementation.

![](/v2/img/working-with-us/diversity-600x300.webp)

##### Diverse & Inclusive Environment

Be part of a truly international and dynamic team that encourages knowledge sharing across jurisdictions.

# Areas of Work

![Standards Development](/v2/img/working-with-us/Standards-Development.png)

##### Standards Development

Contributes to create global regulatory principles to guide securities markets, addressing emerging issues like fintech, ESG, and crypto-assets. Collaborates with regulators and policymakers to ensure market transparency and resilience.

![Standards Implementation](/v2/img/working-with-us/Standards-Implementation.webp)

##### Standards Implementation

Ensures IOSCO guidelines are applied globally. Offers technical support, assessments, and training to help align national practices with international standards for consistent financial supervision.

![Enforcement and Cooperation](/v2/img/working-with-us/Enforcement and cooperation.webp)

##### Enforcement and Cooperation

Enhances cross-border regulatory collaboration to fight misconduct, fraud, and abuse. Supports enforcement actions and promotes transparency and accountability in markets.

![Operations and Legal](/v2/img/working-with-us/Operations.png)

##### Operations, Legal and Corporate Affairs and Communication

Manages internal operations including Finance, HR, and IT. Supports IOSCO's strategy, ensuring regulatory compliance and smooth day-to-day operations.

# Our Values: Why Join Us?

At IOSCO, our culture is built on strong values that define how we work and collaborate:

![](/v2/img/working-with-us/SVG/TEAM.svg)

We work as a TEAM – We foster cooperation, knowledge-sharing, and collective success.

![](/v2/img/working-with-us/SVG/TRUST.svg)

We build TRUST – Integrity, accountability, and transparency are at the core of our work.

![](/v2/img/working-with-us/SVG/EXCELLENCE.svg)

We are committed to EXCELLENCE – We strive for the highest standards in financial market regulation.

![](/v2/img/working-with-us/SVG/OPENNESS.svg)

We believe in OPENNESS – We encourage open dialogue, collaboration, and continuous learning.

![](/v2/img/working-with-us/SVG/DIVERSITY.svg)

We embrace DIVERSITY – We value different perspectives and promote an inclusive work environment.

![](/v2/img/working-with-us/SVG/RESPECT.svg)

We treat each other with RESPECT – We create a positive and supportive workplace where everyone is valued.
