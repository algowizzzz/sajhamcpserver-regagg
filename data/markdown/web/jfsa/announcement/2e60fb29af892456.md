## Public Comments

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| May 8, 2026 | The FSA published a draft partial amendment to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Financial Instruments Business Operators, etc.  In May 2025, the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO) published the Revised Recommendations for Liquidity Risk Management for Collective Investment Schemes and accompanying Guidance. Based on the recommendations, etc., the Comprehensive Guidelines are to be amended as required in order to encourage investment management business operators to take appropriate measures concerning liquidity risk management for publicly offered investment trusts.  Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), June 8, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/shouken/20260508-2/20260508-2.html) |
| May 1, 2026 | Publication of information on public consultation on the draft Public Notice Partially Amending the Public Notice Designating the Business Accounting Standards to be Specified by the FSA Commissioner as Prescribed in the Regulation on Terminology, Forms and Preparation Methods of Financial Statements, etc. and the draft Public Notice Partially Amending the Public Notice Designating the Business Accounting Standards to be Specified by the FSA Commissioner as Prescribed in the Regulation on Terminology, Forms and Preparation Methods of Consolidated Financial Statements  Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), June 1, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260501/20260501.html) |

### Others

| May 1, 2026 | Announcement of the establishment of the Management Guarantee Information Network for M&A and Business Succession  There are cases wherein personal guarantee provided by business owners hinders smooth M&A or business succession. Accordingly, by establishing a network for sharing information on business owners and successors facing such situations, it is aimed to achieve consensus on the necessity of guarantee agreements and other matters among related parties, including business owners and successors, financial institutions, and Credit Guarantee Corporations, and encourage business owners and successors to deepen understanding and accept their situations, thereby facilitating smooth M&A and business succession. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/receipt/k_network/k_network.html) |
