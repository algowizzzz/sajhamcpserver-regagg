### Securities

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| March 10, 2026 | The FSA published information on an administrative measure against Select Asset Management Limited.  As the location of the registered business office of Select Asset Management Limited, which is a financial instruments business operator (investment advisory and agency business), cannot be confirmed, the Director-General of the Kanto Local Finance Bureau rescinded the registration today based on the provisions of Article 52, paragraph (4) of the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/shouken/20260310.html) |

| March 13, 2026 | Updated lists of entities that are operating financial instruments businesses without proper registration [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/ordinary/chuui/mutouroku.html) |
| March 13, 2026 | The FSA published the results of the experiment of the project supported by the FinTech PoC Hub (10th project).  This proof-of-concept experiment verified the provision of services using the AMM\* function to customers, etc. that hold (a wallet linked to) an address which shows that the Know Your Customer (KYC) process was undertaken by a financial institution, etc. using tokens resembling cryptoassets, electronically recorded transferable rights, etc. to be indicated on securities, and electronic payment instruments, as well as measures to reduce risks of misuse for money laundering and terrorist financing.  \* Automated Market Maker (AMM) generally refers to a mechanism wherein a smart contract (automatic enforcement program) automatically calculates transaction prices (exchange rates) based on the volume of cryptoassets deposited in a liquidity pool (a pair of cryptoassets to be exchanged). [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260313-02/20260313-02.html) |
| March 13, 2026 | The FSA published the results of the experiment of the project supported by the FinTech PoC Hub (9th project).  This proof-of-concept experiment verified the availability of Verifiable Credentials (VC) for identity confirmation by financial institutions.
