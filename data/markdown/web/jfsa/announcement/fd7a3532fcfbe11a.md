# Financial Services Agency’s Efforts to Promote Innovation

## Support System

[FinTech Support Desk](/en/news/2018/20180717.html)

One-stop assistance of financial regulations in English for startups with innovative proposition.

[Financial Market Entry Office](/en/policy/marketentry/index.html)

One-stop service in English for business newly entering Japanese market. Step-by-step assistance for registration procedures and post registration supervision.

[FinTech PoC Hub](/en/news/2022/20221031-2/20221031.html)

A Hub where dedicated FSA team assists innovative firms, both incumbents and new entrants to conduct proof of concept in financial services.

## Activity

[Japan Fintech Week](/en/policy/bgin/innovation.html)

One of the largest events in Japan　communicating the appeal of Japanese fintech to the world and creating business opportunities for further development of fintech.

[DeFi Governance](/en/policy/bgin/information.html)

Research on decentralized finance and regulatory efforts on DeFi. Details of Blockchain Governance Initiative Network (BGIN).

[Fintech Meetup](/en/policy/bgin/innovation.html)

The FSA sponsors online meetups with fintech firms from overseas hosted by JETRO and Embassies.
