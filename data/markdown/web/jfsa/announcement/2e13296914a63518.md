# Minister KATAYAMA meets Mr. Hernández de Cos, General Manager of the BIS, in Tokyo

![photo (Minister KATAYAMA meets Mr. Hernández de Cos, General Manager of the BIS)](/inter/etc/20260421/01.JPG)

On April 20, 2026, Ms. KATAYAMA Satsuki, Minister of Finance and Minister of State for Financial Services, met with Mr. Pablo Hernández de Cos, General Manager of the Bank for International Settlements (BIS). They exchanged views on recent financial sector developments including digital finance.
