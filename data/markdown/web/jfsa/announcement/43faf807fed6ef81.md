### Others

|  |  |

| March 17, 2026 | The FSA published a list of initiatives for Global Money Week 2026.  Global Money Week is a global awareness-raising campaign organized by the OECD International Network on Financial Education (INFE) for the purpose of promoting financial education and financial inclusion for children and young people. The FSA previously issued a notice concerning Global Money Week 2026 and publicly sought initiatives regarding financial education for young people to be implemented during the period. The FSA compiled initiatives being implemented by entities and groups, etc. that support Global Money Week and published the list. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260317-1/20260317.html) |
