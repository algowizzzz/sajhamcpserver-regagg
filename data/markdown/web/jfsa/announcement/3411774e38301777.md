## Public Comments

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| July 31, 2026 | Publication of the draft revision of “Venture Capitals: Recommendations and Hopes (VCRHs)”  The published draft is for revising “Venture Capitals: Recommendations and Hopes (VCRHs),” which was formulated on October 17, 2024. The VCRHs clearly provides that follow-ups and reviews should be conducted as required based on changes in the market environment and in viewpoints regarding practices and preferable forms of VCs. Based on this provision, the VCRHs is to be reviewed as required. Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), August 31, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/singi/20260731.html) |
| July 31, 2026 | Publication of information on public consultation on the draft Cabinet Office Order Partially Amending the Cabinet Office Order on Financial Instruments Business and the draft Public Notice Partially Amending the Public Notice to Establish Criteria for Whether the Capital Adequacy of a Special Financial Instruments Business Operator and Its Subsidiary Corporations, etc. is Appropriate in Light of the Assets, etc. Held by the Special Financial Instruments Business Operator and Its Subsidiary Corporations, etc.  The amendments aim to develop relevant provisions regarding the handling of fixed assets relating to cancelable leases, out of fixed assets to be deducted from equity capital, in the calculation of the capital adequacy ratios of financial instruments business operators. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/shouken/20260731/20260731.html) |
| July 30, 2026 | Publication of draft partial amendments to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision of Major Banks, etc. and other Guidelines (related to bank agency services, etc.)  With regard to services provided by banks, etc. in collaboration with other businesses by the use of API, etc., the amendments aim to clarify measures necessary for preventing service users’ false recognition and what acts by other businesses fall under bank agency services (brokerage). Public comments will be accepted until no later than 6:00 p.m. (JST), August 31, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/sonota/20260730-2/20260730-2.html) |
| July 30, 2026 | Publication of draft partial amendments to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision of Major Banks, etc. and other Guidelines (related to structured deposits)  The amendments aim to clarify supervisory viewpoints regarding explanations on risks to be provided by banks upon accepting structured deposits, which have complicated structures, and other matters. Public comments will be accepted until no later than 6:00 p.m. (JST), August 31, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/sonota/20260730-3/20260730-3.html) |
| July 28, 2026 | Publication of information on public consultation on the draft Cabinet Office Order Partially Amending the Regulation for Enforcement of the Act on Investment Trusts and Investment Corporations  With regard to the portion corresponding to the constituents of in-kind ETFs for which the ownership of those applying for establishing the ETFs exceeds 30%, the Cabinet Office Order aims to develop provisions to make it possible to establish the relevant portion with money. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/shouken/20260728/20260728.html) |
