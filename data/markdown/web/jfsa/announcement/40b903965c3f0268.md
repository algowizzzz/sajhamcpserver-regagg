## Public Comments

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| July 17, 2026 | Publication of information, including the results of the public consultation on the draft Guidelines Concerning Anti-Money Laundering, Countering the Financing of Terrorism, and Countering Proliferation Financing (AML/CFT/CPF) by Certified Public Accountants and Audit Corporations [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/sonota/20260717-3/20260717.html) |
| July 17, 2026 | Promulgation of the Cabinet Office Order Partially Amending the Regulation on Accountings for Investment Corporations and publication of the results of public consultation on the Cabinet Office Order  The amendment aims to develop the provisions of the Regulation so as to permit investment corporations to provide their asset investment reports, etc. to investors by posting them on their websites. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/shouken/20260717/20260717.html) |
| July 17, 2026 | Publication of information, including the results of the public consultation on the draft partial amendment to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies, etc.  The relevant Comprehensive Guidelines, etc. are to be amended as required for the purpose of strengthening countermeasures against cyber risks that have been heightening in recent years. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/sonota/20260717-2/20260717.html) |

### Banks

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| July 17, 2026 | Publication of a statistical report on business revitalization, etc. utilizing the "Guidelines for Business Revitalization, etc. of Small- and Medium-sized Enterprises"  The FSA has been encouraging financial institutions to actively utilize the "Guidelines for Business Revitalization, etc. of Small- and Medium-sized Enterprises" for their clients. From the perspective of further encouraging the utilization of the Guidelines, the FSA published a statistical report on the utilization of the Guidelines by financial institutions. The statistical report reflects the utilization status during the period from April 1, 2025, to March 31, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/ginkou/20260717-2/20260717-2.html) |
| July 15, 2026 | The FSA published "Progress Report on the Efficiency of Financial Intermediation," which outlines key initiatives and approaches taken by the FSA and the respective Regional Finance Bureaus toward the enhancement of the efficiency of financial intermediation by regional financial institutions. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/ginkou/20260715/20260715.html) |
| July 13, 2026 | Issuance of a request for financial measures to deal with disasters caused by the landslide that occurred in Koka City, Shiga Prefecture, in July 2026 [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r8/ginkou/20260713.html) |
