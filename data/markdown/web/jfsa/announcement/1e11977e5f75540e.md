# Japan Weeks 2026

![Japan Weeks 2026](/policy/financialcenter/japanweeks/logo-01.png)

The Government of Japan is advancing its initiatives to establish Japan as an international financial center and promote Japan as a leading asset management center.

Since the first “Japan Weeks” was held in 2023, we have continued to showcase the attractiveness of Japan’s financial and capital markets, as well as government initiatives, through a series of events that attract overseas investors and asset management firms.

In Fall 2026, we will host the fourth of edition of "Japan Weeks".  The core week is from October 26 to October 30, accompanied by a variety of finance-focused events scheduled in the weeks before and after.

For more details, visit our [Japan Weeks 2026 official website![open new window](/en/images/common/icon_newwin.gif)](https://www.fsa.go.jp/internationalfinancialcenter/lp/japanweeks2026/index_en.html).

For collaboration inquiries regarding Japan Weeks 2026, please feel free to contact us:
