# International Forum of Independent Audit Regulators published the Factsheet on Sustainability Assurance Survey

The International Forum of Independent Audit Regulators (IFIAR) released "Public Factsheet on the 2025 Sustainability Survey Results" on June 9, 2026. The factsheet provides an overview of the results of IFIAR’s 2025 Sustainability Survey, covering sustainability reporting requirements, assurance practices, and oversight frameworks across IFIAR member jurisdictions as of September 30, 2025.

The Certified Public Accountants and Auditing Oversight Board has participated in the task force to prepare the report, and will continue to contribute to the project.

Please see the following documents for more details.
