# “Supervisory cooperation in a fragmented world: The IAIS work agenda” –The Eurofi Magazine interview with IAIS ExCo Chair (March 2026)

An interview article titled “Supervisory cooperation in a fragmented world: The IAIS work agenda” featuring MIYOSHI Toshiyuki, Vice Minister for International Affairs and Chair of the International Association of Insurance Supervisors (IAIS) Executive Committee, was published in The Eurofi Magazine (issued on March 25, 2026).

The article highlights the IAIS’s efforts to promote cross-border supervisory convergence and strengthen global cooperation in the insurance sector amid increasing geopolitical fragmentation, as well as its key priorities going forward.
