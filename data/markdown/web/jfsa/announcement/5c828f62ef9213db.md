# Financial Services AgencyFSA Weekly Review No.682
:   April 7, 2026

**What’s New on the FSA Website**

Week of March 30, 2026 - April 3, 2026

This page contains the latest in events, developments, and updates to the FSA website.  
  
For files in HTML formats, when you click original press release links, and click the "Language" button on the upper right of the Japanese website, and select "Machine translation" displayed on the screen, the results of the translation by AI are displayed. ## Public Comments

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| April 3, 2026 | Publication of information, including the results of the public consultation on the draft Policies on Initiatives for Strengthening Cybersecurity by Cryptoasset Exchange Service Providers, etc.  The draft Policies presents the direction of efforts to be made by cryptoasset exchange service providers, etc. for strengthening their cybersecurity under the current situation where threats to cryptoassets are becoming increasingly sophisticated and diverse. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260403/20260403.html) |
| April 1, 2026 | Publication of the promulgation of the Public Notice Partially Amending the Public Notice Designating the Business Accounting Standards to be Specified by the FSA Commissioner as Prescribed in the Regulation on Terminology, Forms and Preparation Methods of Consolidated Financial Statements and the results of the public consultation  This Public Notice is promulgated and put into effect as of today. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260401/20260401.html) |
| March 31, 2026 | Promulgation of the Cabinet Office Order Partially Amending the Regulation on Terminology, Forms and Preparation Methods of Financial Statements, etc. and the Regulation on Terminology, Forms and Preparation Methods of Consolidated Financial Statements, etc. and publication of the results of the public consultation on the relevant Cabinet Office Order, etc. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260331/20260331.html) |
| March 31, 2026 | The FSA published information on public consultation on the Public Notice on Designation of the Sustainability Disclosure Standards Prescribed in Article 19-9, Paragraph (5) of the Cabinet Office Order on Disclosure of Corporate Affairs, etc.  In light of the fact that the Sustainability Standards Board of Japan (SSBJ) revised the standards on March 13, 2026, the revised standards are to be designated as the sustainability disclosure standards (SSBJ Standards) prescribed in Article 19-9, paragraph (5) of the Cabinet Office Order on Disclosure of Corporate Affairs, etc. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/shouken/20260331/20260331.html) |
| March 31, 2026 | Promulgation of the Cabinet Office Order Partially Amending the Regulation for Enforcement of the Insurance Business Act and publication of the results of the public consultation on the relevant Cabinet Office Order  The Cabinet Office Order aims to exclude the amount of asset investment in insurance subsidiary companies from the coverage of the upper limit for the amount of asset investment by an insurance company in the same person (large exposure regulations). This Cabinet Office Order is promulgated and put into effect as of today. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/hoken/20260331-3/20260331-3.html) |
| March 31, 2026 | [Revision of "Guidelines for Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism"](/en/news/2026/20260331/20260331.html) |
| March 30, 2026 | Publication of the draft partial amendments to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies, etc.  The FSA compiled and published the draft partial amendments to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies, etc. with the aim of strengthening countermeasures against cyber risks, which have been increasing in recent years. Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), April 30, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260330/20260330.html) |
| March 30, 2026 | Promulgation of the Cabinet Office Order Concerning the Amendment of the Insurance Business Act in 2025, etc. and publication of the results of the public consultation on the relevant Cabinet Office Order, etc.  The Cabinet Office Order, etc. aim to rearrange provisions of the Regulation for Enforcement of the Insurance Business Act and other relevant provisions in relation to the Act Partially Amending the Insurance Business Act (Act No. 54 of 2025) enacted on May 30, 2025. This Cabinet Office Order, etc. are promulgated today and will be put into effect on Monday, June 1, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/hoken/20260330/20260330.html) |
| March 30, 2026 | Publication of information, including the results of the public consultation on the draft partial amendments to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies, etc. in relation to the 2025 Amended Insurance Business Act (to be enforced within one year)  In relation to the Act Partially Amending the Insurance Business Act (Act No. 54 of 2025) enacted on May 30, 2025, the provisions of the Regulation for Enforcement of the Insurance Business Act and other relevant provisions are to be rearranged. Accompanying this, the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies, etc. are also to be rearranged as required. The amended Comprehensive Guidelines will become applicable from Monday, June 1, 2026, which is the enforcement date for the Amended Insurance Business Act. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/hoken/20260330-2/20260330-2.html) |
| March 30, 2026 | Publication of information, including the results of the public consultation on the draft partial amendment to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies  The report of the Expert Panel on Structural Issues and Competition in the Non-Life Insurance Sector, which was published on June 25, 2024, made recommendations on insurance companies' internal structures for the management of payment of insurance proceeds, etc. and for the promotion of business operations, and the report of the Working Group on Regulatory System Especially for Non-Life Insurance Business of the Financial System Council, which was published on December 25, 2024, made recommendations on collaboration between insurance agencies and insurance brokers and other matters. Based on the details of these recommendations, the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies was also amended as required. The amended Comprehensive Guidelines will become applicable from Monday, June 1, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/hoken/20260330-3/20260330-3.html) |
