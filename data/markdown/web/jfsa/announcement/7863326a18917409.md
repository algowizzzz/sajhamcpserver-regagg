### Others

|  |  |

| January 30, 2026 | The FSA announced the commencement of the operation of the Financial Institutions Collective Search Function (provisional translation).  The FSA has published information on financial institutions that have obtained a license, permission, registration, etc. as a "List of Licensed (Registered) Financial Institutions" by type of business on its official website. The FSA developed a function to enable collective crossover search of the status of registration, etc. of these financial institutions by PC or smartphone and posted it on the website. You can use this function for checking whether a counterparty is a registered financial institution before making a transaction in order to avoid becoming a victim of damage due to fraudulent investment solicitation, etc. by any unregistered financial institution. [The original press release is available in Japanese.](/news/r7/sonota/20260130/20260130.html)[open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260130/20260130.html) |

| January 28, 2026 | The FSA published information on responses to complaints that clearly go beyond the scope and extent of regular administrative counseling services.  The burden borne by staff members in responding to requests beyond the scope of regular administrative counseling services or to requests by means or forms that are clearly beyond socially acceptable norms has become a problem that cannot be overlooked. Accordingly, in order to protect staff members in charge and to ensure full functioning of administrative counseling services, the FSA set a policy on responses to complaints that clearly go beyond the scope and extent of regular services. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/receipt/soudansitu/gyouseisoudan.html) |
