# Publication of "FSA Analytical Notes (2026.6) vol.1"

The FSA published the Japanese version of "FSA Analytical Notes (2026.6) vol.1". ![PDF](/images/common/icon_pdf.gif)[full text](/en/about/fsaanalyticalnotes/20260619/01.pdf)

* ![PDF](/images/common/icon_pdf.gif)[Attempt to Identify Early Warning Signals on Credit Risks using Regional Banks’ Loan Data and Macroeconomic Indicators vol.2](/en/about/fsaanalyticalnotes/20260619/02.pdf)
* ![PDF](/images/common/icon_pdf.gif)[Dynamic Models Analysis of Managerial-Talent Recruitment and Firm Performance at Client Firms of Regional Banks](/en/about/fsaanalyticalnotes/20260619/03.pdf)

An English version will be published shortly.
