| June 25, 2026 | The FSA published a report on the “Survey on Collaborative Internal Audits of Regional Financial Institutions” (provisional translation), etc.  The FSA commissioned a survey to Deloitte Touche Tohmatsu LLC. for the purpose of considering the feasibility of collaboration in each process of internal audits as part of the initiative to improve business efficiency and mitigate the burden of regional financial institutions. In the survey, a questionnaire and interviews were conducted targeting regional financial institutions on their needs for and challenges regarding collaborative internal audits, and points of issue were compiled.
