# Financial Services AgencyFSA Weekly Review No.671
:   January 20, 2026

**What’s New on the FSA Website**

Week of January 13, 2026 - January 16, 2026

This page contains the latest in events, developments, and updates to the FSA website.  
  
For files in HTML formats, when you click original press release links, and click the "Language" button on the upper right of the Japanese website, and select "Machine translation" displayed on the screen, the results of the translation by AI are displayed. Please note that machine translated pages are not necessarily correct.

## Public Comments

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| January 16, 2026 | The FSA published the draft Order Partially Amending the Order on Authorized Specified Insurers.  This is for amending the terms and "Precautions for Making Entries" for Appended Form 1 of the Order on Authorized Specified Insurers, which specifies the forms for business reports. Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), February 16, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/hoken/20260116/20260116.html) |
| January 15, 2026 | The FSA published information about public consultation on its Public Notice concerning the designation of the business accounting standards to be specified by the FSA Commissioner as prescribed in the Regulation on Terminology, Forms and Preparation Methods of Consolidated Financial Statements. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260115/20260115.html) |

### Banks

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| January 16, 2026 | The FSA published information on approval for Special Business Enhancement Plan, etc.  The FSA received the submission of the Special Business Enhancement Plan and the Special Business Enhancement Guidance Plan from Iwaki Shinkumi, Ltd. and the Shinkumi Federation Bank based on Article 16 of the Supplementary Provisions of the Act on Special Measures for Strengthening Financial Functions. As a result of examination based on the provisions of paragraph (3) of the same Article, the FSA decided to approve these plans. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/ginkou/20260116.html) |

### Others

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| January 16, 2026 | The FSA published information on Global Money Week 2026.  Global Money Week is a global awareness-raising campaign organized by the OECD International Network on Financial Education (INFE) for the purpose of promoting financial education and financial inclusion for children and young people. In Japan, in order to facilitate initiatives with more flexible schedules, Global Money Week 2026 will be held for about four weeks from Sunday, March 1, to Tuesday, March 31, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260116/20260116.html) |
