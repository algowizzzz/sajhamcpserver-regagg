# International Forum of Independent Audit Regulators published the new Report about use of technology in audits

The Technology Task Force (TTF), a Task Force of the International Forum of Independent Audit Regulators (IFIAR), published "Use of technology in audits - Innovation and audit quality 2026" on the IFIAR website on Monday 20 April. Building on “[Use of technology in audits - observations, risks and further evolution](/en/ifiar/20250411.html),” published in 2025, the report summarizes the latest trends in the use of technology tools such as AI in audit engagements, as well as the measures expected of audit firms and others to enhance audit quality.

The Certified Public Accountants and Auditing Oversight Board has participated in TTF and will continue to contribute to improve global audit quality.

Please see the following documents for more details.
