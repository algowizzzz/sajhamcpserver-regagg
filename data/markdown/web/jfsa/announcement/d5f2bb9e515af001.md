# Finalization of the Corporate Governance Code (2026 Revision)

The Financial Services Agency of Japan and the Tokyo Stock Exchange have finalized the 2026 revision of the Corporate Governance Code.

Please also refer to the Tokyo Stock Exchange website [here![open new window](/images/common/icon_newwin.gif)](https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/rules-participants/public-comment/detail/d1/20260410-01.html).

## Finalization of the 2026 Revision and Overview of the Public Consultation

[The Expert Panel on the Revision of the Corporate Governance Code](/en/refer/councils/revision_corporategovernance/index.html) (Chair: Yuri Okina, Senior Fellow, The Japan Research Institute, Limited), began discussions to review the Corporate Governance Code in October 2025.

The draft revisions to the Code were published for [public consultation](/en/news/2026/20260410.html) between 10 April, 2026 and 15 May, 2026.

As a result, comments were received from 147 individuals and entities. We express our sincere gratitude to all those who submitted their comments.

Based on the comments received, the Corporate Governance Code (2026 Revision) has been finalized. The final version of the revised Code (English translation) is provided in [Material 1](#Material1). The revised Code with track changes from the previous version (English translation) is provided in [Material 2](#Material2).

Please refer to [Material 3](#Material3) for the background and key revisions, and [Material 4](#Material4) for an overview of the 2026 revision.

A summary of the comments received and our responses are provided in [Materials 5 and 6](#Material5). Some comments that are not directly related to this revision will be used as a reference for future policymaking.

Case studies on initiatives to enhance board effectiveness are also published as [Appendix 2](#Appendix2). Please refer to this as well.
