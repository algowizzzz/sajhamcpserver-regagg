# Initiative Summary of Upgrading the FSA

In August 2025, the FSA established a team “Financial Services Agency 20-Year Committee” directly under the Commissioner. Led by this committee, the FSA considered initiatives to realize a continuously evolving organization that will continue delivering high-quality financial sector policy over the next five, ten, and twenty years through continuous upgrades of its organization and staff capabilities.

The FSA held intensive agency-wide discussions on the objectives of financial sector policy and adopted a following slogan to enable staff to feel familiar with them.

"Upholding Trust, Empowering the Future."

The FSA will continue to provide high-quality financial sector policy services through continuous self-transformation to upgrade the FSA.

* ![PDF](/images/common/icon_pdf.gif)[Initiative Summary of Upgrading the FSA](/en/about/Upgrading.pdf)
  + \* Financial sector policy slogan and the message behind it is [here](/en/about/slogan.html).
  + \* The objectives of financial sector policy  
    (i) stabilizing the financial system and having financial institutions fully perform their financial intermediary function, (ii) ensuring user protections and enhancing user convenience, (iii) securing fairness and transparency in, and invigorating, the market.
