# Publication of the Report of the "Working Group on Disclosure and Assurance of Sustainability-related Financial Information" of the Financial System Council

On January 8, 2026, the FSA published Report of the Working Group on Disclosure and Assurance of Sustainability-related Financial Information (of the Financial System Council) and Japanese Roadmap for sustainability disclosures and assurance.

* ![PDF](/images/common/icon_pdf.gif)[Report of the Working Group on Disclosure and Assurance of Sustainability-related Financial Information (of the Financial System Council)  [available only in Japanese]](/singi/singi_kinyu/tosin/20260108/01.pdf)
* ![PDF](/images/common/icon_pdf.gif)[Japanese Roadmap for sustainability disclosures and assurance [available in English]](/en/news/2026/20260409/02.pdf)
