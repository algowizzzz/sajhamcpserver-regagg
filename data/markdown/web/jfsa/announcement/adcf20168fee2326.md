## Public Comments

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| April 17, 2026 | Publication of a draft partial amendment to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Authorized Specified Insurers  Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), May 17, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/hoken/20260417/20260417.html) |
| April 15, 2026 | The FSA published information on public consultation on the draft Cabinet Order Partially Amending the Cabinet Order on Travel Expenses and Allowances for Witnesses and Expert Witnesses in Trial Procedures, etc. under the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act and the draft Cabinet Order Partially Amending the Cabinet Order on Travel Expenses and Allowances for Witnesses and Expert Witnesses in Trial Procedures, etc. under the Certified Public Accountants Act.  In consideration of the recent fluctuations in economic conditions, the amendments aim to raise the maximum amounts of daily allowances for witnesses and expert witnesses in trial procedures in relation to payment orders for administrative monetary penalties from 8,450 yen to 8,750 yen and from 8,050 yen to 8,350 yen, respectively, based on the provisions of the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act and the Certified Public Accountants Act. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260415/20260415.html) |
