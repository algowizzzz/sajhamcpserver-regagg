# Conservative leader urges prime minister to get tariff-free deal for Canadian goods

Conservative Leader Pierre Poilievre is warning Prime Minister Mark Carney against giving ground to U.S. negotiators unless Canada wins tariff-free access for Canadian lumber, steel, aluminum and cars.
