# Westland Insurance acquires Interior Savings Insurance

Westland Insurance, one of Canada’s largest insurance brokerages, today announced that it acquired Interior Savings Insurance Services Inc. (ISI), part of Beem Credit Union, effective August 1. This acquisition reflects Westland’s strategic focus on expanding its presence in BC and deepening the organization’s capabilities in personal and commercial insurance solutions, while bringing greater value to clients within their local communities.
