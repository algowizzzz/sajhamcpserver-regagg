# New Peer-Reviewed Research Reinforces What the Data Has Shown for Years: Vaping Has Saved Canadian Lives

In response to a recent wave of alarming media coverage about vaping, the Canadian Vaping Association (CVA) is urging journalists, policymakers, and the public to look past the headlines and toward the growing body of peer-reviewed research on nicotine vaping products (NVPs) as a smoking-cessation and harm-reduction tool.
