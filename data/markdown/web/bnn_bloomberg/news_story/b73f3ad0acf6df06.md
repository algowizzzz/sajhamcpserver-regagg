# GoldHaven Awards Contract for Eight-Hole, 1,650-Metre Phase II Drill Program at Copeçal West Target, Brazil

GoldHaven Resources Corp. (“GoldHaven” or the “Company”) (CSE: GOH) (OTCQB: GHVNF) (FSE: 4QS) is pleased to announce that it has contracted Layne do Brasil Ltda. to complete a second phase of diamond drilling at the West Target of its 100%-owned Copeçal Gold Project in Mato Grosso State, Brazil.
