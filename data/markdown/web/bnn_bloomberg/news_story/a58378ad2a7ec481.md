# Blackstone-owned Safe Harbor nears US$1.5 billion deal to buy MarineMax, sources say

Blackstone Infrastructure’s Safe Harbor Marinas, the world’s largest owner and operator of marinas, is nearing a US$1.5 billion deal to acquire MarineMax , people familiar with the matter said.
