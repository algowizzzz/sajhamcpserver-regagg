# Airbnb hits four-year high as investors cheer revenue forecast raise, AI payoff

Airbnb shares surged 14 per cent to a more-than-four-year high on Friday as the vacation-rental firm’s raised annual revenue forecast eased worries over the fallout on global travel demand from the conflict in the Middle East.
