# Intel plans US$15 billion share sale as turnaround rally lifts stock

Intel on Monday said it is planning to raise US$15 billion through a share sale, as it looks to fund the costly build-out of its chip contract manufacturing business by cashing in on a stock surge fueled by its turnaround efforts.
