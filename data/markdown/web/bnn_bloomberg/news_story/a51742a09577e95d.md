# South Star Achieves Graphite Purchase Order Milestone

South Star Battery Metals Corp. (“South Star” or the “Company”) (TSXV: STS) (OTCQB: STSBF) is pleased to announce that it has successfully completed the qualification process for the graphite concentrate produced at the Santa Cruz mine with a significant graphite customer and has received a purchase order from this customer for 36 tonnes of graphite concentrate. This marks a significant achievement for South Star on the path to revenues.
