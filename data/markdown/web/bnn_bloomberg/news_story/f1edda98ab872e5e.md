# Northern Trust Pension Universe Data: Canadian Pension Plan Returns Rise in Q2

Canadian pension plan returns rose in the second quarter, supported by robust equity market performance, with the median pension plan returning 6.6% for the quarter and 6.8% year-to-date, according to the Northern Trust Canada Universe.
