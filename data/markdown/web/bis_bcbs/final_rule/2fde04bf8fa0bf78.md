*Revised 18 November 2025*[\*](#fns42)

The report sets out the impact of the Basel III framework, including the December 2017 [finalisation of the Basel III reforms](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d424.htm) and the January 2019 [finalisation of the market risk framework](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d457.htm). A web-based version of the report with interactive graphs is available on the [highlights page](/bcbs/publ/d599_highlights.htm).

Dashboards now provide an interactive visualisation of the results for credit, market, operational, counterparty credit and credit valuation adjustment risks. These and all other Basel III monitoring dashboards are compiled on a [dashboards page](/bcbs/dashboards.htm?m=3117).

* [Methodology of Basel III monitoring analyses](/bcbs/publ/d599_methodology.pdf)

---

[\*](#fn_s42)  Since its publication on 23 October, the BCBS received corrected data from one participating jurisdiction and republished the results on 18 November 2025. This led to a revision of the average impact of the fully phased-in final Basel III framework on Tier 1 minimum required capital (MRC) for Group 1 banks as of the December 2024 reporting date.
