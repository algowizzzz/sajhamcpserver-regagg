The report sets out the impact of the Basel III framework, including the December 2017 [finalisation of the Basel III reforms](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d424.htm) and the January 2019 [finalisation of the market risk framework](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d457.htm).

#### Highlights of the Basel III monitoring exercise as of 30 June 2024

* Basel III risk-based capital ratios increase while leverage ratio and NSFR remain stable for large internationally active banks.

[More...](/bcbs/publ/d592_highlights.htm)

Dashboards provide an interactive visualisation of the results for credit, market, operational, counterparty credit and credit valuation adjustment risks. These and all other Basel III monitoring dashboards are compiled on a [dashboards page](/bcbs/dashboards.htm?m=3117).

A Basel III monitoring methodology note provides additional background information on the concepts, definitions and calculation techniques used in dashboards, monitoring reports and other analyses.
