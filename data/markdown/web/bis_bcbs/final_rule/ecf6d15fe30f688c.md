The Basel Committee on Banking supervision has launched a [new section of its website](/basel_consolidated_guidelines/index.htm) that sets out a consolidated version of its guidelines and sound practices for banks and supervisors. The consolidated guidelines and sound practices aim to improve the accessibility of the Committee's outputs in a user-friendly format. The website has been published, initially in draft form, together with a consultative document to gather feedback from stakeholders.

[Guidelines](/bcbs/charter.htm#:~:text=and%20their%20outcome.-,13.%20Guidelines,-Guidelines%20elaborate%20the) and [sound practices](/bcbs/charter.htm#:~:text=of%20their%20implementation.-,14.%20Sound%20practices,-Sound%20practices%20generally) are currently published on the Committee's section of the Bank for International Settlements (BIS) website, as a series of pdf documents. The new website reorganises the contents of existing guidelines and sound practices into a modular format.
