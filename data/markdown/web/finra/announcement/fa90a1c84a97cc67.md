# Protect Your Money

Investor protection is an integral part of FINRA's mission. Our information and resources can help you build knowledge and avoid problems such as the latest frauds, which all too often are perpetrated by people outside FINRA's jurisdiction. Learn about common fraud tactics and scams, how to check out sellers and investments, and what questions to ask to protect yourself and your assets.
