# Regulatory Filing Systems

FINRA employs advanced technology to monitor the markets and regulated firms. Our electronic filing systems help firms comply with applicable regulations efficiently and securely through FINRA Gateway and specialized platforms.

## Filing Systems by Category

### Advertising and Communications

Submit retail communications, institutional communications and correspondence for review and filing in accordance with FINRA advertising and communications rules.

* [Advertising Regulation Electronic Files (AREF)](/industry/advertising-regulation-electronic-files) – Advertising and communications filing

### Financial Reporting

Report your firm's financial condition and operational data through required periodic filings, including monthly and quarterly financial statements and supplemental schedules.

* [Derivatives and Other Off-Balance Sheet Items (OBS)](/filing-reporting/derivatives-and-other-balance-sheet-items-obs/) – OBS schedule submission
* [eFOCUS](/filing-reporting/efocus) – Financial and Operational Combined Uniform Single Reports
* [Subordination Agreements](/filing-reporting/regulatory-filing-systems/subordination-agreements) – Capital subordination filings
* [Supplemental Statement of Income (SSOI)](/filing-reporting/supplemental-statement-income-ssoi) – Quarterly income reporting

### Firm Information and Notifications

Maintain current firm contact information and notify FINRA of material changes to your business operations, organizational structure or other significant events.

* [Annual Reports](/rules-guidance/key-topics/annual-reports) – Consolidated FINRA reports
* [FINRA Contact System](/filing-reporting/finra-contact-system) – Designated contact information
* [Firm Clearing Arrangements Filing](/filing-reporting/firm-clearing-arrangements-filing) – Clearing relationships
* [Regulatory Notifications](/filing-reporting/regulatory-notifications) – Material changes and notifications

### Margin and Customer Accounts

Report customer margin balances, options exercise allocations and requests for extensions of time to meet margin requirements under Regulation T.

* [Customer Margin Balance Reporting](/rules-guidance/key-topics/margin-accounts) – Monthly customer margin balances
* [Options Allocation of Exercise Assignment Notices](/filing-reporting/options-allocation-exercise-assignment-notices) – Options exercise data
* [Regulation T Filings](/filing-reporting/regulation-t-filings) – Extensions of time for margin requirements

### Other Reporting Requirements

Specialized reporting requirements for specific business activities and responses to FINRA requests for information or testimony in connection with investigations or examinations.

* [FINRA Rule 4311(h)(2)](/filing-reporting/finra-rule-4311h2) – Insurance company separate account submissions
* [Information & Testimony Requests](/rules-guidance/key-topics/information-and-testimony-requests) – Respond to FINRA information requests

### Securities Offerings and Transactions

File required information for private placements, public offerings, unlisted trading privileges and distribution-related activities involving new or existing securities.

* [Distribution Manager](/filing-reporting/distribution-manager) – Syndicate and distribution information
* [Form 211 Filings](/filing-reporting/otcbb/otcbb-forms-documentation) – Unlisted trading privilege applications
* [Private Placements](/industry/private-placements) – Rule 5123 filing requirements
* [Public Offerings](/industry/public-offerings) – Corporate financing filings

### Trading and Market Activity

Submit transaction data, position reports and market activity information related to trading surveillance, market regulation and regulatory compliance obligations.

* [Blue Sheets](/filing-reporting/blue-sheets) – Electronic Blue Sheet (EBS) trading data
* [FINRA Rule 6151 (SEC Rule 606)](/filing-reporting/rule-6151-reporting-compliance) – Order routing disclosure
* [Regulation M Filings](/filing-reporting/regulation-m-filings) – Distribution participant activities
* [Short Interest Reporting](/filing-reporting/short-interest-reporting) – Semi-monthly short interest positions
