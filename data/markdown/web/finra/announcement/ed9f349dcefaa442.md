# Need Help?

([Centro de Reclamo para el inversionista](/investors/have-problem/file-complaint/complaint-center/centro-de-reclamo-para-el-inversionista))

Most business in the securities industry is conducted fairly, efficiently and in a manner that satisfies everyone involved. But problems can arise. If you believe that a brokerage firm or other industry professional has treated you unfairly, contact the firm to see if you can resolve the issue. If you are still not satisfied with the firm's response, you can file a complaint with FINRA.

We investigate complaints against brokerage firms and their employees—and can take disciplinary actions against those who violate the rules. Sanctions may include fines, suspensions, a barring from the securities industry or other appropriate sanctions.

Use these resources to help you resolve potential problems.

### File a Complaint

Use FINRA's online form to report any potentially fraudulent or suspicious activities by brokerage firms or their employees.

[Learn More](/investors/need-help/file-a-complaint)

![Dispute Resolution](/sites/default/files/2019-04/dr_podcast_061918_ch.jpg)

### Dispute Resolution

FINRA operates the largest securities dispute resolution forum in the United States, and has extensive experience in providing a fair, efficient and effective venue to handle a securities-related dispute. The resolution of problems and disputes is accomplished through two non-judicial proceedings: arbitration and mediation.

[Learn More](/arbitration-mediation)

![Protecting Seniors](/sites/default/files/2019-04/Senior%20Helpline_iStock-847681944_80x415.jpg)

### Securities Helpline for Seniors

Are you a senior investor with questions about your brokerage account statement or an investment in a brokerage account?  Are you concerned that your brokerage account may have been mishandled? To help you with these and other investment-related questions, FINRA provides the Securities Helpline for Seniors®.

[Learn More](/investors/have-problem/helpline-seniors)

### Other Avenues for Recovery of Investment Losses

There are other avenues for recovery of investment loses. Our [FINRA Enforcement](/rules-guidance/enforcement) teams are authorized to take actions that may include financial restitution for investors. [The Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC)](https://www.sipc.org/ "Follow link") provides [limited protections](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-101 "Follow link") to investors. Additionally, the SEC maintains a list of cases involving Fair Funds and Disgorgement Plans. Investors also may be eligible to participate in Class Action Lawsuits.

[Learn More](/investors/have-problem/legitimate-avenues-recovery-investment-losses)

### Support for Recovering from Investment Fraud

The impacts from investment fraud can include compromised identity, damaged credit, financial loss and emotional trauma. If you’ve been the victim of a financial scam, there are resources available to help you, including support if you’re experiencing emotional consequences.

[Learn More](/investors/insights/recovering-from-investment-fraud)
