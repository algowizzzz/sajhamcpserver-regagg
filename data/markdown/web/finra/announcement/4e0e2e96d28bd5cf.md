# FINRA Rule Consolidation

FINRA is working toward a new consolidated rulebook, which seeks not only to harmonize and streamline existing rules, but also to give consideration to the rapidly evolving nature of the securities business and the broad diversity of firms subject to FINRA regulation. To learn more about the process, read our Information Notices.

* [Information Notice 12/8/08](/node/7421) – continuing application of NASD Rules and Incorporated NYSE Rules
* [Information Notice 10/6/08](/node/7389) – discusses protocols to announce effective dates of new Consolidated Rules and introduces the Rule Conversion Chart
* [Information Notice 3/12/08](/node/7333) – describes the rulebook consolidation process

## Rule Conversion Charts

To facilitate the transition to the consolidated rulebook, FINRA has created three Rule Conversion Charts to show how new FINRA Rules relate to the NASD and/or Incorporated NYSE Rules that they replace.

FINRA updates these charts as the effective dates of rules for the consolidated FINRA rulebook are announced.

**Important Note:** These conversion charts are intended as a reference aid only—member firms are responsible for carefully consulting the relevant new rule language.

* [NASD to FINRA Conversion Chart Spreadsheet](/sites/default/files/spreadsheet_nasd_finra_011918.xls) (Excel *628 KB*)
* [NYSE to FINRA Conversion Chart Spreadsheet](/sites/default/files/spreadsheet_nyse_finra_011918.xls) (Excel *190 KB*)

## Request for Comments

As discussed in [Information Notice 03/12/08](/node/7333), FINRA may determine to file proposals related to the rulebook consolidation process directly with the SEC for comment or to first seek comment in a Regulatory Notice. FINRA strongly encourages member firms and interested members of the public to engage in the rule comment process. For information on how to respond to FINRA and SEC requests for comment, see the links below.

* [FINRA Requests for Comment](/node/9210)
* [SEC Requests for Comment](http://www.sec.gov/rules/sro/finra.shtml)
