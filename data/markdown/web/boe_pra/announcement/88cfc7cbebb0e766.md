# Statistical Notice 2026/07 - Bank of England Levy: Notification Document Levy Year 2026/27

Statistical Notices update the definitions and guidance contained in the Banking Statistics Yellow Folder

Published on
08 July 2026

## Statistical Notice 2026/07

Today the Bank has published the [Bank of England Levy Notification Document - Levy Year 2026/27](/about/governance-and-funding/levy) on its website.

This is the annual notification of the Bank’s Anticipated Levy Requirement for the current Levy Year as stated in the [Bank of England Levy Framework Document](/about/governance-and-funding/levy/levy-framework) (1.16).

### Next steps:

Levy Payers will receive an invoice outlining their contribution to the Levy for the 2026/27 Levy Year.

Any questions on the above should be sent to [BoELevy@bankofengland.co.uk](mailto:BoELevy@bankofengland.co.uk )
