# HKMCA introduces new insurance company as referral partner

## Press Releases

07 Aug 2026

### HKMCA introduces new insurance company as referral partner

HKMC Annuity Limited (HKMCA), a wholly-owned subsidiary of The Hong Kong Mortgage Corporation Limited, has been collaborating with various institutions to promote the HKMC Annuity Plan.  The HKMCA announced today (7 August) that riding on its strategic partnership with BOC Group Life Assurance Company Limited (BOC Life), designated licensed insurance intermediaries of BOC Life are authorised to introduce the HKMC Annuity Plan to their customers and refer eligible customers to the HKMCA.

Mr Daniel Leong, Executive Director and Chief Executive Officer of the HKMCA, said, “The HKMCA is committed to developing the local annuity market.  This collaboration with BOC Life enables a greater number of licensed insurance intermediaries to help the public better understand the critical roles that the HKMC Annuity Plan plays in retirement planning.  We are looking forward to working closely with more institutions to empower the public to achieve their long-term sustainable retirement goal.”

For enquiries or to verify the identity of designated licensed insurance intermediaries, please contact the HKMCA customer service hotline at (852) 2512 5000.

HKMC Annuity Limited
