# HKICL alerts public of fraudulent website

## Press Releases

10 Aug 2026

### HKICL alerts public of fraudulent website

The Hong Kong Interbank Clearing Limited (HKICL) has recently noted a fraudulent website at hxxps[:]//fpshkicl[.]com purported to be from the HKICL. The fraudulent website imitates as “Buyer Online Protection” to provide services including 1) refund to buyer, 2) unauthorised online transaction reporting, and 3) online transaction support, for online transaction over FPS payment.

The HKICL advises that this fraudulent website has no affiliation with the HKICL, or any business of the HKICL. The HKICL will not directly provide FPS service to individual members of the public or contact individual members of the public proactively under usual circumstance. The genuine official website addresses of the HKICL are <https://www.hkicl.com.hk> and <https://fps.hkicl.com.hk>.

The HKICL urges the public to beware of suspicious calls, websites or other communications, and to remain vigilant in protecting personal information. If members of the public receive any suspicious communication purportedly from the HKICL, please call the HKICL’s general line at 2533 1111 to verify. Members of the public who suspect that they have become the victim of fraudulent acts should report promptly to the Police.

The Hong Kong Interbank Clearing Limited
