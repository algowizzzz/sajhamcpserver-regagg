# Tender of 10-year HKD HKSAR Institutional Government Bonds through re-opening to be held on Wednesday, 12 August 2026

## Press Releases

06 Aug 2026

### Tender of 10-year HKD HKSAR Institutional Government Bonds through re-opening to be held on Wednesday, 12 August 2026

The Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA), as representative of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region Government (HKSAR Government), announced today (6 August) that a tender of 10-year HKD institutional Government Bonds (Bonds) through the re-opening of existing 10-year Government Bond issue 10GB3507001 under the Infrastructure Bond Programme will be held on Wednesday, 12 August 2026, for settlement on Thursday, 13 August 2026.

An additional amount of HK$1.0 billion of the outstanding 10-year Bonds (issue no. 10GB3507001) will be on offer. The Bonds will mature on 24 July 2035 and will carry interest at the rate of 3.17% per annum payable semi-annually in arrear. The Indicative Pricings of the Bonds on 6 August 2026 are 98.01 with an annualised yield of 3.459%.

Tender is open only to Primary Dealers appointed under the Infrastructure Bond Programme. Anyone wishing to apply for the Bonds on offer can do so through any of the Primary Dealers on the latest published list, which can be obtained from the Hong Kong Government Bonds website at <https://www.hkgb.gov.hk>.  Each tender must be for an amount of HK$50,000 or integral multiples thereof.

Tender results will be published on the HKMA’s website, the Hong Kong Government Bonds website, Bloomberg (GBHK <GO>) and Refinitiv (IBPGSBPINDEX). The publication time is expected to be no later than 3:00 pm on the tender day.
