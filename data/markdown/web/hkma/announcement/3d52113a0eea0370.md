# Register of Licensees under the Stablecoins Ordinance (Cap. 656)

This Register, established and maintained in accordance with section 21 of the Stablecoins Ordinance, is a register of persons who hold a licence granted under section 15(1) of the Stablecoins Ordinance to issue specified stablecoins in Hong Kong. By publication on the HKMA website, the Register is made available for inspection by the public in accordance with section 21(7) of the Stablecoins Ordinance.

For the definition of “regulated stablecoin activity”, please refer to section 5 of the Stablecoins Ordinance.
