# Behari Lal Engineering IPO price band set at Rs 271-285, issue opens on August 12

Behari Lal Engineering will launch its IPO on August 12 with a price band of Rs 271–285 per share, aiming to raise over Rs 301 crore through a fresh issue and an offer for sale. The company plans to use the proceeds to expand manufacturing capacity, install rooftop solar systems, repay debt, and support general corporate purposes.
