# Elon Musk vs Michael Burry: World's richest man says AI internet traffic will outpace humans, market expert asks who is paying

The contrasting views highlight a broader debate over AI’s rapid expansion, massive infrastructure spending and whether the technology can generate enough economic value to justify current valuations.
