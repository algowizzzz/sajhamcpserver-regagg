# Bitcoin holds near $65K as $1.1 billion crypto ETF inflows lift sentiment; CPI in focus

Bitcoin is trading near the $65,000 mark after a volatile week, with US spot Bitcoin and Ether ETFs attracting around $1.1 billion in combined net inflows. Analysts say crypto markets are turning cautiously constructive, but the upcoming US CPI data could determine the next move, with key support and resistance levels in focus.
