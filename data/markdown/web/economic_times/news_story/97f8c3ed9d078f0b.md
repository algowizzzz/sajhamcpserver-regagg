# Rupee weakness cushioned by central bank intervention, shorts grow wary

The Indian rupee ended the day with minimal fluctuations, influenced primarily by oil market dynamics and moves from the central bank. State-owned banks reportedly intervened by supplying dollars on behalf of the Reserve Bank of India, helping to buffer the currency against rising oil costs and global tensions. Traders are taking a measured approach, with a noted resistance threshold at 96.
