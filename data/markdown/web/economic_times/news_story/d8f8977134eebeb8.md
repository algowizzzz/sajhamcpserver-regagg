# Pix Transmissions shares surge over 12% after Q1 profit spikes 98% YoY

Pix Transmissions shares surged after the company reported a 97.8% YoY jump in Q1 FY27 PAT to Rs 4,829 crore. Revenue from operations rose 21.6% YoY to Rs 13,453 crore, while total comprehensive income increased 98% to Rs 4,817.52 crore.
