# Vodafone Idea Q1 Results: Loss narrows to Rs 3,754 crore; revenue rises 6% YoY

Vodafone Idea Q1 Results: Debt-ridden telco Vodafone Idea on Monday narrowed its consolidated losses in the first quarter to Rs 3,754 crore. The loss stood at Rs 6,608 crore in the last year quarter. Revenue from operations increased 6% YoY to Rs 11,689 crore.
