# Indian bonds steady as soft US data offsets oil rise

Indian government bonds were largely steady on Monday as softer US jobs data supported expectations of lower US rates, while rising crude prices capped gains. The benchmark 6.94% 2036 bond yield eased to 6.7594%. Investors are also watching upcoming Indian and US inflation data for fresh interest-rate cues.
