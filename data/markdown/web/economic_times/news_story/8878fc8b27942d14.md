# Intel plans $15 billion share sale as turnaround rally lifts stock

Intel is gearing up for a monumental fifteen billion dollar share sale aimed at boosting its chip manufacturing capabilities. This strategic decision aligns with the company's commitment to enhancing its production facilities and advanced packaging technologies. By amplifying its operations, Intel is setting its sights on competing with industry giants like TSMC, as soaring demand for CPUs surpasses its manufacturing capacity.
