# US stocks: US market opens flat as markets monitor Hormuz situation

Major Wall Street indexes opened with minimal changes on Monday. Investors considered Middle East developments and awaited key inflation data. The Dow Jones Industrial Average saw a slight increase at the opening. The S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite experienced minor declines. This trading session reflects investor caution ahead of significant economic reports.
