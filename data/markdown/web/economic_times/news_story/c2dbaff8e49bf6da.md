# Cupid shares fall 2% even as Q1 profit jumps 3x. What’s ahead for multibagger stock that rose 680% in a year?

Cupid shares fell on Monday despite a threefold rise in Q1 FY27 profit to Rs 44 crore, as revenue surged 159% and EBITDA jumped 265%. The multibagger stock has gained 150% in 2026 and 683% over the past year. The company has also raised its FY27 revenue and profit guidance.
