# Stock Market: Senate moves to advance Crypto Clarity Act in major win for Trump, industry

US Senate Majority Leader John Thune has moved to advance the Clarity Act, a proposed framework for regulating cryptocurrencies. The bill could face a 60-vote hurdle in the Senate, with Democrats seeking stronger safeguards. Its passage would mark another major crypto policy win for President Donald Trump.
