# US Stock Market: Warsh's Fed leaves markets searching for policy clues

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh is changing the central bank’s communication approach, reducing reliance on forward guidance and leaving markets to interpret economic data more independently. The shift has triggered volatility in bond markets, with investors seeking clarity on future rate moves. As inflation remains elevated, the Fed’s new strategy faces scrutiny ahead of key economic indicators like the July jobs report.
