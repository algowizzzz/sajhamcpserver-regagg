# Aarti Pharmalabs shares hit 20% upper circuit as Q1 net profit surges 65% YoY to Rs 76 crore

Aarti Pharmalabs shares hit the 20% upper circuit after the company reported a 65.4% year-on-year rise in Q1 FY27 net profit to Rs 76.14 crore. Revenue surged 38.7% to Rs 535.79 crore, while the company also approved a Rs 149-crore capex plan to expand manufacturing capacity.
