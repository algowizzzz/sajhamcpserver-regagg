# Info Edge Q1 Results: Standalone Profit falls 6% YoY to Rs 245 crore

Info Edge reported a 6% YoY decline in Q1 standalone net profit to Rs 245 crore, impacted by a Rs 72.1 crore exceptional loss. However, revenue rose 12% to Rs 824 crore, while profit before tax before exceptional items jumped 23% to Rs 427 crore, driven by strong recruitment and real estate growth.
