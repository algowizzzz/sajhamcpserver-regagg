# Quote of the day by Philip Carret: "I’m an optimist, both as a person and an investor. It’s a big mistake to be pessimistic as long as we have a viable civilization which is reasonably well managed"

Legendary investor Philip Carret said optimism is essential for successful investing, helping investors remain disciplined through market volatility. He advocated balancing confidence in long-term economic growth with careful research, patience and risk awareness to benefit from compounding and enduring market resilience.
