# Hindalco share price: Why brokerages are reiterating their Buy calls, raising target prices after Q1 earnings

Hindalco shares fell despite the company reporting a record Q1 profit. However, brokerages remained bullish, citing strong India operations, improving downstream mix and a recovery at Novelis, while raising their target prices and retaining Buy ratings on the stock.
