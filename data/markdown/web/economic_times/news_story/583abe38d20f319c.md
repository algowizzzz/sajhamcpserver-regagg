# Hindustan Copper Q1 Results: PAT zooms 163% YoY to Rs 353 crore; revenue rises to Rs 937 crore

Hindustan Copper reported a 163% year-on-year jump in Q1FY27 net profit to Rs 353 crore, while revenue from operations rose 81.4% to Rs 936.5 crore. EBITDA more than doubled to Rs 507.5 crore from Rs 212.3 crore, with EBITDA margin expanding to 54% from 41% a year earlier, the state-owned miner said in a regulatory filing.
