# Paytm shares jump 5% after Bernstein assigns target price above IPO price for first time

Paytm Share Price: Paytm shares rallied after Bernstein raised its target price to Rs 2,200, implying 52% upside, while retaining an Outperform rating. The target is the highest on the Street and the first above Paytm’s Rs 2,150 IPO price, with Bernstein factoring in UPI MDR from FY28.
