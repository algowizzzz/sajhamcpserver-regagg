# Dynamatic Technologies shares soar 9% after Q1 profit jumps 93% YoY to Rs 21 crore; outlook stays strong

Dynamatic Technologies shares surged 12% after the company reported a near-doubling in Q1 FY27 net profit to Rs 20.79 crore. Revenue rose 14.5% year-on-year to Rs 424.81 crore, while EBITDA jumped 45.9% to Rs 55.11 crore. EBITDA margin expanded 280 basis points to 13%, with EBIT rising 83.7% and EBIT margin improving 310 basis points.
