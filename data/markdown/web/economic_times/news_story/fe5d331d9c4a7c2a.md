# Zee Entertainment Q1 Results: PAT falls 48% YoY to Rs 74 crore; ad revenue hit by Middle East crisis

Zee Entertainment’s Q1 FY27 profit fell 48% to Rs 74.3 crore, while EBITDA plunged 65% despite 5% revenue growth. Advertising faced pressure from Middle East tensions and cricket, partly offset by FIFA rights, subscription growth and higher digital ARPU.
