# 9 IPOs get Sebi approval as primary market pipeline gathers pace

Sebi has approved IPOs of nine companies spanning engineering, travel technology, gaming, drones, paper, renewable energy and digital platforms. The approvals strengthen India’s IPO pipeline, with firms including Laxyo, Garuda Aerospace, PlaySimple Games, Rediff.com and Jakson Green preparing to list.
