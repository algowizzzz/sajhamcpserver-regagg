# Shopify shares soar 18% as AI push helps revenue beat estimates

Shopify's stock soared following the release of strong second-quarter financial results. The e-commerce giant outperformed analyst projections in terms of revenue and gross merchandise volume, mitigating concerns about the effects of artificial intelligence on service demand.
