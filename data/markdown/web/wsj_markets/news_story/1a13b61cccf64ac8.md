# Oil Futures Settle Mixed as Iran Says Hormuz Route Agreed With Oman

Oil futures ended mixed after Iran said it has agreed with Oman on a shipping route through the Strait of Hormuz, but that safe passage will depend on third parties not obstructing the process, an apparent reference to the U.S.
