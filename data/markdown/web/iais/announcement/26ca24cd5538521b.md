# 2025 Graduate Internship Programme (Insurance and Risk)

[3 Mar 2025](https://www.iais.org/2025/03/2025-graduate-internship-programme-insurance-and-risk-2/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Uncategorized](https://www.iais.org/category/uncategorized/)

The International Association of Insurance Supervisors (IAIS) is looking for recent graduates to join the 2025 Bank for International Settlements (BIS) cohort of Graduate Interns.

For more information about the position, go to our [Recruitment page](https://www.iais.org/about-the-iais/recruitment-2/) or download the vacancy notice [here](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2025/03/250228-2025-Graduate-Internship-Programme-Insurance-and-Risk.pdf).

Applications can be submitted until Monday 24 March 2025 23:59 CET. Click [here](https://lde.tbe.taleo.net/lde01/ats/careers/v2/viewRequisition?org=BIS&cws=40&rid=1317) to apply.
