### **Tomislav Ridzak, PhD**

*Board Member, Croatian Financial Services Supervisory Agency (Hanfa)*

Tomislav Ridzak, PhD, has been a member of the Board of the Croatian Financial Services Supervisory Agency (Hanfa) since 2018. He started his professional career at the Institute of Economics in Zagreb. From 2003 he worked as an investment manager at InterCapital. InterCapital is an investment firm operating in the SEE region and offering its clients access to capital markets, finance and investing in a wide range of investment funds. Since 2009, he moved to Croatian National Bank where he was leading Financial Stability Department. In 2018, he was appointed member of the Board of Hanfa  
He finished his undergraduate and graduate studies at the Faculty of Economics and Business, University of Zagreb, he got a master’s degree in economics and finance from the University of York, United Kingdom, and a doctoral degree from the Juraj Dobrila University in Pula. He has written many scientific papers in the field of finance published in Croatia and abroad.
