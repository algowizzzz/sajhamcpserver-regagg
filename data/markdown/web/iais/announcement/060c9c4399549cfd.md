# IAIS announces location for 2023 Annual Conference

[7 Apr 2022](https://www.iais.org/2022/04/iais-announces-location-for-2023-annual-conference/)

in [Annual Conference](https://www.iais.org/category/annual-conference/), [Events](https://www.iais.org/category/events/), [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

We are pleased to announce that the 2023 IAIS Annual Conference will take place in Tokyo, Japan in November 2023. The Annual Conference will be preceded by Committee Meetings and the Annual General Meeting of IAIS Members. With hundreds of attendees, the Annual Conference is the foremost event of the IAIS and provides an excellent platform for insurance supervisors and the insurance industry from around the world to exchange views on issues relevant to the supervision of insurance markets. As a long-standing member of the IAIS, we would like to thank the [Financial Services Agency (FSA) of Japan](https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/) for their generous support and leadership. The exact dates will be communicated in due course.

For more information, visit [FSA Japan](https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/newsletter/weekly2022/484.html).
