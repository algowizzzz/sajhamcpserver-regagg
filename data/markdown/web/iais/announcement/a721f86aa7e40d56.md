# Registration is now open for the IAIS Global Seminar 2024

[27 May 2024](https://www.iais.org/2024/05/the-iais-global-seminar-2024-will-take-place-virtually-on-9-11-july-each-days-session-will-start-at-1300-cest-and-will-last-up-to-two-hours/)

in [Events](https://www.iais.org/category/events/), [Global Seminar](https://www.iais.org/category/global-seminar/), [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

The **IAIS Global Seminar 2024** will take place virtually on **9-11 July**. Each day’s session will start at **13:00 CES**T and will last up to two hours.

The Global Seminar offers opportunities to engage with the IAIS leadership for valuable insights into our strategic initiatives and panel sessions:

• **9 July:** Dialogue with the IAIS Executive Committee to discuss delivery against its 2024 work programme and development of the 2025-2029 Strategic Plan

• **10 July:** Panel sessions on artificial intelligence in the insurance sector and insurers’ asset management in a changing interest rate environment

• **11 July:** 90-minute session for engaging with committee chairs on the work of their committees

Please visit the [**IAIS Global Seminar 2024**](https://www.iaisweb.org/news-and-events/2024-global-seminar/) webpage for the full programme and more details about the speakers.
