## IAIS email alert

Follow the news that matters to you with IAIS email alerts service. You will receive an email notification when a new document or update is posted on the IAIS website that matches the topic(s) you have selected.

For IAIS Members,  you can additionally [subscribe](https://extranet.iaisweb.org/page/e-mail-alert) to member-only email alerts on topics or (sub)committees that matter to you on the [IAIS Extranet (login required)](https://extranet.iaisweb.org/page/e-mail-alert). This will allow you to receive a daily alert informing you of relevant documents uploaded to or made available on the Extranet.
