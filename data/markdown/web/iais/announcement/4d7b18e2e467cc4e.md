# New Committees now operational: MRC, SSC and IAC

[2 Jan 2026](https://www.iais.org/2026/01/new-committees-now-operational-mrc-ssc-and-iac/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The new IAIS Committee-level structure announced last year has become effective from 1 January 2026.

Full explanation of the changes was provided in the [November announcement](https://www.iais.org/2025/11/iais-announces-new-organisational-structure-and-leadership-at-the-committee-level/). In short, the new committees and their chairs are:

* Monitoring and Risk Assessment Committee (MRC)
  + MRC will continue to oversee the IAIS’ activities on monitoring and assessing global insurance sector developments and risks and detecting the possible build-up of systemic risk.
  + Chair: Dieter Hendrickx, Head of Insurance Supervision, National Bank of Belgium

* Standards and Supervisory Practices Committee (SSC)
  + SSC is responsible for setting and maintaining IAIS global standards and for supporting Members in implementing these standards and promoting good supervisory practices.
  + Co-Chair: Farzana Badat, Deputy Commissioner, Financial Sector Conduct Authority, South Africa
  + Co-Chair: Judi French, Director, Ohio Department of Insurance, USA
* Implementation Assessment Committee (IAC)
  + IAC will focus exclusively on assessing the globally consistent and comprehensive implementation of IAIS standards.
  + Chair: Vicky White, Director of Insurance and Cross-Sectoral Policy, Prudential Regulation Authority, UK

There is also now a more flexible reporting structure for the subcommittees. Subcommittees will report to one or more committee(s) on a per project/activity basis determined by the annual Roadmap.

For more background, see the updated [IAIS organigram](https://www.iais.org/about-the-iais/organisational-structure/) and read about the [IAIS leadership](https://www.iais.org/about-the-iais/iais-leadership/). You can also watch the recording of [In conversation with IAIS committee chairs at the IAIS Annual Conference 2025](https://www.iais.org/news-and-events/iais-annual-conference-2025/) where Romain Paserot, IAIS Deputy Secretary General, discussed the committee changes and 2026 priorities with the newly appointed chairs.
