**Andrea Vetrone**

*Senior Expert and Head of Digital Operational Resilience act, EIOPA*  
*Chair, Operational Resilience Working Group*

Andrea Vetrone is a Senior Expert and Head of the Digital Operational Resilience act (DORA) implementation at EIOPA, leading a team charged with the implementation of the DORA project. He is also the Secretary of the Joint Committee sub-Committee on Digital Operational Resilience tasked with implementing DORA at the European level.

Andrea joined the EIOPA Oversight Department in 2018 working with EIOPA’s members on prudential supervisory issues, and also worked with a Big4 audit firm and a large multinational insurance group dealing with projects relating to operations and operational risks of complex and large financial entities.
