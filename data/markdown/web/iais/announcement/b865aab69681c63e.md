### Janina Voss

**Interim Head of Secretariat, Access to Insurance Initiative (A2ii)**

Janina Voss joined the Access to Insurance Initiative (A2ii) in 2015. Before taking over the role of Interim Head of Secretariat, Janina led different workstreams on capacity building within the A2ii team, including the Initiative’s work on actuarial capacity building for insurance supervisors. Prior to joining A2ii, she worked as a Financial Systems Development Advisor at GIZ for five years with a focus on developing and monitoring technical assistant projects in the field of insurance. Janina is a political scientist and economist by training.
