# IAIS publishes Application Paper on supervising diversity, equity and inclusion – the governance, risk management and culture perspective

[25 Nov 2024](https://www.iais.org/2024/11/iais-publishes-application-paper-on-supervising-diversity-equity-and-inclusion-the-governance-risk-management-and-culture-perspective/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

Following consultation between March and June 2024, the IAIS has now published its [Application Paper on supervising diversity, equity and inclusion – the governance, risk management and culture perspective](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2024/11/Application-paper-on-supervising-DEI-the-governance-risk-management-and-culture-perspective.pdf).

Full details on the paper, the public consultation comments received and the IAIS’ responses are available on this [page](https://www.iais.org/2024/03/public-consultation-of-application-paper-on-supervising-diversity-equity-and-inclusion-the-governance-risk-management-and-culture-perspective/).

The IAIS is continuing to review public consultation comments received more recently on the draft Application Paper on how to achieve fair treatment for diverse consumers and anticipates publishing the final paper in mid-2025.

The IAIS’ work on diversity, equity and inclusion will form part of discussions at the [IAIS Annual Conference 2024](https://www.iais.org/news-and-events/2024-annual-conference/) on 5-6 December in Cape Town, South Africa.
