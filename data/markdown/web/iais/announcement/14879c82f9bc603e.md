# Invitation: Public webinar on IAIS Guidance on transitioning to a risk-based solvency regime

[18 Jun 2025](https://www.iais.org/2025/06/invitation-public-webinar-on-iais-guidance-on-transitioning-to-a-risk-based-solvency-regime/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

We are pleased to invite you to a public webinar to present insights from the IAIS Guidance on transitioning to a risk-based solvency (RBS) regime on **26 June 2025**, from **13:00 to 14:00 CEST**.

Developed in collaboration with the International Monetary Fund (IMF), this Guidance provides valuable insights from the experiences of several IAIS member jurisdictions. It also offers practical advice on key considerations for designing and implementing an RBS regime.

The webinar will be hosted by the IAIS Risk-based Solvency Implementation Forum (RBSIF) leadership and will provide an opportunity for supervisors and stakeholders to gain insights on this document as well as ongoing work by the forum. The session will also include a Q&A session.

To participate, please register online [here](https://www.iais.org/events/public-webinar-on-the-iais-guidance-on-transitioning-to-a-risk-based-solvency-rbs-regime/). Dial-in details will be sent to you immediately upon registration.

We look forward to your participation in this session.
