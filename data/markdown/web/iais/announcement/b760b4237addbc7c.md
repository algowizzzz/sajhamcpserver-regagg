# IAIS seeks stakeholder input to its Strategic Plan 2025-2029

[12 Jun 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/06/iais-seeks-stakeholder-input-to-its-strategic-plan-2025-2029/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Strategic Plan and Roadmap](https://www.iais.org/category/strategic-plan-and-roadmap/)

The IAIS has launched a process to develop its next Strategic Plan, which will guide its work over the five-year period from 2025 to 2029. It will replace the [current Strategic Plan](https://www.iaisweb.org/about-the-iais/strategic-plan-and-roadmap/), which concludes at the end of 2024.

Stakeholder engagement is essential to the IAIS’ ability to deliver on its mission. Input from stakeholders will allow the IAIS to have a more comprehensive picture of emerging risks and trends in the sector and to include a range of perspectives in its planning, ensuring that the work we will embark upon in the coming years is most effective and meaningful.

The IAIS is therefore inviting stakeholders to share their views through this survey. Responses to the survey can be submitted [here](https://survey.iaisweb.org/919573?lang=en) and are invited by **18 July 2023**. Only responses submitted through the survey tool will be considered.

In addition, two panel discussions including Q&A at the IAIS Global Seminar in Seattle (15-16 June) will explore future risks and other topics for consideration in our new Strategic Plan. We hope many stakeholders will join us in Seattle, and we will share a recording of the panels on our [website](https://www.iaisweb.org/news-and-events/2023seattle/) after the event.

**Download the [survey questions](https://www.iaisweb.org/uploads/2023/07/IAIS-Strategic-Plan-2025-2029-Stakeholder-Survey.docx).**  
**Submit responses [here](https://survey.iaisweb.org/919573?lang=en).**

3 Jul 2023
