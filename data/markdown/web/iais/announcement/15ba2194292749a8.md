# Registration is open for the 2023 IAIS Annual Conference in Tokyo

[24 Aug 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/08/registration-is-open-for-the-2023-iais-annual-conference-in-tokyo/)

in [Annual Conference](https://www.iais.org/category/annual-conference/), [Events](https://www.iais.org/category/events/), [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

The **[2023 IAIS Annual Conference](https://iaisweb.us20.list-manage.com/track/click?u=863024fec344714c0eec40618&id=e4823f3278&e=29b51bff00)** will take place in person on **9-10 November 2023** in **Tokyo, Japan**.   
  
A special thank you to the [**Financial Services Agency of Japan (JFSA)**](https://iaisweb.us20.list-manage.com/track/click?u=863024fec344714c0eec40618&id=8479426a33&e=29b51bff00) for hosting the event.  
  
**Conference venue**  
Hilton Tokyo Odaiba  
1-9-1, Daiba  
Minato-ku, Tokyo 135-8625  
Tel: +81 3 5500 5500  
  
**Programme and speakers**  
Please visit the [**2023 IAIS Annual Conference**](https://iaisweb.us20.list-manage.com/track/click?u=863024fec344714c0eec40618&id=13c0493ecc&e=29b51bff00) webpage.  
  
**Annual Conference registration fee**  
Please note that there is a registration fee of **45,000 JPY** for stakeholders to attend the Annual Conference. Upon registration, you have the option to pay either by credit card or bank transfer.  
  
**Registration**  
[**Register now!**](https://iaisweb.us20.list-manage.com/track/click?u=863024fec344714c0eec40618&id=fdbeb0aafd&e=29b51bff00) The deadline for registration is **Tuesday, 17 October 2023**.   
  
\* For IAIS Members, registration is open for the [**2023 IAIS AGM, Annual Conference and Committee Meetings**](https://iaisweb.us20.list-manage.com/track/click?u=863024fec344714c0eec40618&id=23fb3c079f&e=29b51bff00) on the [**IAIS Members Extranet**](https://iaisweb.us20.list-manage.com/track/click?u=863024fec344714c0eec40618&id=9b8a8684f6&e=29b51bff00) (login required).

24 Aug 2023
