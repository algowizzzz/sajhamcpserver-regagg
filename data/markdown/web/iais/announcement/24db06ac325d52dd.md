# Contact us

The IAIS is pleased to receive general inquiries, comments and suggestions. You can contact us by filling in the form on this page.

* For questions related to recruitments, you may contact us via [recruitment@iais.org](mailto:recruitment@iais.org) or visit our [Recruitment page](https://www.iais.org/about-the-iais/recruitment/) for more information.
* For questions related to the IAIS website, Members Extranet or the Volunteers/Global Monitoring Exercise website, please send an email to [helpdesk@iais.org](mailto:helpdesk@iais.org).
* For questions related to Membership applications, please visit our [How to join page](https://www.iais.org/about-the-iais/how-to-join/).

The IAIS develops international standards and principles for insurance supervisors. If your inquiry concerns any of the following topics, please refer to the [Members list](https://iais.org/about-the-iais/iais-members/) and contact the relevant jurisdiction directly:

* Statistical information
* Product specific matters
* Company specific matters
* Domestic issues.
