# Post-event information – Stakeholder session on the IAIS’ work on DEI

[16 Feb 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/02/post-event-information-stakeholder-session-on-the-iais-work-on-dei/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

The IAIS has published a recording of the stakeholder session on the IAIS’ work on diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) in the insurance sector, held **Thursday 9 February 2023**. We have also listed the questions that registered participants were sent in advance of the session and the poll that participants responded to during the session. Further stakeholder responses to the questions, or other comments, are invited by COB **Monday 27 February 2023**.

Please refer [**here**](https://iaisweb.us20.list-manage.com/track/click?u=863024fec344714c0eec40618&id=3e6fd52410&e=58c9a839a5).
