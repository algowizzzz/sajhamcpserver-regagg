### **Daniel Wang**

**Chair, IAIS Climate Risk Steering Group**  
**Executive Director, Insurance Department, Monetary Authority of Singapore**

Mr Daniel Wang heads the Insurance Department in the Monetary Authority of Singapore (“MAS”). The Insurance Department supervises and regulates insurance companies in Singapore and oversees policy formulation for the insurance sector.

Mr Wang is Chair of the Sustainable Insurance Forum and also chairs the Climate Risk Steering Group at the International Association of Insurance Supervisors. He is also MAS’ representative at the IAIS parent committees. Mr Wang is concurrently serving as the Vice-Chair of the ASEAN Taxonomy Board, which is developing a sustainable finance taxonomy for ASEAN member states, and is Chair of the Singapore College of Insurance.
