# 2021 Global Seminar videos

[25 Jun 2021](https://www.iais.org/2021/06/2021-global-seminar-videos/)

in [Global Seminar](https://www.iais.org/category/global-seminar/), [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

The videos from the Global Seminar 2021 are available for replay. Hosted by IAIS ExCo Chair, Victoria Saporta, and moderated by IAIS Secretary General, Jonathan Dixon, highlights include:

* IAIS Executive Committee update on the work of the Association and delivery against the 2021-2022 Roadmap
* Details from IAIS research, including data from the Global Monitoring Exercise, on the impact of Covid-19 on the insurance sector, including some initial insights on lessons learnt.
* Interactive Q&A session with stakeholders covering compelling questions about key issues for the global insurance sector, including sustainability, climate risk, conduct, culture, cyber, resilience, ICS and more.
