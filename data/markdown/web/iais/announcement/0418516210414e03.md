# Registration opens for the 2022 IAIS Annual Conference

[17 Aug 2022](https://www.iais.org/2022/08/registration-opens-for-the-2022-iais-annual-conference/)

in [Annual Conference](https://www.iais.org/category/annual-conference/), [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

The [**2022 IAIS Annual Conference**](https://www.iaisweb.org/news-and-events/2022santiago/) will take place in person in **Santiago, Chile** on **10 – 11 November 2022**.

This year’s theme is a **“Changing landscape: the role of the insurance sector in meeting emerging economic and social challenges”** with panel sessions on diversity, equity and inclusion; changing consumer preferences; macroeconomic risks; climate risk and the Insurance Capital Standard.

Visit the webpage of the [**2022 IAIS Annual Conference**](https://www.iaisweb.org/news-and-events/2022santiago/) to preview the agenda, register online and find more information about the event.

A special thank you to the Financial Market Commission of Chile for hosting the event.
