# Career opportunity at the IAIS

[5 Dec 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/12/career-opportunity-at-the-iais-3/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The IAIS is looking for a Senior Policy Advisor – Stakeholder Management and Outreach, to further enhance the impact and reach of the IAIS. For more information, go to our [Recruitment page](/about-the-iais/recruitment/) or download the vacancy notice [here](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2023/12/Senior-Policy-Advisor-–-Stakeholder-Management-and-Outreach.pdf).

Applications can be submitted until **8 January 2024, 24:00 CET**.

For any questions, please send a message to [IAIS.Recruitment@bis.org.](mailto:IAIS.Recruitment@bis.org)
