### **Suzette Vogelsang**

*Vice Chair, IAIS Executive Committee*  
*Head, Department (Banking, Insurance and FMI Supervision Department), Prudential Authority, South Africa*

Suzette Vogelsang is a Senior Financial Sector Expert: Insurance at the IMF (1 year and 4 months).

Prior to joining the IMF, she was the Head of the Banking, Insurance, and FMI Supervisory Department in the Prudential Authority of the South African Reserve Bank (32 years being a frontline supervisor). Suzette’s supervisory responsibilities included the prudential supervision (solo and group) life and non-life insurance companies, banks (including foreign branches), and the solo prudential supervision of FMIs (securities and derivatives only).

She represented the Prudential Authority in various industry forums in South Africa. She was the 3rd Vice Chair of the IAIS Exco representing EMDEs She was also the IAIS Regional Coordinator of the Sub-Saharan Africa Region. She is currently the Vice Chair of the IAIS Risk-based Solvency Implementation Forum.
