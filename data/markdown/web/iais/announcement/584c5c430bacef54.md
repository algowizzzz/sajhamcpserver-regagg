# Save the date: 2023 IAIS Annual Conference

[7 Jul 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/07/save-the-date-2023-iais-annual-conference/)

in [Annual Conference](https://www.iais.org/category/annual-conference/), [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The **[2023 IAIS Annual Conference](https://www.iais.org/news-and-events/2023tokyo/)** will take place in person on 9-10 November 2023 in Tokyo, Japan. Registration details will follow in August.

A special thank you to the **[Financial Services Agency of Japan](https://www.fsa.go.jp/en/)** for hosting the event.

**Conference venue**  
Hilton Tokyo Odaiba  
1-9-1, Daiba  
Minato-ku, Tokyo 135-8625  
Japan  
Tel: +81 3 5500 5500

**Accommodation**  
Kindly note that details about the block booking will be available once registration opens.

**Japan entry guidelines**  
Japan has taken measures on visa exemptions for some countries and regions. Please check the [**list of visa exemptions for short-term stays**](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/short/novisa.html). Full details can be found on the website of the [**Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Japan**](https://www.mofa.go.jp/j_info/visit/visa/index.html) and/or contact the Embassy of Japan in your respective country.

We look forward to a successful event in Tokyo!
