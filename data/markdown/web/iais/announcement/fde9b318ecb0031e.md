**The consultation closed on 18 August 2025**

The IAIS has now published its revised [Global Monitoring Exercise document](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2025/11/Global-Monitoring-Exercise-Document.pdf), which includes the outcome of the review of the Individual Insurer Monitoring (IIM) assessment methodology, which will be effective starting 2026. The IIM assessment methodology calculates a total score for each of the IIM participating insurers. This score provides an indication of the extent of the possible build-up of systemic risk in the individual insurer.

Comments on the consultation document were sought between 19 June 2025 and 18 August 2025  and responses were received from a combination of external stakeholders and IAIS Members. The responses generally supported the proposed updates  and provided helpful insights on how the IAIS could continue refining the GME going forward. The comments received and the IAIS’ responses to such are available [here](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2025/11/Resolution-of-public-consultation-comments-on-the-review-of-the-Global-Monitoring-Exercise.pdf).

**A public background** session webinar was held on **Friday, 27 June 2025 13:00 – 14:30 CEST** to present the consultation document and answer any questions from stakeholders. Click [here](https://www.iais.org/events/public-background-session-for-the-public-consultation-on-the-review-of-the-global-monitoring-exercise-individual-insurer-monitoring-assessment-methodology/) for the replay.
