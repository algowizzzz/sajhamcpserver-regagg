# Career opportunities at the IAIS

[4 Jul 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/07/career-opportunities-at-the-iais/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The International Association of Insurance Supervisors is inviting applications for several positions at the Secretariat:

* [**Senior Policy Advisor, Legal Counsel and Executive Committee support**](https://www.iaisweb.org/uploads/2023/07/IAIS-Senior-Policy-Advisor-Legal-Counsel-and-Executive-Committee-support.pdf)
* [**Senior Policy Advisor, Financial Stability**](https://www.iaisweb.org/uploads/2023/07/IAIS-Senior-Policy-Advisor-Financial-Stability.pdf)
* [**Senior Data Analyst**](https://www.iaisweb.org/uploads/2023/07/IAIS-Senior-Data-Analyst.pdf)
* [**Administrative Officer**](https://www.iaisweb.org/uploads/2023/07/IAIS-Administrative-Officer.pdf)

Applications should be submitted by 24 July 2023, 24.00 CEST, using the relevant link indicated in the notice.

For more information, go to our [Recruitment page](/about-the-iais/recruitment/).

For any questions, please send a message to [IAIS.Recruitment@bis.org.](mailto:IAIS.Recruitment@bis.org)

25 Jul 2024
