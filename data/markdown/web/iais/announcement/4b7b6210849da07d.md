# IAIS publishes 2022 Financial Report

[9 Nov 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/11/iais-publishes-2022-financial-report/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The IAIS is pleased to publish today the Association’s 2022 Financial Report, containing the financial statements of the IAIS for the 12 months from January to December, as well as reports from the Chairs of the Executive Committee, Budget Committee and Audit and Risk Committee.

Read the report [here](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2023/11/IAIS-Financial-Report-2022.pdf).

9 Nov 2023
