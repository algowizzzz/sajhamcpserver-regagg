## Accommodation

The Hong Kong Insurance Authority has made block bookings at the meeting venue and alternative hotels from 8-14 November 2026.

Please book your rooms as soon as possible and make your reservations directly with the hotels, using the respective reservation links. Prices and availability are guaranteed until the specified cut-off date or until rooms are sold out, whichever comes first.

**Telephone:** +852 2252 5888  
**Cost/night:**HKD1,900 for a deluxe city view room (single occupancy, breakfast included). A 10% service charge and a 3% government tax are not included in the rate.
