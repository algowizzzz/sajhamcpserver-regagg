### **Monica Ruggiero Guglielmini**

***Chair, IAIS Macroprudential Supervision Working Group  
Senior Policy Officer,*** *Istituto per la vigilanza sulle assicurazioni (IVASS)*

Monica Ruggiero Guglielmini is an economist with a PhD in Business Management Science from the University of Rome “La Sapienza.” She has significant experience in micro and macroprudential supervision of the insurance industry and a background in management consulting. Monica is currently a Senior Policy Officer at IVASS and Chair of the Macroprudential Supervision Working Group at IAIS. She coordinates risk analysis activities, stakeholder discussions, and macroprudential scenario analyses at IVASS to identify potential vulnerabilities in the insurance sector.

Her career includes key roles in implementing IAIS principles and EIOPA guidelines on governance, risk assessment, key functions, and ORSA for prudential aspects. Monica’s expertise and leadership continue to enhance insurance supervision and macroprudential oversight.
