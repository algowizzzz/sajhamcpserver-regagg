# IAIS publishes 2024 Financial Report

[12 Nov 2025](https://www.iais.org/2025/11/iais-publishes-2024-financial-report/)

in [Annual Report](https://www.iais.org/category/annual-report/), [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

Following the Annual General Meeting this afternoon in Tirana, the IAIS has published the Association’s [2024 Financial Report](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2025/11/IAIS-Financial-Report-2024.pdf), containing the financial statements of the IAIS for the 12 months from January to December 2024, as well as reports from the Chairs of the Executive Committee, Budget Committee and Audit and Risk Committee.  

The IAIS Financial Report is a companion publication to the IAIS [Year in Review](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2025/04/IAIS-Year-in-Review-2024.pdf), published earlier this year in April, that provides an overview of the work of our Association. 

Read the report [here](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2025/11/IAIS-Financial-Report-2024.pdf).

12 Nov 2025
