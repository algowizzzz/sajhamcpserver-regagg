# IAIS releases Year in Review 2022, sharing highlights from the past year

[27 Mar 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/03/iais-releases-year-in-review-2022-sharing-highlights-from-the-past-year/)

in [Annual Report](https://www.iais.org/category/annual-report/), [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The IAIS Year in Review 2022 is released today! The report highlights the progress we made over the past year to support our dual mission of policyholder protection and doing our part to maintain global financial stability.

Spanning 57 pages, the report provides an overview of the IAIS’ role, mission and strategy, then delves into the progress and key achievements of the IAIS committees, working groups, task forces and forums in 2022. The report also provides a consolidated overview of the work delivered that is increasingly cross sectoral and global in nature.

This year’s edition has a new section on social responsibility, which highlights the IAIS’ efforts in progressing its own diversity, equity and inclusion (DEI) and measuring its impact against the Environmental Policy.

The About the IAIS section has been expanded to include details on the IAIS’ framework of supervisory standards and practices, specifically the Insurance Core Principles, ComFrame (including the Insurance Capital Standard) and the Holistic Framework. And the one-pager “IAIS 2022 in numbers” highlights many of the Association’s quantifiable deliverables.

Read more [here](https://www.iaisweb.org/about-the-iais/annual-report/).
