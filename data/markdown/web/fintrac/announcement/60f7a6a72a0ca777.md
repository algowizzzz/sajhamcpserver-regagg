# Accessibility

The goal of the Accessible Canada Act  is to achieve a barrier-free Canada by 2040. It requires organizations to proactively identify, remove, and prevent barriers to accessibility where Canadians interact with areas under federal jurisdiction, whether they be internal or external to an organization.

For FINTRAC, our aim is to be a model for accessibility by embodying accessibility, embracing it as a core value of our corporate culture, and leading by example.
