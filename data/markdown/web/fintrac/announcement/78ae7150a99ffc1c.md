# Find out if you have obligations

This page provides information for businesses who are new to the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act and associated Regulations, and to being supervised by FINTRAC. Be sure to also consult our [Obligations and guidance](/guidance-directives/guidance-directives-eng).

## [Businesses that we regulate](/reporting-declaration/info/re-ed-eng)

Consult the list of the business sectors that must report to FINTRAC

## [Check to see if you need to register with FINTRAC as a money services business](/msb-esm/questions/2-eng)

Money services businesses and foreign money services businesses that offer certain services to the public need to register with FINTRAC

## [Check to see if you are covered under the Act and Regulations as a mortgage administrator, broker or lender](/re-ed/mortgage-hypotheque/2-eng)

Mortgage administrators, brokers, and lenders that offer certain services may be subject to the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act and associated Regulations.

## [FINTRAC's supervisory framework](/introduction/cpf/cpf-eng)

FINTRAC's supervisory framework provides information on how we assist, assess and enforce compliance with the Proceeds of Crime (Money Laundering) and Terrorist Financing Act and associated Regulations.
