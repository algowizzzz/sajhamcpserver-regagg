# Departmental Sustainable Development Strategy plan

The Departmental Sustainable Development Strategy (DSDS) outlines departmental actions and programming in support of the [Federal Sustainable Development Strategy (FSDS)](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/climate-change/federal-sustainable-development-strategy.html), and focusses on issues related to the department's core responsibilities. It also demonstrates our support of the United Nations' 2030 Sustainable Development Agenda. A new DSDS is tabled one year after the tabling of each new FSDS. DSDS progress reports also inform Canadians of strides made in meeting our commitments.
