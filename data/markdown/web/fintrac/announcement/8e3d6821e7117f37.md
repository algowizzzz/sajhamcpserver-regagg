# FINTRAC mailing list

Stay up-to-date on FINTRAC information by subscribing to the FINTRAC
Mailing List. By subscribing, you will receive e-mail reports on
FINTRAC's activities and notification of new publications and
guidelines. You will also be notified of changes made to reporting
mechanisms and reporting requirements.

## To subscribe

1. A pre-filled e-mail will appear on your screen by clicking on "[SUBSCRIBE](mailto:List-Liste@fintrac-canafe.gc.ca?Subject=SUBSCRIBE)".
2. Send this e-mail to FINTRAC to complete your subscription. Please
   note that modifying this e-mail in any way could result in a
   non-subscription to FINTRAC's automated mailing service.

Or, if a pre-filled e-mail does not appear on your screen

1. Send an e-mail to List-Liste@fintrac-canafe.gc.ca with SUBSCRIBE in the subject line.

## To unsubscribe

1. A pre-filled e-mail will appear on your screen by clicking on "[UNSUBSCRIBE](mailto:List-Liste@fintrac-canafe.gc.ca?Subject=UNSUBSCRIBE)".
2. Send this e-mail to FINTRAC to cancel your subscription. Please note
   that modifying this e-mail in any way could result in a non-cancellation
   of FINTRAC's automated mailing service.

Or, if a pre-filled e-mail does not appear on your screen

1. Send an e-mail to List-Liste@fintrac-canafe.gc.ca with UNSUBSCRIBE in the subject line.
