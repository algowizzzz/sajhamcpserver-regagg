## Disclosure of briefing note titles

The briefing note titles were prepared for the Director and Chief Executive Officer of the Financial Transactions and Analysis Centre of Canada (FINTRAC). The lists of titles are generated at the end of each month by FINTRAC and represents a snapshot in time of documents created for that month. In accordance with both the *Access to Information Act* and the *Privacy Act*, the titles of briefing notes that contain either personal or confidential information, or information related to security, have not been included in this listing. If you find a title of interest, you may make an [access to information request](/atip-aiprp/privacy-privee3-eng) in order to obtain the records.
