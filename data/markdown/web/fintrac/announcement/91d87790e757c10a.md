# FINTRAC's data on the Open Government portal

The Government of Canada has an "open by default" policy. This means all government data and information resources of business value are made public, unless there is a valid reason not to. Those valid reasons include ownership, security, privacy, and confidentiality.

Learn more about Canada's Action Plan on Open Government by visiting the [Open Government website](https://open.canada.ca/en).

FINTRAC's datasets on the Open Government portal:

* [Completed Access to Information Act requests](https://open.canada.ca/en/search/ati?search_api_fulltext=Financial+Transactions+and+Reports+Analysis+Centre+of+Canada+&=Search)
* [Briefing note titles](https://search.open.canada.ca/briefing_titles/?owner_org=fintrac-canafe&page=1&sort=date_received+desc)
* [Government contracts awarded](https://search.open.canada.ca/contracts/?owner_org=fintrac-canafe&page=1&sort=contract_date+desc)
* [Hospitality expenses](https://search.open.canada.ca/hospitality/?owner_org=fintrac-canafe&page=1&sort=start_date+desc)
* [Travel expenses](https://search.open.canada.ca/travel/?owner_org=fintrac-canafe&page=1&sort=start_date+desc)
* [Money services businesses registry](/msb-esm/reg-eng)
* [Report volume data](https://open.canada.ca/data/en/dataset/81cc47ac-e88d-4b7f-9318-8774a2d919e6)
