## Press Release

May 12, 2026

### Federal Reserve Board announces termination of enforcement actions with F & M Holding Company, Inc. and Thread Bancorp, Inc.

For release at 11:00 a.m. EDT

* [Share](# "Share")

The Federal Reserve Board on Tuesday announced the termination of the enforcement actions listed below:

F & M Holding Company, Inc., Manchester, Georgia  
[Written Agreement dated May 31, 2011 (PDF)](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/files/enf20110602a1.pdf)  
Terminated May 6, 2026

Thread Bancorp, Inc., Rogersville, Tennessee (formerly known as Volunteer Bancorp, Inc.)  
[Written Agreement dated May 25, 2010 (PDF)](https://www.federalreserve.gov/newsevents/pressreleases/files/enf20100603b1.pdf)  
Terminated May 6, 2026

Additional enforcement actions can be searched for [here](https://www.federalreserve.gov/apps/enforcementactions/search.aspx).
