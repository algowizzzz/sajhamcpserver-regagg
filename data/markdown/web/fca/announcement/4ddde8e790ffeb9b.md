On 1 June 2026, Prosper Capital LLP (Prosper) went into creditors’ voluntary liquidation. Jeremy Karr and Simon Killick of BTG Begbies Traynor (Central) LLP were appointed as joint liquidators.

## On this page

* [Who to contact](#section-who-to-contact)
* [Support for consumers](#section-support-for-consumers)
* [Being alert to scams](#section-being-alert-to-scams)

Prosper, an FCA-authorised firm (firm reference number (FRN): 453007), was an alternative investment fund manager and arranged deals in investments.

Prosper has applied to cancel its authorisation, which is subject to review by the FCA.

Prosper was also responsible for the activities of its appointed representative, Crowd2Let Capital Limited (Crowd2Let) (FRN: 743371).

Below we set out:

* What to do if you’re a customer who wishes to make a complaint or has an existing complaint against the firm.
* How to contact the joint liquidators.
* How to protect yourself from fraudsters claiming to act on behalf of the firm or the joint liquidators.

If you believe you have a claim against Prosper, contact the joint liquidators using the details below.

### [Why has Prosper entered into liquidation?](#expandable1896181)

The Financial Ombudsman Service (FOS) upheld complaints about property investments made through Crowd2Let, which was an appointed representative of Prosper.

Having considered the financial position and received professional insolvency advice, Prosper stopped trading. Its designated members took steps to place Prosper into creditors’ voluntary liquidation.

### [What is the Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) and will it cover any losses?](#expandable1896186)

The Financial Services Compensation Scheme (FSCS) protects consumers when financial services firms fail. It is the compensation scheme for customers of UK authorised financial services firms. It has eligibility criteria for both the people and businesses who are covered.

If a claim is covered by the FSCS, eligible customers may receive up to £85,000.

Find out more about [what the FSCS covers and compensation limits](https://www.fscs.org.uk/what-we-cover/ "What we cover | Financial Services Compensation Scheme").

The FSCS is now open to claims for Prosper about the activities of its appointed representative, Crowd2Let.

The FSCS is currently investigating Prosper to decide whether claims qualify for compensation.

Find more information on the [FSCS’s dedicated Prosper page](https://www.fscs.org.uk/making-a-claim/failed-firms/prosper-capital/ "Prosper Capital LLP | Financial Services Compensation Scheme").

### [What should I do if I want to complain?](#expandable1896191)

If you want to make a complaint, contact the joint liquidators using the [contact details above](#section-who-to-contact "Who to contact").

### [What should I do if I have an existing complaint against Prosper?](#expandable1896196)

If you have an existing complaint against Prosper, please provide details of your complaint in writing to the joint liquidators using the [contact details above](#section-who-to-contact "Who to contact").

If you have already referred a complaint to the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://www.financial-ombudsman.org.uk/ "Financial Ombudsman Service") (FOS), you don’t need to take any action at the moment. As Prosper is in liquidation, the FOS will contact you in due course about next steps.

### [Do I need to use a claims management company to get my money back?](#expandable1896201)

You should proceed with caution if you are approached by a [claims management company (CMC)](/consumers/using-claims-management-companies "Using claims management companies").

For most clients, there is no benefit in involving a third party in making your claim.

If you use a CMC to help with your claim, the CMC is likely to seek a fee which may reduce how much money you get back.

If you are considering using a CMC to help with your claim, we suggest you first discuss this with the joint liquidators using the [contact details above](#section-who-to-contact "Who to contact").

### [Is the FCA overseeing the liquidation?](#expandable1896206)

Prosper is still authorised by the FCA and remains subject to supervisory oversight and our rules. The firm has submitted an application to cancel its authorisation, which is subject to review by the FCA.

The FCA does not regulate insolvency processes or the joint liquidators. The individuals appointed as joint liquidators are regulated by the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England & Wales (ICAEW). They must follow insolvency law and are answerable to the courts.

We are liaising as appropriate with the joint liquidators.

## Being alert to scams

All customers should remain alert to the possibility of fraud.

If you get an unexpected phone call from someone claiming to be from Prosper or BTG Begbies Traynor, please end the call and contact the joint liquidators using the contact details above.
