# Deferral of the public consultation on Guideline B-15 disclosure expectation for financed emissions related to off-balance sheet assets under management

On January 8, 2026, we paused the planned [public consultation](/en/guidance/guidance-library/letter-industry-we-are-updating-guideline-b-15-final-cssb-standards "Letter to Industry – We are Updating Guideline B-15 for the Final CSSB Standards") on the disclosure expectation for financed emissions related to off-balance sheet assets under management (AUM) under [Guideline B-15](/en/guidance/guidance-library/climate-risk-management#ann2.2 "Climate Risk Management"). In line with this pause, we will also defer the implementation for this expectation to a future date. We have reflected this change in Annex 2-2 of the guideline.

Financial institutions are still expected to quantify and manage their climate-related transition risks, including for off-balance sheet AUM. The deferral of the disclosure expectation allows financial institutions time to strengthen data collection and measurement. We will continue to monitor progress through our supervisory channels.

Please contact [CatastrophicRisks-RisqueCatastrophe@osfi-bsif.gc.ca](mailto:CatastrophicRisks-RisqueCatastrophe@osfi-bsif.gc.ca) if you have any questions.
