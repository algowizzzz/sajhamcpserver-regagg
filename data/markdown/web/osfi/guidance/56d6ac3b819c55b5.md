**To:** All federally regulated financial institutions

Pursuant to Section 3(2) of the [Assessment of Financial Institutions Regulations, 2017](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/regulations/SOR-2016-297/FullText.html#834991), the minimum base assessments applicable to federally regulated financial institutions will be adjusted as follows for the 2026/27 fiscal year (April 1, 2026 – March 31, 2027):

| Institution type | 2026/27  minimum assessment | 2025/26  minimum assessment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. a society or a foreign fraternal benefit society | $2,560 | $2,510 |
| 2. an authorized foreign bank that is subject to the restrictions and requirements referred to in subsection 524(2) of the [Bank Act](http://www.laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/B-1.01); 3. a trust and loan company whose activities are restricted to carrying on any of the fiduciary activities referred to in section 412 of the [Trust and Loan Companies Act](http://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/T-19.8/) and any ancillary activities; 4. a cooperative credit association; 5. a company, a provincial company or a foreign company to which the [Insurance Companies Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/i-11.8/) applies | $19,190 | $18,800 |
| 6. a bank; 7. an authorized foreign bank that is not subject to the restrictions and requirements referred to in subsection 524(2) of the [Bank Act](http://www.laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/B-1.01); 8. a trust and loan company not referred to in c) above; 9. a retail association. | $38,370 | $37,590 |

The adjustments are due to the change in the Consumer Price Index[Footnote 1](#fn1) from 2024 to 2025. Any questions on the change can be directed to Melvin Green, Senior Manager, Financial Reporting, Operations and Systems ([melvin.green@osfi-bsif.gc.ca](mailto:melvin.green@osfi-bsif.gc.ca) or (613) 614-2134).
