# Service standards and fees

Did you receive an email from the email address **do.not.reply\_OSFI@tbs-sct.gc.ca** with the subject line “Dunning Notice / Lettre de Relance”? Be assured that these are legitimate, safe emails coming from OSFI. If you receive one of these emails and you have questions, please contact our Finance team at [Finance@osfi-bsif.gc.ca](mailto:Finance@osfi-bsif.gc.ca).

We charge fees for some of our services per the *[Service Fees Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/S-8.4/)*, which are adjusted every year to keep pace with inflation. The act requires that we have service standards and a remission policy in place to refund fee payers if those standards aren’t met. We must also report service costs to Treasury Board and annually publish revenue from fees.

## On this page

## Measuring against our standards

We start measuring our response time when we receive all the information we need to perform our assessment. If applicable, we’ll issue an application receipt to you effective this day. If we don’t have all the necessary information, we’ll issue a deficiency letter.

We stop measuring our response time when, as applicable:

* We send a recommendation to the minister to approve or deny the application (for ministerial approvals only)
* The Superintendent approves or denies the request
* We issue an interpretation, ruling, or confirmation
* The requester withdraws or places their request on hold

If we don’t meet our standards for services subject to a fee, we will reimburse some or all of the fee. [Refer to our policy on the remission of service fees](/en/data-forms/applications-approvals/service-standards-fees/remission-service-fees "Remission of service fees").

## Service standards for high-volume regulatory authorizations

We publish our service standard performance for high-volume regulatory authorizations to increase transparency. Only one of our services (insurance institution Form 298 approval) is currently a high-volume regulatory authorization.

### Service standards for Form 298 approval

When insurance institutions submit a Form 298 request, we aim to provide a decision within 3 business days. It may take longer than that if we need more information or if the request is more complex than usual.

See [instructions for completing Form 298](/en/data-forms/applications-approvals/procedures-completing-osfi-form-298 "Procedures for Completing OSFI Form 298").

### Performance results

Our performance target is to achieve our service standard at least 80% of the time. We have exceeded this target in past fiscal years. We met our service standard of providing a decision within 3 business days:

* 94% of the time in 2020-21
* 97% of the time in 2021-22
* 99% of the time in 2022-23
* 100% of the time in 2023-24
* 98% of the time in 2024-25
* 98% of the time in 2025-26
