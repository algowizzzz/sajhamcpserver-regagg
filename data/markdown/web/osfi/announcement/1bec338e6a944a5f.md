# How Do I File a Complaint?

If you are not satisfied with the manner in which your request was processed under the *[Access to Information Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/a-1/)*, you have the right to file a complaint with the [Information Commissioner of Canada](http://www.oic-ci.gc.ca/eng/):

The Information Commissioner of Canada  
Place de Ville, Tower B  
112 Kent Street, 7th Floor  
Ottawa, Ontario  K1A 1H3

If you are not satisfied with the manner in which your request was processed under the *[Privacy Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/P-21/)*, you have the right to file a complaint with the [Privacy Commissioner of Canada](http://www.priv.gc.ca/):

The Privacy Commissioner of Canada  
Place de Ville, Tower B  
112 Kent Street, 3rd floor  
Ottawa, Ontario  K1A 1H3

In either scenario, you may wish to contact us before doing so as we may be able to rectify the situation or may be able to more clearly explain why we could not provide the information to you.
