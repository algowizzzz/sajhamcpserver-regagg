# OSFI's CAR Guideline

60-day public consultation is underway on the draft revisions to the Capital Adequacy Requirements (CAR) Guideline.

**Video duration:** 00 hours:00 minutes:45 seconds  
**Date:** February 20, 2025

Transcript

[Capital Adequacy Requirements Guideline]

[Mark Joshua – Director, Banking Capital and Liquidity Standards Division]

We would like to hear your feedback during the 60-day public consultation on updates to our Capital Adequacy Requirements Guideline for 2026.

We are clarifying the capital rules and have made some revisions to improve the consistency of their application to enhance the financial resilience and stability of our banks and proposing changes under the market risk Standardized Approach.

Comments received during this consultation will inform the final guidelines which come into effect in Q1 2026.
