# Domestic Stability Buffer - Decision Summary Note - December 2024

## Decision

Today, the Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions (OSFI) announced that the Domestic Stability Buffer (DSB) will remain at 3.5% of the total risk-weighted assets. This reflects our assessment that although systemic vulnerabilities remain elevated, they are stable while near-term risks continue to be low but rising.

## Rationale

Since the June 2024 DSB announcement, vulnerabilities in the financial system have been broadly stable, although remain elevated. External vulnerabilities have risen since June due to escalating geopolitical tensions while there have been no material changes to other key vulnerabilities. Canadian household debt levels and house prices remain high but have slightly declined relative to incomes, while credit growth has been relatively stable. At the same time, household debt serviceability remains a challenge given mortgage payment stress at renewal for some borrowers but is less of a concern given lower interest rates. Non-financial corporate balance sheets and liquidity positions have slightly weakened, while uncertainty in commercial real estate valuations persists.

In addition, near-term risks are assessed to be low but rising. Continued strength in DSIB performance despite an uncertain economic outlook, suggests that near-term risks to capital levels remain low. At the same time, gross impaired loans have continued to increase along with provisions for bad loans. Financial market conditions remain positive, despite significant volatility during the summer from geopolitical tensions and global policy uncertainty.

DSIBs must remain vigilant and exercise sound judgement in their capital management practices given the heightened vulnerabilities. They should not rely on the DSB as a substitute or the primary means of prudent capital management. OSFI is committed to proactively adjusting the buffer as the risk environment changes and stands ready to act in line with our early response principle, as warranted.
