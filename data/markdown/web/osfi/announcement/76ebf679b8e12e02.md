# Applications and approvals

## Streamlined framework for new entrants

Visit [Streamlined approvals framework for targeted new entrants](/en/data-forms/applications-approvals/streamlined-approvals-framework-targeted-new-entrants "Streamlined Approvals Framework for Targeted New Entrants ") to learn more about the requirements and process for entities seeking to enter Canada's federal financial system.

For certain transactions and some business activities, financial institutions need regulatory approval from the Superintendent of Financial Institutions or the Minister of Finance (on the advice of OSFI).

The recommendations we make to the Superintendent and the Minister are in the best interest of:

* creditors
* depositors
* policyholders
* the Canadian financial sector

## Transaction instructions

Refer to our [list of transaction instructions](/en/data-forms/applications-approvals/transaction-instructions "Transaction instructions") to learn how to submit your applications for approval.
