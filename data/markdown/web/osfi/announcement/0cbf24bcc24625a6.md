# Regulatory Officer Development Program

Our development program offers opportunities for new graduates to learn more about how we regulate Canada’s financial industry.

In this 24-month program, you’ll:

* learn about our regulatory framework
* develop your analytical and leadership skills
* understand our corporate strategies, systems, and practices
* increase your knowledge of the financial industry legislation and regulations
* enhance your project management and presentation skills
* apply your financial analysis skills

When positions are available, you can find them on [GC Jobs](https://emploisfp-psjobs.cfp-psc.gc.ca/psrs-srfp/applicant/page2440?tab=2&department=119&toggleLanguage=en).
