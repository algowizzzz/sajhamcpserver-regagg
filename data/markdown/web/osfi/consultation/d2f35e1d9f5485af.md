# We want to hear from you on our Quarterly Release approach

OSFI has now completed two Quarterly Releases as part of the pilot for the new approach.

If you have feedback on the Quarterly Release approach that you’d like to share, please participate in an anonymous 2-minute survey.

Please [complete the survey](https://osfibsif.qualtrics.com/jfe/form/SV_3RbDQzzyYzA1t8W) by January 17, 2025.

If you have any questions about the survey, please contact [Surveys-sondages@osfi-bsif.gc.ca](mailto:Surveys-sondages@osfi-bsif.gc.ca).

## Privacy notice

The information collected through the following form complies with the [Privacy Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/ACTS/P-21/index.html) and is collected under the authority of section 3 of the [Office of the Superintendent of Financial Institutions Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/o-2.7/index.html).

The use of the form is voluntary and personal information collected through the forms will be used by OSFI to obtain feedback.

Personal information will be protected under the provisions of the Privacy Act and will be stored in the Personal Information Bank (PIB), [PSU 914 Public Communications](/en/transparency/sources-federal-government-employee-information-info-source "Sources of Federal Government and Employee Information (Info Source)").

Under the Privacy Act, you have the right to access your personal information and request correction of your information. You have the right to file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada regarding the handling of your personal information ([File a formal privacy complaint - Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/report-a-concern/file-a-formal-privacy-complaint/)). Should you wish to exercise your rights under the Privacy Act, or have any question, please contact OSFI’s ATIP Office ([ATIP-AIPRP@osfi-bsif.gc.ca](mailto:ATIP-AIPRP@osfi-bsif.gc.ca)).
