SUMMARY: The Securities and Exchange Commission published a document in 
the Federal Register on May 28, 2026, concerning Notice of Filing of 
Amendment No. 1 and Order Granting Accelerated Approval of a Proposed 
Rule Change, as Modified by Amendment No. 1, to List and Trade Nasdaq 
Bitcoin Index Options. The document contained an error.

Commission, 100 F Street NE, Washington, DC 20549, (202) 551-5400.

SUPPLEMENTARY INFORMATION:

Correction

    In the Federal Register of May 28, 2026, in FR Doc. 2026-10537, at 
91 FR 31769 on page 31794, in the third column under the heading 
``Conclusion,'' add a paragraph following the ordering paragraph that 
states ``For the Commission, by the Division of Trading and Markets, 
pursuant to delegated authority.'' \179\
