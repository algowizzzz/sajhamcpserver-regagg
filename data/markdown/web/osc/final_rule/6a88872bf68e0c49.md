**PERSONAL HOLDING COMPANIES AND REGISTERED RETIREMENT SAVINGS PLANS**  

**Notice of Amendment**

The Commission has, under section 143 of the *Securities Act* (the "Act"), amended the rule entitled *In the Matter of Trades by an Issuer in Securities of Its OwnIssue to Senior Officers, Directors, Personal Holding Companies and Registered Retirement Savings Plans and a Controlling Shareholder in Securities of anIssuer to Employees, Senior Officers, Directors, Personal Holding Companies and Registered Retirement Savings Plans* (1997), 20 OSCB 1218 (the "Rule").The amendment extends the expiration date of the Rule from July 1, 1998 to December 31, 1998. The amendment, however, does not materially change the Ruleand, accordingly, under section 143.2 of the Act, the Commission has not published the amendment for comment.

The amendment and the material required by the Act to be delivered to the Minister of Finance were delivered on April 8, 1998. If the Minister does not approvethe amendment, reject the amendment or return it to the Commission for further consideration, the amendment will come into force on June 22, 1998. If theMinister does approve the amendment, the amendment will come into force 15 days after it is approved.

**Substance and Purpose of Amendment**

The Rule replaced the deemed rule entitled *In the Matter of Trades by an Issuer in Securities of Its Own Issue to Senior Officers, Directors, Personal HoldingCompanies and Registered Retirement Savings Plans and a Controlling Shareholder in Securities of an Issuer to Employees, Senior Officers, Directors,Personal Holding Companies and Registered Retirement Savings Plans* (1994), 17 OSCB 5518.

The Rule provides that it will expire on the earlier of the date on which a new rule intended to replace it comes into force and July 1, 1998. Proposed Rule45-503 Trades to Employees, Executives and Consultants, which is intended to replace the Rule, was published for comment on February 20, 1998 at (1998), 21OSCB 1123. The comment period on proposed Rule 45-503 expires on May 22, 1998. The purpose of the amendment is to extend the expiration date of theRule from July 1, 1998 to December 31, 1998 in order to allow sufficient time for consideration of the comments to be received on proposed Rule 45-503 andfor review of proposed Rule 45-503 by the Minister.
