# Notice of Ministerial Approval: NI - 55-101 - Insider Reporting Exemptions

Notice of Ministerial Approval
National Instrument

## September 7, 2007

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20070907_55-101_not-insider-reporting.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE OF MINISTERIAL APPROVAL**

**AMENDMENTS TO**

**NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 55-101**

**AND COMPANION POLICY**

**55-101CP INSIDER REPORTING EXEMPTIONS**

On July 24, 2007, the Minister of Government Services approved, pursuant to section 143.3 of the *Securities Act* (Ontario), amendments to National Instrument 55-101 *Insider Reporting Exemptions* (the rule).

The amendments to the rule and related amendments to Companion Policy 55-101CP *Insider Reporting Exemptions* (the policy) will come into force in Ontario on September 10, 2007.

The amendments to the rule and the policy were previously published in the Bulletin on June 8, 2007. The amending instrument together with blacklined versions of the rule and policy are published in Chapter 5 of this Bulletin.

September 7, 2007
