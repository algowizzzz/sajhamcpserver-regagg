# Notice - Notice of Amendment: OSC Rule - 31-501 - Registrant Relationships

Notice of Commission Approval
OSC Rule

## November 29, 2002

**NOTICE OF AMENDMENTS TO OSC RULE 31-501 -   
REGISTRANT RELATIONSHIPS -- OSC RULE 31-504 -**

**APPLICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION -- OSC RULE 35-502 -**

**NON-RESIDENT ADVISERS**

The Commission is publishing in today's Bulletin amendments to OSC Rule 31-501: Registrant Relationships; OSC Rule 31-504: Applications for Registration; and OSC Rule 35-502: Non-resident Advisers.

The Notices and Amendments are published in Chapter 5 of the Bulletin and at http://www.osc.gov.on.ca/en/Regulation/Rulemaking/rule.html.
