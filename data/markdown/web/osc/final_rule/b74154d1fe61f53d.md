# Notice of Ministerial Approval: OSC Rule - 24-501 - Designation as a Market Participant

Notice of Ministerial Approval
OSC Rule

## September 14, 2007

**NOTICE OF MINISTERIAL APPROVAL OF**

***OSC RULE 24-501***

***DESIGNATION AS MARKET PARTICIPANT***

On August 30, 2007, the Minister of Government Services approved OSC Rule 24-501 *Designation as Market Participant* as a rule under the *Securities Act* (Ontario) (OSC Rule 24-501). OSC Rule 24-501 was published for comment on January 12, 2007. The Ontario Securities Commission made OSC Rule 24-501 on June 12, 2007.

OSC Rule 24-501 will come into force on **October 1, 2007**.

OSC Rule 24-501 is published in Chapter 5 of this Bulletin and at http://www.osc.gov.on.ca/Regulation/Rulemaking/Current/rrn\_part2\_index.jsp. No changes have been made to OSC Rule 24-501 since publication in the Bulletin on July 13, 2007.

September 14, 2007
