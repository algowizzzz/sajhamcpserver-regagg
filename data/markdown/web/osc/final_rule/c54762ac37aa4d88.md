### AMENDMENT TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 13-502 FEES

  
  

***1. Ontario Securities Commission Rule 13-502 Fees is amended by this Instrument.***

***2. Row E5 in Column A of Appendix C is amended by***

> ***(a) replacing "***; and***" with "***,***", and***
>
> (***b) adding "***, and***" at the end of paragraph (b) and by adding the following paragraph:***
>
> > ***"***(c) under subsections 144(1) and 127(4.3) of the Act to revoke a cease trade order made under subsection 127(4.1) of the Act that has been in effect for 90 days or less.***".***

3. This Instrument comes into force on December 15, 2015.
