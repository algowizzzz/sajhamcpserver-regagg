# Notice of Ministerial Approval - Ontario Amendment Instrument: NI - 44-101 - Short Form Prospectus Distributions

Notice of Ministerial Approval
National Instrument

## September 1, 2006

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20060901_44-101_nma-short-prospectus.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE** **OF MINISTERIAL APPROVAL**

**OF**

**ONTARIO AMENDMENT INSTRUMENT**

**AMENDING NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 44-101**

***SHORT FORM PROSPECTUS DISTRIBUTIONS***

**The document listed below is being published in today's Bulletin.**

On **August 22, 2006**, the Minister of Government Services approved, pursuant to section 143.3 of the *Securities Act* (Ontario), the Ontario Amendment Instrument Amending National Instrument 44-101 *Short Form Prospectus Distributions* (the **Amendment Instrument**).

The Amendment Instrument was previously approved by the Commission on **June 29, 2006**.

The Amendment Instrument was previously published in the Bulletin on **July 14, 2006**. The Amendment Instrument will come into force in Ontario on **September 6, 2006**.

The text of the Amendment Instrument can be found in Chapter 5 of today's Bulletin.

September 1, 2006
