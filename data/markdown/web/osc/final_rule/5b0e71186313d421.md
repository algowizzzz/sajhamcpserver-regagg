### AMENDMENTS TO NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 81-107 *INDEPENDENT REVIEW COMMITTEE FOR INVESTMENT FUNDS*

***1.             National Instrument 81-107 Independent Review Committee for Investment Funds is amended by this*** ***Instrument.***

***2.             Subsection 5.2(1) is amended***

> > ***(a)           in paragraph (b) by deleting “***or***”,***
> >
> > ***(b)           in paragraph (c) replacing “***.***” with “***; or***”, and***
> >
> > ***(c)           by adding the following paragraph:***
> >
> > > (d)           a transaction in which an investment fund intends to borrow cash from a person or company that is an associate or affiliate of the investment fund manager.***.***

3.             This Instrument comes into force on January 3, 2019.
