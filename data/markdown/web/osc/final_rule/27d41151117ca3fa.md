# Correction to Date for Comment Period: NI - 31-101 - National Registration System

CSA Notice
Notice of Correction

## February 6, 2004

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20040206_31-101-31-102-correctdate-req-com.pdf "Open document in new window")

**CORRECTION TO DATE FOR COMMENT PERIOD FOR REQUEST FOR  
COMMENT ON PROPOSED NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 31-101 --  
REQUIREMENTS UNDER THE NATIONAL REGISTRATION SYSTEM  
AND PROPOSED NATIONAL POLICY 31-201 -- NATIONAL REGISTRATION SYSTEM**

A Request for Comment on Proposed National Instrument 31-101 -- Requirements under the National Registration System and proposed National Policy 31-201 -- National Registration System was published in the OSC Bulletin on January 9, 2004 (27 OSCB 618). It incorrectly stated that the deadline for submitting comments is March 30, 2004. The correct deadline for submitting comments is April 8, 2004.
