# Amendments to National Instrument 81-106 Investment Fund Continuous Disclosure

Amendments to National Instrument

## February 16, 2017

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/ni_20170216_81-106_continuous-disclosure.pdf "Open document in new window")

### AMENDMENTS TO NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 81-106 INVESTMENT FUND CONTINUOUS DISCLOSURE

***1. National Instrument 81-106 Investment Fund Continuous Disclosure is amended by this Instrument.***

***2. Section 11.2 is amended by replacing paragraph (1)(d) with the following:***

> (d) file an amendment to its prospectus, simplified prospectus, fund facts document or ETF facts document that discloses the material change in accordance with the requirements of securities legislation.***.***

3. This Instrument comes into force on March 8, 2017.
