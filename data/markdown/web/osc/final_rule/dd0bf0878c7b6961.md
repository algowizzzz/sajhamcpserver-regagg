# Notice of Ministerial Approval: OSC Rule 91-506 Derivatives: Product Determination and OSC Rule 91-507 Trade Repositories and Derivatives Data Reporting

Notice of Ministerial Approval
OSC Rule

## January 3, 2014

### NOTICE OF MINISTERIAL APPROVAL OF ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 91-506 DERIVATIVES: PRODUCT DETERMINATION AND ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 91-507 TRADE REPOSITORIES AND DERIVATIVES DATA REPORTING

  
  

**January 3, 2014**

Ontario Securities Commission Rule 91-506 *Derivatives: Product Determination* and Ontario Securities Commission Rule 91-507 *Trade Repositories and Derivatives Data Reporting* have received Ministerial approval pursuant to section 143.3(3)(a) of the *Securities Act* (Ontario).

The Rules were published in the Bulletin on November 14, 2013. [See (2013) 36 OSCB 11015](/en/SecuritiesLaw_rule_20131114_91-506_91-507_derivatives.htm "OSC Rule 91-506: Derivatives: Product Determination, Companion Policy 91-506CP, OSC Rule 91-507 Trade Repositories and Derivatives Data Reporting, , Companion Policy 91-507CP"). The Rules are effective December 31, 2013. The text of the Rules is reproduced in Chapter 5 of this Bulletin.
