# Notice of Request for Comment: OSC Notice- 15-901 - Proposed Procedures for Opportunities to be Heard Before Director's Decisions on Registration Matters

Request for Comment
OSC Notice

## January 13, 2006

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20060113_15-901_not-dec-registration.pdf "Open document in new window")

**REQUEST FOR COMMENT**

**PROPOSED PROCEDURES**

**FOR OPPORTUNITIES TO BE HEARD BEFORE**

**DIRECTOR'S DECISIONS ON**

**REGISTRATION MATTERS**

made under the *Statutory Powers Procedure Act*

Staff of the Ontario Securities Commission is publishing in today's Bulletin a request for comment on proposed procedures for the exercise of opportunities to be heard before the Director that are held at the request of an individual or firm that would be affected by a Director's decision denying or restricting registration as contemplated by subsection 26(3) of the *Securities Act*.
