### OSC STAFF NOTICE 11-739 (REVISED) POLICY REFORMULATION TABLE OF CONCORDANCE AND LIST OF NEW INSTRUMENTS

  
  

**April 9, 2015**

The following revisions have been made to the Table of Concordance and List of New Instruments. A full version of the Table of Concordance and List of New Instruments as of March 31, 2015 has been posted to the OSC Website at www.osc.gov.on.ca.
