# Notice of Commission Approval: OSC Rule - 13-503 - (Commodity Futures Act) Fees

Notice of Commission Approval
OSC Rule

## September 19, 2003

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20030919_13-503_c1-commapprov.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE OF COMMISSION APPROVAL OF  
ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 13-503 (*COMMODITY FUTURES ACT*)  
FORMS 13-503F1 AND 13-503F2 AND  
COMPANION POLICY 13-503CP  
FEES**

**AND**

**NOTICE OF REVOCATION OF SCHEDULE 1 OF REGULATION 90  
MADE UNDER THE *COMMODITY FUTURES ACT***

On September 16, 2003, the Commission made Rule 13-503 (*Commodity Futures Act*) Fees and Forms 13-503F1 and 13-503F2 as a rule (the Rule) under the *Commodity Futures Act* (CFA) and adopted Companion Policy 13-503CP (the Companion Policy) as a policy under the Act. The Rule and Companion Policy were published for comment on May 16, 2003 at (2003) 26 OSCB 3712.

Concurrently with making the Rule, the Commission has revoked Schedule 1 to Regulation 90 made under the CFA.

The Rule and the amending regulation were delivered to the Minister of Finance on September 17, 2003 and are published in Chapter 5 of the Bulletin.
