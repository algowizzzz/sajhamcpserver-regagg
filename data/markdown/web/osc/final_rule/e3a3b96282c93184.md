# Notice - Over-allotment Options and Bought Deals - Exemptive Relief No Longer Necessary: CSA Staff Notice - 47-302 - Pre-Marketing of Underwriters' Options on Bought Deals

CSA Notice

## September 5, 2006

On May 24, 2006, we posted a notice relating to certain requests for relief for any pre-marketing associated with securities underlying post-closing over allotment options. Effective September 6, 2006, such relief should no longer be necessary because of the coming into force of the Ontario Amendment Instrument Amending National Instrument [44-101](/en/securities-law/instruments-rules-policies/4/44-101) *Short Form Prospectus Distributions*.

Please refer to the Notice of Ministerial Approval published in Chapter 1 of the Bulletin on September 1, 2006 (29 OSCB 7085) and the Notice of Commission Approval published in Chapter 5 of the Bulletin on July 14, 2006 (29 OSCB 5731).
