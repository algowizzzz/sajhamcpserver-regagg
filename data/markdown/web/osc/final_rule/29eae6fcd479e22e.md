## May 5, 2016

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20160505_48-501-trading-during-distributions.pdf "Open document in new window")

### AMENDMENTS TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 48-501 TRADING DURING DISTRIBUTIONS, FORMAL BIDS AND SHARE EXCHANGE TRANSACTIONS

  
  

***1. Ontario Securities Commission Rule 48-501 Trading During Distributions, Formal Bids and Share Exchange Transactions is amended by this Instrument.***

***2. Paragraph 3.2(c) is amended by replacing "***clauses 93(3)(a) through (d) of the Act***" with "***sections 4.6 and 4.7 of National Instrument 62-104 *Take-Over Bids and Issuer Bids**".***

3. This Instrument comes into force on the later of the following:

> (a) May 9, 2016;
>
> (b) the day on which sections 1, 2 and 3, subsections 4 (2) and (3), and sections 5, 7, 8 and 10 of Schedule 18 of the *Budget Measures Act, 2015* (Ontario) are proclaimed into force.
