# Part 2 General Duties

## General Duties

2.1 (1)    A registered dealer or adviser shall deal fairly, honestly and in good faith with its clients.

(2)          A representative of a registered dealer or registered adviser shall deal fairly, honestly and in good faith with his or her clients.
