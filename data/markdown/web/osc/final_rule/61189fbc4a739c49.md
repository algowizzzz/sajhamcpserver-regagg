### AMENDMENTS TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 13-502 FEES

  
  

***1. Ontario Securities Commission Rule 13-502 Fees is amended by this Instrument.***

***2. The table at Appendix C is amended in part B. Fees relating to exempt distributions under OSC Rule 45-501 Ontario Prospectus and Registration Exemptions and NI 45-106***

> ***(a) by replacing***
>
> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | B2 | Filing of a Form 45-501F1 or Form 45-106F1 for a distribution of securities of an issuer | $500 |
>
> ***with***
>
> |  |  |  |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | B2 | Filing of a Form 45-501F1 or Form 45-106F1 for a distribution of securities of an issuer under an exemption from the prospectus requirement other than section 2.9 [*Offering memorandum*] of NI 45-106 | $500 |
> |  | | |
> | B2.1 | Filing of a Form 45-106F1 for a distribution of securities of an issuer under section 2.9 [*Offering memorandum*] of NI 45-106 | Greater of (i) $500 or (ii) 0.025% of the gross proceeds realized by the issuer from the distribution in Ontario |

3. This Instrument comes into force on January 13, 2016.
