### Ontario Securities Commission Policy Hearing on Proposed Enforcement Initiatives OSC Staff Notice 15-704

  
  

The Ontario Securities Commission will hold a public policy hearing on **June 17, 2013**, commencing at 10:00 a.m. at the Offices of the Commission, 20 Queen Street West, 22nd Floor, Toronto, Ontario, to receive submissions from parties who submitted written comments and requested to make oral submissions in response to OSC Staff Notice 15-704 Request for Comments on Proposed Enforcement Initiatives.

The following is the schedule of oral submissions for the policy hearing. The time allocated to each presentation includes the time required to respond to any questions from the panel of Commissioners:

  

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 am – 10:05 am |  | Opening remarks, Vice-Chair Condon |
| 10:05 am – 10:25 am |  | T. Atkinson, Director OSC Enforcement Branch, |
| 10:25 am – 10:45 am |  | E. Pascutto, Canadian Foundation for the Advancement of Investor Rights (FAIR) |
| 10:45 am – 11:05 am |  | J. Douglas, Borden Ladner Gervais LLP (on behalf of: W. Berman, N. Campbell, L. Fuerst, A. Gray, J. Groia, D. Hausman, J. Kimmel, J. Leon, P. LeVay, K. Richard, P. Steep and J. Wiesenfeld) |
| 11:05 am – 11:20 am |  | Break |
| 11:20 am – 11:40 am |  | H. G. Emerson, Emerson Advisory |
| 11:40 am – 12:00 pm |  | N. Clarke and R. Sorrell, Canadian Bankers Association |
| 12:00 pm – 1:30 pm |  | Lunch Break |

In connection with the policy hearing, the Commission has today issued *OSC Staff Notice 15-706 Update to OSC Staff Notice 15-704 on Proposed Enforcement Initiatives*. A copy of *[OSC Staff Notices 15-704](/en/SecuritiesLaw_sn_20111021_15-704_rfc-enforcement-initiatives.htm "OSC Staff Notice: 15-704 - Request for Comments on Proposed Enforcement Initiatives")* and *[15-706](/en/SecuritiesLaw_sn_20130605_15-706_update-sn15-704.htm "OSC Staff Notice: 15-706 - Update to OSC Staff Notice 15-704 on Proposed Enforcement Initiatives")* and the [comment letters](/en/33789.htm "Comment Letters for OSC Staff Notice 15-704 - Request for Comments on Proposed Enforcement Initiatives") submitted in response to *OSC Staff Notice 15-704* are available at [www.osc.gov.on.ca](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca "Ontario Securities Commission website"). Participants in the policy hearing are invited to review *OSC Staff Notice 15-706* in advance of the hearing.
