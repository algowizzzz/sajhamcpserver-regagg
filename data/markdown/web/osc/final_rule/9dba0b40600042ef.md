# CSA: Request Underway to Investment Fund Managers for Mutual Fund Fees Data

Concept Proposal

## November 10, 2014

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/csa_20141110_81-407-csa-cover-letter-mutual-fund.pdf "Open document in new window")

### REQUEST UNDERWAY TO INVESTMENT FUND MANAGERS FOR MUTUAL FUND FEES DATA

Working on behalf of the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA), independent third party research is now underway to assess the impact of commissions and embedded fees on mutual fund flows. Requests have been sent to all Investment Fund Managers offering public mutual funds across Canada for data, which will inform the CSA's examination of the current mutual fund fee structure.

A copy of the [CSA Letter to Investment Fund Managers](/documents/en/Securities-Category8/csa_20141110_81-407-csa-cover-letter-mutual-fund.pdf "CSA Letter to Investment Fund Managers") and [Mutual Fund Fees Research Data Request](https://www.osc.ca/sites/default/files/2021-12/csa_20141110_81-407-csa-data-request.pdf "Mutual Fund Fees Research Data Request") is published on our website <http://www.osc.gov.on.ca>.

The researcher signed a [Confidentiality Undertaking](https://www.osc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/csa_20150205_81-407_cummingd.pdf "Confidentiality Undertaking of Douglas Cumming") which was delivered with a [CSA Letter to Investment Fund Managers](https://www.osc.ca/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/csa_20150205_81-407_cover-letter.pdf "CSA cover letter for confidentiality undertaking").
