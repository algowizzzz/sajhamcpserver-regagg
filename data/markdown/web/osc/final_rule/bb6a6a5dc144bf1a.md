# 55-701 - Automatic Securities Disposition Plans and Automatic Securities Purchase Plans

As of December 10, 2020, the content in this notice relating to automatic securities disposition plans is superseded by CSA Staff Notice 55-317 Automatic Securities Disposition Plans. This notice otherwise continues to be relevant for issuers and insiders that establish automatic securities purchase plans under Ontario securities law.
