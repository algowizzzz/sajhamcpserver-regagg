# Notice: 13-501 - Payment of Fees

OSC Rule

## November 21, 1997

**ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 13-501**  
**PAYMENT OF FEES**

1.1 Fees - Fees payable under Ontario securities law shall be paid to the "Ontario Securities Commission".
