# Notice of Amendment: OSC Rule - 41-502 - Prospectus Requirements for Mutual Funds (Repealed by 41-801 on March 17, 2008)

Notice of Commission Approval
OSC Rule

## September 20, 2002

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20020920_41-502_noa.pdf "Open document in new window")

**AMENDMENT TO**  
**ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 41-502**   
**PROSPECTUS REQUIREMENTS FOR MUTUAL FUNDS**

**PART 1 AMENDMENTS**

> **1.1 Amendments**
>
> Rule 41-502 Prospectus Requirements for Mutual Funds is amended by deleting Part 10.

**PART 2 EFFECTIVE DATE**

> **2.1 Effective Date**
>
> This Amendment comes into force on the date that National Instrument 81-106 Investment Fund Continuous Disclosure comes into force.
