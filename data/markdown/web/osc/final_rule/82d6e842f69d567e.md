### REPEAL OF ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 62-504 TAKE-OVER BIDS AND ISSUER BIDS

  
  

***1. Ontario Securities Commission Rule 62-504 Take-Over Bids and Issuer Bids is repealed.***

2. This Instrument comes into force on the later of the following:

> (a) May 9, 2016;
>
> (b) the day on which sections 1, 2 and 3, subsections 4 (2) and (3), and sections 5, 7, 8 and 10 of Schedule 18 of the *Budget Measures Act, 2015* (Ontario) are proclaimed into force.
