# Staff Notice: OSC Staff Notice - 33-701 - Calculation of Regulatory Capital

OSC Notice

## June 27, 1997

**ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION STAFF NOTICE 33-701**   
**CALCULATION OF REGULATORY CAPITAL**[(1)](#N_1_)

Registrants required to maintain specified levels of working capital are reminded that they arenot permitted under generally accepted accounting principles to include deposits withcontingency funds as part of working capital because amounts deposited with contingencyfunds are not available in the normal course of business to satisfy requirements for operatingfunds.

June 27, 1997

1.This Notice replaces a staff notice published on September 9, 1988.
