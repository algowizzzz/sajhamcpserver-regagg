# Notice of Ministerial Approval: NI - 52-109 - Certification of Disclosure in Issuers' Annual and Interim Filings

Notice of Ministerial Approval
National Instrument

## June 3, 2005

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20050603_52-109_not-amend-cp.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE OF MINISTERIAL APPROVAL**

**AMENDMENTS TO**

**MULTILATERAL INSTRUMENT 52-109**

**CERTIFICATION OF DISCLOSURE IN ISSUERS' ANNUAL AND INTERIM FILINGS**

**AND**

**COMPANION POLICY 52-109CP**

On May 2, 2005, the Chair of the Management Board of Cabinet approved, pursuant to subsection 143.3(3) of the *Securities Act* (Ontario) (the **Act**), an amendment instrument amending Multilateral Instrument 52-109 *Certification of Disclosure in Issuers' Annual and Interim Filings* (the **Amendment Instrument**) as a rule under the Act.

The Amendment Instrument, together with amendments to Companion Policy 52-109CP (the **CP Amendments**), were previously published in the Bulletin on April 1, 2005. The Amendment Instrument and the CP Amendments are published in Chapter 5 of this Bulletin.

The Amendment Instrument and the CP Amendments will come into force in Ontario on June 6, 2005.
