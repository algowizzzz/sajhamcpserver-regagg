# CSA Staff Notice 11-305 Withdrawal of CSA Staff Notices 42-301 and 52-302

CSA Notice

## January 16, 2004

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/csa_20040116_11-305_withdraw-csans.pdf "Open document in new window")

Staff of the members of the CSA has determined that the following Notices are no longer required and therefore will be withdrawn in all CSA jurisdictions, effective March 30, 2004. Dual reporting issuers should refer to National Instrument 52-107 *Acceptable Accounting Principles, Auditing Standards and Reporting Currency.*

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| CSAN 42-301 | Dual Reporting of Financial Information |
| CSAN 52-302 | Dual Reporting of Financial Information |
