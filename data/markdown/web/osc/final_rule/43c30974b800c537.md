**ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION STAFF NOTICE 11-729**

**WITHDRAWAL OF STAFF NOTICE**

Commission staff has reviewed OSC Notice 57-701 -- *Revocation of Cease Trade Orders* (26 OSCB 2319 and 18 OSCB 5) and determined that it is no longer required. Accordingly, the notice is withdrawn effective immediately.
