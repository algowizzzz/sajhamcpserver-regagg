### OSC Staff Notice 11-739 (Revised) – Policy Reformulation Table of Concordance and List of New Instruments

  
  

The following revisions have been made to the Table of Concordance and List of New Instruments. A full version of the Table of Concordance and List of New Instruments as of September 30, 2015 has been posted to the OSC Website at www.osc.gov.on.ca.

**Item Key**
