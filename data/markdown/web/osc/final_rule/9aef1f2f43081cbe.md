# Form 45-108F2 *Risk Acknowledgement*

# 

***Instructions:*** *This form must be completed by the purchaser before the purchaser enters into an agreement to purchase securities under the exemption in Multilateral Instrument 45-108 Crowdfunding.*

Issuer name:       i.e., ABC Company

Type of security offered: i.e., common share
