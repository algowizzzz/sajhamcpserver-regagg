# Withdrawal of Staff Notice: 11-719 - A Risk-based Approach for More Effective Regulation

OSC Notice
Notice of Withdrawal

## December 12, 2008

**ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION STAFF NOTICE 11-719**

**WITHDRAWAL OF STAFF NOTICE**

Commission staff has reviewed OSC Staff Notice 11-719 -- *A Risk-based Approach for More Effective Regulation* (25 O.S.C.B. 2410) and has determined that it does not reflect current practice. Accordingly, the notice is withdrawn effective immediately.
