# Notice of Proposed Amendments: NI - 54-101 - Communication with Beneficial Owners of Securities of a Reporting Issuer

Request for Comment
National Instrument

## October 3, 2003

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20031003_54-101_c1-pro-amend.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE OF PROPOSED AMENDMENTS TO**

**NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 54-101**

**COMMUNICATION WITH BENEFICIAL OWNERS OF SECURITIES**

**OF A REPORTING ISSUER**

**AND**

**COMPANION POLICY 54-101CP**

The Commission is publishing in today's Bulletin a Notice requesting comment on proposed amendments (the Proposed Amendments) to National Instrument 54-101 *Communication with Beneficial Owners of Securities of a Reporting Issuer* and Companion Policy 54-101CP. The Notice and the Proposed Amendments are published in Chapter 6 of the Bulletin.
