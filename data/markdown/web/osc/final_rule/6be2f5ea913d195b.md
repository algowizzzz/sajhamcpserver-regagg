# Amendments to OSC Rule 91-502 Trades in Recognized Options

Amendments to OSC Rule

## May 5, 2016

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20160505_91-502_amd-recognized-options.pdf "Open document in new window")

### AMENDMENTS TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 91-502 TRADES IN RECOGNIZED OPTIONS

  
  

***1. Ontario Securities Commission Rule 91-502 Trades in Recognized Options is amended by this Instrument.***

***2. Section 1.1 is amended by replacing "***has the meaning ascribed to that term in subsection 89(1) of the Act***" with "***means a security of an issuer that carries a residual right to participate in the earnings of the issuer and, on liquidation or winding up of the issuer, in its assets***" in the definition of "***equity security***".***

3. This Instrument comes into force on the later of the following:

> (a) May 9, 2016;
>
> (b) the day on which sections 1, 2 and 3, subsections 4 (2) and (3), and sections 5, 7, 8 and 10 of Schedule 18 of the *Budget Measures Act, 2015* (Ontario) are proclaimed into force.
