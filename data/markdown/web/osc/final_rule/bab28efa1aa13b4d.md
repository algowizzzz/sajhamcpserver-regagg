# Amendments to NI 51-102 Continuous Disclosure Obligations Relating to the Accredited Investor and Minimum Amount Investment Prospectus Exemptions

Amendments to National Instrument

## April 30, 2015

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/ni_20150430_51-102_amendments-prospectus-exemptions.pdf "Open document in new window")

### AMENDMENTS TO NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 51-102 *CONTINUOUS DISCLOSURE OBLIGATIONS*

***1. National Instrument 51-102 Continuous Disclosure Obligations is amended by this Instrument.***

***2. Paragraphs 13.3(2)(c)(iv), 13.3(3)(e)(iv) and 13.4(2)(c)(iv) are amended by replacing "***exemptions from the prospectus requirement in section 2.35 and registration requirement in section 3.35 of National Instrument 45-106 *Prospectus and Registration Exemptions**" with "***exemption from the prospectus requirement in section 2.35 of National Instrument 45-106 *Prospectus Exemptions**".***

3. Except in Ontario, this Instrument comes into force on May 5, 2015. In Ontario, this Instrument comes into force on the later of the following:

> (a) May 5, 2015 and
>
> (b) the day on which subsection 12(2) of Schedule 26 of the *Budget Measures Act, 2009* is proclaimed in force.
