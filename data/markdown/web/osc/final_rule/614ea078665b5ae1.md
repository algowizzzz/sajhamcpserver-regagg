# Notice of Commission Approval: NP - 11-201- Delivery of Documents by Electronic Means

Notice of Commission Approval
National Policy

## February 14, 2003

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/pol_20030214_11-201_app.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE OF**

**COMMISSION APPROVAL OF AMENDMENTS TO**

**NATIONAL POLICY 11-201**

On January 7, 2003, the Commission made amendments to National Policy 11-201 Delivery of Documents by Electronic Means (the Amendments). The Amendments come into force on February 14, 2003.

The Amendments were published for comment on August 9, 2002 at (2002) 25 OSCB 5364. The Amendments are published in Chapter 5 of this Bulletin.
