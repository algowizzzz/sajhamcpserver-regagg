# Form 32-505F1 *INFORMATION REPORT FOR UNITED STATES BROKER-DEALERS AND ADVISERS SERVICING U.S. CLIENTS FROM ONTARIO*

Complete the applicable sections.

Indicate if you intend to rely on any of the following:

□ the dealer registration exemption in Part 2 of the Rule.

□ the adviser registration exemption in Part 3 of the Rule.

Indicate the jurisdiction(s) in which:

(i)           the U.S. broker-dealer has representatives that trade to, with, or on behalf of, U.S. clients, or

(ii)          the U.S. adviser has representatives who are acting as advisers to U.S. clients.
