# Changes to Companion Policy 44-103CP Post-Receipt Pricing

Amendments to National Instrument

## August 8, 2013

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/ni_20130808_44-103CP_changes-post-receipt-pricing.pdf "Open document in new window")

### CHANGES TO COMPANION POLICY 44-103CP *TO NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 44-103 POST-RECEIPT PRICING*

***1. The changes to Companion Policy 44-103CP to National Instrument 44-103 Post-Receipt Pricing are set out in this Schedule.***

***2. Part 3 is changed by adding the following after section 3.4:***

> **3.5 Marketing Activities -** Issuers and investment dealers should also refer to the guidance on marketing activities in Part 6 of the Companion Policy to NI 41-101. While the Instrument has provisions on marketing after a receipt for a final base PREP prospectus, NI 41-101 has general provisions that apply to marketing during the waiting period.**.**

***3. These changes become effective on August 13, 2013.***
