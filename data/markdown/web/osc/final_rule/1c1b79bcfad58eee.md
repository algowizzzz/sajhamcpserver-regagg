# Correction to Notice: OSC Staff Notice - 11-728 - Withdrawal of Staff Notices

OSC Notice
Notice of Correction

## May 9, 2003

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/sn_20030509_11-728_correction.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE OF CORRECTION TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION STAFF NOTICE 11-728 WITHDRAWAL OF STAFF NOTICES**

Ontario Securities Commission Notice - *Office of the Chief Accountant: Report on the Review Program* (11 OSCB 4277) was incorrectly referenced in the above notice on page 2321 in Chapter 1 of the OSC Bulletin, Volume 26, Issue 12 dated March 21, 2003. The correct reference is OSC Notice 52-710 *Office of the Chief Accountant: Report on the Review Program* (11 OSCB 4277). The notice was withdrawn on March 21, 2003.
