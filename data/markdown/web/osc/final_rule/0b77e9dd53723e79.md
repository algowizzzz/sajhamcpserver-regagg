# Notice of Minister of Finance Request for Further Consideration: OSC Rule - 56-501 - Restricted Shares

Request for Comment
OSC Rule

## July 11, 1997

**Notice of Minister of Finance Request for Further Consideration**

**Ontario Securities Commission Rule 56-501 on Restricted Shares**

  

On April 9, 1997 the Commission delivered Rule 56-501 on Restricted Shares (the "Rule") to the Minister of Finance for approval under section 143.3 of theSecurities Act. The Minister of Finance returned the Rule to the Commission for further consideration by the Commission of its jurisdiction to make a rule thatassigns voting rights to securities that are otherwise non-voting through the minority approval provisions contained in Part 3 of the Rule. The Commission ispublishing this notice in accordance with subsection 143.6(b).

July 11, 1997
