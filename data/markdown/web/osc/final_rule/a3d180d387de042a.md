### NOTICE OF MINISTERIAL APPROVAL OF AMENDMENTS TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 13-502 *FEES*

  
  

**December 3, 2015**

On November 19, 2015, the Minister of Finance approved amendments (the **Amendments**) made by the Ontario Securities Commission (**OSC** or the **Commission**) to Ontario Securities Commission Rule 13-502 *Fees*.

The Amendments were made by the Commission on September 22, 2015.

The Amendments were published on the OSC website at http://www.osc.gov.on.ca and in the OSC Bulletin at (2015) 38 OSCB 8533 on October 1, 2015.

The Amendments come into force on December 15, 2015.

The text of the Amendments is reproduced in Chapter 5 of this Bulletin.
