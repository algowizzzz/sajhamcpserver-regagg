### AMENDMENTS TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 14-501 DEFINITIONS

  
  

***1. Ontario Securities Commission Rule 14-501 Definitions is amended by this Instrument.***

***2. Subsection 1.1(2) is amended by repealing the definition of "***offeree issuer***" and "***published market***".***

3. This Instrument comes into force on the later of the following:

> (a) May 9, 2016;
>
> (b) the day on which sections 1, 2 and 3, subsections 4 (2) and (3), and sections 5, 7, 8 and 10 of Schedule 18 of the *Budget Measures Act, 2015* (Ontario) are proclaimed into force.
