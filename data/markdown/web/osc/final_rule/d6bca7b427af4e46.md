# Notice of Request for Comments: OSC Rule - 13-503 - (Commodity Futures Act) Fees

Request for Comment
OSC Rule

## May 16, 2003

**NOTICE OF REQUEST FOR COMMENTS**

**PROPOSED ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 13-503 (*COMMODITY FUTURES ACT*) FORMS 13-503F1 AND 13-503F2 AND COMPANION POLICY 13-503CP FEES**

The Commission is publishing in today's Bulletin proposed OSC Rule 13-503 (*Commodity Futures Act*), Forms 13-503F1 and 13-503F2, and Companion Policy 13-503CP - Fees for comment for 90 days.

The Notice, Rule, Forms and Companion Policy are published in Chapter 6 of the Bulletin.
