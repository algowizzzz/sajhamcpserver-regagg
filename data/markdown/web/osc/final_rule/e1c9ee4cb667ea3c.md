# Amendments to Companion Policy 13-502CP Fees

Amendments to OSC Rule

## April 2, 2010

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20100401_13-502_amd-fees-cp.pdf "Open document in new window")

### AMENDMENTS TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION COMPANION POLICY 13-502CP FEES

  
  

#### *1. Companion Policy 13-502CP Fees is amended by this Instrument.*

#### *2. Part 4 is amended by added the following:*

> **4.8 Confidentiality of Forms** - The material filed under Part 3 of the Rule will be kept confidential. The Commission is of the view that the material contains intimate financial, commercial and technical information and that the interests of the filers in non-disclosure outweigh the desirability of the principle that the material be available for public inspection.

#### *3. This Instrument becomes effective on April 5, 2010.*
