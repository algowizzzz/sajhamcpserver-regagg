# Notice of Ministerial Approval: OSC Rule - 62-503 - Financing of Take-Over Bids and Issuer Bids

Notice of Ministerial Approval
OSC Rule

## December 16, 2005

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20051216_62-503_not-fin-takeover.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE OF MINISTERIAL APPROVAL**

**RULE 62-503 -- FINANCING OF TAKE-OVER BIDS**

**AND ISSUER BIDS**

On December 7, 2005, the Minister of Government Services approved Rule 62-503 --*Financing of Take-over Bids and Issuer Bids*. The Rule will come into force on December 22, 2005.

The Rule is published in Chapter 5 of today's Bulletin. Materials relating to the Rule were previously published in the Bulletin on July 1, 2005 and October 21, 2005.
