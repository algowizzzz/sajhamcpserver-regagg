### NOTICE OF MINISTERIAL APPROVAL OF NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 23-103 ELECTRONIC TRADING

  
  

On August 16, 2012, the Minister of Finance approved National Instrument 23-103 *Electronic Trading* (the Rule) made by the Ontario Securities Commission (the Commission) on May 22, 2012.

On May 22, 2012 the Commission adopted the related Companion Policy 23-103CP (Companion Policy). The Rule and Companion Policy are published in Chapter 5 of this Bulletin and at www.osc.gov.on.ca. No changes have been made to the Rule or Companion Policy since their publication in the Bulletin on June 28, 2012.

The Rule will come into force on March 1, 2013.

**September 20, 2012**
