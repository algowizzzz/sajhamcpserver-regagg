**NOTICE OF REQUEST FOR COMMENTS**

**PROPOSED AMENDMENTS TO RULE 13-502 FEES, INCLUDING**

**FORMS 13-502F1, 13-502F2, 13-502F3 AND 13-502F4 AND**

**COMPANION POLICY 13-502CP**

**Request for Public Comment**

The Commission is publishing for a 90-day comment period the following materials in today's Bulletin:

* proposed amendments to Rule 13-502 -- *Fees* including Forms 13-502F1, 13-502F2, 13-502F3 and 13-502F4;
* proposed amendments to Companion Policy 13-502CP - *Fees*; and

These materials are intended to amend the current Rule 13-502 -- *Fees*, the current Forms 13-502F1 and F3 and the Companion Policy to current Rule 13-502, which came into effect on March 31, 2003. We request comments on the proposed amendments by **August 14, 2003**.

These materials are published in Chapter 6 of the Bulletin.
