# Notice of Correction: NI - 44-101 - Short Form Prospectus Distributions

National Instrument
Notice of Correction

## November 11, 2004

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20041112_44-101_not-cor-amend-sfpd.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE OF CORRECTION**

**FOR**

**AMENDMENTS TO NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 44-101 --**

**SHORT FORM PROSPECTUS DISTRIBUTIONS**

The Notice of Commission Approval of Amendments to National Instrument 44-101 *Short Form Prospectus Distributions*, as published in Chapter 1 (2004, 27 OSCB 8651) and Notice of Amendments to National Instrument 44-101 *Short Form Prospectus Distributions*, as published in Chapter 5 (2004, 27 OSCB 8709) of the OSC Bulletin, has been corrected to indicate that the rule was delivered to the Chair of Management Board of Cabinet ("the Minister"). The corrected versions of the Notices are being published in Chapters 1 and 5 of today's Bulletin. The amendments to the Rule and Companion Policy are also being published in Chapter 5 of today's Bulletin.
