# Notice of Correction: OSC Bulletin, Volume 37, Issue 42, Supplement 5 - Final Amendments to NI 31-103, NI 33-109 and Related Instruments, Policies and Forms

Amendments to National Instrument

## November 13, 2014

On page 34, subsection 59(2) reads:

> "Paragraph 16(d) and section 29 of this Instrument come into force on July 11, 2015."

Subsection 59(2) should read:

> "Paragraph 16(c) and section 29 of this Instrument come into force on July 11, 2015.
>
> [**Editor's Note:** "Paragraph 16(d)" has been changed to "Paragraph 16(c)". This change was approved on November 6, 2014.]"
