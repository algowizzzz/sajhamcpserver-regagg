# [Withdrawn by CSA Staff Notice 11-341, March 7, 2019] Extension of Comment Period (for 21-306): CSA Staff Notice - 21-101 - Marketplace Operation, Companion Policy, Forms 21-101F1, 21-101F2, 21-101F3 and 21-101F4

**EXTENSION OF COMMENT PERIOD**

**CSA STAFF NOTICE 21-306**

**NOTICE OF FILING OF FORMS 21-101F5 INITIAL OPERATION REPORT**

**FOR INFORMATION PROCESSOR**

On April 20, 2007, staff of the CSA published CSA Staff Notice 21-306 *Notice of Filing of Forms 21-101F5 Initial Operation Report for Information Processor* at (2007) 30 OSCB (Supp-3) at page 111. The notice summarizes the filings received from entities applying to be the information processor and the criteria to be used by the CSA in evaluating these filings.
