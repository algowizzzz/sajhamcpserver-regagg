### CHANGE TO COMPANION POLICY 81-104CP TO NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 81-104 COMMODITY POOLS

***1. The change to Companion Policy 81-104CP to National Instrument 81-104 Commodity Pools is set out in this Annex.***

***2. Section 1.1 is changed by replacing "***National Instrument 81-102 Mutual Funds***" with "***National Instrument 81-102 *Investment Funds**".***

3. This change becomes effective on September 22, 2014.
