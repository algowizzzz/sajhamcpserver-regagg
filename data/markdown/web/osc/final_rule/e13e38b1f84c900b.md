# Consequential Amendments: OSC Rule - 45-501 - Ontario Prospectus and Registration Exemptions

Amendments to OSC Rule

## April 2, 2004

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20040402_45-501-consq-amends.pdf "Open document in new window")

**CONSEQUENTIAL AMENDMENTS TO ONTARIO SECURITIES   
COMMISSION RULE 45-501 *EXEMPT DISTRIBUTIONS***

As a result of the coming into force of Multilateral Instrument 45-102 *Resale of Securities*, Form 45-102F1, and the related companion policy, Companion Policy 45-102CP to Multilateral Instrument 45-102 *Resale of Securities*, the following consequential amendments to Rule 45-501 came into force on March 30, 2004.

**PART 1 AMENDMENTS TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 45-501**

> **1.1 Amendments** - Ontario Securities Commission Rule 45-501 *Exempt Distributions* is amended by:
>
> > (a) deleting "Form 45-102F3" in section 2.4(1)(d) and replacing it with "Form 45-102F1"; and
> >
> > (b) deleting "conditions in subsection (2) or (3) of section 2.8 of MI 45-102" in sections 6.1, 7.5(3) and 8.4 and replacing it with "conditions in subsection (2) of section 2.8 of MI 45-102".

**PART 2 EFFECTIVE DATE**

**Effective Date** - These Amendments come into force on March 30, 2004.
