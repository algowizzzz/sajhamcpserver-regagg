# Notice of Correction: NI - 54-101 - Communication with Beneficial Owners of Securities of a Reporting Issuer

National Instrument
Notice of Correction

## May 3, 2002

**Notice of Correction - NI 54-101 Notice**

The Notice regarding National Instrument 54-101 Communication with Beneficial Owners of Securities of a Reporting Issuer (the "Instrument"), that was published in the April 5, 2002 Bulletin at (2002) 25 OSCB 1863 incorrectly stated that the Instrument and National Instrument 54-102 Interim Financial Statement and Report Exemption ("NI 54-102") were each sent to the Minister on April 2, 2002. The Notice should have stated that the Instrument and NI 54-102 were sent on April 3, 2002.
