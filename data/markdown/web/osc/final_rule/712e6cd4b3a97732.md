# Consequential Amendments: OSC Rule - 41-502 - Prospectus Requirements for Mutual Funds (Repealed by 41-801 on March 17, 2008)

Amendments to OSC Rule

## June 3, 2005

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20050603_41-502_pro-req-mf.pdf "Open document in new window")

**ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 41-502**

**PROSPECTUS REQUIREMENTS FOR MUTUAL FUNDS**

**AMENDMENT**

> 1. Rule 41-502 Prospectus Requirements for Mutual Funds is amended by this Amendment.
>
> 2. Subparagraph 5.2(3)(a)(ii) is amended by striking out "140" and substituting "90".
>
> 3. Section 5.3 is amended by striking out "Part IV of the Regulation" and substituting "National Instrument 81-106 Investment Fund Continuous Disclosure".
>
> 4. Part 10 is revoked.
>
> 5. This Amendment comes into force on June 1, 2005.
