**NOTICE OF AMENDMENT TO RULE UNDER**  
**THE SECURITIES ACT**  
**IN THE MATTER OF CERTAIN ADVISERS**  

**Notice of Amendment**

The Commission has, under section 143 of the *Securities Act* (the "Act"), amended therule entitled *In the Matter of Certain Advisers* (1997), 20 OSCB 1217 (the "Rule"). Theamendment extends the expiration date of the Rule from December 31, 1998 toDecember 31, 1999. This amendment, however, does not materially change the Ruleand, accordingly, under section 143.2 of the Act, the Commission has not published theamendment for comment.

The amendment and the material required by the Act to be delivered to the Minister ofFinance were delivered on October 7, 1998. If the Minister does not approve theamendment, reject the amendment or return it to the Commission for furtherconsideration, the amendment will come into force on December 21, 1998. If theMinister does approve the amendment, the amendment will come into force 15 daysafter it is approved.

**Substance and Purpose of Amendment**

The Rule replaced the deemed rule entitled *In the Matter of Certain Advisers* (1992),15 OSCB 1955.

The Rule provides that it will expire on the earlier of the date on which a new ruleintended to replace it comes into force and December 31, 1998. Proposed Rule 35-502International Advisers, which is intended to replace the Rule, will be published forcomment shortly. The purpose of the amendment is to extend the expiration date fromDecember 31, 1998 to December 31, 1999 in order to permit the statutorily-requiredpublication for comment of proposed Rule 35-502, and the subsequent consideration ofthose comments by the Commission and its staff and the review of proposed Rule 35-502 by the Minister.

**Text of Amendment**

The text of the amendment follows.

**AMENDMENT TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE**

**IN THE MATTER OF CERTAIN ADVISERS**

**1.1 Amendment** - The Rule entitled *In the Matter of Certain Advisers* is amended bydeleting "December 31, 1998" in the last sentence and replacing it with"December 31, 1999."
