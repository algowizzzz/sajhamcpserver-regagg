### Notice of Correction Notice of Ministerial Approval of Multilateral Instrument 32-102 *Registration Exemption for Non-Resident Investment Fund Managers*

  
  

In the Notice of Ministerial Approval of Multilateral Instrument 32-102 *Registration Exemption for Non-Resident Investment Fund Managers*, published on September 13, 2012, at (2012), 35 OSCB 8388, the word “on” was inadvertently omitted from the following sentence.

The sentence read:

The Rule was made by the Commission June 19, 2012 and was published in Chapter 5 of the Bulletin on July 5, 2012.

The sentence should have read:

The Rule was made by the Commission on June 19, 2012 and was published in Chapter 5 of the Bulletin on July 5, 2012.
