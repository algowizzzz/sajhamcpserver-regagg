# Notice of Commission Approval: Notice - 13-501 - Payment of Fees

Notice of Commission Approval
OSC Rule

## March 10, 1998

**NOTICE OF COMMISSION APPROVAL OF**  
**RULE UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT**  
**Rule 13-501**  
**Payment Of Fees**

On March 10, 1998, the Commission made Rule 13-501 regarding the payment of fees to the Ontario Securities Commission. The Rule and its accompanyingNotice is published in Chapter 5 of today's Bulletin.
