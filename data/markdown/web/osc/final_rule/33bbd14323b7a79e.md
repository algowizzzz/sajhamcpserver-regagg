# Notice: OSC Rule - 44-801 - Implementing National Instrument 44-101 - Short Form Prospectus Distributions

Implementing Instrument

## April 13, 2001

**NOTICE OF MINISTER OF FINANCE   
APPROVAL OF RULE 44-801   
IMPLEMENTING NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 44-101   
SHORT FORM PROSPECTUS DISTRIBUTIONS**

On April 6, 2001, the Minister of Finance approved Rule 44-801 Implementing National Instrument 44-101 Short Form Prospectus Distributions (the "**Rule**"). Materials relating to the Rule were published in the Bulletin on February 9, 2001. **The Rule will come into force on April 21, 2001.**

The final Rule is published in chapter 5 of this Bulletin.
