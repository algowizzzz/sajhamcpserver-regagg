**NOTICE OF AMENDMENT TO RULE UNDER**  
**THE SECURITIES ACT**  
**IN THE MATTER OF THE LIMITATIONS ON A REGISTRANT UNDERWRITING**  
**SECURITIES OF A RELATED ISSUER OR CONNECTED ISSUER**  
**OF THE REGISTRANT**

**Notice of Amendment**

The Commission has, under section 143 of the *Securities Act* (the "Act"), amended the ruleentitled *In the Matter of the Limitations on a Registrant Underwriting Securities of a RelatedIssuer or Connected Issuer of the Registrant* (1997), 20 OSCB 1217 (the "Rule"). Theamendment extends the expiration date of the Rule from December 31, 1998 to December31, 1999. This amendment, however, does not materially change the Rule and,accordingly, under section 143.2 of the Act, the Commission has not published theamendment for comment.

The amendment and the material required by the Act to be delivered to the Minister ofFinance were delivered on October 7, 1998. If the Minister does not approve theamendment, reject the amendment or return it to the Commission for further consideration,the amendment will come into force on December 21, 1998. If the Minister does approvethe amendment, the amendment will come into force 15 days after it is approved.

**Substance and Purpose of Amendment**

The Rule replaced the deemed rule entitled *In the Matter of the Limitations on a RegistrantUnderwriting Securities of a Related Issuer or Connected Issuer of the Registrant* (1992),15 OSCB 3645.

The Rule provides that it will expire on the earlier of the date on which a new rule intendedto replace it comes into force and December 31, 1998. Proposed Multilateral Instrument33-105 Underwriting Conflicts, which is intended to replace the Rule, was published forcomment on February 6, 1998 at (1998), 21 OSCB 781 and the comment period expiredMay 8, 1998. The purpose of the amendment is to extend the expiration date fromDecember 31, 1998 to December 31, 1999 in order to permit the consideration ofcomments received on the proposed Multilateral Instrument by the Commission and itsstaff and the review of the proposed Multilateral Instrument by the Minister.
