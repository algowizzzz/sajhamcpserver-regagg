# Notice of Minister of Finance Approval of Amendments: OSC Rule - 31-501 - Registrant Relationships

Notice of Ministerial Approval
OSC Rule

## February 14, 2003

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20030214_31-501_31-504_35-502_app.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE OF MINISTER OF FINANCE APPROVAL OF   
AMENDMENTS TO OSC RULE 31-501 - REGISTRANT RELATIONSHIPS,   
OSC RULE 31-504 - APPLICATIONS FOR REGISTRATION   
AND OSC RULE 35-502 - NON-RESIDENT ADVISERS**

On January 10th, 2003 the Minister of Finance approved amendments to OSC Rule 31-501: Registrant Relationships; OSC Rule 31-504: Applications for Registration; and OSC Rule 35-502: Non-resident Advisers. The amendments were published in November 2002 and made by the Commission on November 26th, 2002.

The amendments will come into force on **March 31, 2003**.

The amendments are published in Chapter 5 of the Bulletin and at [http://www.osc.gov.on.ca/en/HotTopics/nrd.html#expanded](../../../../Dealers/NRD/nrd_index.jsp). The amendments will be published in the Gazette on February 15th, 2003.
