# Toronto Stock Exchange: Notice of Revocation of Deemed Rule Made Under the Securities Act

Pre-Reformulation

## July 4, 1997

**NOTICE OF REVOCATION OF DEEMED RULE MADE UNDER THE SECURITIES ACT - IN THE MATTER OF THE TORONTO STOCK EXCHANGE**

The Commission allowed the deemed rule In the Matter of the Toronto Stock Exchange (1990), 13 OSCB 3007 to expire on March 1, 1997. A notice relating to the expiry of the deemed rules was published in the November 1, 1996 edition of the OSC Bulletin at (1996) 19 OSCB 5952.

No comments were received.
