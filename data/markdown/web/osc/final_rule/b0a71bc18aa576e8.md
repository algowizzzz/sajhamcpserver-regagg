# Notice of Ministerial Approval of Amendments to OSC Rule 13-503 (Commodity Futures Act) Fees

Notice of Ministerial Approval
OSC Rule

## October 18, 2019

### MINISTERIAL APPROVAL OF AMENDMENTS TO ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 13-503 (COMMODITY FUTURES ACT) FEES

  
  
On October 3, 2019, the Minister of Finance approved the amendments referred to in the [September 12, 2019 Notice of Amendments](/en/SecuritiesLaw_rule_20190912_13-502_13-503_amendments-fees.htm) to Ontario Securities Commission Rule 13-502 *Fees* and OSC Rule 13-503 (*Commodity Futures Act*)*Fees*. The amendments are consequently in force on October 18, 2019.  A formal notice of Ministerial approval will be published in the October 24, 2019 Bulletin.
