### AMENDMENTS TO NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 52-109 CERTIFICATION OF DISCLOSURE IN ISSUERS' ANNUAL AND INTERIM FILINGS

  
  

***1. National Instrument 52-109 Certification of Disclosure in Issuers' Annual and Interim Filings is amended by this Instrument.***

***2. Section 1.1 is amended in the definition of "***venture issuer***" by adding "***Aequitas NEO Exchange Inc.,***" after "***Toronto Stock Exchange, ***".***

3. This Instrument comes into force on November 17, 2015.
