**ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION NOTICE 11-727**

**ASSIGNMENT OF NOTICE NUMBERS**

As part of the Policy Reformulation Project, staff has determined that the following notices are to be reclassified according to the numbering system adopted by the Canadian Securities Administrators. An explanation of that numbering system can be found at 19 OSCB 4258. In some instances, staff has determined that minor changes to the notices are needed so that the notices conform to existing statutory references. The assignment of these new numbers is effective immediately.
