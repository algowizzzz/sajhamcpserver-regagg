# Notice of Ministerial Approval: NI - 14-101 - National Definitions

Notice of Ministerial Approval
National Instrument

## December 20, 2002

[Download document](/sites/default/files/pdfs/irps/rule_20021220_14-101_notice_approval.pdf "Open document in new window")

**NOTICE OF MINISTERIAL APPROVAL**

**NATIONAL INSTRUMENT 14-101 DEFINITIONS - AMENDMENTS**

On December 5, 2002, the Ontario Minister of Finance approved amendments to National Instrument 14-101 *Definitions*. In accordance with section 143 of the *Securities Act*, the amendments to National Instrument 14-101 *Definitions* are published in Chapter 5 of the Bulletin. The amendments will come into force on December 31, 2002.
