**NOTICE OF COMMISSION APPROVAL**

**RULE 62-503 -- FINANCING OF TAKE-OVER BIDS AND ISSUER BIDS**

The Commission is publishing Rule 62-503 -- *Financing of Take-over Bids and Issuer Bids* in today's Bulletin. The Rule clarifies the bid financing requirement that is set out in section 96 of the *Securities Act* by confirming the extent to which conditionality in a bid financing arrangement is acceptable.

The Rule was sent to the Minister of Government Services on October 20, 2005. It is published in Chapter 5 of this Bulletin.
