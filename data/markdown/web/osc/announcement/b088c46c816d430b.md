Barry Landen Receives Jail Sentence and Fine Related to Illegal Insider Trading

  
  
**TORONTO** - Justice Rebecca Shamai of the Ontario Court of Justice today sentenced Barry Landen in connection with insider trading contrary to subsections 76(1) and 122(1)(c) of the *Securities Act*.  
  
Justice Shamai sentenced Mr. Landen to a 45-day jail term and ordered him to pay a fine of $200,000.  
  
On October 24, 2008, Justice Shamai pronounced Mr. Landen guilty of insider trading in securities of Agnico-Eagle Mines Ltd. Justice Shamai found that Mr. Landen, who was a senior officer of Agnico-Eagle in October 2003, traded in securities while in possession of material undisclosed information, contrary to the *Securities Act*.  
  
[Appendix A to the Information respecting charges against Barry Landen](/documents/en/Proceedings-OTH/app_20081024_landenb.pdf "Appendix A to the Information") is available on the OSC website at [www.osc.gov.on.ca](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca)
