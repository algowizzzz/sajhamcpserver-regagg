[OSC in the Community](/en/news-events/events?type%5B801%5D=801)

Greenway Retirement Home, 100 Ken Whillans Dr., Brampton

Event Details

OSC staff will be speaking to members of CARP Brampton Chapter. The Protect Your Money presentation will help club members learn how to recognize frauds and scams, what to do if you suspect a scam and where to find unbiased information.
