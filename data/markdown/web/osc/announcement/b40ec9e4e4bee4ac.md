# Women in Commercial Litigation: Bringing Men into the Conversation

[Industry event](/en/news-events/events?type%5B806%5D=806)
Past Event

Apr 3, 2018 

08:00 am
 - 
09:30 am

The Advocates’ Society - 2700 - 250 Yonge Street, Toronto

Event Details

Join the Commercial Litigation Practice Group for this annual breakfast program. We welcome all commercial litigators to take this opportunity to hear the perspectives of our panelists as they discuss how men can be part of the conversation on women in commercial litigation.

**Panelists:**  
The Honourable Fred Myers, Ontario Superior Court of JusticeM  
Bindu Dhaliwal, BMO Bank of Montreal  
Tim Moseley, Vice Chair, Ontario Securities Commission  
Linda M. Plumpton, Torys LLP  
  
**Moderators:**  
Robert B. Macdonald, Fogler, Rubinoff LLP  
Shara Roy, Lenczner Slaght LLP
