# OSC Charges Michael Cowpland of Corel Corporation With Violations of Ontario Securities Act

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## October 14, 1999

## For Immediate Release

**Toronto** - The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) announced today that an Information has been laid in the Ontario Court of Justice against Michael Cowpland, President and CEO of Corel Corporation, charging Cowpland with three counts of violating Ontario securities law.

Cowpland's personal holding company, M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc., is also charged with one count of violating the Ontario *Securities Act*.

One count in the Information alleges that in mid-August, 1997, Cowpland informed M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc. of a material fact with respect to Corel, other than in the necessary course of business, before the material fact had been generally disclosed, contrary to subsection 76(2) (also known as tipping) and paragraph 122(1)(c) of the Act.

A second count in the Information alleges that Cowpland, as a director of M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc., contravened subsection 122(3) of the Act in mid-August, 1997, by authorizing the commission of an offence by M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc. The offence was the sale of 2,431,200 shares of Corel for $20.4 million, which was made with knowledge of a material fact with respect to Corel that had not been generally disclosed, contrary to subsection 76(1) of the Act (also known as "insider trading".)  
  
The third count alleges that on May 20, 1998, Cowpland submitted untrue or misleading statements to the Commission, contrary to paragraph 122(1)(a) of the Act.

The Information also alleges that M.C.J.C. Holdings sold 2,431,200 shares of Corel stock in mid-August, 1997, for $20.4 million with knowledge of a material fact with respect to Corel which had not been generally disclosed, contrary to subsection 76(1) and paragraph 122(1)(c) of the Act.

Cowpland (or his counsel or agent) will be required to appear before the Ontario Court of Justice at the Old City Hall, Toronto, Ontario, on November 22, 1999 at 9:00 a.m. in courtroom 111.

In addition, the Commission today issued a temporary cease trade order against Cowpland and M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc., which prohibits both from trading in Corel stock for a period of 15 days. A hearing to extend the cease-trade order is scheduled for 9:30 a.m., October 28, 1999, in the 8th Floor Hearing Room at the OSC's offices at 20 Queen Street West, Toronto.

Copies of the [Appendix](#a) to the Information, which contains the counts with which Cowpland and M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc. have been charged, [the cease trade order](/en/Proceedings_rad_19991014_mcjc.htm "cease trade order"), the [Notice of Hearing](/en/Proceedings_noh_19991014_mcjc.htm "Notice of Hearing") and the [Statement of Allegations](/en/Proceedings_soa_19991014_mcjcholdingsincandmichaelcowpland.htm "Statement of Allegations") associated with the Hearing are attached to this release and available on the OSC website, [www.osc.gov.on.ca](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca), or from the Commission, 8th floor, 20 Queen Street West, Toronto, Ontario.

**APPENDIX "A" TO THE INFORMATION OF RIMA PILIPAVICIUS**

**M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc.**

1. On the 11th, 12th and 14th days of August, 1997, at the City of Toronto and elsewhere in the Province of Ontario, M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc. being a company in a special relationship with Corel Corporation ("Corel"), a reporting issuer in the Province of Ontario listed and posted for trading on the Toronto Stock Exchange, did sell securities of Corel, to wit: 2,431,200 shares for $20,400,000, more or less, with knowledge of a material fact with respect to Corel that had not been generally disclosed contrary to subsection 76(1) and paragraph 122(1)(c) of the *Securities Act*, R.S.O. 1990, c. S.5, as amended (the "Act").

**Michael Cowpland**

2. On or about the 11th, 12th and 14th days of August 1997, at the City of Toronto and elsewhere in the Province of Ontario, Michael Cowpland, being a person in a special relationship with Corel, did inform, other than in the necessary course of business, M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc., of a material fact with respect to Corel before the material fact had been generally disclosed contrary to subsection 76(2) and paragraph 122(1)(c) of the Act.

3. On or about the 11th, 12th and 14th days of August 1997, at the City of Toronto and elsewhere in the Province of Ontario, Michael Cowpland, being a director of M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc., did authorize, permit and acquiesce in the commission of an offence by M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc. contrary to subsection 122(3) of the Act to wit: on the 11th, 12th and 14th days of August, 1997, M.C.J.C. Holdings Inc., being a company in a special relationship with Corel, a reporting issuer in the Province of Ontario did sell 2,431,200 shares of Corel for $20,400,000 more or less, with knowledge of a material fact with respect to Corel that had not been generally disclosed contrary to subsection 76(1) and paragraph 122(1)(c) of the Act.

4. On or about the 20th day of May, 1998, at the City of Toronto in the Province of Ontario, Michael Cowpland did make statements in information submitted to the Commission that, in material respects and at the time and in the light of the circumstances under which they were made, were misleading or untrue contrary to paragraph 122(1)(a) of the Act.
