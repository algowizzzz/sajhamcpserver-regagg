# Canadian Securities Regulators Outline Next Steps in Examination of Mutual Fund Fees

[CSA](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5041%5D=5041)

## June 29, 2016

## For Immediate Release

**Toronto** – The Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA) today published [CSA Staff Notice 81-327 *Next Steps in the CSA’s Examination of Mutual Fund Fees*](/en/securities-law/instruments-rules-policies/8/81-327/csa-staff-notice-81-327-next-steps-csas-examination-mutual-fund-fees "CSA Staff Notice 81-327 Next Steps in the CSA’s Examination of Mutual Fund Fees"), in fulfilment of its commitment to communicating a policy direction on mutual fund fees by mid-2016.

CSA Staff Notice 81-327 can be found on CSA members’ websites.

The CSA, the council of the securities regulators of Canada’s provinces and territories, co-ordinates and harmonizes regulation for the Canadian capital markets.
