**OSC Prosecution of Jose L. Castaneda to Resume April 6, 2006**

**TORONTO –** The prosecution of Jose L. Castaneda under the *Provincial Offences Act* by the Ontario Securities Commission is scheduled to return to court on Thursday April 6, 9 a.m., Courtroom “C”, Old City Hall courthouse.

A copy of the *Provincial Offences Act* charges laid by the Commission is available on the Commission website ([www.osc.gov.on.ca](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca)).
