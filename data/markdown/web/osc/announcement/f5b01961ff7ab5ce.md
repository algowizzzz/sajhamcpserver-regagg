Event Details

As head of the Ontario Securities Commission, Maureen Jensen has a unique view into the unprecedented market evolution unfolding globally. Regulators are responding as new industries are being formed, new technology platforms are adopted, and demographics and investor expectations are shifting. Maureen will discuss the current trends and the need for a renewed collective effort to support financial stability and economic prosperity.
