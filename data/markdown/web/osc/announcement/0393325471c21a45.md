# Report of the Over-the-Counter Derivatives Regulators Group (ODRG) to G20 Leaders on Cross-Border Implementation Issues

[Other](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5056%5D=5056)

## November 6, 2015

## For Immediate Release

  
  
  
  

The [Report of the Over-the-Counter Derivatives Regulators Group (ODRG) to G20 Leaders on Cross-Border Implementation Issues](https://www.cftc.gov/idc/groups/public/@internationalaffairs/documents/file/odrgreportg20_1115.pdf) has been posted to the website of the [U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC)](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/pr7275-15#PrRoWMBL).

The ODRG includes principles of the various regulatory authorities, including the Ontario Securities Commission, with responsibility for over-the-counter (OTC) derivatives markets. Participating jurisdictions have been implementing the G20’s reform agenda through legislative and regulatory action. Since 2011, the ODRG has sought to identify and resolve cross-border issues association with the implementation.
