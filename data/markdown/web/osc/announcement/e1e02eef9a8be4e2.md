# 2018 IFIC Annual Leadership Conference

[Industry event](/en/news-events/events?type%5B806%5D=806)
Past Event

Sep 27, 2018 

Event Details

IFIC’s Annual Leadership Conference brings together some of the best minds of the investment funds industry and beyond to expand your knowledge and challenge your thinking.

* 10:15 AM - 11:15 AM Effective Disclosure: Limits and Possibilities - Doug Sarro, Senior Advisor, Research and Regulatory Innovation, Ontario Securities Commission
* 1:20 PM - 2:20 PM Track One - Regulatory Update Part 1: The Latest Rules, Reforms and Recommendations - Raymond Chan, Acting Director, Investment Funds and Structured Products Branch, Ontario Securities Commission and Debra Foubert, Director, Compliance and Registrant Regulation Branch, Ontario Securities Commission
* 2:45 PM - 3:45 PM Track One - Regulatory Update Part 2: Recent SRO Activity and What Lies Ahead - Felicia Tedesco, CPA, CA, Deputy Director, Operations, Compliance and Registrant Regulation Branch, Ontario Securities Commission
* 2:45 PM - 3:45 PM Track Two - Meeting the Needs of Elderly Investors - Denise Morris, Manager, Policy, The Investor Office, Ontario Securities Commission
