# OSC Office of the Whistleblower Investigation Counsel, Ashok Menen to speak at the National Corporate Ethics and Integrity Summit

[Speech](/en/news-events/events?type%5B816%5D=816)
Past Event

Apr 10, 2018 

02:00 pm

Beanfield Centre, 105 Princes' Blvd., Toronto Ontario M6K 3C3

Event Details

**Concurrent Session C2  
Speak Up! The Evolving Landscape for Whistleblowing in Canada**

The creation of the OSC's Office of the Whistleblower changed the landscape for whistleblowing in Canada. In this session, you will hear observations and reflections on this new office and engage in a discussion on key aspects shaping the practice of whistleblowing in Canada. Whistleblowers are a crucial part of ethics and compliance program, but this area is highly complicated for organizations and for whistleblowers as well. Panellists will engage in a discussion of key dimensions needed to support a speak up culture within organizations.

**Moderator:**

* Patrice Poitevin Co-Founder and Senior Advisor, Strategic and International Development Canadian Centre of Excellence for Anti-Corruption (CCEAC)

**Panellists:**

* Ashok  Menen , Investigation Counsel, Ontario Securities Commission
* Kelly Donovan, Owner, Fit4Duty
