# Registrant Outreach: New Custody Rules and CRM2 Refresher (Session B: In-Person)

[OSC Registrant Outreach Seminar](/en/news-events/events?type%5B821%5D=821)
Past Event

Oct 9, 2018 

10:00 am
 - 
12:00 pm

22nd Floor Training Room, Ontario Securities Commission, 20 Queen Street West Toronto, ON

Event Details

This seminar will provide an overview of:

* The new Custody Rules that have recently come into effect, focusing on the impact of these rules on registered firms and addressing certain frequently asked questions
* The CRM2 rules, including highlights of our findings from our recent CRM2 reviews, and guidance and best practices on policies and procedures for preparing CRM2 reports

**Who should attend:**

Registered representatives, ultimate designated persons, chief compliance officers, persons responsible for client reporting, and legal counsel of registrants (e.g., IFMs, PMs, EMDs and SPDs).

**Benefits:**

* Enhance understanding of the recent changes to Custody Rules that are now in effect
* Obtain a refresher on the CRM2 rules
