## Featured resources

Image

![GetSmarterAboutMoney.ca](/sites/default/files/styles/pattern_card_desktop_1x/public/2025-08/Feature-Image-GSAM-EN-Spring2025-1200x900-NEW-GK.png.webp?h=6eb229a4&itok=yhI8GT01)

GetSmarterAboutMoney.ca

GetSmarterAboutMoney.ca is one of Canada’s most visited financial literacy websites, featuring articles, tools, calculators, worksheets, videos, and other resources to help people understand investing and make informed financial decisions.

[Learn more](https://www.getsmarteraboutmoney.ca/)

Image

![Woman pointing at paper notes taped to a window](/sites/default/files/styles/pattern_card_desktop_1x/public/2021-01/Homepage-Innovation-woman-pointing-at-postits-on-screen_1.jpg.webp?h=827069f2&itok=aL1YLS9K)

Innovation at the OSC

The Office of Economic Growth and Innovation (The Innovation Office) is a dedicated team at the OSC focussed on initiatives that support economic growth and innovation in Ontario’s capital markets. The Innovation Office contributes to processes that support innovation and drive efficiency at the OSC.

[More](https://oscinnovation.ca/)

Image

![Graphic of a blue whistle](/sites/default/files/styles/pattern_card_desktop_1x/public/2021-02/Homepage-OSC-Whistleblower-Program.jpg.webp?h=827069f2&itok=H60VHpyP)

OSC Whistleblower Program

The OSC Whistleblower Program accepts tips on possible violations of Ontario securities law, offers protections for individuals who come forward, as well as a reward of up to $5 million for tips that lead to enforcement action.

[More](/en/enforcement/osc-whistleblower-program)

Image

![Man typing on laptop](/sites/default/files/styles/pattern_card_desktop_1x/public/2021-01/Homepage-SEDAR-man-typing-on-laptop.jpg.webp?h=827069f2&itok=-qyO5mpn)

Company and fund information

SEDAR+ is the platform used by all market participants to file, disclose, and search for issuer information in Canada’s capital markets. SEDAR+ provides access to documents filed by public companies, investment funds and certain third-party filers.

[More](https://www.sedarplus.ca/landingpage/)
