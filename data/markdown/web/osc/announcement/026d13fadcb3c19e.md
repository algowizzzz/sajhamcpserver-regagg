# Current Trends in Prospectus Filings: What SME issuers need to know

Past Event

01:30 pm
 -

Webinar

Event Details

This seminar will review common issues and errors that arise in the context of prospectus filings under National Instrument 41-101 *General Prospectus Requirements* (NI 41-101), National Instrument 44-101 *Short Form Prospectus Distributions* (NI 44-101) and National Instrument 44-102 *Shelf Distributions* (NI 44-102), as well as several hot topics, including:

* General Overview of Types of Prospectuses and Offerings
* Prospectus Filing and Review Process
* Prospectus Procedural Requirements, Timing and Fees
* Hot Buttons in Prospectus Reviews
  + Financial condition and sufficiency of proceeds
  + Mining issues
  + M&A matters (e.g., dual-share structures)
* Reverse Take-overs
* Syndicated Mortgages
* Reducing regulatory burden initiatives affecting the prospectus process

**Who should attend:**

* General counsel of SME reporting issuers
* CEOs and CFOs of SMEs
* Directors of SMEs
* Legal counsel and other external advisors to SMEs

**Why You Should Attend:**

* To learn more about the requirements of NI 41-101, NI 44-101 and NI 44-102 when filing a prospectus
* To understand certain specific disclosure requirements and common disclosure errors
* To hear directly from OSC staff on timely topics
* Individuals and advisors of private companies considering going public may wish to attend to learn about disclosure requirements for an initial public offering prospectus
