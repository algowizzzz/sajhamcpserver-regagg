# LMI Diffracto Limited

Order

## April 10, 2000

Headnote

Issuer deemed to have ceased to be reporting issuer under the Act.

**Statutes Cited**

Securities Act, R.S.O. 1990, c. S.5, as am., ss. 1(1), 6(3) and 83.

  
**IN THE MATTER OF THE SECURITIES ACT,**  
**R.S.O. 1990, CHAPTER S.5, AS AMENDED (THE "ACT")**  
  
**AND**  
  
**IN THE MATTER OF**  
**LMI DIFFRACTO LIMITED**  
  
**ORDER**  
**(Section 83)**  
  

**WHEREAS** LMI Diffracto Limited, a corporation continued under the laws of Canada,has applied for an order pursuant to section 83 of the Act;

**AND UPON** it being represented that LMI Diffracto Limited has fewer than fifteensecurity holders whose latest address as shown on its books is in Ontario;

**AND UPON** the undersigned Manager being satisfied that to do so would not beprejudicial to the public interest;

**IT IS ORDERED** pursuant to section 83 of the Act that LMI Diffracto Limited isdeemed to have ceased to be a reporting issuer for the purposes of the Act.

April 10th, 2000.

"Heidi Franken"
