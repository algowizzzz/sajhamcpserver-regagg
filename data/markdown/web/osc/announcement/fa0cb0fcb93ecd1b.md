# Investor Office Releases Second Video in Trilogy

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## January 12, 2016

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) today released *Financial Success is a Marathon, Not a Sprint*, the second in a trilogy of YouTube videos highlighting the purpose and focus of Ontario’s new Investor Office.

This latest video follows on the success of the first in the series entitled *Behind Every Statistic is a Person*, which quickly became the OSC’s most-viewed video ever.

*[Financial Success is a Marathon, Not a Sprint](https://youtu.be/I7sU9hUBwhs)* can be viewed here.

*[Behind Every Statistic is a Person](https://youtu.be/ZdoVyHKO0LQ)* can be viewed here.

You can subscribe to *Investor News* and read past issues at [InvestorOffice.ca](http://www.investoroffice.ca/).

At the Investor Office, we’re invested in people. The mission of the Investor Office is to give investors information to help them invest wisely and confidently, ensure their needs are understood by those who make the rules, and share their voices with the boardrooms of Bay Street. The Investor Office is an OSC initiative.

The mandate of the OSC is to provide protection to investors from unfair, improper or fraudulent practices and to foster fair and efficient capital markets and confidence in the capital markets. Investors are urged to check the registration of any persons or company offering an investment opportunity and to review the OSC investor materials available at [www.osc.ca](/)
