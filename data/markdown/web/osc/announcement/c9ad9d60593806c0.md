# Dialogue 2019: Regulation for Confidence and Growth

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## November 13, 2019

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO -** The Ontario Securities Commission’s (OSC) annual conference, [OSC Dialogue](/en/news-events/events/osc-dialogue-2019) takes place on Thursday, November 21, 2019, at the Metro Toronto Convention Centre (South Building). The registration desk will open at 7:45 a.m.

This year, OSC Dialogue will focus on the future of securities regulation and streamlining regulation to reduce burden while benefiting investors. Panellists will discuss the changing global economy, the impact of innovation, key market trends, and opportunities for Canadian businesses.

Media interested in attending OSC Dialogue 2019 should register in advance by contacting [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#f09d95949991af999e81859982999583b09f8393de979f86de9f9ede9391).

OSC Dialogue is eligible for [IIROC](https://www.iiroc.ca/members/continuing-education) and [FP Canada](https://www.fpcanada.ca/planners/continuing-education) continuing education credits and may be eligible for [Law Society of Ontario](https://lso.ca/lawyers/enhancing-competence/continuing-professional-development-requirement) and [CPA Ontario](https://assets.cpaontario.ca/members/regulations-guidance/pdfs/2019-CPD-Requirements-Guide.pdf) substantive hours.

For more information and further updates on the event, visit [OSC Dialogue 2019](/en/news-events/events/osc-dialogue-2019), follow #OSCDialogue on Twitter, or [click here](/en/news-events/subscribe) to sign up for OSC email alerts.
