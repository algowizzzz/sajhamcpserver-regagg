# OSC Seeks Leave to Appeal the Decision in respect of Andrew Rankin

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## December 11, 2006

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) today served a Notice of Motion seeking leave to appeal the decision of Mr. Justice Ian Nordheimer, dated November 9, 2006.

It is expected that the motion will be heard Friday, February 2, 2007 by a single Justice of the Court of Appeal for Ontario.

The [Notice of Motion for Leave to Appeal](/en/Proceedings_nom_20061211_rankina.htm "Notice of Motion") is available at [www.osc.gov.on.ca](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca)
