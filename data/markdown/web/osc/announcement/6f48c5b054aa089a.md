Telephone Townhall

Event Details

Telephone townhalls are a way for the Investor Office to connect with thousands of Ontarians at a time, in the comfort of their own home. Telephone townhalls work much like a call-in radio show where OSC staff provide information, answer questions and conduct live polls over the course of one hour. To participate by phone or online, please register by March 27, 2019 12:00pm
