# OSC LaunchPad Chief Pat Chaukos will be speaking at the Sibos 2017 conference

[Other](/en/news-events/events?type%5B831%5D=831)
Past Event

Oct 16, 2017 

Event Details

October 16, 2017 to October 19, 2017  
  
OSC LaunchPad Chief Pat Chaukos will be speaking at the upcoming Sibos 2017 conference. Under the theme of “Building for the Future”, the 2017 conference programme will study the financial industry’s burning issues, including cybersecurity, Artificial Intelligence (AI), the impact of geopolitical and regulatory shifts, and data and identity.
