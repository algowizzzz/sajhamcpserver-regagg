# Registrant Outreach: Cyber Security (Session A: Webinar)

[OSC Registrant Outreach Seminar](/en/news-events/events?type%5B821%5D=821)
Past Event

02:00 pm
 - 
04:00 pm

Webinar

Event Details

This seminar will focus on cyber security matters. The topics that will be discussed include:

* An overview of the common types of cyber threats and the risks associated with these threats.
* Practical solutions to protect your business from cyber threats.
* Results from a survey (conducted by the CSA) of cyber security practices of registered firms.

**Who should attend:**

Ultimate designated persons, chief compliance officers, and information technology staff of registered firms.

**Benefits:**

* Enhance understanding of cyber security threats.
* Obtain practical solutions on how to counter cyber risk and protect your business from these threats.
