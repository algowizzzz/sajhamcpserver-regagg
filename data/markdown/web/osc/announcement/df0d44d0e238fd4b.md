# Tribunal

Image

![Gradient of blue-green colours](/sites/default/files/styles/pattern_hero_desktop_1x/public/2022-04/Hero-main-banner.jpg?h=b6cd0f36&itok=x2H8hFlc)

The Capital Markets Tribunal is an independent division of the Ontario Securities Commission established by the [Securities Commission Act, 2021](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/21s08). The Commission regulates Ontario’s capital markets by making rules that have the force of law and by adopting policies that influence the behaviour of capital markets participants. The Tribunal has exclusive jurisdiction to exercise the powers conferred to it under the Ontario *[Securities Act](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90s05)* and the *[Commodity Futures Act](https://www.ontario.ca/laws/statute/90c20)* and to determine all questions of fact or law in any proceeding before it under those Acts.
