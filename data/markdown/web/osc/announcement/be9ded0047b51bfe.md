# Court Dismisses Appeals of Ronald Crowe and Vernon Smith regarding Ontario Securities Commission rulings in XI Biofuels Inc. matter

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)
[Enforcement](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B7526%5D=7526)
[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)

## December 12, 2011

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – On December 5, 2011, the Superior Court of Justice (Divisional Court) dismissed an appeal by Ronald Crowe and Vernon Smith in respect of Commission decisions on the merits and on sanctions in the matter of XI Biofuels Inc. et al.

In ruling on the appeal, the Court upheld the Commission’s finding that it had jurisdiction over the appellants’ conduct and found the Commission’s order as to costs to be reasonable.

Copies of the Reasons for Judgment can be obtained through the Court.
