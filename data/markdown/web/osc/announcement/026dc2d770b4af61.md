# Northwind’s 3rd Annual FinTech Invitational Forum Policymakers’ and Regulators’ Roundtable

[OSC Roundtable](/en/news-events/events?type%5B826%5D=826)
Past Event

Mar 1, 2019 

Event Details

11:00 - 11:30 AM Discussion Leader Remarks

11:30 - 12:30 PM Open Discussion

Join Pat Chaukos, Deputy Director of OSC LaunchPad, and other policymakers and regulators as they reflect on what they have heard at the Forum and to outline current and future plans for FinTech regulation and how to reconcile the many challenges that are present in Canada’s federal system of federal (stability) and provincial jurisdiction (marketplace, contracts, accreditation).

Moderator:

Jeffrey S. Graham,*Borden Ladner Gervais LLP*

Discussion Leaders:

Julien Brazeau, *Department of Finance*

Pat B. Chaukos, *OSC LaunchPad*

Jean-Philippe Petit, *Autorité**des marchés financiers (AMF)*

David Wai, *Ontario Ministry of Finance*
