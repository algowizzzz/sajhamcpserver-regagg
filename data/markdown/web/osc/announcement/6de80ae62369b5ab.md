# RBC Target 2021 Corporate Bond Index ETF -- s. 1.1 of OSC Rule 48-501 Trading During Distributions, Formal Bids and Share Exchange Transactions

Order

## September 19, 2012

Headnote

Certain mutual funds designated as exchange-traded funds for the purposes of OSC Rule 48-501.

**Rules Cited**

Ontario Securities Commission Rule 48-501 Trading During Distributions, Formal Bids and Share Exchange Transactions, s. 1.1.

**IN THE MATTER OF**

**ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 48-501 --**

**TRADING DURING DISTRIBUTIONS, FORMAL BIDS AND SHARE EXCHANGE TRANSACTIONS (Rule)**

**AND**

**IN THE MATTER OF**

**RBC Target 2021 Corporate Bond Index ETF**

**(the Fund)**

**DESIGNATION ORDER**

**Section 1.1**

**WHEREAS** the Fund is or will be listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange;

**AND WHEREAS** under the Universal Market Integrity Rules (UMIR), the Fund is considered an Exempt Exchange-traded Fund that is not subject to prohibitions related to trading during certain securities transactions;

**AND WHEREAS** the definition of "exchange-traded fund" in the Rule is substantially similar to the definition of Exempt Exchange-traded Fund in UMIR, and the purpose of the Rule and UMIR are substantially similar;

**THE DIRECTOR HEREBY DESIGNATES** the Fund as an exchange-traded fund for the purposes of the Rule.

Dated September 19, 2012

"Susan Greenglass"

Director, Market Regulation
