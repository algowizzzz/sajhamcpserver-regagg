# Court Sets Schedule for OSC’s S. 128 Hearing

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## December 7, 2004

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – At an appointment before a Justice on the Commercial List in the Superior Court of Ontario, the Court approved a schedule of proceedings for the OSC’s S. 128 Application against Emilia von Anhalt and Jurgen von Anhalt.

The first return date is December 17, 2004 for the hearing of motions brought by the Respondents.
