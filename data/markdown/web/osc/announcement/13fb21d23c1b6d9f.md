# Ontario Securities Commission will not appeal Felderhof decision

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## August 23, 2007

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO –** The Ontario Securities Commission announced today that having considered the reasons for decision issued July 31, 2007 by The Honourable Mr Justice Peter Hryn of the Ontario Court of Justice, it has decided not to pursue an appeal in R. v. Felderhof.
