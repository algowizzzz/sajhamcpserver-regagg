# Roundtable on the Option of Discontinuing Embedded Commissions

[OSC Roundtable](/en/news-events/events?type%5B826%5D=826)
Past Event

Sep 18, 2017 

12:30 pm
 - 
04:30 pm

The Globe and Mail Centre, 351 King Street East, 17th Floor, Toronto ON, M5A 0N1

Event Details

Roundtable to facilitate further stakeholder input on [Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA) Consultation Paper 81-408 *Consultation on the Option of Discontinuing Embedded Commissions*](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca/en/53264.htm).

The event will include panel discussions on the themes emerging from the consultation. The panel discussions will reflect a diversity of views, so as to facilitate a balanced debate.

Stakeholders wishing to participate as panelists at the roundtable are asked to send an email with full contact details to [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#d891b6aebdabacb5bdb6ac9eadb6bcab98b7abbbf6bfb7aef6b7b6f6bbb9).

Any media interested in attending the roundtable should contact [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#bfd2dadbd6dee0d6d1cecad6cdd6daccffd0ccdc91d8d0c991d0d191dcde).

**Registration is closed.** We are maintaining a waiting list. If you wish to be added to the waiting list, please e-mail [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#2d64435b485e59404843596b5843495e6d425e4e034a425b034243034e4c)
