# Date Set for Trial of Former Bre-X Minerals Ltd. Vice Chairman John Bernard Felderhof

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## September 1, 1999

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** - The Ontario Court of Justice has set a date for the trial of John Bernard Felderhof, former Vice-Chairman of Bre-X Minerals Ltd. Mr. Felderhof is charged with eight counts of violating Ontario securities laws.

The trial will commence at 10:00 AM on March 13, 2000 in Court room 122 at the Old City Hall, Toronto, Ontario.
