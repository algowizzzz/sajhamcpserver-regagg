# Innovation at the OSC

Image

![Workers walking in an open office space](/sites/default/files/styles/pattern_hero_desktop_1x/public/2021-01/Hero-Innovation-workers-walking-in-an-open-office-space_0.jpg?h=11f3d111&itok=O3eF4LLS)

The Innovation Office is a dedicated team at the OSC that works to support responsible innovation in Ontario’s capital markets and economic growth for Ontario. By promoting greater collaboration among innovators, regulators, and market participants, we believe we can chart a path forward toward more efficient, responsive, and innovative capital markets in Ontario.

## What we do

The constant change in markets and technology, combined with shifting attitudes and behaviours among consumers and investors, continue to present both opportunities and challenges. The Innovation Office helps the OSC identify and respond to emerging trends, new technologies, and novel business models in a way that effectively balances our mandates and positions Ontario’s markets as inviting, thriving and secure.  
  
With hands-on support and a real-world testing ground, we aim to support a thriving innovation ecosystem in Ontario and inspire confidence in Ontario’s capital markets.
