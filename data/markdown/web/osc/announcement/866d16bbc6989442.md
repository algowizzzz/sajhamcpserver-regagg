# China International Capital Group

As part of the Ontario Securities Commission’s (OSC) mandate to protect investors and the integrity of our markets, we issue [investor warnings and alerts](/en/investors/investor-warnings-and-alerts). Investor warnings caution the public about individuals or companies that may pose a risk to investors.

The OSC has issued an investor warning with the following information:

Name:

China International Capital Group

Date:

November 27, 2012

Addresses:

The Exchange Tower 2, 189 Nanjing Road, Level 3, Annex Heping District

City: 
Tianjin

Country: 
Peoples Republic of China, 300051

Other Names Used:

Additional Information:

Representatives of China International Capital Group have cold-called shareholders of York-Rio Resources Ltd. with an offer to buy their shares at a substantial premium. Shareholders should be aware that this may be a re-victimisation scheme. China International Capital Group is not registered in Ontario to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities. OSC Investor Alert: China International Capital Group

If you have been approached by the individual or company above, [contact us](/en/about-us/contact-us) immediately. Please also contact us if you are approached by anyone who is not registered.
