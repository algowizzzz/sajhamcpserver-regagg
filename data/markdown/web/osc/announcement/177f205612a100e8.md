# ProTec Advisors, Fortress Global Markets, SARL and Norex Trade Advisors

As part of the Ontario Securities Commission’s (OSC) mandate to protect investors and the integrity of our markets, we issue [investor warnings and alerts](/en/investors/investor-warnings-and-alerts). Investor warnings caution the public about individuals or companies that may pose a risk to investors.

The OSC has issued an investor warning with the following information:

Name:

ProTec Advisors, Fortress Global Markets, SARL and Norex Trade Advisors

Date:

January 22, 2014

Addresses:

Fortress Global Markets

SARL Suite 6/402, G7 Cornwalls Centre

Gibraltar ProTec Advisors Gibraltar and Norex Trade Advisors Gibraltar

City:

Country:

Other Names Used:

Additional Information:

ProTec Advisors doing business as protecadvisorsonline.com is not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities. Fortress Global Markets, SARL doing business as www.fxfortress.com is not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities. Norex Trade Advisors doing business as www.norexadvisorsonline.com is not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities.

If you have been approached by the individual or company above, [contact us](/en/about-us/contact-us) immediately. Please also contact us if you are approached by anyone who is not registered.
