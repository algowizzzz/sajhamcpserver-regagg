## Join our team

Are you interested in playing a key role in protecting investors from unfair practices in Ontario’s fast-paced capital markets?

These are exciting times in securities regulation. Evolving markets mean new ways of thinking and new ways of addressing complex challenges. Every day at the OSC is an opportunity to learn, innovate and grow professionally in a supportive, stimulating workplace.

Check out the current Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) opportunities.

[Apply for a job with the OSC](/en/about-us/careers-osc/applying-job-osc)

## Working at the OSC

A career with the OSC is an opportunity to learn, innovate and grow professionally. Find out [why the OSC is a great place to work](/en/about-us/careers-osc/working-osc).

## Student employment

A [student employment opportunity](/en/about-us/careers-osc/student-employment-opportunities) with the OSC can help you gain valuable work experience, learn from our experienced staff, and build your network.

## Making a difference

We are proud of how our employees are delivering strong investor protection and fostering confidence in Ontario capital markets, including:

[Reforming legislation to require those who work in the investment industry to put their clients’ interests first](/en/securities-law/instruments-rules-policies/8/81-330/csa-staff-notice-81-330-status-report-consultation-embedded-commissions-and-next-steps)

[Introducing disclosure requirements of women on boards and in executive roles at public companies in Canada](/en/news-events/news/canadian-securities-regulators-release-data-regarding-women-boards-and-executive-officer-positions)

[Speaking directly with retail investors across Ontario about investment fraud and prevention](/en/investors/osc-community)

[Addressing conflicts of interest related to embedded fees in mutual funds](/en/securities-law/instruments-rules-policies/8/81-330/csa-staff-notice-81-330-status-report-consultation-embedded-commissions-and-next-steps)
