# OSC Seeks Leave to Appeal Divisional Court Decision in Donnini Matter

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)
[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)

## November 14, 2003

## For Immediate Release

**Toronto** - The Ontario Securities Commission filed today its notice seeking leave to appeal to the Ontario Court of Appeal two aspects (sanctions and costs) of the decision of the Ontario Divisional Court in respect of Piergiorgio Donnini.
