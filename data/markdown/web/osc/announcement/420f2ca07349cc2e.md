Event Details

**Topic: FEARLESS Regulators: Dialogue with the CSA and Key Updates on Emerging Regulation**

Join Jonathan Yeung of the OSC LaunchPad as he discusses key updates on emerging regulations with the fellow members of the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA).

****Some topics that will be discussed****

* GFIN (global sandbox initiative)
* Digital securities and tokenization
* Crypto asset custody
* Harmonize crowdfunding regime
* Crypto-asset trading platform consultation Regtech solutions

**Panelists:**

* Jonathan Yeung, Senior Business Adviser, OSC LaunchPad, Ontario Securities Commission
* Gabriel Perras, Lawyer / Analyst, Corporate Finance Department, Autorité des marchés financiers
* Marco St-Jacques, Senior Analyst, Autorité des marchés financiers
* James Leong, Senior Legal Counsel, Capital Markets Regulation, BC Securities Commission
* Alixe Cormick, President, Venture Law Corporation
