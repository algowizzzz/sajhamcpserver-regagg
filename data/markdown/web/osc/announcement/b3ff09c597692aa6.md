# OSC in the Community (Thunder Bay)

[OSC in the Community](/en/news-events/events?type%5B801%5D=801)
Past Event

Sep 28, 2016 

09:30 am
 - 
12:00 pm

Georgian Room, St. Joseph's Heritage, 63 Carrie Street, Thunder Bay, ON

Event Details

Ontario Securities Commission, Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre, Elder Abuse Ontario and Centre for Education and Research on Aging & Health will present a half-day workshop entitled How to Protect Your Money!. OSC staff will talk about fraud prevention, working with an advisor and where to find unbiased information. See attached [flyer](/sites/default/files/2022-01/thunderbay_08282016_osc-itc-flyer.pdf "thunderbay_08282016_osc-itc-flyer.pdf") for more information and to register.
