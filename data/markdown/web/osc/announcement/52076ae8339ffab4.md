# OSC in the Community (Cobourg)

[OSC in the Community](/en/news-events/events?type%5B801%5D=801)
Past Event

Aug 24, 2017 

11:00 am
 - 
11:45 am

Lions Community Centre, 157 Elgin Street East, Cobourg

Event Details

OSC staff will be speaking to members of the Probus Northshore Cobourg Club. The Protect Your Money presentation will help club members learn how to recognize frauds and scams, what to do if you suspect a scam, what questions to ask before you invest and where to find unbiased information.
