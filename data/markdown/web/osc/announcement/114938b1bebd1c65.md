# OSC announces new webinars for small and medium enterprises

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## November 6, 2018

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) is pleased to announce several new webinars for small and medium enterprises (SMEs). The free webinars are offered through the ‘OSC SME Institute,’ which helps SMEs understand and navigate the requirements of being or becoming a public company in Ontario.

The new webinars will cover the following topics:

* Report of exempt distribution: how to complete it, when to file it, what to avoid, and what’s new
* Hot topics in continuous disclosure: what SME issuers need to know; and
* Current trends in prospectus filings: what SME issuers need to know.

For full details on the webinars and registration, visit the [SME Institute calendar](/en/news-events/events) on the OSC website. For questions related to course content and registration, please contact [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection).

Established in 2012, the OSC SME Institute offers SMEs the opportunity to hear first-hand from OSC Staff on the latest issues that have an impact on their organization.

Archived replays from previous seminars are available on the OSC’s [website](/en/news-events/events) and [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCvzJUqJ7WNN-JY4uhy7dyQw).

The mandate of the OSC is to provide protection to investors from unfair, improper or fraudulent practices, to foster fair and efficient capital markets and confidence in the capital markets, and to contribute to the stability of the financial system and the reduction of systemic risk. Investors are urged to check the registration of any persons or company offering an investment opportunity and to review the OSC investor materials available at [www.osc.ca](/).
