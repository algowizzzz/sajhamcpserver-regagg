# Roundtable on Derivatives Registration and Business Conduct Regimes

[OSC Roundtable](/en/news-events/events?type%5B826%5D=826)
Past Event

Sep 20, 2018 

09:00 am
 - 
12:00 pm

The Globe and Mail Centre, located at 351 King Street East in Toronto, Ontario.

Event Details

Roundtable to discuss stakeholder input on the CSA notices [Proposed National Instrument 93-102 Derivatives: Registration and Proposed Companion Policy 93-102 Derivatives: Registration](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca/en/SecuritiesLaw_csa_20180419_93-102_rfc-derivatives-registration.htm) and [Proposed National Instrument 93-101 Derivatives: Business Conduct and Proposed Companion Policy 93-101 CP Derivatives: Business Conduct](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca/en/SecuritiesLaw_csa_20180614_93-101_second-rfc-derivatives.htm)

The event will feature panel discussions on the major policy proposals outlined in the notices. Panellists will be selected to reflect a diversity of roles and views. Representatives of banks, pension funds and buy-side market participants have been invited.
