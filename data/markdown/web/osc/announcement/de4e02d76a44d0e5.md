# OSC Appeals Divisional Court Decision in Donnini Matter

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## February 9, 2004

## For Immediate Release

**Toronto** - On February 3, 2004, the Ontario Court of Appeal granted to the Ontario Securities Commission leave to appeal two aspects (sanctions and costs) of the decision of the Ontario Divisional Court in respect of Piergiorgio Donnini. The Commission filed today its Notice of Appeal to the Ontario Court of Appeal.
