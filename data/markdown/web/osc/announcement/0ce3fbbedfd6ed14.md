# Trial Dates Set for Charges Against Maitland Capital Ltd. and Others

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## September 20, 2007

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The trial of the charges laid by the Ontario Securities Commission (“OSC”) against Maitland Capital Ltd. (“Maitland”), Abraham (“Allen”) Herbert Grossman, and Hanouch Ulfan will proceed before the Ontario Court of Justice at 10:00 a.m. on January 7, 2008. The trial will be held at 393 University Avenue and is scheduled to last for three weeks.

A copy of the charges filed against Maitland, Abraham Herbert Grossman, and Hanouch Ulfan is available at [www.osc.gov.on.ca](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca)
