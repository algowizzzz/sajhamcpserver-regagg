**Andrew Rankin Sentencing Adjourned to October 19, 2005**

**TORONTO** **–** The sentencing of Andrew Rankin has been adjourned to October 19, 2005 , at 9:30 am in Courtroom 121 at Old City Hall .

On July 15, 2005, Rankin was found guilty of ten counts of tipping, a violation of the Ontario *Securities Act*.
