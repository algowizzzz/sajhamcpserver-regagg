# G6 Trading, Ran Armon and Yaniv Avnon

As part of the Ontario Securities Commission’s (OSC) mandate to protect investors and the integrity of our markets, we issue [investor warnings and alerts](/en/investors/investor-warnings-and-alerts). Investor warnings caution the public about individuals or companies that may pose a risk to investors.

Additional Information:

G6 Trading doing business as www.gcitrading.com is not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities. Yaniv Avnon is not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities. Ran Armon is not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities.

If you have been approached by the individual or company above, [contact us](/en/about-us/contact-us) immediately. Please also contact us if you are approached by anyone who is not registered.
