# OSC Announces New Securities Advisory Committee Members

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## January 12, 2017

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) announced today the new membership of the Securities Advisory Committee (SAC) for the 2017-2020 term.

The SAC advises the OSC on legislative and policy initiatives as well as trends in capital markets; provides feedback on legal, regulatory and market implications of Commission rules, policies, operations and administration; and brings various issues to attention of the Commission and staff.

The SAC meets monthly, with members serving staggered, three-year terms. The committee is chaired by James Sinclair, General Counsel.

The mandate of the OSC is to provide protection to investors from unfair, improper or fraudulent practices and to foster fair and efficient capital markets and confidence in the capital markets. Investors are urged to check the registration of any persons or company offering an investment opportunity and to review the OSC investor materials available at [www.osc.ca](/)
