# OSC LaunchPad Information Day

[Other](/en/news-events/events?type%5B831%5D=831)
Past Event

May 15, 2019 

02:00 pm
 - 
03:00 pm

Ontario Securities Commission, 20 Queen Street West, 20th Floor

Event Details

Once again, the OSC LaunchPad team is opening its doors to the fintech community.

We welcome individuals in the fintech community to come in, have a coffee, and chat about developments since launching the initiative, including the recent Joint CSA/IIROC Consultation Paper 21-402 Proposed Framework for Crypto-Asset Trading Platforms.

**Registration is free** and is first come, first served. **(ONLY 1 registrant per company/firm will be invited to attend this session. We will confirm attendance via confirmation email by April 29, 2019)**
