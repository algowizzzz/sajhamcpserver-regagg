# Dialogue 2018: Market Evolution, Regulatory Change

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## November 5, 2018

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission’s (OSC) annual conference, OSC Dialogue takes place on Thursday, November 8, 2018, in Toronto. The registration desk will open at 7:45 a.m.

This year, OSC Dialogue will focus on how regulation is evolving amid changing investor expectations and rapidly shifting capital markets. Panellists will discuss global market developments and their impact in Canada, increasing complexities in enforcement, opportunities to reduce regulatory burden, and how C-suite leaders are preparing for what is ahead.

Media interested in attending OSC Dialogue 2018 are asked to register in advance by contacting [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#8fe2eaebe6eed0e6e1fefae6fde6eafccfe0fceca1e8e0f9a1e0e1a1ecee).

For further updates on the event, visit [OSC Dialogue 2018](/en/news-events/events/osc-dialogue-2018), follow #OSCDialogue on Twitter, or [click here](/en/news-events/subscribe) to sign up for OSC email alerts.
