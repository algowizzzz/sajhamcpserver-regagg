# OSC in the Community (Dundas)

[OSC in the Community](/en/news-events/events?type%5B801%5D=801)
Past Event

08:00 pm

Dundas Library, 55 Cootes Drive, Dundas

Event Details

OSC staff will be at the Dundas Library delivering the Protect Your Money presentation. Community members will learn how to protect themselves from investment fraud, what questions to ask before they invest and how to work with a financial advisor.
