# OSC Chair and CEO Maureen Jensen to Deliver First Keynote Address on September 27, 2016

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## September 7, 2016

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – Maureen Jensen, Chair and CEO of the Ontario Securities Commission (OSC), will be delivering her first keynote address on September 27, 2016 at the Toronto Region Board of Trade.

Chair Jensen’s keynote address, *Protecting Investors, Fostering Confidence: New Initiatives from the OSC,* will outline game-changing initiatives by regulators and what these initiatives will mean for market participants and investors.

Chair Jensen will discuss timely industry topics in her remarks, including the proposed best interest standard, regulatory consideration of mutual fund fees and the launch of the OSC’s Office of the Whistleblower.

**Details of the event:**

**Date:** Tuesday, September 27, 2016  
**Time:** Event starts at 12:00 p.m. with registration at 11:30 a.m.  
**Location:** Toronto Region Board of Trade, 1 First Canadian Place, Toronto, Ontario – 4th Floor Ballroom

Register for the event at [BOT.COM/EVENTS](https://www.bot.com/EventDetails.aspx?meetingid=%7bC4C7080F-5F3D-E611-BF7F-005056914667%7d) or call 416-862-4500.

Media interested in attending the event are asked to confirm attendance in advance by contacting [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#2c414948454d7345425d59455e45495f6c435f4f024b435a024342024f4d).

The mandate of the OSC is to provide protection to investors from unfair, improper or fraudulent practices and to foster fair and efficient capital markets and confidence in the capital markets. Investors are urged to check the registration of any persons or company offering an investment opportunity and to review the OSC investor materials available at [www.osc.ca](/)
