# DTR LLC - Notice of Correction

Notice of Correction

## August 16, 2019

### DTR LLC – Notice of Correction *DTR LLC*, dated August 16, 2019, was published on February 20, 2020 at (2020), 43 OSCB 1600. The applicable legislative provision was omitted from the introductory headnote and is National Instrument 62-104 *Take-Over Bids and Issuer Bids*, Part 2 and s. 6.1.
