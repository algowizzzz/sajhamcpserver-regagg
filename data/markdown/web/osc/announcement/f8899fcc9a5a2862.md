# OSC in the Community (Bradford)

[OSC in the Community](/en/news-events/events?type%5B801%5D=801)
Past Event

Aug 8, 2017 

11:00 am
 - 
12:00 pm

St. John’s Presbyterian Church, 2940 Side Road 10, Bradford

Event Details

OSC staff will be speaking to members of the Probus Club of Bradford. The presentation, Protect Your Money, will cover: common types of fraud, warning signs of fraud and what questions to ask before investing so you can make more informed investing decisions.
