# OSC Proceedings Against Andrew Rankin to Continue July 8, 2005

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## July 6, 2005

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Court of Justice has confirmed that oral submissions are scheduled to be heard on Friday, July 8, 2005 at Old City Hall, 60 Queen Street West, Courtroom #125 at 9:30 a.m.

The charges against Mr. Rankin are available on the OSC's web site ([www.osc.gov.on.ca](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca)) as an attachment to a news release dated February 4, 2004.
