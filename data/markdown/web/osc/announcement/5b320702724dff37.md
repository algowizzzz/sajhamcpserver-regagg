**Divisional Court Dismisses Appeal of Sears Holdings Corporation**

**TORONTO** **–** The appeal by Sears Holdings Corporation and SHLD Acquisition Corp. in respect of the decision of the Ontario Securities Commission dated August 8, 2006 , was heard on September 18 and 19, 2006 by the Superior Court of Justice ( Divisional Court ). On September 19, 2006 , the appeal was dismissed and on October 11, 2006 , the Reasons for Judgment in respect of the Court’s decision were issued.

Copies of the Reasons for Judgment can be obtained through the Court.
