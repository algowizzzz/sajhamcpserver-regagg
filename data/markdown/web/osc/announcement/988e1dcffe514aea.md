# OSC Requests Applications for the Securities Proceedings Advisory Committee

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## October 20, 2016

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) is seeking applications for membership on its Securities Proceedings Advisory Committee (SPAC).

Established in 2013, the SPAC advises the Office of the Secretary to the Commission on a variety of policy and procedural initiatives relating to proceedings before the OSC’s administrative tribunal.

The committee consists of Staff from the Office of the Secretary and the Enforcement Branch and 10 to 12 members representing the private sector, and meets approximately three to four times a year. Private sector members serve three-year terms, and must be in good standing with the Law Society of Upper Canada and currently practising, or have within the last three years practised, in the area of securities litigation.

Applicants should have an excellent knowledge of Ontario securities law and have significant practice experience in securities litigation. Applicants are expected to have excellent technical knowledge and experience and a strong interest in the development of securities regulatory proceedings policy.

The committee is chaired by Grace Knakowski, Secretary to the Commission. Interested persons should submit their resume indicating their relevant experience by November 25, 2016 to [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#c5a2aeaba4aeaab2b6aeac85aab6a6eba2aab3ebaaabeba6a4).
