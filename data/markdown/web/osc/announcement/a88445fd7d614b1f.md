# Questus Global Capital Market Ltd. (Canada), Vault Emerging Market Treasuries Ltd. (Canada), European Caribbean Financial Group Ltd. (Belize)

As part of the Ontario Securities Commission’s (OSC) mandate to protect investors and the integrity of our markets, we issue [investor warnings and alerts](/en/investors/investor-warnings-and-alerts). Investor warnings caution the public about individuals or companies that may pose a risk to investors.

The OSC has issued an investor warning with the following information:

Name:

Questus Global Capital Market Ltd. (Canada), Vault Emerging Market Treasuries Ltd. (Canada), European Caribbean Financial Group Ltd. (Belize)

Date:

June 27, 2013

Addresses:

Questus Global Capital Market Ltd., 96 Norwood Ave, Suite 214

City: 
Moncton, New Brunswick, E1C 6L9

Country:

Other Names Used:

Additional Information:

Questus Global Capital Market Ltd. (Canada) and its affiliates and representatives are not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities. Vault Emerging Market Treasuries Ltd (Canada) and its affiliates and representatives are not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities. European Caribbean Financial Group Ltd (Belize) and its affiliates and representatives are not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities.

If you have been approached by the individual or company above, [contact us](/en/about-us/contact-us) immediately. Please also contact us if you are approached by anyone who is not registered.
