# OSC LaunchPad Chief Pat Chaukos will be speaking at the Payments Canada Summit on Investing in FinTech Innovation

[Other](/en/news-events/events?type%5B831%5D=831)
Past Event

May 25, 2017 

Metropolitan Ballroom, The Westin Harbour Castle Conference Centre

Event Details

Summit will feature leading authorities and innovators on a wide variety of payment related topics including: Payments Modernization, The Payment Experience, Blockchain & DLT, Financial Inclusion, Data Rich Payments / ISO 20022, Emerging Payment Technologies, Digital ID and Authorization, AI in Payments, Fraud & Risk Detection.
