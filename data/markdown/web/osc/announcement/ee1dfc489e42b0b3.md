# OSC Announces Investment Funds Product Advisory Committee Members for 2015-2017

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## August 17, 2015

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – Today the Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) announced the members of the Investment Funds Product Advisory Committee (IFPAC) for the 2015-2017 term.

The IFPAC advises OSC Staff on product developments and innovations in the investment funds industry, the impact of these developments and emerging issues. IFPAC members serve two-year terms and meet four or more times a year.

Effective immediately, the committee members are:
