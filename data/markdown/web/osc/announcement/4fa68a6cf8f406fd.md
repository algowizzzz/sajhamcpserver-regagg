# iShares S&P/TSX Equity Income Index Fund et al. -- s. 1.1

Order

## April 15, 2011

Headnote

Certain mutual funds designated as exchange-traded funds for the purposes of OSC Rule 48-501.

**Rules Cited**

Ontario Securities Commission Rule 48-501 Trading During Distributions, Formal Bids and Share Exchange Transactions, s. 1.1.

**IN THE MATTER OF**

**ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 48-501 -- TRADING DURING DISTRIBUTIONS,**

**FORMAL BIDS AND SHARE EXCHANGE TRANSACTIONS (Rule)**

**AND**

**IN THE MATTER OF**

**iSHARES S&P/TSX EQUITY INCOME INDEX FUND**

**iSHARES S&P/TSX CAPPED CONSUMER STAPLES INDEX FUND**

**iSHARES S&P/TSX CAPPED UTILITIES INDEX FUND**

**iSHARES S&P/TSX VENTURE INDEX FUND**

**iSHARES S&P/TSX GLOBAL BASE METALS INDEX FUND**

**iSHARES S&P GLOBAL HEALTHCARE INDEX FUND (CAD-HEDGED)**

**iSHARES NASDAQ 100 INDEX FUND (CAD-HEDGED)**

**iSHARES J.P. MORGAN USD EMERGING MARKETS BOND INDEX FUND (CAD-HEDGED)**

**(collectively, the Funds)**

**DESIGNATION ORDER**

**Section 1.1**

**WHEREAS** the Funds are or will be listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange;

**AND WHEREAS** under the Universal Market Integrity Rules (UMIR), the Funds are considered Exempt Exchange-traded Funds that are not subject to prohibitions related to trading during certain securities transactions;

**AND WHEREAS** the definition of "exchange-traded fund" in the Rule is substantially similar to the definition of Exempt Exchange-traded Fund in UMIR, and the purpose of the Rule and UMIR are substantially similar;

**THE DIRECTOR HEREBY DESIGNATES** the Funds as exchange-traded funds for the purposes of the Rule.

**Dated** April 15, 2011

"Susan Greenglass"

Director, Market Regulation
