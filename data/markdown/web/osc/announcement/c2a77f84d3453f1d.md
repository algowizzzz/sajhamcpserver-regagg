# OSC offers additional educational seminars for registrants

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## December 11, 2013

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** - The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) announced today the addition of four new sessions to its [Registrant Outreach](/en/industry/registration-and-compliance/registrant-outreach-program "Registrant Outreach") program intended to assist Ontario registrants with strengthening their compliance practices.

The new seminar topics include an overview of the registration process and targeted seminars for exempt market dealers, portfolio managers and investment fund managers, respectively, on relevant compliance issues. Additional seminar topics will be added to the seminar calendar at a later date.

The feedback from participants who attended the 2013 seminars has been positive. In 2013, over 1,000 individuals attended the Registrant Outreach sessions, either in-person or via webinar. The program continues to provide Ontario registrants with practical knowledge on compliance-related matters and gives them the opportunity to hear first-hand from OSC Staff on the latest issues impacting them.

Interested registrants can go to the [Calendar of Events](/en/news-events/events "Registrant Outreach: Calendar of Events") section of the Registrant Outreach page of the OSC website, for seminar descriptions and registration.

To keep up to date on compliance related matters, join the OSC’s [Registrant Outreach community](/en/news-events/subscribe "Registrant Outreach community"). Community members will receive regular information and updates on OSC initiatives. To join, go to the Registrant Outreach page on the OSC’s website at [www.osc.ca](/ "Ontario Securities Commission website").

For questions, please contact [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#7d2f181a140e090f1c13093208090f181c1e153d120e1e531a120b531213531e1c).

The OSC is the regulatory body responsible for overseeing Ontario’s capital markets. The OSC administers and enforces Ontario’s securities and commodity futures laws. Its mandate is to provide protection to investors from unfair, improper or fraudulent practices and to foster fair and efficient capital markets and confidence in capital markets.
