# Dialogue 2017: Embracing Change

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## October 10, 2017

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission’s (OSC) annual conference, OSC Dialogue takes place on Thursday, October 12, 2017 at the Four Seasons Hotel in Toronto. Registration will open at 7:30 a.m.

OSC Dialogue 2017 will feature opening remarks by the Honourable Charles Sousa, Minister of Finance, Ontario. The event will focus on the benefits and challenges of embracing change amid disruption in the financial services industry. Panels will discuss the impact of emerging technologies, data analytics in enforcement, long-term trends in corporate governance, and the evolving investor experience.

Media interested in attending OSC Dialogue 2017 are asked to register in advance by contacting [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#3e535b5a575f6157504f4b574c575b4d7e514d5d10595148105150105d5f).

For further updates on the event, visit [OSC Dialogue 2017](/en/osc-dialogue-2017-embracing-change "OSC Dialogue 2017: Embracing Change") on the OSC’s website, follow #OSCDialogue on Twitter, or [click here](/en/news-events/subscribe "Subscribe") to sign up for OSC email alerts.
