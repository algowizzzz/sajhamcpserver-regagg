Event Details

The OSC’s teletownhalls are a way for the Investor Office to connect with thousands of Ontarians at a time. Our upcoming teletownhall is being held as part of Financial Literacy Month, with special guests from the Investment Industry Regulatory Organization of Canada (IIROC), the Mutual Fund Dealers Association of Canada (MFDA) and the Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments (OBSI). Similar in format to a call-in radio show, during the teletownhall OSC staff and guests provide information, answer questions and conduct live polls over the course of one hour. If you would like to be called on Thursday, November 23 at 6:30 to participate in the upcoming teletownhall, please [register online by noon, November 23](https://osceventregistration.ca/tth20171123/registration "https://osceventregistration.ca/tth20171123/registration").
