# OSC LaunchPad Information Day for Lawyers

[Other](/en/news-events/events?type%5B831%5D=831)
Past Event

Nov 10, 2017 

02:00 pm
 - 
03:00 pm

Ontario Securities Commission, 17th Floor, Cadillac Building, 20 Queen St. W, Toronto ON, M5H 3S8

Event Details

The OSC LaunchPad team is opening its doors to lawyers who practice in the fintech space.

We welcome members of the legal community to come in, have a coffee, and chat about developments that may affect their practices, including the Canadian Securities Administrators’ Regulatory Sandbox and staff notice on cryptocurrency offerings.

**Registration is free** and is first come, first served.  Please note that due to space constraints, and efforts to ensure representation from across the legal community, **ONLY 1 registrant per firm will be invited to attend this session. We will confirm attendance via confirmation email by November 8, 2017.**
