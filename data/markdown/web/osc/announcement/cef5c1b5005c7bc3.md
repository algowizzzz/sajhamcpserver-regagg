# Investors

Image

![Man and woman with their child going through financial documents with their advisor](/sites/default/files/styles/pattern_hero_desktop_1x/public/2021-01/Hero-Investors-landing-page.jpg?h=34299631&itok=ePRcu54t)

The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) regulates capital markets in Ontario, and an essential part of its role is developing rules and guidelines that protect investors.

[Investor protection

A key part of the OSC’s mandate is investor protection. Learn how we work to protect investors and promote investor engagement and education through our Investor Office.](/en/investors/investor-protection)

[Investor resources

Learn about the OSC resources available to investors, like our investor education website GetSmarterAboutMoney.ca, and other investing tools and calculators.](/en/investors/investor-resources)

[Research and Behavioural Insights

The OSC strengthens its understanding of investor needs and challenges by conducting comprehensive literature reviews, surveys, and behavioural science experiments. Explore the research reports prepared by the OSC’s Research and Behavioural Insights Team here.](/en/investors/investor-research-and-reports)

[Investor warnings and alerts

The OSC issues investor warnings and alerts to the public about possible harmful activity.](/en/investors/investor-warnings-and-alerts)

[Check before you invest

Learn how to recognize the signs of fraud and protect yourself against investment scams.](/en/investors/check-before-you-invest)

[OSC in the Community

Our outreach program offers investor education sessions about fraud prevention, the basics of investing, and the client-advisor relationship. Find upcoming events in your area, and learn how to request a presentation.](/en/investors/osc-community)

[Investor Advisory Panel

The Investor Advisory Panel solicits and represents the views of investors on the OSC’s policies and rule-making initiatives. Read their latest submissions, research and initiatives.](/en/investors/investor-advisory-panel)

[Investor News

Keep up to date with the latest investor news and resources by signing up for our bi-weekly newsletter.](https://www.getsmarteraboutmoney.ca/resources/publications/investor-news/investor-news-sign-up/)

[The OBSI Joint Regulators Committee

Learn about the OSC’s role on the Joint Regulators Committee and the Ombudsman for Banking Services and Investments.](/en/investors/investor-protection/obsi-joint-regulators-committee)

[Returning funds to harmed investors

If you are an investor who has lost money as a result of a wrongdoing, you may be eligible to make a claim to get some of your money back. Learn more.](/en/enforcement/sanctions/returning-funds-harmed-investors)
