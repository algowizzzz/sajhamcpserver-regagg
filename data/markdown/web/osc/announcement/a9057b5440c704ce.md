# OSC staff to speak at 16th Annual OSC, TSX and IIROC Update

[Speech](/en/news-events/events?type%5B816%5D=816)
Past Event

Jun 5, 2018 

08:00 am
 - 
11:00 am

Twenty Toronto Street Conferences and Events

Event Details

Join your colleagues to gain practical advice and updates on the ever-changing world of securities and business law at this valuable annual program.

You will hear directly from senior representatives of the Ontario Securities Commission, the Toronto Stock Exchange and the Investment Industry Regulatory Organization of Canada about new regulations, developments and policy initiatives.

OSC staff participating:

* Johanna Superina, Deputy Director, Enforcement Branch
* Andre Moniz, Manager, Office of the Whistleblower
* Katrina Janke, Senior Legal Counsel, Corporate Finance Branch
* Steven Oh, Senior Legal Counsel, Corporate Finance Branch
* Adeline Lee, Senior Legal Counsel, Mergers and Acquisitions

This program is eligible for up to 2.5 Substantive Hours

The OBA has been approved as an Accredited Provider  of Professionalism Content by The Law Society of  Upper Canada.
