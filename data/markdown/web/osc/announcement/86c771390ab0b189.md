**DESIGNATION ORDER**

**Section 1.1**

**WHEREAS** the Funds are or will be listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange;

**AND WHEREAS** under the Universal Market Integrity Rules (UMIR), the Funds are considered Exempt Exchange-traded Funds that are not subject to prohibitions related to trading during certain securities transactions;

**AND WHEREAS** the definition of "exchange-traded fund" in the Rule is substantially similar to the definition of Exempt Exchange-traded Fund in UMIR, and the purpose of the Rule and UMIR are substantially similar;

**THE DIRECTOR HEREBY DESIGNATES** the Funds as exchange-traded funds for the purposes of the Rule.
