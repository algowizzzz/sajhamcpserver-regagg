# OSC Announces Agenda for OSC Dialogue 2015

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## September 10, 2015

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) today announced the final agenda and list of speakers for OSC Dialogue 2015. The annual event brings together hundreds of market participants, including prominent figures from the securities world, to discuss issues and trends facing the capital markets.

OSC Dialogue 2015 takes place on Thursday, October 8, 2015 at the Four Seasons Hotel in Toronto. Registration is now open.

Any media interested in attending OSC Dialogue should contact [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection) to register.

For further updates on the event, follow #OSCDialogue on Twitter.

The agenda is as follows:
