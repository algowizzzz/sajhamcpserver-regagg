# R. v. Felderhof

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## April 19, 2004

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** - Counsel appeared before Judge Hyrn this morning to set the trial date in the proceedings against John Felderhof . The trial will resume on December 6, 2004 for 2 weeks. It is expected that the trial will continue on February 28, 2005.

The next appearance is on May 17, 2004 before Judge Hyrn to confirm the court dates at 10:00 a.m. in Courtroom 111 of Old City Hall.
