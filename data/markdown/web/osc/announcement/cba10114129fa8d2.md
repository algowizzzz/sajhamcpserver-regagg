Teletownhall

Event Details

The OSC’s teletownhalls are a way for the Investor Office to connect with thousands of Ontarians at a time. Similar in format to a call-in radio show, during the OSC teletownhall OSC staff provide information, answer questions and conduct live polls over the course of one hour. Learn how to spot some common scams and ask OSC experts your fraud-related questions. To participate by phone or online, please [register](https://osceventregistration.ca/tth20180614/registration) by June 14, 2018, 12:00pm.
