# Claymore Premium Money Market ETF - s. 1.1

Order

## February 20, 2008

**Headnote:**

A mutual fund designated as an exchange-traded fund for the purposes of OSC Rule 48-501.

**Rules Cited**

Ontario Securities Commission Rule 48-501 -- Trading during Distributions, Formal Bids and Share Exchange Transactions, s. 1.1.

**IN THE MATTER OF**

**ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 48-501 -- TRADING DURING DISTRIBUTIONS, FORMAL BIDS AND SHARE**

**EXCHANGE TRANSACTIONS**

**(Rule)**

**AND**

**IN THE MATTER OF**

**CLAYMORE PREMIUM MONEY MARKET ETF**

**(the Fund)**

**DESIGNATION ORDER**

**Section 1.1**

**WHEREAS** the Fund is listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange;

**AND WHEREAS** Market Regulation Services Inc. has designated, or intends to designate, the Fund as an Exchange-traded Fund for the purposes of the Universal Market Integrity Rules (UMIR);

**AND WHEREAS** the definition of "exchange-traded fund" in the Rule is substantially similar to the definition of Exchange-traded Fund in UMIR;

**THE DIRECTOR HEREBY DESIGNATES** the Fund as an exchange-traded fund for the purposes of the Rule.

Dated February 20, 2008

"Brigitte Geisler"

Director, Market Regulation

Ontario Securities Commission
