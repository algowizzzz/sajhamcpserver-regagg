# iShares MSCI Emerging Markets IMI Index ETF et al. -- s. 1.1 of OSC Rule 48-501 Trading During Distributions, Formal Bids and Share Exchange Transactions

Order

## April 10, 2013

Headnote

Certain mutual funds designated as exchange-traded funds for the purposes of OSC Rule 48-501.

**Rules Cited**

Ontario Securities Commission Rule 48-501 Trading During Distributions, Formal Bids and Share Exchange Transactions, s. 1.1.

  

### IN THE MATTER OF ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 48-501 – TRADING DURING DISTRIBUTIONS, FORMAL BIDS AND SHARE EXCHANGE TRANSACTIONS (Rule) AND IN THE MATTER OF iSHARES MSCI EMERGING MARKETS IMI INDEX ETF iSHARES MSCI EAFE IMI INDEX ETF iSHARES S&P 500 INDEX ETF (the Funds) DESIGNATION ORDER Section 1.1 **WHEREAS** each of the Funds is or will be listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange; **AND WHEREAS** under the Universal Market Integrity Rules (UMIR), each Fund is considered an Exempt Exchange-traded Fund that is not subject to prohibitions related to trading during certain securities transactions; **AND WHEREAS** the definition of "exchange-traded fund" in the Rule is substantially similar to the definition of Exempt Exchange-traded Fund in UMIR, and the purpose of the Rule and UMIR are substantially similar; **THE DIRECTOR HEREBY DESIGNATES** each of the Funds as an exchange-traded fund for the purposes of the Rule. Dated April 10, 2013 "Susan Greenglass" Director, Market Regulation
