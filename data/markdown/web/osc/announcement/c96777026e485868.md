# OSC announces new SME Institute webinars

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## October 31, 2017

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) today announced a new lineup of free webinars for small and medium enterprises (SMEs) as part of the ‘[OSC SME Institute](/en/industry/companies/resources-companies "Resources for small and medium enterprises").’

An archived replay of the webinars will be available on our website and YouTube channel and will cover recent developments and the regulator’s observations in the following areas: hot topics in continuous disclosure; current trends in prospectus filings and other capital offerings; and administrative and filing essentials.  Please visit our website and YouTube channel for previous archived webinars on mining issues; financing options for SMEs; and navigating insider reporting and the System for Electronic Disclosure by Insiders (SEDI).

For full details on the webinars and to register, visit the [SME Institute calendar](/en/news-events/events?keyword=&type%5B811%5D=811&date%5Bmin%5D=&date%5Bmax%5D=&sort_bef_combine=field_start_date_ASC) on the OSC website. For questions related to course content and registration, please contact [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection#194a545c50776a6d706d6c6d7c59766a7a377e766f377677377a78).

The OSC SME Institute offers free seminars and webinars for SMEs to help them understand and navigate the requirements of being or becoming a public company in Ontario. The program informs SMEs in industries across Ontario on avoiding common deficiencies and gives SMEs the opportunity to hear first-hand from OSC Staff on the latest issues that have an impact on their markets.

The mandate of the OSC is to provide protection to investors from unfair, improper or fraudulent practices and to foster fair and efficient capital markets and confidence in the capital markets. Investors are urged to check the registration of any persons or company offering an investment opportunity and to review the OSC investor materials available at [www.osc.ca](/)
