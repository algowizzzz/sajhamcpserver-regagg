**von Anhalt Sentencing Reserved to March 19, 2007**

**TORONTO –** Justice J.C. Moore of the Ontario Court of Justice today reserved his decision on sentencing Emilia von Anhalt and Jurgen von Anhalt to March 19, 2007.

The proceedings will take place in Courtroom 510, College Park Courthouse on March 19, 2007 at 10:00 a.m.
