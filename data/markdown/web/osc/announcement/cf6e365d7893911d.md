# CSA seeks comment on proposals for derivatives market registration and regulation

[CSA](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5041%5D=5041)

## April 18, 2013

## For Immediate Release

**Vancouver** – The Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA) today published for comment [Consultation Paper 91-407 – Derivatives: Registration](/en/securities-law/instruments-rules-policies/9/91-407/csa-consultation-paper-91-407-derivatives-registration "CSA Consultation Paper: 91-407 - Derivatives: Registration"), which sets out the CSA’s recommendations on registration and regulation of market participants trading in derivatives.

This paper details recommendations on issues such as:

* the activities that will trigger derivatives registration;
* the categories of derivatives registrants; and
* the obligations of the derivatives registrants.

“The G-20 has called for improvements to the over-the-counter derivatives markets that improve transparency, mitigate systemic risk, and protect against market abuse,” said Bill Rice, Chair of the CSA and Chair and CEO of the Alberta Securities Commission. “Responding to the G-20, the CSA has developed key recommendations on the regulation of key participants in Canada’s derivatives markets that will allow Canada to comply with international standards.”

This consultation paper is part of a series of eight CSA papers developed to improve regulatory oversight of the OTC derivatives market in Canada. Market participants are invited to provide comments to this consultation paper by June 17, 2013. All responses received will be published on the Autorité des marchés financiers ([www.lautorite.qc.ca](http://www.lautorite.qc.ca "Autorité des marchés financiers website")) and the Ontario Securities Commission ([www.osc.ca](/ "Ontario Securities Commission website")) websites.

Copies of the consultation paper are available on the following websites: the [Alberta Securities Commission](http://www.albertasecurities.com/Pages/Default.aspx "Alberta Securities Commission website"), [Autorité des marchés financiers](https://www.lautorite.qc.ca "Autorité des marchés financiers website"), the [British Columbia Securities Commission](http://www.bcsc.bc.ca/ "British Columbia Securities Commission website"), the [Manitoba Securities Commission](https://mbsecurities.ca/ "Manitoba Securities Commission website"), the [New Brunswick Securities Commission](https://www.fcnb.ca/ "New Brunswick Securities Commission website"), the [Nova Scotia Securities Commission](https://nssc.novascotia.ca/ "Nova Scotia Securities Commission website"), the [Ontario Securities Commission](/ "Ontario Securities Commission website") and the [Financial and Consumer Affairs Authority of Saskatchewan](https://www.fcaa.gov.sk.ca/ "Financial and Consumer Affairs Authority of Saskatchewan website").

The CSA, the council of the securities regulators of Canada’s provinces and territories, co-ordinates and harmonizes regulation for the Canadian capital markets.
