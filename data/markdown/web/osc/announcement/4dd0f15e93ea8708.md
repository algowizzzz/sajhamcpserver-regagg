# OSC LaunchPad Information Day

[Speech](/en/news-events/events?type%5B816%5D=816)
Past Event

Jun 27, 2018 

02:00 pm

Ontario Securities Commission 17th Floor, Cadillac Building 20 Queen St. W. Toronto, ON M5H 3S8

Event Details

The OSC LaunchPad team is opening its doors to the public to answer questions related to fintech.

We welcome members of the community to come in, have a coffee, and chat about developments that may affect their business, including the Canadian Securities Administrators’ Regulatory Sandbox and staff notice on cryptocurrency offerings.

Registration is free and is first come, first served. Please note that due to space constraints, and efforts to ensure representation from across the community, ONLY 1 registrant per firm will be invited to attend this session.
