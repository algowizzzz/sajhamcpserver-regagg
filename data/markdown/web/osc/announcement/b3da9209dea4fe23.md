# Andrew Rankin Trial Date Tentatively Set for May 2, 2005/ Further Judicial Pre-Trial and Court Attendance Set for September 9, 2004

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## May 11, 2004

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** **–** On May 10, 2004 , OSC Enforcement Staff and counsel for Andrew Rankin attended in Provincial Court at Old City Hall . Mr. Rankin's trial is tentatively set to commence May 2, 2005.

A second judicial pre-trial has been scheduled for September 9, 2004 when counsel for Mr. Rankin and Enforcement Staff will re-attend before a Provincial Court Judge. It is not open to the public. The main purpose of this judicial pre-trial is to confirm trial scheduling. Following the judicial pre-trial, counsel will attend at 9:00 a.m. in Courtroom "C" at Old City Hall.

The charges against Mr. Rankin (Appendix A to the Information) and previous news releases are available at [www.osc.gov.on.ca](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca)
