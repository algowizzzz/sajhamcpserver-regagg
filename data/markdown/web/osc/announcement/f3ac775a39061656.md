# OSC Decides Not to Seek Leave to Appeal In the Matter of John Bernard Felderhof

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)
[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)

## February 9, 2004

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** - The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) announced that it will not seek leave to appeal the decision of the Court of Appeal for Ontario in the matter of John Bernard Felderhof. Consequently, staff of the OSC will request that dates be scheduled for a resumption of its action in the Ontario Court of Justice.
