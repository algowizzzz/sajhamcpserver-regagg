# Kat Szybiak and Tyler Fleming to speak at PMAC Montreal Compliance Forum

[Speech](/en/news-events/events?type%5B816%5D=816)
Past Event

Jun 7, 2018 

08:35 am
 - 
10:15 am

Sofitel Montreal Le Carré Doré, 1155 Sherbrooke St W, Montreal, QC

Event Details

The Montreal Regulatory & Compliance Forum will cover a variety of topics that address new developments, critical issues, and insights on how to sustain a successful compliance program.

08:35 – 10:15

**Regulatory Roundtable with the AMF and the OSC**

Overview of recent and upcoming regulatory initiatives, key audit findings and enforcement initiatives.

Moderator

Alix d’Anglejan-Chatillon, Partner, Stikeman Elliott LLP

Speakers:

* Sophie Jean, Director, Supervision of Intermediaries Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF)
* Hugo Lacroix, Senior Director, Investment Funds Management Autorité des marchés financiers (AMF)
* Kat Szybiak, Senior Legal Counsel, Compliance and Registrant Regulation, Ontario Securities Commission (OSC)

10:45 – 12:00

**How to Protect Senior Clients – Vulnerable Investors and your Firm**

Join legal, compliance, elder law and regulatory experts in a practical discussion of how firms can improve their practices and processes with respect to senior and vulnerable clients.

Speakers

* Tyler Fleming, Director, Investor Office, Ontario Securities Commission (OSC)
* Laura Paglia, Partner, Borden Ladner Gervais LLP
* Lorne Steinberg, President, Lorne Steinberg Wealth Management
