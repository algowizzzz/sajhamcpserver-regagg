# OSC Announces Market Structure Advisory Committee Members for 2015 - 2017

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## September 9, 2015

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) announced today the Market Structure Advisory Committee (MSAC) for the 2015 – 2017 term.

The MSAC serves as a forum to discuss issues associated with market structure and marketplace operations in the Canadian markets.  The committee also provides OSC Staff with meaningful feedback regarding the development of policy and rule-making initiatives that promote investor protection, fair and efficient capital markets and confidence in those markets.

“Given the pace of change in the marketplace, it is critically important that we continue to consult and receive feedback from industry,” said Susan Greenglass, Director, Market Regulation. “The Market Structure Advisory Committee is a source of valuable feedback and we look forward to benefitting from their experience and expertise.”

The MSAC will meet four or more times a year, with members serving two-year terms.  Effective November 1st, 2015, the MSAC members are:
