Insights From The Regulator – It Pays To Get Ahead of Misconduct

Event Details

Now more than ever, the potential cost of compliance is so high, that it pays to get ahead of misconduct. Just over a year into the program, the OSC presents their whistleblowing initiative and how buy-side institutions can work closely with regulators to reduce costs and reputational risk of misconduct.

Insights & Takeaways: This session will focus on themes like: Similarities and differences between the OSC initiative and that of the SEC; the anonymous and confidential process in place for whistle-blowers for misconduct; how firms can work with the regulator with No Contest Settlements and Credit for Cooperation; the OSC’s use of data analytics to identify misconduct.
