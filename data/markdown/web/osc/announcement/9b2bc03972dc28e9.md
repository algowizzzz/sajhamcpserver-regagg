# Investor Office Releases Conclusion to Video Trilogy

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## January 26, 2016

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) today released *Every. Person. Matters*.*,* the conclusion to a trilogy of YouTube videos highlighting the purpose and focus of Ontario’s new Investor Office.

“This video trilogy introduces the Investors Office’s approach and our focus on people. The common theme across the trilogy is this: At the Investor Office, we’re invested in people,” said Tyler Fleming, Director of the Investor Office, “Our mission is to give investors information to help them invest wisely and confidently, ensure their needs are understood by those who make the rules, and share their voices in the boardrooms of Bay Street.”

This successful trilogy began with the launch of *Behind Every Statistic is a Person*, followed by *Financial Success is a Marathon, Not a Sprint*. Watch all of the videos here:

Part 1: [*Behind Every Statistic is a Person*](https://youtu.be/ZdoVyHKO0LQ)

Part 2: [*Financial Success is a Marathon, Not a Sprint*](https://youtu.be/I7sU9hUBwhs)

Part 3 (Conclusion): [*Every. Person. Matters.*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=harczv3l98E)

The mandate of the OSC is to provide protection to investors from unfair, improper or fraudulent practices and to foster fair and efficient capital markets and confidence in the capital markets. Investors are urged to check the registration of any persons or company offering an investment opportunity and to review the OSC investor materials available at [www.osc.ca](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca)
