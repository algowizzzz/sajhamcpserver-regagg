# January 20, 2012 - Investor Advisory Panel - Agenda

## January 20, 2012

### AGENDA Investor Advisory Panel Meeting Friday January 20, 2012 11:00 pm – 1:00 pm 22nd Floor Meeting Room 20 Queen Street West Toronto, Ontario

  
  
  

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MEMBERS:** |  | Anita Anand, Chair  Nancy Averill  Paul Bates  Stan Buell  Lincoln Caylor  Steven Garmaise  Michael Wissell |
|  |  |  |

1. Introduction from the Chair (Anita Anand) (5 minutes)

2. Presentation and discussion with Investment Funds staff regarding mutual fund risk disclosure. (45 minutes)

3. Update on proposal to expand IAP mandate and discussion of OBSI letter. (25 minutes)

4. Update and discussion regarding proposed research and investor outreach. (30 minutes)

5. CSA Consultation Note concerning minimum amount and accredited investor prospectus exemptions. These exemptions are under review to ensure that they meet the needs of market participants and balance investor protection with the efficiency of capital markets. Comment period closes on February 29, 2012. (10 minutes)

6. Any new business and correspondence. (5 minutes)
