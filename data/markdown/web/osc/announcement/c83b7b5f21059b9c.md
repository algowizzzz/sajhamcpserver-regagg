# Roundtable on Reducing Regulatory Burden Related to Trading, Marketplaces, Issuer Requirements, and Derivatives

Past Event

 -

Design Exchange, 234 Bay Street, Toronto, ON M5K 1B2

Event Details

This roundtable is part of the Ontario Securities Commission’s ongoing consultation on reducing regulatory burden in Ontario’s capital markets and improving the investor experience.

In this working meeting, senior OSC staff will discuss how the OSC is considering the evaluation and prioritization of the burden reduction opportunities identified during the consultation process on [OSC Staff Notice 11-784 *Burden Reduction*](https://www.osc.gov.on.ca/en/SecuritiesLaw_sn_20190114_11-784_burden-reduction.htm). This will be a collaborative discussion that will allow market participants and investor representatives the opportunity to provide real time feedback on the OSC’s approach to reducing regulatory burden.

This roundtable will be moderated by OSC Vice-Chair Grant Vingoe and will focus on trading, marketplaces, issuer requirements, and derivatives. Susan Greenglass, Director, Market Regulation will join Kevin Fine, Director, Derivatives, Sonny Randhawa, Director, Corporate Finance and Pat Chaukos, Deputy Director, OSC Launchpad|Policy to provide an overview of how their respective branches are considering the potential burden reduction initiatives identified by stakeholders and by staff.

The discussion will cover several specific topics that emerged in the consultation process.

This event follows the roundtable on May 6, 2019 to discuss topics related to registration, compliance and investment funds.
