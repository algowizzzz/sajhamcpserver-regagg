# TD Securities Inc. - MRRS Decision

MRRS Decision

## February 5, 2007

Headnote

Mutual Reliance Review System for Exemptive Relief Applications -- Extension of the sunset clause in the original decision, in which a registered investment dealer is granted relief from section 228 of the Ontario Regulation for recommendations in respect of securities of its parent bank, subject to conditions.

**Applicable provision**

Section 228 of R.R.O. 1990, Regulation 1015, amended to O. Reg. 500/06.

**February 5, 2007**

**IN THE MATTER OF**

**THE SECURITIES LEGISLATION OF**

**ONTARIO, NOVA SCOTIA, AND**

**NEWFOUNDLAND AND LABRADOR**

**(the Jurisdictions)**

**AND**

**IN THE MATTER OF**

**THE MUTUAL RELIANCE REVIEW SYSTEM**

**FOR EXEMPTIVE RELIEF APPLICATIONS**

**AND**

**IN THE MATTER OF**

**TD SECURITIES INC. (the Filer)**

**MRRS DECISION DOCUMENT**

**WHEREAS** the local securities regulatory authority or regulator (the **Decision Maker**) in each of Ontario, Nova Scotia, and Newfoundland and Labrador (the **Jurisdictions**) has received an application (the **Application**) for an order (the **Variation** **Order**) under the applicable securities legislation (the **Legislation**) of the Jurisdictions varying the MRRS Decision Document dated February 4, 2005 (the **Original Exemptive Relief**) that provides that certain provisions in the Legislation that provide that no registrant shall, in any medium of communication, recommend, or cooperate with any person or company in the making of any recommendation, that the securities of the registrant, or a related issuer of the registrant, or, in the course of a distribution, the securities of a connected issuer of the registrant, be purchased, sold or held, shall not, in certain circumstances, apply to the Filer, in respect of securities of its parent bank, The Toronto Dominion Bank;

**AND WHEREAS** under the Mutual Reliance Review System for Exemptive Relief Applications (the System), the Ontario Securities Commission is the principal regulator for the Application;

**AND WHEREAS**, unless otherwise defined, terms used herein have the meaning set out in National Instrument 14-101 Definitions;

**AND WHEREAS** each of the Decision Makers is satisfied that the test contained in the Legislation that provides the Decision Maker with the jurisdiction to grant the Variation Order has been met;

**AND WHEREAS** under the System, this MRRS Decision Document evidences the decision of each Decision Maker (collectively, the Decision);

**IT IS ORDERED** pursuant to the Legislation that section (D) of the Original Exemptive Relief be amended as follows:

> (D) this Decision shall terminate on the day that is four years after the date of this Decision.
