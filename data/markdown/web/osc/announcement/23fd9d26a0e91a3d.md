# Registrant Outreach: Registration Applications and OSC’s Whistleblower Program (Session A: In-Webinar)

[OSC Registrant Outreach Seminar](/en/news-events/events?type%5B821%5D=821)
Past Event

02:00 pm
 - 
04:00 pm

Webinar

Event Details

**Description:**

This seminar will provide an overview of:

The rules relating to the disclosure of information required by applications for individual registration and the ongoing disclosure obligation for registrants. The seminar will include a discussion of CSA Staff Notice 33-320 *The Requirement for True and Complete Applications for Registration,* as well as cases arising since the publication of that notice.

* Best practices when completing registration applications (including other business activities) and sections of the Form 45-106F1 *Report of Exempt Distributions* filings as they relate to registrants.
* The OSC’s Whistleblower Program and discussion of recent developments which may impact registrants.

**Who should attend:** Registered representatives, ultimate designated persons, chief compliance officers, and legal counsel of registrants (e.g., IFMs, PMs, EMDs and SPDs).

**Benefits:**

* Enhanced understanding of Staff’s approach in cases where applicants or registrants may not have satisfied their disclosure obligations under Ontario securities law.
* Additional guidance on how to correctly complete registration applications and Form 45-106F1 *Report of Exempt Distributions*.
* Enhanced understanding of recent amendments to the Securities Act (Ontario) (the “Act”) and learning about findings from a recent desk review of registrants that focused on the anti-reprisal provisions of the Act.
