# OSC in the Community (Mississauga)

[OSC in the Community](/en/news-events/events?type%5B801%5D=801)
Past Event

Mar 31, 2016 

01:30 pm
 - 
03:00 pm

Square One Older Adult Centre, Square One Shopping Centre, 100 City Centre Drive, Mississauga

Event Details

Ontario Securities Commission will be at the Seniors Information and Active Living Fair. Attend the OSC's investor presentation, Protect Your Money to learn about fraud prevention, selecting and working with an advisor and how to make more informed investing decisions.

To register for the presentation, call 416-593-8314 or toll free 1-877-785-1555 or email [[email protected]](/cdn-cgi/l/email-protection).
