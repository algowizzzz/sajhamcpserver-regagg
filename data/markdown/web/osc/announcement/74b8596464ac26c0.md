# Court continues freeze directions in the matter of Andrew Keith Lech et al

[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)
[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)

## June 13, 2003

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** - On June 5, 2003, the Ontario Superior Court of Justice continued nine freeze Directions previously issued by the Ontario Securities Commission requiring several Ontario banks to hold the contents of accounts held in the name of Andrew Keith Lech, Daniel Shuttleworth, Dennis Yacknowiec, and G.N.A. Holdings. The Directions have now been continued pending further order of the Court.
