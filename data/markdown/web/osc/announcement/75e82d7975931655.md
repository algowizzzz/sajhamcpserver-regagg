# Current Trends in Prospectus Filings

[OSC SME Institute Seminar](/en/news-events/events?type%5B811%5D=811)
Past Event

Jan 25, 2018 

01:30 pm
 - 
03:30 pm

Webinar

Event Details

This seminar will review common issues and errors that arise in the context of prospectus filings under National Instrument 41-101 *General Prospectus Requirements* (NI 41-101), National Instrument 44-101 *Short Form Prospectus Distributions* (NI 44-101) and National Instrument 44-102 *Shelf Distributions* (NI 44-102).  OSC staff will discuss hot topics including primary business, going concern and sufficiency of proceeds, disclosure and procedural issues.

**Who should attend:**

* General counsel of reporting issuers
* CEOs and CFOs
* Directors
* Legal counsel and other advisors

  

**Why You Should Attend:**

* To learn more about the requirements of NI 41-101, NI 44-101 and NI 44-102 when filing a prospectus
* To understand certain specific disclosure requirements and common disclosure errors
* To hear directly from OSC staff on best practices for engaging with staff on pre-filing novel issues
* Individuals and advisors of private companies considering going public may wish to attend to learn about disclosure requirements for an initial public offering prospectus
