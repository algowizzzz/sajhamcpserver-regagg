Event Details

**CSA Report on Climate Change-Related Disclosure**

* Oujala Motala, Accountant - Corporate Finance
* Steven Oh, Senior Legal Counsel - Corporate Finance

The PRI Climate Forum: Toronto launches a new global series of climate events which will focus on key climate topics and practical advice for investors.

This full-day event will feature brand-new content including the launch of a Climate Scenario Analysis Tool, a guide to investing in the low carbon transition, and a guide to engagement on climate lobbying. For full details, please read the agenda below.
