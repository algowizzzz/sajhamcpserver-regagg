# OSC in the Community (Hamilton)

[OSC in the Community](/en/news-events/events?type%5B801%5D=801)
Past Event

Sep 28, 2017 

02:30 pm
 - 
03:30 pm

Terryberry Library, 100 Mohawk Road, Hamilton

Event Details

OSC staff will be at the Terryberry Library delivering the Protect Your Money presentation. Community members will learn how to protect themselves from investment fraud, what questions to ask before they invest and how to make more informed financial decisions.
