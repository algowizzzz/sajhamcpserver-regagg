# iShares Core MSCI All Country World ex Canada Index ETF et al - s. 1.1

Order

## February 10, 2015

Headnote

Certain mutual funds designated as exchange-traded funds for the purposes of OSC Rule 48-501.

**Rules Cited**

Ontario Securities Commission Rule 48-501 Trading During Distributions, Formal Bids and Share Exchange Transactions, s. 1.1.

  
  

### IN THE MATTER OF ONTARIO SECURITIES COMMISSION RULE 48-501 – TRADING DURING DISTRIBUTIONS, FORMAL BIDS AND SHARE EXCHANGE TRANSACTIONS (the Rule) AND IN THE MATTER OF iShares Core MSCI All Country World ex Canada Index ETF (“XAW”) iShares Core MSCI EAFE IMI Index ETF (CAD-Hedged) (“XFH”) iShares U.S. High Dividend Equity Index ETF (“XHU”) iShares Core S&P U.S. Total Market Index ETF (CAD-Hedged) (“XUH”) iShares Core S&P U.S. Total Market Index ETF (“XUU”) (individually, a Fund and collectively, the Funds) DESIGNATION ORDER Section 1.1 **WHEREAS** the Funds are or will be listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange; **AND WHEREAS** under the Universal Market Integrity Rules (UMIR), each of the Funds is an Exempt Exchange-traded Fund that is not subject to prohibitions related to trading during certain securities transactions; **AND WHEREAS** the definition of “exchange-traded fund” in the Rule is substantially similar to the definition of Exempt Exchange-traded Fund in UMIR; **AND WHEREAS** the purpose of the Rule and UMIR are substantially similar; **AND WHEREAS** each of the Funds would be subject to prohibitions relating to trading during certain securities transactions under the Rule if it is not designated by the Director; **THE DIRECTOR HEREBY DESIGNATES** each of the Funds as an exchange-traded fund for the purposes of the Rule. Dated February 10, 2015 "*Susan Greenglass*" Director, Market Regulation
