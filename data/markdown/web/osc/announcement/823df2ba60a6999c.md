# Accredited International, Chris Bently and Andrew Reilly

As part of the Ontario Securities Commission’s (OSC) mandate to protect investors and the integrity of our markets, we issue [investor warnings and alerts](/en/investors/investor-warnings-and-alerts). Investor warnings caution the public about individuals or companies that may pose a risk to investors.

The OSC has issued an investor warning with the following information:

Name:

Accredited International, Chris Bently and Andrew Reilly

Date:

September 30, 2013

Addresses:

Accredited International Centro Corporativo Plaza Roble, Excazu Eduficio El Portico Primer Nivel

City: 
San Jose

Country: 
Costa Rica

Other Names Used:

Additional Information:

Accredited International doing business as www.accreditedfx.com.com and accreditedoffshore.com is not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities. Chris Bently is not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities. Andrew Reilly is not registered to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities.

If you have been approached by the individual or company above, [contact us](/en/about-us/contact-us) immediately. Please also contact us if you are approached by anyone who is not registered.
