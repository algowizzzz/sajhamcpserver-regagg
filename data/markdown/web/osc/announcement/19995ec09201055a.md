# OSC Prosecution Against Emilia von Anhalt and Jurgen von Anhalt

[Before the Court](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5051%5D=5051)
[OSC](/en/news-events/news-releases?category%5B5036%5D=5036)

## August 26, 2005

## For Immediate Release

**TORONTO** – At an appearance yesterday in the Ontario Court of Justice at Old City Hall, the charges against Emilia von Anhalt and Jurgen von Anhalt were adjourned to October 11, 2005 in Court Room 111 at 9:00 a.m. and a judicial pre-trial hearing was set for October 7, 2005 at 9 a.m.

On May 5, 2005, the OSC charged Emilia von Anhalt and Jurgen von Anhalt with violations of the Ontario Securities Act. Information on the charges is summarized in a news release dated May 10, 2005 available on the OSC's web site ([www.osc.gov.on.ca](http://www.osc.gov.on.ca)).
