Additional Information:

Market City International SRL operating as TNT Options doing business as www.tntoptions.com is not registered in Ontario, Canada to engage in the business of (i) trading in securities or (ii) advising anyone with respect to investing in, buying or selling securities.

If you have been approached by the individual or company above, [contact us](/en/about-us/contact-us) immediately. Please also contact us if you are approached by anyone who is not registered.
