Find out how to get in touch with EIOPA

* Phone

  Tel: +49 (0) 69 95 1119-20

  EIOPA’s reception is open from Monday to Friday between 8:30 and 17:30 (CET).
* Address

  **European Insurance and Occupational Pensions Authority (EIOPA)**  
  Westhafenplatz 1  
  60327 Frankfurt am Main - German​y

  When you arrive at the Westhafen Tower, the reception team will welcome you at the ground level. Please announce your visit to EIOPA and proceed directly to the 28th floor, irrespective of your final meeting location (EIOPA’s offices and meeting rooms are located on 2nd and 25th-28th floors).  
    
  A valid identity document (passport or ID card with a picture) will be needed for registration purposes.
* Planning your visit

  To plan your visit, please download the [handbook for visitors](/document/download/93d678b5-bb0c-49d0-8828-e5a667696753_en?filename=EIOPA%20Handbook%20for%20Visitors.pdf).
* Media enquiries

  If you are a journalist and would like to receive information from EIOPA, you can reach us by

  + using the [media enquiry form](/form/media-contact_en)
  + alternatively, you can send an %20press![at](/modules/contrib/spamspan/image.gif)eiopa [dot] europa [dot] eu (subject: Media%20Request)  (email) to EIOPA's press office

  The press office inbox is regularly checked during EIOPA’s opening hours. Enquiries will be responded to as quickly as possible.
* Invitation to speak at an event

  Please fill out [this form](https://ec.europa.eu/eusurvey/runner/EIOPA_Speaker_Request_Form_2024)to invite a member of EIOPA's management or an expert to speak at a conference or event.
* Organising a visit at EIOPA

  Please fill out [this form](/form/organise-a-visit_en) to organise a visit for a group or request a meeting with EIOPA's management.
* Questions about the application and implementation of legislative acts

  Please see the [questions and answers (Q&As)](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/tools-and-data/qa-regulation_en) relating to the application and implementation of legislative acts within EIOPA’s remit. You may also submit a question by filling out [this form](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/form/q-a-on-regulation_en).

  Find QAs on topics handled jointly by the three European Supervisory Authorities (EBA, ESMA and EIOPA), such as EMIR, DORA, FICOD, MiCA, PRIIPs, SFDR, and Securitisation Regulation on the dedicated [joint QAs section](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/about/governance-structure/joint-committee/joint-qas_en).

  If your enquiry is linked with the Data Point Model (DPM) or XBRL taxonomy then please send an email to: xbrl![at](/modules/contrib/spamspan/image.gif)eiopa [dot] europa [dot] eu (xbrl[at]eiopa[dot]europa[dot]eu).
* Access to documents request

  If you would like to submit an access to documents request, please visit our [public access to documents section](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/about/accountability-and-transparency/public-access-documents_en).
* Frauds and scams on insurance and pensions

  Visit the [Warning and risk section](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/consumers_en#warnings-and-risks).
* Filing a complaints

  Find out what steps you need to take in order to file a complain against a national competent authority by visiting this page [How can I complain](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/how-can-i-complain_en).
* Stakeholder Groups enquiries

  For questions linked with EIOPA Stakeholder Groups, please send your enquiries to: irsg [dot] shg![at](/modules/contrib/spamspan/image.gif)eiopa [dot] europa [dot] eu (irsg[dot]shg[at]eiopa[dot]europa[dot]eu) and/or opsg [dot] shg![at](/modules/contrib/spamspan/image.gif)eiopa [dot] europa [dot] eu (opsg[dot]shg[at]eiopa[dot]europa[dot]eu).
* Whistleblowing

  Please see the [Whistleblowing section](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/about/accountability-and-transparency/whistleblowing_en) to find out more about what it is and how you can report it.
* Vacancies

  Please see the [Careers section](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/about/careers_en) as well as our [candidate manual](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/document/download/937bbd10-b304-4b22-a8b9-83932012ae86_en?filename=Candidates%20Manual_2024.pdf). Please bear in mind that EIOPA does not receive unsolicited applications. We therefore encourage you to keep an eye on our careers section.
* Working hours and public holidays

  Offices are open from Monday to Friday: 8:30 to 17:30 CET. Saturday, Sunday, and public holidays, our offices are closed.

  In 2026, EIOPA's offices are closed during the following days:

  + 1 January New Year’s Day
  + 2 January Day after New Year’s Day
  + 2 April Maundy Thursday
  + 3 April Good Friday
  + 6 April Easter Monday
  + 1 May Labour Day
  + 14 May Ascension Day
  + 15 May Day after Ascension Day
  + 25 May Whit Monday
  + 4 June Corpus Christi Day
  + 2 November All Saints’ Day
  + 24 to 31 December Christmas and end of year (6 days)
* Contact form

  If you have not found the answer to your question on our website, please send us your general enquiry using this [form](https://www.eiopa.europa.eu/form/send-us-your-enquiry_en).
