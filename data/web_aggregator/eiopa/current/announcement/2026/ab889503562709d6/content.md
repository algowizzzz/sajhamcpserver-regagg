Today, EIOPA published a new set of statistical information on the European insurance sector based on Solvency II regulatory reporting.

This set continues the series of quarterly statistics on solo undertakings launched on 28 June 2017. It contains country breakdowns and distributions of key variables based on reporting from solo insurance and reinsurance undertakings for the second quarter of 2017.

The statistics can be accessed via [EIOPA's website](https://goo.gl/Ndq1pK).

**IMPORTANT NOTE:** In the future, statistics updates will no longer be accompanied by web-alerts sent by email. In order to continue receiving the updates, the [statistics webpage](/tools-and-data/insurance-statistics?prefLang=en) offers the possibility to subscribe to RSS feeds.
