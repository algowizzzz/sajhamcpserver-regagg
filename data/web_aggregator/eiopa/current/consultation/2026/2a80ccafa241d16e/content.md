EIOPA has responded to the European Commission on its public consultation on the operations of the European Supervisory Authorities.  
In summary, EIOPA believes an integrated and holistic approach to prudential and conduct supervision, further refinements in relation to supervisory convergence, and an enhanced approach to equivalence assessments should be a high priority in the ESA’s review.
