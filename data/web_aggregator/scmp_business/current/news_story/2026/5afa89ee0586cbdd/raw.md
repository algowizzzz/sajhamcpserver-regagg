# China’s stock market sees drop in new A-share, margin-trading accounts amid tech sell-off

New account openings and leveraged trading in mainland China’s stock market slowed in July, as a global artificial intelligence-linked sell-off dented investor appetite following a positive first half of the year for technology shares.
The Shanghai Stock Exchange opened 2.66 million new A-share accounts in July, down 7 per cent from June, according to exchange data. That brought the cumulative tally for the January-to-July period to 22.82 million new accounts.
