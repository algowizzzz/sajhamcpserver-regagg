# Cheung Kong Center II tests higher rents amid rebound in Central office market

Cheung Kong Center II is testing office rents above HK$100 (US$12.75) per square foot, as the once slow-leasing tower begins to close the gap with Central’s most in-demand office buildings, according to property agents.
The increase marks a turnaround for one of Hong Kong’s most closely watched office projects.
