# Thomson Reuters lifts full-year revenue forecast with focus on AI rollout

Thomson Reuters has revised its full-year organic revenue outlook, now anticipating an increase of around eight percent. The company witnessed a remarkable nine percent revenue growth in the second quarter, achieving $1.95 billion. Excluding certain items, earnings per share rose to ninety-nine cents, exceeding market predictions. CEO Steve Hasker highlighted the company's commitment to substantial investment in artificial intelligence, aiming to boost product offerings and explore innovative commercialization pathways.
