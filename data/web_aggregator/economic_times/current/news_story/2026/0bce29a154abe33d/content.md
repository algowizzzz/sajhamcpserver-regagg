# Shiprocket IPO price band fixed at Rs 92-97; Rs 1,618 crore issue to open on August 12

Shiprocket is set to launch its Rs 1,617.59 crore IPO on August 12, 2026, with a price band of Rs 92 to Rs 97 per share. The issue closes on August 14. Net proceeds will be deployed toward technology enhancement, marketing, debt repayment, and strategic growth initiatives.
