# Arvind SmartSpaces Q1 Results: Profit zooms 8-fold to Rs 97 crore

In the June quarter, Arvind SmartSpaces achieved remarkable results with an eight-fold rise in net profit. The company's total income surged to Rs 322.02 crore, while sales bookings more than doubled to Rs 432 crore. This exceptional performance stemmed from robust pre-sales and overall income growth, solidifying the firm's position as a leading player under the Lalbhai Group from Ahmedabad.
