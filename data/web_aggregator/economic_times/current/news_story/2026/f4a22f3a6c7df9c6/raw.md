# US stocks: US stocks rise as surprise payrolls decline cools rate-hike fears

US stock markets opened higher on Friday after unexpected job losses.  The S&P 500 and Nasdaq saw gains at the opening bell.  This economic data raised doubts about a September interest rate hike.  The Dow Jones Industrial Average experienced a slight decline at the open.  Markets reacted to the latest employment figures released by the government.
