# Foreign borrowings hit over $6 billion in June as 140 firms tap global market

In June, external commercial borrowings and foreign currency convertible bonds amassed an impressive $6.07 billion. Prominent borrowers included Shriram Finance and Microsoft Corp India, while Air India acquired necessary funding for aircraft imports through a leasing company. A total of 140 companies participated in these significant financial maneuvers, with data from the Reserve Bank of India highlighting these crucial transactions.
