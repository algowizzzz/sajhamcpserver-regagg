# US Treasury keeps coupon auction sizes steady

The US Treasury has announced it will keep its issuance of both coupon and floating-rate notes consistent for the upcoming quarters. This strategy aims to alleviate worries around yield increases and inflation. Analysts suggest auction sizes will hold steady until next year, with a projected $125 billion in notes to be sold next week, reflecting robust market demand for Treasury bills.
