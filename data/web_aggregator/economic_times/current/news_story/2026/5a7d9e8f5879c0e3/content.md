# Global equity funds extend inflow streak to 11 weeks as earnings optimism lifts risk appetite

Global equity funds extended their inflow streak to 11 weeks, attracting $21.15 billion in the week ended August 5. Strong corporate earnings and easing crude prices boosted risk appetite. European and Asian equity funds led regional inflows, while emerging market equities saw their strongest weekly inflows in over five months.
