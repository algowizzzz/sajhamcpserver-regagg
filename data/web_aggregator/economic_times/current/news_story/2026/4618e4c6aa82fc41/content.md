# Hitachi Energy India Q1 Results: Net profit rises over twofold to Rs 294 crore

Hitachi Energy India has displayed remarkable financial growth with a net profit soaring over 100% in the June quarter. The company reported a substantial increase in operational revenue compared to the previous year. With first-quarter orders reaching Rs 5,096.5 crore for FY27, the firm has achieved a record order backlog totaling Rs 32,222.1 crore, fueled by robust progress in energy transition and security sectors.
