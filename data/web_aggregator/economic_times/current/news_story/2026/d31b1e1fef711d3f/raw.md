# Ahead of Market: 10 things that will decide stock market action on Thursday

Indian equities ended marginally higher despite sharp swings driven by closing auction session confusion. RBI maintained rates, raised FY27 GDP outlook and lowered inflation forecast. Realty, auto and metal stocks outperformed, while analysts expect Nifty to remain range-bound unless it decisively crosses 24,800.
