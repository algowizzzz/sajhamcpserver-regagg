# Titan Q1 Results: Profit soars 63% YoY to Rs 1,777 crore, total income surges 40%

Titan Q1 Results: Consumer discretionary major Titan Ltd on Friday reported 63% year-on-year (YoY) growth in its consolidated net profit at Rs 1,777 crore in the first quarter, compared with Rs 1,091 crore in the last year quarter. Total income in the reporting period rose 40% YoY to Rs 20,753 crore.
