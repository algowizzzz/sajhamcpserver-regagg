PROTECTION OF CRITICAL INFRASTRUCTURES (COMPUTER 
SYSTEMS) ORDINANCE (CHAPTER 653) 
 
NOTICE OF ISSUANCE OF CODE OF PRACTICE  
 
Pursuant to section 8(1) of the Protection of Critical Infrastructures (Computer 
Systems) Ordinance (Chapter 653) (hereinafter referred to as "the Ordinance"), a Code 
of Practice entitled " Code of Practice pursuant to the Protection of Critical 
Infrastructures (Computer Systems) Ordinance  for Stored Value Facility Licensees 
designated by the Monetary Authority as Critical Infrastructure Operators " 
(hereinafter referred to as “the Code”) is hereby issued. 
 
In accordance with section 8(3) of the Ordinance, this notice hereby specifies that the 
Code shall take effect on 12 June 2026, and that the Code is issued for the purpose of 
providing practical guidance for Stored Value Facility Licensees , that are designated 
by the Monetary Authority as Critical Infrastructure Operators under section 12 of the 
Ordinance, on compliance with category 1 obligations and category 2 obligations 
under the Ordinance. 
 
The Code is available for viewing on the website of Hong Kong Monetary Authority  
(https://www.hkma.gov.hk/eng/regulatory-resources/regulatory-guides/svf-and-
rps/stored-value-facilities-and-retail-payment-systems/).
