# Register of SVF Licensees

The Register of Stored Value Facility (SVF) Licensees is a list of current SVF licensees which are granted a licence to issue or facilitate the issue of SVF as defined under the Payment Systems and Stored Value Facilities Ordinance (“the Ordinance”). Section 8ZZZF of the Ordinance requires that the Register is made available online for inspection by the public.
