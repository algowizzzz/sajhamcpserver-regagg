On [eight scheduled dates](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/core-functions/monetary-policy/key-interest-rate/#schedule) each year, the Bank of Canada announces the setting for the overnight rate target in a press release explaining the factors behind the decision. Four times a year, Governing Council presents the [*Monetary Policy Report*](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/publications/mpr/): the Bank’s base-case projection for inflation and growth in the Canadian economy, and its assessment of risks.
