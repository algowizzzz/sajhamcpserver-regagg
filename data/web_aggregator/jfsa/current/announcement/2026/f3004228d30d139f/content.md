# The third meeting of the Expert Panel on the Revision of the Corporate Governance Code (2025-2026)

The Expert Panel on the Revision of the Corporate Governance Code (2025-2026) will convene for its third meeting as follows:

## Information

1. Date and Time:

   April 3, 2026 (Fri) 3:00 pm - 5:30 pm
2. Venue:

   Common Special Conference Room No.1, 13th floor, Common Government Office No.7
3. Draft Agenda:

   1. Opening
   2. Introduction by the Secretariat
   3. Discussion
   4. Closing

* \* Minutes will be published in this Website after the meeting.
* \* [Past Releases](/en/refer/councils/revision_corporategovernance/index.html)

Contact
:   Corporate Accounting and Disclosure Division, Policy and Markets Bureau, Financial Services Agency

    Tel +81-(0)3-3506-6000 (Ex. 3659, 3624）
