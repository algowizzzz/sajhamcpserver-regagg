# Publication of "FSA Analytical Notes (2026.7) vol.2"

The FSA published the Japanese version of "FSA Analytical Notes (2026.7) vol.2". ![PDF](/images/common/icon_pdf.gif)[full text](/en/about/fsaanalyticalnotes/20260731/01.pdf)

* ![PDF](/images/common/icon_pdf.gif)[Analysis of Lending to Overseas Borrowers by Major Banks, etc.](/en/about/fsaanalyticalnotes/20260731/02.pdf)
* ![PDF](/images/common/icon_pdf.gif)[An Analysis Toward the Development of a Corporate Default Rate Estimation Model](/en/about/fsaanalyticalnotes/20260731/03.pdf)
* ![PDF](/images/common/icon_pdf.gif)[Attempt to Understand Deposit Trends and Quantitatively Analyze Factors Affecting Changes in Deposit Balances](/en/about/fsaanalyticalnotes/20260731/04.pdf)

An English version will be published shortly.
