Financial Services Agency

To Operators of Specially Permitted Businesses for
Qualified Institutional Investors, etc.

Changes in notification procedures due to law revisions regarding
Specially Permitted Businesses for Qualified Institutional Investors, etc. (SPBQII)
and Specially Permitted Investment Management Businesses (SPIMB)

A law to revise provisions pertaining to operators of SPBQII under the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act (FIEA) was passed on May 27, 2015, promulgated on June 3, 2015 and took effect on March 1, 2016. The revisions changed the notification procedures and notification forms as detailed below.

1. Please make sure to review following important notices:

  • Important Notice Regarding Regulatory Revisions: The submission of Business Report is required. (Feb. 28, 2017)

Notifiers are required to prepare Business Report for each fiscal year and submit it within three months from the end of each fiscal year. (Submission of this Business Report is required from the fiscal year starting on or after March 1, 2016)

(*) There are rules permitting foreign operators to extend the submission deadline. Extension application form (Word): Click here (Word:52KB)

[Article 17-13-3 of the Enforcement ordinance of the Financial Instruments and Exchange Law, Article 246-4 of the Cabinet Office Ordinance regarding Financial Instruments Business, etc.]

Please be advised that for the download and submission of Business Report form, Norifiers are required, in principle, to access to Financial Monitoring System (hereinafter FIMOS).

Please note, however, that in case of overseas business operators, download of Business Report form (Excel) from FIMOS has to be done through representative (agent) in Japan.

  • Important Notice Regarding Regulatory Revisions: Laws related to Specially Permitted Business Operators and Specially Permitted Investment Management Business Operators for Qualified Institutional Investors, etc. have been revised, making it necessary for these providers to make additional submissions. (Nov. 20, 2015)
  • Notice Regarding Regulatory Revisions: Introduction of a new system (requirement for Additional Notifications, etc.) regarding Specially Permitted Businesses for Qualified Institutional Investors, etc., and Specially Permitted Investment Management Businesses following 2015 revisions to the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act, etc. (Feb. 3, 2016)
  • We have released the List of Notified Business Operators of Specially Permitted Businesses for Qualified Institutional Investors, etc., whom our authority can not make contact.

≪IMPORTANT REMINDER≫

(1)Those notified operators whose names were published on this list are those competent Authority cannot make contact with them and is not able to identify the location of their business offices.

(2)Those notified operators whose names were published on this list are required to contact competent Local Finance Bureau, etc. immediately.

(3)If no contacts were made to competent Local Finance Bureau, etc. though 30 days elapsed since the date of the publication of the name, competent Authority may, having performed the administrative procedures such as legal hearings, etc. under the provisions of the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act, order the notified operator to abolish the Specially Permitted Businesses for Qualified Institutional Investors, etc. operation.

About jurisdictions of foreign operators:

Kanto Local Finance Bureau has the jurisdiction over foreign SPBQII notifiers (1) who have Principal Business office or sales office in Japan (jurisdiction areas: Kanto area including TOKYO and Koshin’etsu area), or (2) who do not have any Principal Business office and sales office in Japan.

Those foreign SPBQII notifiers who have Principal Business office or sales office in other area of Japan are requested to check competent Local Finance Bureau in charge of your area on Japanese language website of FSA, or consult with above Kanto Local Finance Bureau.
