**What’s New on the FSA Website**

## Public Comments

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| April 10, 2026 | Publication of information on public consultation on the draft Cabinet Office Order Partially Amending the Cabinet Office Order on Financial Instruments Business  The amendment aims to ease the requirements for individuals who may change their title to “professional investor” from the perspective of vitalizing investments by professional investors. Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), May 9, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/shouken/20260410/20260410.html) |
| April 10, 2026 | Publication of the draft document titled “Basic Ideas on Cash Flow-Based Lending Based on the Relationship of Trust between Businesses and Financial Institutions” (provisional translation) and others  The document compiles basic ideas on matters, including how a relationship of trust should be built between businesses and financial institutions, how they should communicate with each other, and how the enterprise value charge should be utilized, all of which are important in promoting cash flow-based lending. Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), May 10, 2026.[The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260410/20260410.html) |
| April 10, 2026 | [Draft revisions to the Corporate Governance Code for public consultation](/en/news/2026/20260410.html) |
| April 8, 2026 | Publication of a draft partial amendment to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies  In recent years, life insurance companies have been increasingly employing reinsurance deals for purposes such as transferring risks and utilizing the operational capability of reinsurance companies. In light of such trends, the FSA amends the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies as required with the aim of encouraging insurance companies to enhance their risk management and clarifying the ideas prescribed in the existing guidelines. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/hoken/20260408/20260408.html) |
