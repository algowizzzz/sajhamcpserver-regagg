FSA Weekly Review No.683

April 14, 2026

What’s New on the FSA Website

Week of April 6, 2026 - April 10, 2026

This page contains the latest in events, developments, and updates to the FSA website.

For files in HTML formats, when you click original press release links, and click the "Language" button on the upper right of the Japanese website, and select "Machine translation" displayed on the screen, the results of the translation by AI are displayed. Please note that machine translated pages are not necessarily correct.

Public Comments

April 10, 2026 Publication of information on public consultation on the draft Cabinet Office Order Partially Amending the Cabinet Office Order on Financial Instruments Business
The amendment aims to ease the requirements for individuals who may change their title to “professional investor” from the perspective of vitalizing investments by professional investors. Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), May 9, 2026.
The original press release is available in Japanese.
April 10, 2026 Publication of the draft document titled “Basic Ideas on Cash Flow-Based Lending Based on the Relationship of Trust between Businesses and Financial Institutions” (provisional translation) and others
The document compiles basic ideas on matters, including how a relationship of trust should be built between businesses and financial institutions, how they should communicate with each other, and how the enterprise value charge should be utilized, all of which are important in promoting cash flow-based lending. Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), May 10, 2026.The original press release is available in Japanese.
April 10, 2026 Draft revisions to the Corporate Governance Code for public consultation
April 8, 2026 Publication of a draft partial amendment to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies
In recent years, life insurance companies have been increasingly employing reinsurance deals for purposes such as transferring risks and utilizing the operational capability of reinsurance companies. In light of such trends, the FSA amends the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Insurance Companies as required with the aim of encouraging insurance companies to enhance their risk management and clarifying the ideas prescribed in the existing guidelines.
The original press release is available in Japanese.

Securities

April 7, 2026 The FSA published information on an administrative action against Yamabiko Investment Advisor Inc.
The FSA received a recommendation from the Securities and Exchange Surveillance Commission to take an administrative action against Yamabiko Investment Advisor Inc. as a problem was found as a result of its inspection. Accordingly, the Director-General of the Kanto Local Finance Bureau took an administrative action against the company on April 7, 2026.
The original press release is available in Japanese.
