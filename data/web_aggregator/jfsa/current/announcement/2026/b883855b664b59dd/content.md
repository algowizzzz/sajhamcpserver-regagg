# Financial Services AgencyFSA Weekly Review No.676
:   February 24, 2026

**What’s New on the FSA Website**

Week of February 16, 2026 - February 20, 2026

This page contains the latest in events, developments, and updates to the FSA website.  
  
For files in HTML formats, when you click original press release links, and click the "Language" button on the upper right of the Japanese website, and select "Machine translation" displayed on the screen, the results of the translation by AI are displayed. Please note that machine translated pages are not necessarily correct.

## Public Comments

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| February 20, 2026 | Promulgation of the Cabinet Office Order Partially Amending the Cabinet Office Order on Disclosure of Corporate Affairs and the Cabinet Office Order on Disclosure of Information on Regulated Securities, etc. and publication of the results of the public consultation on the relevant Cabinet Office Order, etc.  Major amendments: [1] development of an environment toward the commencement of the application of the sustainability disclosure standards, [2] review of the system concerning the disclosure of human capital information, etc. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/shouken/20260220/20260220.html) |
| February 20, 2026 | Publication of the results of public consultations on the draft partial amendments to the Comprehensive Guidelines for Supervision for Small- and Medium-Sized and Regional Financial Institutions, etc.  Based on the Regional Financial Power Enhancement Plan, the draft is for the required amendments for promoting financial institutions' support for companies' M&A and business succession, provision of loans not dependent on personal guarantee provided by business owners, support for digitalization, and staffing services. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/ginkou/20260220/20260220.html) |

### Banks

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| February 18, 2026 | The FSA decided on the purchase of beneficial interest, etc. by the Shinkin Central Bank.  Based on the provisions of Article 28, paragraph (1) of the Act on Special Measures for Strengthening Financial Functions as applied pursuant to the provisions of Article 28, paragraph (5) of the Supplementary Provisions of the same Act, the FSA decided on the Shinkin Central Bank's purchase of beneficial interest, etc. issued by the NOTOKYOUEI SHINYOUKINKO through preferred equity investment. Accordingly, based on the provisions of Article 29 of the same Act as applied pursuant to Article 28, paragraph (5) of the Supplementary Provisions of the same Act, their Special Business Enhancement Plan and Special Business Enhancement Guidance Plan are publicized. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/ginkou/20260218.html) |
