## Public Comments

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| May 22, 2026 | The FSA compiled draft Cabinet Order and Cabinet Office Orders, etc. in relation to the amendments of the Act on Special Measures for Strengthening Financial Functions and other FSA-related Acts in 2026, and will seek public comments.  Major amendments: (1) capital participation system; (2) fund-grant system; and (3) others  Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), June 5, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260522-2/20260522-2.html) |
| May 22, 2026 | Promulgation of the Cabinet Order and Cabinet Office Orders, etc. in relation to the amendment of the Payment Services Act in 2025, and publication of the results of the public consultation on the relevant Cabinet Order, etc.  Major amendments: (1) development of the provisions concerning electronic payment instruments and cryptoassets; (2) development of the provisions concerning funds transfer services; (3) development of the provisions concerning banks and insurance companies and their subsidiaries, etc.; and (4) other required amendments [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260522/20260522.html) |
| May 22, 2026 | Promulgation of the Cabinet Order Partially Amending the Order for Enforcement of the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act, etc. and publication of the results of the public consultation on the relevant Cabinet Order, etc.  Major amendments: (1) expansion of the scope of specified beneficial interests in trusts that are not deemed to be securities; and (2) review of the definition of the term “parent company” under insider trading regulations [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/shouken/20260522/20260522.html) |
| May 19, 2026 | Promulgation of the Cabinet Office Order Partially Amending the Cabinet Office Order on Electronic Payment Instruments Service Providers, etc. and publication of the results of the public consultation on the relevant Cabinet Office Order, etc.  Outline of the amendment: (1) expansion of the scope of foreign beneficial interests in trusts that are treated as electronic payment instruments, etc.; and (2) expansion of the scope of foreign beneficial interests in trusts that are not deemed to be securities. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/sonota/20260519/20260519.html) |
| May 19, 2026 | Promulgation of the Public Notice Partially Amending the Public Notice on Designation of the Sustainability Disclosure Standards Prescribed in Article 19-9, Paragraph (5) of the Cabinet Office Order on Disclosure of Corporate Affairs, etc. and publication of the results of the public consultation on the relevant Public Notice [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/shouken/20260519/20260519.html) |
| May 18, 2026 | Publication of information on public consultation on the draft Cabinet Office Order Partially Amending the Regulation for Enforcement of the Banking Act  The Act on Financial Debt Adjustment Procedures for Enterprises to Facilitate Business Recovery, which was enacted on June 13, 2025, provides for new corporate rehabilitation procedures (referred to below as “early corporate rehabilitation procedures”). Accordingly, the Cabinet Office Order aims to develop provisions and make amendments as required for the purpose of clarifying the point that banks, etc. are allowed to make a company under early corporate rehabilitation procedures a subsidiary.  Public comments will be accepted until no later than 5:00 p.m. (JST), June 17, 2026. [The original press release is available in Japanese.open new window](/news/r7/ginkou/20260518/20260518.html) |
