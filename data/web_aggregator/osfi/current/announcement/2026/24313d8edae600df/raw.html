Key Intra-Return Rules

Rules for Encumbered and Unencumbered Assets

Encumbered Assets = Cash (encumbered)
+ Cash Margin Given
+ Collateral In (non-rehypothecateable)
+ Collateral out
+ Adjustments
  • a106 = (-1)*a4 + (-1)*a15 + (-1)*(a41+a44+a52+a56+a65) + a38 + a102

Rules for Collateral Movements Section to Reconcile with Part B

Part A: OTC Derivative Collateral Out = Part B: CMB Program - Sellers Swap -> Collateral Pledged + Sum of OTC Derivative -> Collateral Pledged (includes FIs only)

  • a57 + (-1)*a16 = b22+b99+b107+b115+b123+b131+b139+b147+b155

Part A: Pledged FMI/CCP = Part B: LVTS -> Collateral Pledged + Sum of FMIs -> All Transactions -> Collateral Pledged (includes FMIs only)

Part A: Collateral Swapped Out = Part B: Sum of Collateral Swaps -> Collateral Pledged (includes CBs and FIs)

  • a53 = b12+b25+b33+b42+b54+b97+b105+b113+b121+b129+b137+b145+b15

Part A: Repo (Gross) Collateral = Part B: Sum of Repo -> Collateral Pledged (includes CBs and FIs) + CMHC/CHT On Balance Sheet Repos [Note repo collateral pledged to a CCP is recorded under 'Pledged FMI/CCP']

Key Cross Return Rules

Rules to Reconcile with M4 Balance Sheet (accurate within 2% threshold)

Part A: Securities Long (adjustments: non-reported sub and other) = M4 Securities

  • Cell (a5, c52) = M4 0865 + M4 0866 + M4 0518 + M4 0520 [reporting required at month-end with values carried forward at the business day frequency]
  • Cell (a5, c52) = Cell (a5, c53) [accurate within 2% threshold on last day of month]

Part A: Secured Lending (adjustments: netting, non-reported subs and other) = M4 Reverse Repos

  • Cell (a6, c52) = M4 0666 [reporting required at month-end with values carried forward at the business day frequency]
  • Cell (a6, c52) = Cell (a6, c53) [accurate within 2% threshold on last day of month]

Part A: Loans (adjustments: non-reported subs and other) = M4 Loans less Reverse Repos

  • Cell (a10, c52) = M4 2310 + M4 2057 + M4 0524 + M4 0526 + M4 2067 + M4 0534 +M4 0572 + M4 0540 + M4 0542 + M4 0608 + M4 2117 [reporting required at month-end with values carried forward at the business day frequency]
  • Cell (a10, c52) = Cell (a10, c53) [accurate within 2% threshold on last day of month]

Part A: Secured Funding (adjustments: netting, non-reported subs and other) = M4 Repos

  • Cell (a20, c52) = M4 0634 [reporting required at month-end with values carried forward at the business day frequency]
  • Cell (a20, c52) = Cell (a20, c53) [accurate within 2% threshold on last day of month]

Part A: Securities Short (adjustments: non-reported sub and other) = M4 Obligations related to borrowed securities

  • Cell (a28, c52) = M4 0632 [reporting required at month-end with values carried forward at the business day frequency]
  • Cell (a28, c52) = Cell (a28, c53) [accurate within 2% threshold on last day of month]

Rules to Reconcile with LCR (accurate within 2% threshold)

Part A: Level 1 Available Unencumbered Assets = LCR market value of Level 1 assets

  • Cell (a73, c17) = LCR 11001 + LCR 11002 + LCR 11003 + LCR 11004 + LCR 11005 + LCR 11006 + LCR 11007 + LCR 11008 + LCR 11009 + LCR 11010 [reporting required at month-end with values carried forward at the business day frequency]
  • Cell (a73, c17) = Cell (a72, c17) [accurate within 2% threshold on last day of month]

Part A: Level 2A Available Unencumbered Assets = LCR market value of Level 2A assets

  • Cell (a73, c25) = LCR 99004 [reporting required at month-end with values carried forward at the business day frequency]
  • Cell (a73, c25) = Cell (a72, c25) [accurate within 2% threshold on last day of month]

Part A: Level 2B Available Unencumbered Assets = LCR market value of Level 2B assets

  • Cell (a73, c30) = LCR 99009 + LCR 99012 [reporting required at month-end with values carried forward at the business day frequency]
  • Cell (a73, c30) = Cell (a72, c30) [accurate within 2% threshold on last day of month]
