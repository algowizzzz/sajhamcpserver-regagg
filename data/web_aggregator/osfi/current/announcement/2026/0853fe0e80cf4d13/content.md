# Banks

We regulate and supervise all banks operating in Canada. There are 6 systemically important banks and many small and medium banks in our financial system.

![](/sites/default/files/styles/narrow_banner_lg/public/2023-05/modern-building-exterior-blue-toned-image_1.jpg?itok=lCZtsVyu)

[Systemically Important Banks](/en/supervision/financial-institutions/banks/systemically-important-banks "Systemically important banks")  
Canada has two types of systemically important banks: domestic systematically important banks (D-SIBs) and global systemically important banks (G-SIBs).

[Domestic stability buffer](/en/supervision/financial-institutions/banks/domestic-stability-buffer "Domestic Stability Buffer")  
The domestic stability buffer is a capital buffer that domestic systemically important banks need to set aside to cover losses during financial uncertainties.

[Minimum qualifying rate on uninsured mortgages](/en/supervision/financial-institutions/banks/minimum-qualifying-rate-uninsured-mortgages "Minimum qualifying rate for uninsured mortgages")  
The minimum qualifying rate on uninsured mortgages aims to test if a borrower can repay a mortgage during financial hardship.

[Loan-to-income limits](/en/supervision/financial-institutions/banks/loan-income-limits-uninsured-mortgage-portfolios "Loan-to-income limits for uninsured mortgage portfolios")  
This measure helps to reduce the proportion of highly leveraged loans secured against a residential property.
