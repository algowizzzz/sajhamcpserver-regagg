# Rocket Doctor AI Inc. Announces Closing of Oversubscribed First Tranche of Unsecured Convertible Debenture Financing

Rocker Doctor AI Inc. (the “Company” or “Rocket Doctor AI”) (CSE: AIDR, OTC: AIRDF, Frankfurt: 939) is pleased to announce, further to its news release of July 28, 2026, that it has closed a first tranche (the “First Tranche”) of its previously announced private placement (the “Offering”) of unsecured convertible debentures (the “Debentures”) for aggregate gross proceeds of CAD $2,269,000.
