# Viridian Reports Continuation of Massive Sulphides at Kraken Main; First Assays Pending

Assays are now pending from the first diamond drill holes completed as part of Viridian Metals Inc.’s (CSE: VRDN) (OTCQB: VIRMF) 2026 drill program at the Kraken project in Labrador. The initial assay submission includes the extension of hole VKS25-024, which previously returned 24.05 metres grading 0.77% CuEq¹ (0.21% Cu, 0.30% Ni, 0.04% Co) from 1 metre before terminating in mineralization (see Viridian news release dated February 26, 2025).
