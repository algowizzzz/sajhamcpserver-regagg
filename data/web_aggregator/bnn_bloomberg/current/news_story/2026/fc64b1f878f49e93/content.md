# Three in four Canadians wouldn’t buy U.S. booze if it returned to shelves: Nanos

Canadian sentiment against buying U.S. alcohol is higher than a year ago, with new polling done by Nanos exclusively for CTV News showing just under three-quarters of Canadians are likely not buying the products if they are put back on the shelves.
