# Borderless AI Launches Alberni: AI for Global Compliance

Borderless AI, the AI-native global employment platform, today announced the launch of Alberni, the AI-powered legal operating system for global employment law and global entity management. Alberni combines legal expertise with custom AI agents, allowing companies to hire and operate internationally without building a legal department in every country.
