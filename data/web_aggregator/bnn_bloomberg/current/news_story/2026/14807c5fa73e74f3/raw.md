# PM Carney says tone with U.S. already ‘quite firm’ as trade talks continue

Prime Minister Mark Carney said Canada’s tone toward the United States is already 'quite firm' and insisted trade negotiations with Washington remain 'constructive' as an Aug. 19 deadline for new U.S. tariffs draws near.
