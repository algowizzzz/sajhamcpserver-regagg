# Gordie Howe International Bridge Opens to Pedestrians and Cyclists, Completing First International Trail Crossing on the Trans Canada Trail

Trans Canada Trail is proud to welcome the Gordie Howe International Bridge as the first-ever international border crossing to join its network. With the new bridge’s multi-use path officially open to cyclists and pedestrians, trail users can cross the Canada-U.S. border entirely on foot or by bike.
