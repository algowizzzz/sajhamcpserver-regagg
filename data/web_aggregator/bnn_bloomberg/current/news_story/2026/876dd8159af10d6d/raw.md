# Reflex Advanced Announces Private Placement of up to C$1,500,000 of Unsecured Convertible Debentures

Reflex Advanced Materials Corp. (CSE:RFLX) (FSE:HF2) (“Reflex” or the “Company”) is pleased to announce that it intends to complete a non-brokered private placement of unsecured convertible debentures of the Company (the “Convertible Debentures”) for gross proceeds of up to C$1,500,000 (the “Offering”).
