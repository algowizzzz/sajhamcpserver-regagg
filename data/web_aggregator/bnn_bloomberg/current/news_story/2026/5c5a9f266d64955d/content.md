# Gas prices fall across Canada as oil prices ease, but experts warn relief may be short-lived

Drivers across Canada are seeing some relief at the pumps after gas prices dropped sharply in several provinces, but experts say the savings could disappear within days as uncertainty continues to rattle global oil markets.
