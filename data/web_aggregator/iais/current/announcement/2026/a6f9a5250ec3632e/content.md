# Annual Reports

The **IAIS Year in Review** provides an overview of the work of our Association and is published in Q1 of each year to provide a timely summary of our activities of the preceding year. 

The **IAIS Financial Report** contains the financial statements of the IAIS for the 12 months from January to December, as well as reports from the Chairs of the Budget Committee and Audit and Risk Committee. 

The Year in Review 2025 showcases our ongoing commitment to policyholder protection and global financial stability. It highlights the progress of the past year, which was marked by significant milestones, collaborative efforts and strategic advancements reflecting our dedication to the international community.

*“**T**he IAIS thrives because of the diversity of its**members, who bring a broad range of perspectives**,**experiences and expertise. This diversity enables us to**tackle global challenges with a collaborative, inclusive**approach, ensuring that our work is responsive to the**unique needs of our members and the communities**they serve.**”*

– Toshiyuki Miyoshi, Executive Committee Chair
