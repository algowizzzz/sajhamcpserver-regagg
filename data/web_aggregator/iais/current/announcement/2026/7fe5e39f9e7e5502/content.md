# The role of supervisors in addressing natural catastrophe protection gaps

[28 Apr 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/04/the-role-of-supervisors-in-addressing-natural-catastrophe-protection-gaps/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Public statements](https://www.iais.org/category/public-statements/)

Insurance supervisors can contribute to shared efforts among public and private sectors to address protection gaps, including through supporting disaster risk assessment and risk management practices. 

The IAIS has published a [statement](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2023/04/IAIS-statement-on-natural-catastrophe-protection-gap-2023.pdf) outlining its commitment to further support insurance supervisors’ actions to address protection gaps and highlights the IAIS’ focus areas for 2023.
