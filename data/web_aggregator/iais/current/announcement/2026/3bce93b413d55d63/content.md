the IAIS Secretariat, the Chair of the Executive Committee (“IAIS Chair”) and at (sub)committee meetings. Additional engagement with insurers 
participating in the  Insurance Capital Standard monitoring period and in the Global Monitoring Exercise is not included in this table , due to 
confidentiality reasons.
