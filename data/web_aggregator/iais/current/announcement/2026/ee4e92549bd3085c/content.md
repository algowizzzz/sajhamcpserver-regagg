# Andy Mais appointed Vice Chair of the IAIS Executive Committee

[7 May 2024](https://www.iais.org/2024/05/andy-mais-elected-vice-chair-of-the-iais-executive-committee/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

We are pleased to announce the appointment of [Andrew N. Mais](https://www.iais.org/team/andrew-n-mais/), Insurance Commissioner of Connecticut and President of the National Association of Insurance Commissioners (NAIC), as Vice Chair of the IAIS Executive Committee (ExCo).

The ExCo is responsible for providing the overall strategic direction and the management of IAIS activities in a manner consistent with the specific duties set forth in the Association’s by-laws. More generally, the ExCo is charged to ensure the sound functioning and furtherance of the mission of the IAIS.
