# Registration opens for ICS and AM Comparability Assessment Stakeholder Meeting

[4 Nov 2022](https://www.iais.org/2022/11/registration-opens-for-ics-and-am-comparability-assessment-stakeholder-meeting/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

**Update: Virtual attendance now possible – see registration link below. Details for joining virtually will be circulated to registered virtual attendees in advance of the meeting.**

The IAIS will host an in-person stakeholder meeting on Thursday 15 December 13:30 to 17:00 EST in Tampa, Florida, focusing on topics related to the ICS and the AM comparability assessment. Key topics that will be discussed include:

* Development of scenarios to perform the sensitivity analysis envisaged in the comparability assessment; and
* Scope and nature of the ICS economic impact assessment.

To register, please click [here](https://events.ebis.org/v20/emc00/register.aspx?OrgCode=10&EvtID=47593&AppCode=REG&CC=122102826516).

Questions have been prepared to guide the discussion, available [here](https://www.iaisweb.org/uploads/2022/12/221201-Questions-for-ICS-and-AM-Comparability-Assessment-Stakeholder-Meeting.pdf).
