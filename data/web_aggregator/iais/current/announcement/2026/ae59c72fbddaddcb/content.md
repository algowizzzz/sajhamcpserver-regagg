# Save the date: Public background call for three upcoming consultations

[9 Jun 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/06/save-the-date-public-background-call-for-three-upcoming-consultations/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

The IAIS will hold a virtual public background call to introduce and answer questions from stakeholders on three public consultations that will launch on 23 June 2023:

* Insurance Capital Standard (ICS) as Prescribed Capital Requirement (PCR),
* Revised ICP 14 (Valuation); and
* Revised ICP 17 (Capital Adequacy).

The webinar will take place on **Thursday 29 June 2023 from 13:30 – 15:30 CEST.**

To register for the session, please follow this [link](https://www.iais.org/events/ics-as-a-pcr-icp-14-and-icp-17-consultations-public-background-call/).
