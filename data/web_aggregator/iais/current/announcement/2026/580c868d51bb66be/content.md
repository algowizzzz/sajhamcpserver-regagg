# Career Opportunity with the IAIS – Senior Policy Adviser – Financial Stability team

[5 Sep 2025](https://www.iais.org/2025/09/career-opportunity-with-the-iais-senior-policy-adviser-financial-stability-team-2/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The International Association of Insurance Supervisors (IAIS) is hiring a Senior Policy Adviser within the Financial Stability team.

For more information about the position, visit our [Recruitment page](https://www.iais.org/about-the-iais/recruitment/) or download the vacancy notice [here](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2025/09/Career-Opportunity-with-the-IAIS-–-Senior-Policy-Adviser-Financial-Stability-team.pdf).

Applications can be submitted until 27 September 23:59 CEST.

For any questions, please send a message to [recruitment@iais.org](mailto:recruitment@iais.org).
