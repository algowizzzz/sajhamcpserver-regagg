The International Association of Insurance Supervisors (/AIS) 
The IAIS is a voluntary membership organisation of insurance supervisors from approximately 
200 jurisdictions. The mission of the IAIS is to promote effective and globally consistent 
supervision of the insurance industry in order to develop and maintain fair, safe and stable 
insurance markets for the benefit and protection of policyholders and to contribute to global 
financial stability. Established in 1994, the IAIS is the international standard setting _body of the 
insurance sector. The IAIS also provides a forum for its members to share their· experiences 
and understanding of insurance supervision and insurance markets. 
ln its 2020-2024 Strategic Plan, the IAIS re-emphasised the importance of financial inclusion 
and sustainable development and placed an increasing emphasis on ·engagement and support 
for supervisors from Emerging Markets and Developing Economies (EMDE), as well as more 
exchange of supervisory practices amongst its members. 
The Access to Insurance Initiative 
The Access to Insurance Initiative (A2ii) is a unique global partnership working to inspire and 
support insurance supervisors to promote inclusive and responsible insurance. As the· key 
implementation- arm of the IAIS, A2ii identifies supervisory needs, develops tailored capacity 
building programmes, translates knowledge gained from the regions into' global learning tools, 
contributes to the IAIS standard setting activities and supports the development and 
. implementation of sound regul_atory frameworks. The initiative's ultimate aim is to advance 
insurance markets globally and reduce the vulnerability of low-income populations against risk.
