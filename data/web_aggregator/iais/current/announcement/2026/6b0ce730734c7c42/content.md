# Career opportunity at the IAIS – Senior Policy Advisor (Capital and Solvency)

[20 Dec 2022](https://www.iais.org/2022/12/career-opportunity-at-the-iais-senior-policy-advisor-capital-and-solvency/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The IAIS is looking for a high performing professional to join the Secretariat in the role of Senior Policy Advisor – Capital and Solvency, with a focus on supporting IAIS current and future activities related to quantitative standards, notably the Insurance Capital Standard.

For more information about the position, go to our [Recruitment](/about-the-iais/recruitment/) page or download the vacancy notice [here](/uploads/2022/12/Senior-Policy-Advisor-Capital-And-Solvency.pdf).

Applications can be submitted until 23 January 2023. Click [here](https://lde.tbe.taleo.net/lde01/ats/careers/requisition.jsp?org=BIS&cws=1&rid=1074) to apply.

For any questions, please send a message to [IAIS.Recruitment@bis.org](mailto:IAIS.Recruitment@bis.org).
