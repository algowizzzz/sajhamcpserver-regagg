# Career opportunity at the IAIS – Head of Implementation and Assessment

[17 Jan 2023](https://www.iais.org/2023/01/career-opportunity-at-the-iais-head-of-implementation-and-assessment/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The IAIS is looking for a high performing professional to join the Secretariat in the role of Head of Implementation and Assessment. The successful candidate will become a member of the Senior Management Team, reporting to the Secretary General and leading the implementation team.

For more information, go to our [Recruitment page](/about-the-iais/recruitment/) or download the vacancy notice [here](https://www.iaisweb.org/uploads/2023/01/Head-of-Implementation-and-Assessment.pdf).

Applications can be submitted until 9 February 2023. Click [here](https://lde.tbe.taleo.net/lde01/ats/careers/requisition.jsp?org=BIS&cws=1&rid=1080) to apply.

For any questions, please send a message to [IAIS.Recruitment@bis.org.](mailto:IAIS.Recruitment@bis.org)
