# IAIS publishes 2020 Annual Report

[15 Nov 2021](https://www.iais.org/2021/11/iais-publishes-2020-annual-report/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

Following the Annual General Meeting of Members, the IAIS published today its 2020 Annual Report. Find inside a special focus on how the Association adapted its work programme during the Covid-19 pandemic.

In addition to sharing learnings from this unprecedented year of disruption and change, we also look ahead to trends for upcoming work, keeping abreast of key topics for the global economy

12 Jan 2022
