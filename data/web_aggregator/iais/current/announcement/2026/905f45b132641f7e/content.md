# IAIS issues Public consultation on the Development of Liquidity Metrics – Phase 2

[18 Nov 2021](https://www.iais.org/2021/11/iais-issues-public-consultation-on-the-development-of-liquidity-metrics-phase-2/)

in [Consultations](https://www.iais.org/category/consultations/), [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The IAIS has just launched the public consultation on the Development of Liquidity Metrics – Phase 2, as a part of its process of developing an ancillary indicator for the GME that will serve as a tool to facilitate the IAIS’ monitoring of the global insurance industry’s liquidity risk and for the IAIS to assess insurers’ exposure to liquidity risk. Feedback is invited **by 23 January 2022** and a virtual public background session will be held on **30 November at 13:30 – 15:00 CET**. .  
For more information or to provide feedback please click **[here](https://www.iais.org/page/consultations/current-consultations/development-of-liquidity-metrics-phase-2/ "Development of Liquidity Metrics: Phase 2")**.

For further details on the virtual public background session please click [**here**](https://www.iais.org/page/events/stakeholder-meetings/ "Stakeholder Meetings").
