# 2026 Graduate Internship Programme at the IAIS

[27 Mar 2026](https://www.iais.org/2026/03/2026-graduate-internship-programme-at-the-iais/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The International Association of Insurance Supervisors (IAIS) is looking for recent graduates to join the 2026 Bank for International Settlements (BIS) cohort of Graduate Interns.

For more information about the position, go to our [Recruitment page](https://www.iais.org/about-the-iais/recruitment/) or download the vacancy notice [here](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2026/03/2026-Graduate-Internship-Programme.pdf).

Applications can be submitted until Tuesday 7 April 2026 23:59 CEST.  
Click [here](https://bis.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/External/job/BIS-Headquarters-Basel/XMLNAME-2026-Graduate-Internship-Programme--Insurance-Sector-Supervision-and-Risk-_JR100361/apply) to apply.
