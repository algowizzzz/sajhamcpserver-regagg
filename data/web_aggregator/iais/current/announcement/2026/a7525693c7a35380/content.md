# Registration opens for the June 2022 IAIS Global Seminar

[1 Apr 2022](https://www.iais.org/2022/04/registration-opens-for-the-june-2022-iais-global-seminar/)

in [Global Seminar](https://www.iais.org/category/global-seminar/), [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

The 2022 IAIS Global Seminar will take place in person on 16 – 17 June 2022 in Dubrovnik, Croatia.

The focus of this year’s Global Seminar is resilience in the insurance sector. With hundreds of attendees, the Global Seminar provides a platform for open dialogue on key supervisory issues for the insurance sector and a chance for the IAIS’ Executive Committee and IAIS stakeholders to directly engage with each other.

A special thank you to the Croatian Financial Services Supervisory Agency (Hanfa) for hosting the event.

Please visit the webpage of the [2022 IAIS Global Seminar](https://www.iaisweb.org/news-and-events/2022-global-seminar/) to preview the agenda, register online and find more information about the event.
