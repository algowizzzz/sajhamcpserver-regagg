# The IAIS is committed to diversity, equity and inclusion as a global membership association

[10 Nov 2022](https://www.iais.org/2022/11/the-iais-is-committed-to-diversity-equity-and-inclusion-as-a-global-membership-association/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

Following approval by the IAIS Executive Committee at its meeting in Santiago, Chile on 9 November, today we have published an expression of the value we place in DEI, why we consider it essential to the IAIS being able to accomplish its mission effectively, and our plans to keep improving.

Read the statement on our new DEI webpage [here](https://www.iais.org/about-the-iais/diversity-equity-inclusion-iais/).
