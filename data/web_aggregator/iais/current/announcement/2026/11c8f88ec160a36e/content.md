# ICS public background session

[6 Jun 2024](https://www.iais.org/2024/06/registration-is-open-for-the-public-ics-webinar/)

in [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

On **27 June** from **13:00-14:30 CEST**, the IAIS held a public background session to share details about the changes made to the candidate ICS publicly consulted in 2023.

The IAIS also provided an update on the final stages of development of the ICS ahead of its planned adoption at the end of the year, as well as the items under discussion on the implementation of the ICS from 2025 onwards.

To read more about the ICS, visit our [website](https://www.iaisweb.org/activities-topics/standard-setting/insurance-capital-standard/).

Slides for the webinar are available [here](https://www.iaisweb.org/uploads/2024/06/240626-2024-Public-ICS-webinar.pdf "https://www.iaisweb.org/uploads/2024/04/iais-public-background-session-slides-consultation-on-dei-supervisory-guidance.pdf").
