# AAWG – Stakeholder Call

[6 May 2024](https://www.iais.org/2024/05/aawg-stakeholder-call/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/), [Stakeholder events](https://www.iais.org/category/stakeholder-events/)

On 22 May 2024 from 10:15 – 11:15 EDT, the Accounting and Auditing Working Group of the IAIS hosted a stakeholder webinar to provide updates on the Working Group’s activities and gather feedback on key matters of interest on potential significant changes to IFRS accounting standards and international auditing standards impacting the insurance industry.
