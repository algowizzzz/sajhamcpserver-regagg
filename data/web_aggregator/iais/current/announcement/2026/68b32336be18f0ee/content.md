This timeline recognises that it will take some time for jurisdictions to finalise any necessary regulatory and supervisory changes to align 
with the ICS, taking into account jurisdictional circumstances, and for the IAIS to prepare for implementation assessment. 
ICS implementation assessment will follow a two-step approach, mirroring the successful model used for the IAIS’ 
implementation assessment of the Holistic Framework supervisory material.
• Baseline self-assessment by IAIS 
members of their progress in 
implementing the ICS, which will 
serve as a baseline for future 
implementation progress 
monitoring. 
• Leveraging the IAIS' general 
principles and methodologies for 
assessing its standards
• Taking into account the 
quantitative nature of the ICS
• Will not be a line-by-line exercise 
-- the methodology will specify 
an appropriate level of 
granularity and articulate the 
quantitative and qualitative 
analyses to be used.
