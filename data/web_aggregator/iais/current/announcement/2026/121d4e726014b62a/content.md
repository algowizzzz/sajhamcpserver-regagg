# Career opportunity at the IAIS

[29 Jul 2025](https://www.iais.org/2025/07/career-opportunity-at-the-iais-4/)

**Member of the Senior Management Team (Capital and Solvency)**

The IAIS is inviting applications for a senior leader to join the IAIS as a member of the Senior Management Team (SMT).

For more information about the position, go to our [Recruitment page](https://www.iais.org/about-the-iais/recruitment/) or download the vacancy notice [**here**](https://www.iais.org/uploads/2025/07/Career-Opportunity-with-the-IAIS-–-Member-of-the-Senior-Management-Team-2.pdf).

Applications can be submitted until 15 August 23:59 CEST.
