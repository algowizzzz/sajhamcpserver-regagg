IAIS announces new Executive Committee Chair 
• Toshiyuki Miyoshi, Vice Minister for International Affairs, Financial Services Agency, Japan, 
has been elected Executive Committee Chair of the IAIS.  
• Shigeru Ariizumi, Special Advisor to the Minister of State for Financial Services, Financial 
Services Agency, Japan, steps down as IAIS Executive Committee Chair after a two-year term 
marked by significant achievements. 
Tirana, Albania – The International Association of Insurance Supervisors (IAIS) today announces 
that Toshiyuki Miyoshi, Vice Minister for International Affairs, Financial Services Agency, Japan, has 
been elected IAIS Executive Committe e Chair, succeeding Shigeru Ariizumi, former Vice Minister 
for International Affairs, Financial Services Agency, Japan.  
Shigeru Ariizumi: “It has been a profound privilege to serve as Executive Committee Chair and 
collaborate with such a dedicated and talented global community of insurance supervisors. Together, 
we have achieved significant milestones for our Association, accomplishments made possible 
through every IAIS member’s unwavering support, collaboration and shared commitment to the IAIS 
mission.” 
Jonathan Dixon: “I would like to thank Shigeru for his strong leadership and significant contributions 
during his tenure . Under his guidance, the Association achieved key milestones, including the 
finalisation of the ICS, the first comprehensive global capital standard for insurance supervision—an 
accomplishment that reflects over a decade of collaboration and represents a major step forward for 
global insurance supervision. He also steered the delivery of the IAIS’ next five -year strategic plan, 
providing a clear direction for the Association’s future, and deepened our engagement in global 
policy discussions, particularly in addressing critical natural catastrophe insurance protection gaps. 
His leadership has left a lasting impact, and we wish him all the best for the future. 
At the same time, I warmly welcome Toshiyuki Miyoshi as the new Chair of the IAIS Executive 
Committee. Toshiyuki brings a wealth of financial sector experience and senior -level global 
engagement to this role at a time when the interconnections between the insurance sector and other 
parts of the financial system continue to grow. I am certain that his leadership will build on the strong 
foundation laid by Shigeru.”   
Toshiyuki Miyoshi: “It is an honour to be appointed as the new IAIS Executive Committee  Chair, 
and I am deeply grateful for the trust placed in me to lead this exceptional global community of 
insurance supervisors. I look forward to continuing the strong momentum of the IAIS, particularly as 
we advance the implementation of the ICS, deliver on our strategic objectives and continue to 
address critical challenges, such as supporting insurance to serve its societal role. Collaboration and 
shared commitment have always been the hallmarks of the IAIS, and I am excited to work alongside 
my colleagues on the Executive Committee to further our mission of strengthening effective and 
globally consistent insurance supervision.”  
Background information 
• The IAIS Executive Committee Chair is selected by Executive Committee members for a two-
year term. Toshiyuki Miyoshi’s term will run to November 2027.

• The IAIS Executive Committee is responsible for providing overall strategic direction and 
managing IAIS activities in a manner consistent with the specific duties set forth in the 
Association’s by-laws. It comprises 40 member representatives from all regions of the world, 
encompassing both advanced and developing economies.    
About the IAIS 
The IAIS is a global standard -setting body whose objectives are to promote effective and globally 
consistent supervision of the insurance industry to develop and maintain fair, safe and stable 
insurance markets for the benefit and protection of policyholde rs and to contribute to the 
maintenance of global financial stability. Its membership includes insurance supervisors from more 
than 200 jurisdictions. Learn more at  www.iais.org. Follow  us on LinkedIn: IAIS – International 
Association of Insurance Supervisors.
