# Career opportunity at the IAIS: Secretary General

[2 Sep 2025](https://www.iais.org/2025/09/career-opportunity-at-the-iais-secretary-general/)

in [Latest news](https://www.iais.org/category/latest-news/)

The IAIS is inviting applications for the position of the Secretary General of the IAIS as Jonathan Dixon’s final term concludes in August 2026.

For more information about the position, visit our [Recruitment page](https://www.iais.org/about-the-iais/recruitment/) or download the vacancy notice [here](http://iais.org/uploads/2025/09/Career-Opportunity-with-the-IAIS-%E2%80%93-Secretary-General.pdf).

Applications can be submitted until 22 September 23:59 CEST.

For any questions, please send a message to [recruitment@iais.org](mailto:recruitment@iais.org).

22 Sep 2025
