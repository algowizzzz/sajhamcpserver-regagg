This page provides our interactive dashboard of the intermediary sector, based on the 2025 data drawn from the Retail Mediation Activities Return (RMAR).

The interactive dashboards cover:

* [Revenue from regulatory activities (RMA B)](#profit)
* [Capital requirements (RMA D)](#capital)
* [Adviser staff data (RMA G)](#staff)
* [Retail investment advice and adviser charges (RMA K)](#charges)
