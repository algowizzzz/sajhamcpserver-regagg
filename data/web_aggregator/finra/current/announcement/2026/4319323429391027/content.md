# FINRA Crypto and Blockchain Education Program

Intermediate e-learning courses are now available for member firms to assign within FLEX.

FINRA’s Crypto and Blockchain Education Program is designed to equip financial professionals with a comprehensive education to understand crypto assets and blockchain technology. The program includes self-paced e-learning and an in-person applied learning course provided in partnership with Georgetown University’s McDonough School of Business. [Listen to FINRA's podcast](/media-center/finra-unscripted/previewing-finras-crypto-and-blockchain-education-program) to learn more about the program.

Topics covered in FINRA’s Crypto and Blockchain Education Program include:

* Crypto and blockchain terminology
* Blockchain operations and on-chain transactions
* Evolution of blockchain technology
* Types of crypto assets and related products
* Crypto ecosystem and markets
* Key consideration for firms with touchpoints to crypto assets and blockchain technology
* Common fraud schemes with crypto asset exposure

### Why Participate?

### Trusted Content

The Crypto and Blockchain Education Program was developed with an objective and balanced perspective by FINRA’s crypto asset and blockchain subject matter experts.

### Learning That Sticks

The program includes application-based activities and scenarios that increase retention, leveraging learning industry best practices.

### Multiple Learning Pathways

With both self-paced and in-person learning opportunities, this program includes bite-sized e-learning courses and a multiday immersive in-person course developed with the prestigious Georgetown University.
