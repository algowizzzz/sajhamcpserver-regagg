# FINRA Data

FINRA Data provides centralized access to the abundance of data FINRA makes available to the public, media, researchers and member firms.

## Featured Data

[**NMS Equity and Option Order Routing Reports (SEC Rule 606(a) Reports)**](/finra-data/606-nms-data)  
Broker-dealers that route equity and option orders on behalf of customers are required by SEC Rule 606(a) to prepare quarterly reports that disclose specific information about their order routing practices for non-directed orders in NMS stocks and NMS securities that are options contracts. The reports are published as PDFs and XML files for each calendar quarter.

[About the Data](/finra-data/about-606)

[**Fixed Income Data**](/finra-data/fixed-income)  
Go-to resource for fixed income data, including including bond details, trade activity and market statistics.
