# [ESMA publishes latest edition of its newsletter](/press-news/esma-news/esma-publishes-latest-edition-its-newsletter-44)

ESMA newsletter

31/07/2026

The European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA), the EU’s financial markets regulator and supervisor, has published today the [latest edition](/sites/default/files/2026-07/Newsletter_June_and_July_2026.pdf) of its Spotlight on Markets newsletter, covering key activities and publications from June and July 2026.

This edition opens with the [statement](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/2026-06/ESMA75-113276571-1710_Public_Statement_MiCA_transitional_period_ends.pdf) on the end of the MiCA transitional period, calling on unauthorised crypto-asset service providers to wind down their activities in an orderly manner while safeguarding clients’ interests and protecting market integrity.

Top news highlights include the [final report](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-identifies-eu1-billion-potential-annual-savings-simplifying-eu) on simplifying transaction reporting, identifying up to €1 billion in potential annual savings through a "report once" approach, ESMA’s [call on firms](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-calls-firms-finalise-preparations-ahead-t1-settlement-deadlines) to finalise preparations ahead of T+1 settlement deadlines, and the publication of [2025 Annual Report](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-2025-annual-report-focus-stronger-supervision-regulatory-simplification), focusing on stronger supervision, regulatory simplification and innovation.

Key publications featured in this edition include:

* the ESAs’ first [annual report](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esas-publish-first-report-dora-major-ict-related-incidents) on major ICT-related incidents under the Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA);
* [consultation](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-consults-simplifying-eu-taxonomy-disclosure-framework) on technical advice to simplify the EU Taxonomy disclosure framework and reduce reporting burdens for market participants.
* ESMA's [authorisation of EuroCTP](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-authorises-euroctp-consolidated-tape-provider-shares-and-exchange-traded) as the Consolidated Tape Provider for shares and exchange-traded funds.

Other updates cover the [selection of Etrading Software (Netherlands) B.V.](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-selects-etrading-software-netherlands-bv-consolidated-tape-provider-otc) as Consolidated Tape Provider for OTC derivatives, preliminary findings on the [Active Account Requirement under EMIR](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-publishes-preliminary-findings-active-account-requirement-and-first), a [report](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-publishes-report-cross-border-investment-services-supervision) on the supervision of cross-border investment services, and the [launch](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-launches-common-supervisory-action-casps-digital-operational-resilience) of Common Supervisory Actions on risk management functions and digital operational resilience for CASPs.

The [*Spotlight on Markets newsletter*](https://www.esma.europa.eu/news-publications/newsletter) is published regularly and is available on ESMA’s website. For regular updates, follow ESMA on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/european-securities-and-markets-authority-esma/), [X](https://x.com/ESMAComms) and [Instagram](https://instagram.com/esmacomms/).
