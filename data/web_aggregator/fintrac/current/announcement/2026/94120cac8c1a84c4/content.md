# Departmental plan

**From:** [Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Centre of Canada](/intro-eng) (FINTRAC)

The Departmental Plan provides parliamentarians and Canadians with information on plans and expected performance over a three-year period. The Plan also describes programs and services for Canadians and how work will fulfill departmental mandate commitments and the government's priorities.
