# Bank Secrecy Act Filing Information

![Snippet of BSA E Filing Website](/system/files/shared/BSAEfile_large.jpg)

**REMINDER:** As of April 1, 2013, financial institutions must use the new FinCEN reports, which are available only electronically through the [BSA E-Filing System](http://bsaefiling.fincen.gov/). FinCEN is no longer accepting legacy reports. For more information, click [here](/news/news-releases/notice-e-filing-mandate).

**To File a BSA report please visit us at** [BSA E-Filing System](http://bsaefiling.fincen.gov/)

**To view a BSA report or test your batch filing program please visit us at**[BSA E-Filing Test System](http://sdtmut.fincen.gov/). Do not mail or electronically attempt to file a test report.

#### Customer Due Diligence (CDD) – APPENDIX A - Certification Form

The CDD Certification Form, APPENDIX A (MS Word or fillable PDF version) is an optional form providing a convenient way for institutions to obtain and record information required by the CDD rule.  The MS Word version should be printed out and completed. The PDF version may be completed (filled-in) on a computer then printed out.  Make a copy of the certification form and maintain  the entity’s financial institution where the account is established.

DO NOT SEND TO FinCEN!
