# European Regional Committee

The ERC is one of four regional committees constituted by IOSCO to focus on regional issues relating to securities regulation in the European region.

## Members of the European Regional Committee

### Chair

Mr. Jean-Paul Servais, Chairman, Financial Services and Markets Authority, **Belgium**

![Mr. Jean-Paul Servais](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Jean-Paul-Servais-250.png)

Jean-Paul Servais is the Chairman of the IOSCO Board. He is also Chairman of the IOSCO European Regional Committee.

Mr Servais is the Chairman of the Belgian Financial Services and Markets Authority (FSMA) and a board member of several international supervisory bodies for the financial sector such as ESMA and the ESRB. He sits on a number of supervisory colleges that coordinate the (cross-border) supervision of several financial institutions and infrastructures.

He teaches at the Université Libre de Bruxelles (ULB, University of Brussels), where he is part-time professor in International Business Law and in the specialized Master in Tax Law.

### Vice Chair

Mr. Colm Kincaid, Deputy Governor, Consumer & Investor Protection, Central Bank of Ireland, **Ireland**

![Colm Kincaid](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Colm-Kincaid-250.jpg)

Colm Kincaid was appointed Deputy Governor, Consumer and Investor Protection on 1st August 2025.

Colm is responsible for leading the strategic development and execution of the Central Bank’s consumer and investor protection mandate across all sectors including banking & payments, insurance and capital markets. In addition, Colm has specific responsibility for overseeing Supervisory Risk Analytics & Data, Enforcement and the Horizontal Supervision Directorates.

With over 15 years of senior public service experience, Colm has held several leadership roles in the organisation, namely Director of Enforcement, Director of Consumer Protection and Director of Securities and Markets Supervision. Colm holds a first class honours Law degree from Trinity College and a Diploma in Company Direction from the Institute of Directors. He joined the Central Bank in 2004, having previously practised as a solicitor in London and Dublin.

### Board Representative

Dr. Vasiliki Lazarakou, Chairperson, Hellenic Capital Market Commission, **Greece**

![Vasiliki Lazarakou](/about/chairs/images/tiny/Vasiliki-Lazarakou-250.jpg)

Vasiliki Lazarakou is Chair of the Hellenic Capital Market Commission. She is a Member of ESMA Board of Supervisors and was a member of ESMA Management Board for 5 years, until December 2025.

Vasiliki is a member of the IOSCO Board and Chair of the IOSCO Diversity Network.

She has been re-elected as a member of the Bureau of the OECD Corporate Governance Committee for 2026, continuing her role which began in 2022.

She has been elected Vice Chair of the Mediterranean Partnership of Securities Commission for 2025 and 2026.

She represents Greece at the Women Climate Leaders Network of the European Investment Bank Group.

She has a Doctorate in Law (JSD) and an LL.M. from New York University School of Law, U.S.A. as well as a Bachelor in Law from the Athens University. She is a lawyer admitted at the Athens Bar and the New York Bar Association. She has worked for more than 20 years as a Partner at law firms and Head of the Banking and Finance Departments.
